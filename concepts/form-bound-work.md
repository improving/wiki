# form-bound work

Form-bound work is the part of a job defined by structure, format, and documentation — the shape of a deliverable rather than its substance. Across this corpus it pairs with judgment-bound work: form-bound tasks can be delegated to generative AI with rubric-based verification, while judgment-bound interpretation and recommendation stay with the human.

## Key Claims

- Every development role splits into form-bound and judgment-bound work.
- Form-bound work — structure, format, documentation — can be automated; judgment-bound work needs human reasoning.
- Judgment-bound work benefits from Socratic AI that pressure-tests reasoning; form-bound work benefits from generative AI with human verification.
- Confusing the two produces artifacts that look complete but lack substance.
- Judgment-bound errors are the most expensive because the output ships; architecture has the highest judgment-bound concentration and AI-misuse risk.
- AI should free judgment time, not just produce form-bound artifacts faster.
