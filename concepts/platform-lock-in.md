# Platform Lock-In

Platform lock-in is the cost of building on a single vendor's models, protocols, and interfaces when the vendor landscape is still consolidating. The two sources in this corpus pull in opposite directions: one argues the safe bet is now architectural flexibility because former partners are becoming competitors, the other that converging spend, code share, and valuation have effectively settled the enterprise AI platform question.

## Key Claims

- Microsoft is training salespeople to pitch against OpenAI and Anthropic and is swapping their models out of Word and Excel.
- Google, Salesforce, Snowflake, and ServiceNow are building a counter-protocol, so the alliance map is unstable.
- Open standards and API-first integration are what survive vendor consolidation.
- Against that: Anthropic raised $65B at a $965B valuation and Claude holds 42–54% of enterprise code generation share.
- Ramp's AI Index shows Claude passing GPT in business AI spend, and teams that committed twelve months ago are twelve months ahead.
