# forward deployed engineering

Forward deployed engineering (FDE) puts a delivery team inside the client's environment — with the client's data, constraints, and stakeholders — before an enterprise rollout is approved, and asks them to ship something real in a quarter rather than a year. Across this corpus it is presented as the methodology consultancies must now name, package, and price, not a side effect of good staffing.

## Key Claims

- The winning firms ship something in production in a quarter, not a year; expertise-deck pitches are losing ground to demonstrated delivery.
- [IBM](/entities/ibm.md) embedded GPT-5.6 into the platform consultants use daily, certified consultants, and added forward-deployed engineers — structural change rather than rebranding.
- Most AI practices are rebranding, not restructuring; firms must rebuild delivery infrastructure around AI rather than decorate it.
- TCS plans to convert up to 8,900 engineers into forward-deployed roles, and forward-deployed job postings grew 729% in twelve months.
- FDE stops differentiating by its existence once everyone can field it; differentiation shifts to closeness to the business problem and driving outcomes.
- [Improving](/entities/improving.md) already works this way but never packaged or priced it as the offering.
