# Madman Architect Carpenter Judge

A four-role model of the creative process, credited to [Betty Flowers](/entities/betty-flowers.md): the madman generates, the architect organizes, the carpenter refines, and the judge critiques. Its real insight, per this corpus, is that the roles are thinking modes that must happen in order rather than skills you pick between.

## Key Claims

- The madman freely generates raw ideas without worrying about coherence.
- After a break, the architect groups ideas and shapes the underlying argument.
- The carpenter then refines structure, flow, and sentence-level clarity.
- The judge reviews critically against the audience and seeks feedback.
- The judge showing up early kills divergent generation — sequence is the whole point.
- Distance between stages prevents organizing around enthusiasm instead of insight.
- The framework applies well beyond writing — to strategy, team processes, and building AI systems.
