# Productivity amplification

Productivity amplification is the mechanism by which AI lets one person produce more within the same hours, as distinct from task automation, which removes work outright. It is one of the two forces behind labor compression, and what happens next depends on whether demand or execution capacity is the binding constraint.

## Key Claims

- Automation historically compresses hours per output rather than eliminating roles; the ATM and spreadsheet cases show compression, not replacement.
- Two mechanisms drive compression: productivity amplification and task automation.
- Value capture depends on whether demand or execution capacity is the limiting factor.
- High time impact plus high demand means redeploying freed capacity into demand generation.
- High time impact plus low demand forces honest headcount or role-growth decisions.
- Quadrant assignments expire as AI capability rises and cost falls.
