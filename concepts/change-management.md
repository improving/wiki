# Change Management

Change management is the work of bringing people through a change rather than merely deploying it. This corpus insists the failure is rarely the technology: rollouts break on trust, and a change delivered without its reason leaves the people affected holding only fear.

## Key Claims

- 29% of employees admit sabotaging their company's AI rollout, and employee confidence in company AI strategy fell from 47% to 31%.
- Smooth rollouts scored leadership trust 3.15 points higher than struggling ones; trust must be earned before compliance is asked.
- AI rollouts fail on trust, not technology, because employees were never asked how the change affects them.
- Change has two parts — the change itself and its reason; the change almost always causes fear in those impacted.
- The reason is the anchor point of future vision: its presence inspires courage, while its absence leaves only willpower.
- Giving only the change is not a plan for healthy change.
