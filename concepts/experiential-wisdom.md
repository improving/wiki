# experiential wisdom

The distinction between possessing knowledge and being able to apply it well in a real context. Across this corpus, wisdom is not accumulated information but knowledge tempered by action — and it is gained mainly by misapplying knowledge and learning from the result.

## Key Claims

- Wisdom is the right application of knowledge in a context, not the knowledge itself.
- We gain wisdom by misapplying knowledge in context — the error is the teacher.
- Sites like Quora deliver "virtual wisdom" that is like brain candy: satisfying, but not the real thing.
- People collect knowledge and parade it as wisdom; experience is the only true impartation of it.
- Knowledge untouched by action remains merely academic.
- Leaders bring others along by owning their mistakes; we can only learn from mistakes we actually own.
