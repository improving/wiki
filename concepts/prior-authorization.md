# prior authorization

Prior authorization is the payer approval a patient must obtain before certain care or procedures are covered. It is a flashpoint because denials delay treatment and a large share are overturned on review, which is why regulators are forcing faster, more transparent decisions and why AI governance — not just automation — is the focus.

## Key Claims

- 80% of prior authorization denials are overturned on review, and the AMA reports 60% of physicians believe AI tools cause unnecessary claim denials.
- Texas, Arizona, and Maryland prohibit fully automated medical necessity denials; Colorado mandates annual AI impact assessments.
- CMS interoperability by 2026 requires 80% of prior authorization decisions to be real-time using FHIR, with denial reasons made public.
- The WISER Model is a six-year Medicare pilot using AI to review prior authorizations.
- CMS holds health plans accountable for the AI they license — "we bought it from a vendor" is not a defense.
- Governance is the enabling condition: observability, bias testing, formal risk analyses, acceptable-risk thresholds, and human-in-the-loop review for gray-area decisions.
