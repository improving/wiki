# corporate retreat

A corporate retreat is a company-wide gathering held to build relationships and culture rather than to transact business. Improving's annual Las Vegas retreat is the running case in this corpus: it grew from a few hundred attendees to roughly 1,500 across multiple continents, and every account frames its value as the friendships that outlast the event rather than the scheduled activities.

## Key Claims

- The 2021 Las Vegas retreat drew Improvers from every US, Mexico, and Canada office, with more than 100 first-time attendees including the Nearshore team after the 2020 iTexico merger.
- The 2023 retreat was the third official gathering, with 400+ Improvers at Planet Hollywood over three days.
- A later retreat gathered roughly 1,500 Improvers from three countries and sixteen sites for three days of concurrent activities, meals, and social events.
- The 2025 retreat was the first to include South America, Guatemala, and India alongside teams from Mexico, Canada, and the USA.
- Concurrent activity tracks force attendees to plan their own agenda; recurring elements include the Amazing Race, a Valley of Fire hike, learning and games rooms, and leadership celebrations.
- Organizers judge success by the friendships that outlast the event, not the scheduled activities.
- The retreat is presented as the mechanism that makes a large distributed company feel like a family, tied to belonging, gratitude, and conscious capitalism.
