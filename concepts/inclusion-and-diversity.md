# inclusion and diversity

Inclusion and diversity is the work of building equitable, multicultural workplaces where women, LGBTQ+ employees, and other underrepresented groups can do their best work. In this corpus the case is made both economically — diverse teams innovate more — and culturally, through initiatives that create safe spaces, visible role models, and deliberate moments of celebration rather than tolerance.

## Key Claims

- NCWIT reported women held 27 percent of US professional computing occupations in 2020; McKinsey (2023) put women at 21 percent of tech executive leadership roles.
- A BCG study found companies with more diverse management teams have 19 percent higher revenue due to innovation.
- Barriers include unconscious bias, stereotypes, and a lack of female role models, addressed through mentorship and awareness campaigns.
- Recruitment should diversify channels, use women-focused networking events, and partner with women-in-STEM organizations; retention requires inclusive policies such as flexible hours, remote options, and family-friendly benefits.
- Women of Improving runs global and local events on career confidence, self-advocacy, and imposter syndrome in emotionally safe spaces, and encourages visible role models beyond the company.
- At Guadalajara Pride, Improving covered travel for any employee who wanted to attend; an attendee found a celebration that wanted to "celebrate diversity, not just tolerate it."
- Diverse teams broaden solution options and reduce the bias baked into technology, and meeting design can be adapted to include neurodivergent participants.
