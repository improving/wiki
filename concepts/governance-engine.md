# Governance engine

The governance engine is the layer that makes autonomy safe by enforcing boundaries, recording what happened, and inserting approval points where risk demands them. In this corpus it is the second layer of the [Intelligence Operating System](/concepts/intelligence-operating-system.md) and the prerequisite for shifting from permission to autonomy.

## Key Claims

- The IOS separates foundational from application concerns, as an operating system does; Layer 2 is the governance engine.
- The governance engine provides audit trails, boundary enforcement, and approval points.
- Layers 1 and 3 resist purchase; only parts of governance are buyable off the shelf.
- AI dissolves Agile's scarcity-driven trade-offs, turning the manifesto's "over" into a multiplication between both sides.
- Three eras — Constraint, Synergy, Intent — map to rising capability levels, and the [Intent Era](/concepts/intent-era.md) is where autonomy dominates.
- The permission-to-autonomy shift needs a governance engine to be safe, so governance is the enabler rather than the brake.
