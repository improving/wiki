# Commitment Accounting

Commitment accounting treats every promise as a check drawn on a personal credibility account: it must be covered, or renegotiated before it bounces. It matters because professional trust compounds, and the reputational cost of a missed commitment dwarfs the value of the original promise.

## Key Claims

- The reputational cost of a bounced commitment far exceeds the value of the original promise.
- Each successive ask to the same person costs more than the last.
- Renegotiating early costs a fraction of missing a deadline without communication.
- Closing the loop on completed commitments deposits currency into the relationship.
- Careers compound like wealth; daily habits include notepads, five-minute favors, and commitment confirmations.
- Relationships and reputation outlast depreciating technical skills.
