# shadow IT

Shadow IT is technology adopted by business users outside the sanctioned, IT-approved stack — historically spreadsheet sprawl, now AI tools brought to work without review. The corpus treats the pattern as structural rather than delinquent: users route around a process that is too slow to serve them, so restriction-based governance fails and enablement wins.

## Key Claims

- 78% of AI users at work bring tools outside IT approval.
- Review processes built for six-month IT programs cannot govern day-long AI experiments.
- IBM found unsanctioned AI tools inside 43% of security incidents, more than doubling in a year.
- Governance should scale with reversibility rather than restriction, so the sanctioned option becomes competitive.
- The earlier spreadsheet case shows the deeper cost: manually manipulated data is "dead," rarely reproducible by anyone but its author, and hard to move back into other systems.
