# developer experience

Developer experience (DX) is the sum of every interaction a developer has with a tool or platform — setup, discovery, deployment, debugging — and the corpus treats it as "the new UX," a product to be researched and improved rather than an afterthought. Its key distinction is between external developers who choose your tools and internal developers who have tools chosen for them, which need different playbooks.

## Key Claims

- DX covers both external developers (product, docs, website) and internal developers using your APIs and platform, and companies wrongly apply identical strategies to the two ([know your developer](/sources/know-your-developer.md)).
- External developers discover tools via Google, ChatGPT, Reddit, GitHub issues, and conference talks rather than vendor websites, and success is measured in mind share — GitHub stars and forks, Docker pulls, conference talks, community contributions ([know your developer](/sources/know-your-developer.md)).
- Internal developers adopt a company-standardized platform, discovering tools via service catalogs, internal developer portals, Teams/Slack channels, and lunch-and-learns; internal success is measured in developer velocity, platform adoption, time-to-first-deployment, support-ticket reduction, and infrastructure cost per service ([know your developer](/sources/know-your-developer.md)).
- A shared Find-Investigate-Run-Ship-Transform (FIRST) journey frames DX, with evaluation demanding working examples, architecture diagrams, benchmarks, sandboxes, and one-command installs; anything gated behind "contact sales" is a disqualifier ([know your developer](/sources/know-your-developer.md)).
- "Know Your Developer" should be the foundation of every DX strategy ([know your developer](/sources/know-your-developer.md)).
- Feedback baked into the platform is the capability DORA's 2025 research most correlated with positive developer experience ([IDP cognitive load](/sources/internal-developer-portal-what-reduces-cognitive-load.md)).
- Maersk's IDP standardized security practices and commit formatting, reducing cognitive load so developers could focus on product work ([Maersk IDP](/sources/elevating-developer-experience-maersks-path-to-a-more-user-centric.md)).
