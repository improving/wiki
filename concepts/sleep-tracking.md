# Sleep Tracking

Sleep tracking is the practice of measuring sleep continuously with a wearable so that duration, stages, and recovery can be inspected as data rather than guessed at. Across this corpus it is the entry point of a broader health journey: the same device that tracks sleep also supplies the [heart rate variability](/concepts/heart-rate-variability.md) and strain numbers used to judge recovery.

## Key Claims

- Only one to three percent of people can survive on five hours of sleep or less, which makes sleep debt a real constraint rather than a badge of discipline.
- Whoop's battery-pack charging scheme enables near-100% uptime, so tracking continues without removing the strap.
- Polyphasic sleep, medications, and a 73-hour wake reset all failed to fix the author's sleep; more data, not a magic device, became the hope.
- Continuous wear enables nearly continuous monitoring, and competition with brothers on strain scores drives ongoing engagement.
- The WHOOP strap has been the foundation of the author's health efforts since May 2019.
