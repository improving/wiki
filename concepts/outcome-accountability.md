# outcome accountability

Outcome accountability means an initiative is judged by the business result it produces — ideally a pre-defined dollar figure owned by one named person — rather than by how much work it generated. In this corpus it is the dividing line between enterprise AI programs that show measurable returns and the large majority that do not.

## Key Claims

- 61% of leaders face more pressure to prove AI ROI, but only 23% can; only 5–8% of enterprises show measurable impact at scale.
- 37% of organizations report AI contributing to EBIT — unchanged even as investment roughly doubles.
- Dashboards tracking chatbot usage measure activity, not ROI; winners embed AI in high-volume workflows with clean before-and-after metrics.
- Winners tie each initiative to an outcome with a dollar figure and a single owner, and redesign roles instead of layering AI onto existing ones.
- Defining ROI after the pilot is post-hoc rationalization, not measurement.
