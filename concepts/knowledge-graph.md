# knowledge graph

A graph of typed entities and relationships that grounds retrieval and reasoning in explicit structure rather than vector proximity. It matters because it is the shared substrate that capability registries, semantic search, and principles-first reasoning can all read from, keeping an AI system's knowledge semantically grounded.

## Key Claims

- DNS solved naming but never answered health, version, or authorization; Consul, etcd, and Zookeeper added live, health-checked service indexes.
- MCP, A2A, and NANDA AgentFacts all converge on capability-first descriptions, and a capability registry and semantic search should read one shared knowledge graph.
- Search-index retrieval struggles with reasoning from principles to novel cases.
- [Helix](/entities/helix.md) organizes domains into principles, concepts, processes, and functions.
- Dependency-ordered ingestion keeps the knowledge graph semantically grounded.
- Phase-granularity relearning avoids the seams left by surgical corrections.
