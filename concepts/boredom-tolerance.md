# Boredom Tolerance

Boredom tolerance is the capacity to stay with an idle, under-stimulated moment instead of reaching for a device. This corpus frames it as the price of deep thinking — idle time used to be where reflection happened — and, importantly, as personal wiring rather than a universal virtue.

## Key Claims

- Most people reach for their phone the moment they are bored, and devices fill the idle moments previously used for thinking.
- Deep thinking is hard and requires time and focus; the author commits to sitting quietly instead of reaching for his phone.
- About half of people experience boredom as generative fuel, while others find it aversive and reach for stimulation.
- For some people work itself is the restoration, so rest is personal wiring rather than a universal prescription.
- The two-week unplug is a preference, not a prescription.
