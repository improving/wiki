# functional medicine

Functional medicine approaches health as a set of interconnected systems, using broad testing and personalized recommendations to address root causes rather than isolated symptoms. In this corpus it is the lens behind a personal longevity project aimed at making the 60th birthday life's midpoint.

## Key Claims

- Functional medicine prompted the health journey, which catalogs supplements and testing services as they are tried.
- The stated goal is to make the 60th birthday life's midpoint, an explicit longevity target.
- Epigenetics and DNA methylation research inform the interest, linking the approach to [biological age](/concepts/biological-age.md).
- [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md)'s beta offered two rounds of 100+ [biomarker tests](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md) per year for $499, discovered via [Dr. Mark Hyman](/entities/mark-hyman.md).
- Testing is delivered through partner labs such as Quest Labs.
- Recommendations come from a functional medicine perspective, tying raw results to interpretation.
