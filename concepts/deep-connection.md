# deep connection

Deep connection is the deliberate choice to be less efficient in order to be more present. This corpus argues that optimizing every minute and treating busyness as a badge of honor quietly crowds out the relationships that make work and life worthwhile.

## Key Claims

- Optimizing every minute makes us miss important details that a slower pace would catch.
- The right measure is value provided, not the schedule kept.
- Choose one thing to do less efficiently so you can do it more thoroughly — connection is a prime candidate.
- Meetings should be squeezed for connection, not just minutes.
- Busyness has become a badge of honor rather than a recognized problem, and it is often a way to avoid depth.
- Ask "how is the state of your heart" rather than "how are you" — the second question invites a status report, the first invites a person.
