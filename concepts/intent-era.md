# Intent Era

The Intent Era is the third of three eras of Agile, defined by what binds a team's ability to deliver value. Where Era 1 (Constraint) managed scarce human execution attention and Era 2 (Synergy) replaced tradeoffs with multiplication as AI removed the execution constraint, Era 3 makes human intent the exponent on productivity.

## Key Claims

- The [Agile Manifesto](/entities/agile-manifesto.md) encoded scarcity, prioritizing what limited human capacity produced most value.
- AI turns the manifesto's "over" into a multiplication sign between both sides rather than a trade-off.
- Three eras — Constraint, Synergy, Intent — map to rising capability levels.
- The diagnostic for any team is what binds its ability to deliver value; in the Intent Era that is the clarity and quality of human intent.
- The permission-to-autonomy shift needs a [governance engine](/concepts/governance-engine.md) to be safe.
- Naming recurring patterns precisely, per the [shared vocabulary](/concepts/shared-vocabulary.md) argument, is a force multiplier for the intent layer.
