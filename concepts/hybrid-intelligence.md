# Hybrid intelligence

Hybrid intelligence is the designed integration of neural capability, symbolic constraints, and human judgment rather than a choice between them. Across this corpus it is presented as the foundational layer that makes enterprise AI reliable and deployable, and it is explicitly described as something that must be designed rather than purchased.

## Key Claims

- The neural-versus-symbolic debate rests on a false dichotomy; reliable systems need both.
- Neural capability without [symbolic constraints](/concepts/symbolic-ai.md) produces unpredictable, harmful outputs.
- Symbolic constraints without neural capability produce brittle compliance.
- [Governance](/concepts/ai-governance.md) is the component most often missing and most needed for deployment.
- In the [Intelligence Operating System](/concepts/intelligence-operating-system.md), hybrid intelligence is Layer 1: a designed integration of human judgment and AI execution, which resists being bought off the shelf.
- The [P.O.S.T. AI](/sources/the-p-o-s-t-ai-world-resetting-the-agile-manifesto-for-the-age-of-agents.md) argument ties the permission-to-autonomy shift to a [governance engine](/concepts/governance-engine.md) that keeps it safe.
