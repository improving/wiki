# judgment-bound work

Judgment-bound work is the portion of knowledge work that depends on human interpretation, synthesis, and recommendation. Across this corpus it is the counterpart to [form-bound work](/concepts/form-bound-work.md): the AI split argues that form-bound structure should be automated so scarce judgment time stays protected.

## Key Claims

- Judgment-bound work — interpretation, synthesis, recommendation — belongs to the human; form-bound work — structure, format, documentation — can be automated.
- Judgment-bound work benefits from Socratic AI that pressure-tests reasoning, while form-bound work benefits from generative AI with rubric-based human verification.
- Confusing the two produces artifacts that look complete but lack substance.
- AI-filled prioritization scores can quietly outsource judgment to a formula.
- Every development role splits into form-bound and judgment-bound work; judgment-bound errors are the most expensive because the output ships.
- Architecture has the highest judgment-bound concentration and therefore the highest AI misuse risk; AI should free judgment time, not merely produce form-bound artifacts faster.
