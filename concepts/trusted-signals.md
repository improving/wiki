# trusted signals

Trusted signals are reliable, verifiable indicators that tell a team an autonomous agent's work is actually sound. Across this corpus they are the precondition for delegation: Stages 5 and 6 of the role evolution — delegating whole workflows and coordinating many agents in parallel — are gated on trusted signals and adversarial validation.

## Key Claims

- Delegation requires trusted signals and [adversarial validators](/concepts/adversarial-validators.md) before handoff.
- Each maturity stage, and each [orchestration layer](/concepts/orchestration-layer.md), costs roughly seven times the effort of the one below it.
- Stage 5 delegates whole workflows to autonomous agents on trusted signals; Stage 6 coordinates parallel agent streams through branch isolation, merge strategy, and arbitration.
- Stage 5 demands developer-level skill — the hard gate for non-engineers — while the coding [Competency Floor](/concepts/competency-floor.md) never moves even as judgment work rises.
- Weak status gates multiply false confidence across a whole portfolio.
