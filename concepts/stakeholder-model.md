# stakeholder model

The stakeholder model holds that a business creates value for everyone it touches — employees, suppliers, customers, and the community — not only for shareholders. It sits at the center of [conscious capitalism](/concepts/conscious-capitalism.md): profit is assumed and required, but the question becomes how that profit is spent and who benefits from it.

## Key Claims

- The stakeholder model extends value beyond shareholders to employees, suppliers, community, and customers.
- Business requires profits to keep providing opportunity; the model reframes profit as a means rather than the endpoint.
- Conscious capitalism assumes profit but focuses on how it is spent.
- [Improving](/entities/improving.md) was built on Conscious Capitalism ideas from the start.
- Culture happens by default or by decision, so it needs deliberate attention rather than drifting.
