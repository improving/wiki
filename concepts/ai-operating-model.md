# AI operating model

The AI operating model is the designed structure of roles, decisions, and enforcement that determines who decides at each stage of an AI delivery loop. The sources distinguish it sharply from an "AI service line": 70% of consulting firms now have the latter, but an AI-structured delivery model is an operational commitment, not marketing. Advantage concentrates in the operating model — access to context, speed of integration, and judgment at the margin — because the underlying models are increasingly commoditized.

## Key Claims

- An AI service line is a marketing decision; AI-structured delivery is an operational commitment, and buyers should ask a partner to describe a structural change from the last 12 months, not a new offering.
- An [AI-native organization](/concepts/ai-native-organization.md) shows four marks: roles designed around judgment, the [Intelligence Operating System](/concepts/intelligence-operating-system.md) built as infrastructure, different measurement, and competing on the operating model.
- Feature-model improvement is capped at 10–30%; rebuilding around the foundation lets three people do work that previously needed ten.
- Vendors may ship audit trails and access control as commodity features, but the operating model — which agents get how much autonomy over which decisions — stays with the business.
- An [AI Center of Excellence](/concepts/ai-center-of-excellence.md)'s first job is sorting practices into three tiers (centrally mandatory, shared default, fully local) — an operating-model decision before an org-chart one.
- When AI raises everyone's productivity equally, the gain is competed away; advantage moves to access to context, integration speed, and judgment at the margin.
