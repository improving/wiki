# AI ROI measurement

AI ROI measurement is the discipline of tying AI investment to business outcomes rather than to consumption. The corpus's shared diagnosis is a category error: organizations instrument seats, tokens, and chatbot sessions — activity — and call it return, leaving leaders unable to show what AI actually produced.

## Key Claims

- 61% of leaders face more pressure to prove AI ROI, but only 23% can.
- Dashboards tracking chatbot usage measure activity, not ROI; defining ROI after the pilot is post-hoc rationalization, not measurement.
- Winners tied each initiative to an outcome with a dollar figure and one named owner before starting.
- An employee spent $81,000 on API tokens in one day building a game; cost limits are table stakes, and the real gap is outcome instrumentation.
- Uber reportedly spent its entire 2026 AI budget in four months — spend without a provable return.
- Cognition's $10M AI Productivity Guarantee funds Devin usage up to that figure and requires estimating human-equivalent engineering hours per session, shifting ROI conversations to receipts when the vendor is financially exposed.
