# shitty first draft

The shitty first draft is the deliberately unpolished output produced before any critical judgment is applied — the visible result of letting the madman role run first. Both sources in this corpus derive it from Betty Flowers' four roles (madman, architect, carpenter, judge) and stress that the roles must happen in sequence: the judge showing up early kills divergent generation.

## Key Claims

- Flowers identified four thinking modes that must occur in order, and distance between stages prevents organizing around enthusiasm instead of insight.
- The madman freely generates raw ideas without worrying about coherence.
- After a break, the architect groups ideas and shapes the underlying argument; the carpenter refines structure, flow, and clarity.
- The judge critically reviews against the audience and seeks feedback — and only then.
- The framework extends beyond writing to strategy, team design, and building AI systems.
