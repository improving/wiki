# Quiet Drift

Quiet drift is the slow accumulation of AI errors nobody catches because a busy human is the only checkpoint. In Stage 2 of the AI maturity curve teams keep their process intact and merely draft faster, so gains stay personal and ephemeral while AI is confidently wrong — inventing action items and mis-sequencing schedules.

## Key Claims

- Stage 2 keeps the process intact and makes it faster; gains are personal and ephemeral, so the team cannot compound learning.
- Product and project roles climb the same AI maturity curve as engineers.
- AI is confidently wrong: it invents action items and mis-sequences schedules.
- The PM's competency floor is telling reported status from true status.
- Quiet drift is the real risk when only a busy human checks output, and it flows upward unchecked.
