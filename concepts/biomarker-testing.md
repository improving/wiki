# biomarker testing

Biomarker testing is the practice of drawing blood for a broad panel of measured markers and repeating it over time, rather than relying on a single annual physical or on how you feel. Across this corpus it is the data layer that makes personal health protocols legible, and the subject of a direct comparison between two commercial platforms.

## Key Claims

- [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md)'s beta membership cost $499 a year for two rounds of 100+ biomarkers, delivered through partner labs such as Quest Labs.
- [InsideTracker](/entities/insidetracker.md) was judged solid but not compelling enough to keep; its InnerAge estimate combines tested markers into a single biological age.
- Multiple tests over time matter more than any single data point, which is why recurring baselines beat one-off panels.
- Function Health maps each biomarker to context, causes, foods, and supplements, and its recommendations come from a functional medicine perspective.
- Six months of supplementation, recovery, and diet moved biological age from 47.6 to roughly 40 while workouts stayed the same.
- One post argues biology is too individual for one-size-fits-all advice, so data should guide decisions over subjective feeling.
