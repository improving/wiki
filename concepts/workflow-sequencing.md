# workflow sequencing

Workflow sequencing is the order in which a team moves workflows from human checking to programmatic protection. Stage 4 replaces hand review with a [protection harness](/concepts/protection-harness.md) of automated gates, and this corpus argues the sequencing — automate the simplest workflows first, prove the gates hold, then climb — determines whether the team's velocity compounds or its blind spots multiply.

## Key Claims

- A protection harness automates gates so humans stop inspecting every step.
- Teams should offload the least complex workflows first and earn their way up.
- The harness is only as good as its weakest gate; a weak gate stamps a blind spot across every generated artifact.
- A focused four-to-six person Stage 4 team can outpace an eight-to-ten person team.
- Non-technical practitioners cap out at advising on team-built workflows rather than authoring them.
