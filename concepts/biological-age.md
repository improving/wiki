# biological age

Biological age is an estimate of how old the body appears to be, derived by combining tested blood biomarkers into a single physiological score. In this corpus it is the feedback signal for a self-directed health program: a number that tells you whether supplementation, recovery, and diet are actually moving anything.

## Key Claims

- [InsideTracker](/entities/insidetracker.md)'s InnerAge combines tested biomarkers into a biological age estimate; [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md) maps each biomarker to context, causes, foods, and supplements.
- Six months of supplementation, recovery, and diet cut biological age from 47.6 to roughly 40 — nearly 7.5 years — while workouts stayed the same.
- Multiple tests over time matter more than a single data point, which makes repeated measurement the point.
- Daily achievement tracking did not stick and was abandoned after a week; the platform's data discipline, not its gamification, carried the value.
- The InsideTracker platform was solid but not compelling enough to keep, showing that a metric only matters if it changes behavior.
