# Personal Accountability

Personal accountability is the stance that the person closest to a problem owns its correction, rather than explaining why they could not act. The corpus treats it as both a private discipline and an organizational survival trait: decline is described as a slow accumulation of denial and finger-pointing that only individual ownership reverses.

## Key Claims

- Life, reputation, and influence mirror the choices we make; live in the now, the only thing you control.
- People practice denial through rationalizations about why they could not act, and rarely acknowledge that they themselves are the problem.
- If you see something that can be corrected, you must do it.
- Failure is acceptable; a failure of nerve is not.
- Excellence is a form of deviance — achieved by doing what normal people avoid — and you cannot do everything well, but you can do some things well.
