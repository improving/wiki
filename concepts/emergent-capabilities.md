# Emergent Capabilities

Emergent capabilities are abilities a model displays that were not present, or not predictable, at smaller scales. Across this corpus they are the central anomaly for forecasting: if capability arrives as phase transitions rather than smooth curves, pilots with smaller models bound what is possible rather than predict production behavior.

## Key Claims

- Emergent abilities appeared as phase transitions at critical model scale thresholds.
- Scaling laws made AI look like normal science with predictable returns, but emergence and persistent anomalies challenge that framework.
- Schaeffer et al. argue apparent emergence is often an artifact of discontinuous metrics.
- A non-ergodic framework says aggregate capability can mask per-use-case variability.
- Safety evals on current models may not predict future capability jumps.
- Build evaluation and safety structures that work under both the normal-science and paradigm-shift frames.
