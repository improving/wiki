# Competency Floor

The competency floor is the minimum judgment a role must retain to distinguish a plausible AI draft from a true one. It matters because Stage 2 adoption — using AI to draft faster without changing the process — leaves only a busy human checking output, so quiet drift flows upward unchecked.

## Key Claims

- Stage 2 keeps the process intact and makes it faster, but gains are personal and ephemeral.
- Product roles climb the same AI maturity curve as engineers.
- Quiet drift is the real risk when only a busy human checks output.
- The clerical layer of project management compresses but the role broadens.
- AI is confidently wrong: it invents action items and mis-sequences schedules.
- The PM's competency floor is telling reported status from true status.
