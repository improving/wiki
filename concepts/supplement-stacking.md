# supplement stacking

Supplement stacking is the practice of assembling many individual supplements — and later peptides — into a timed daily protocol, then adjusting the stack as testing and personal experience accumulate. It matters here because it is the concrete output of a [biomarker testing](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md) and self-experimentation loop: products enter or leave the routine based on observed effects, not marketing.

## Key Claims

- The daily stack spans [AG1](/entities/ag1.md), [IDLife](/entities/idlife.md), NAD, and other supplements split across morning, lunch, and evening.
- Peptides BPC-157, TB-500, Epithalon, and DSIP target joints, longevity, and sleep.
- AG1 produced noticeable energy and focus changes within days, and its ingredient list informed adjustments to other supplements.
- Eating protein before carbs and cutting late alcohol improved glucose and sleep.
- Consistent unwinding habits raised nightly sleep from 5 to 6.5 hours.
