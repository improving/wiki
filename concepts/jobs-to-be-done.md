# jobs to be done

Jobs to be done (JTBD) frames a product around the job a user is trying to accomplish, rather than around personality-based personas or a feature list. Improving writers note that UX has shifted from personality-based personas toward task-based framing, and that many experts now favor JTBD — especially for data products, where users arrive with predefined questions. The framing carries into the AI era, where business roles are told to describe their value in outcomes rather than tasks.

## Key Claims

- UX has shifted from personality-based personas toward task-based framing, and many experts favor the Jobs to Be Done framework.
- In data-driven products, users arrive with predefined questions; discovery through interviews, empathy mapping, and user stories surfaces the jobs behind them.
- Discovery should answer who the data is for, what benefit the visualization provides, what mental models and terminology apply, and how it can be tested.
- As generative AI takes over artifact production, the business side's center of gravity moves to judgment, framing, and storytelling — describing work in outcomes, not tasks.
- AI output should be paired with a simple decision rule anchored to the outcome the job is meant to achieve.
