# ESG reporting

ESG reporting measures an organization's environmental, social, and governance performance so it can account for more than financial results and track long-term stakeholder value. The profusion of overlapping frameworks — ESG, SASB, GRI, TCFD, CDSB — makes accurate reporting a genuine burden, especially for site managers who must understand and supply the metrics. This corpus's examples come from mining, where Improving has applied low-code data capture and analytics dashboards to make multi-framework reporting tractable.

## Key Claims

- ESG reporting grew from the need to measure more than financial performance, centered on stakeholder value and KPIs.
- The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), created in 1997, was the first framework for reporting economic, environmental, and social impacts.
- SASB segments 77 industries and picks the most operationally impactful metrics from each, yet site managers still find understanding and reporting those metrics a burden.
- Improving deploys a Power Apps front end where managers select their framework and enter metrics with comments, feeding an analytics layer that organizes metrics by framework and KPI into dashboards for COO or public reporting.
- Improving's ESG "Play," part of its Analytics Playbook, aims to go beyond SASB, GRI, and TCFD and to make tracking thousands of metrics simple with customizable reporting frameworks.
