# internal narrative

The ongoing story we tell ourselves about what happens to us and what we are capable of. Across this corpus it is the variable that determines whether circumstances limit us, because our interpretation of events — not the events themselves — shapes our response.

## Key Claims

- Our response to events, not the events themselves, steals dreams; the inner interpretation is where the loss happens.
- "We are what we think about," so a narrative can be shifted intentionally rather than merely observed.
- Imposter syndrome wins only if you let it — the inner voice supplies the permission.
- The acronym LIE — "Limiting Idea Entertained" — frames self-doubt as a choice: you decide whether a limiting idea is entertained and therefore limiting.
- We all believe lies others feed us or that we tell ourselves; the work is noticing and refusing to host them.
