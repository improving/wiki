# Demand-Limited vs Collapsable Tasks

This split sorts the tasks inside a job by what happens when AI makes them cheaper. Demand-limited tasks face a fixed amount of demand, so speed frees time without producing more output; collapsable tasks can be executed more cheaply, so their volume expands. Each category demands a different response: role evolution versus role redefinition.

## Key Claims

- Jobs are bundles of tasks that technology hits unevenly; AI does not replace a job wholesale.
- Demand-limited tasks need role evolution; collapsable tasks need role redefinition.
- The diagnostic is what you would do with the other half of the freed time.
- A Jevons-style rebound can turn a demand-limited task into an execution-limited one.
- When AI raises everyone's execution floor equally, gains compress into prices and expectations, and advantage shifts to context quality, integration speed, and judgment.
- AI narrows the gap between top and average performers.
- Precise shared vocabulary is a habit, not a glossary document nobody reads.
