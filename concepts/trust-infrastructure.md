# trust infrastructure

Trust infrastructure is the set of mechanisms — [Audit Trail](/concepts/audit-trail.md) records, [Quality Gates](/concepts/quality-gates.md), and [human approval points](/concepts/human-approval-points.md) — that must exist before an organization can safely adopt tooling or extend autonomy. The corpus argues that DevOps and agentic AI both stall on trust rather than capability, so trust infrastructure must be built first.

## Key Claims

- By 2018 DevOps tooling was mature, yet adoption lagged because of organizational distrust; tooling deployed in low-trust environments gets adopted defensively and delivers no ROI.
- Establish the conditions for trust before deploying the tooling that requires it; audit trails, quality gates, and human approval points are the AI-era trust infrastructure.
- Agentic AI leaders stall on trust questions, not capability questions, and most organizations sit at Stage 2 or 3 of the 8-Stage [AI Trust Evolution](/concepts/ai-trust-evolution.md).
- Observability, reversibility, and boundary enforcement are preconditions for extending autonomy.
- As frontier benchmark scores converge to within a point, labs with enterprise exposure compete on governance credentials; AWS made zero data retention the default on [Amazon Bedrock](/entities/amazon-bedrock.md).
