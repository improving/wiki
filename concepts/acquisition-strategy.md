# acquisition strategy

Improving's acquisition strategy grows capability and geography by merging like-minded technology service firms rather than building every market from scratch. Under what its announcements call the "Enterprise Strategy" (earlier the "Enterprise Model"), acquired leadership teams stay in place and continue to run and grow their businesses, so each deal adds talent, clients, and a delivery footprint at once.

## Key Claims

- Trinity Hunt Partners took majority interest in Improving, with co-founder and CEO Curtis Hite and management remaining significant shareholders; growth was to come from organic initiatives plus continued strategic acquisitions ([Trinity Hunt Acquires Majority Interest](/sources/trinity-hunt-acquires-majority-interest-in-improving.md)).
- Acquisitions extended geography and pushed annualized revenue upward: Leniolabs into South America (past $280M), InfraCloud in Pune, India (past $270M), Zigatta with Guadalajara, Mexico (past $275M), and MangoChango with a Guatemala workforce (past $300M).
- ProSource Solutions, a Richfield, Ohio consultancy, added more than $10,000,000 in annualized revenue in 2018 and became Improving's sixth enterprise location ([ProSource Solutions Merger](/sources/prosource-solutions-merger.md)).
- The recurring structure is capability plus culture fit: InfraCloud brought cloud-native and Kubernetes depth (founded 2016, an early Kubernetes partner), Zigatta brought data, cloud, and architecture ([InfraCloud](/sources/infracloud-acquisition.md), [Zigatta](/sources/improving-acquires-zigatta.md)).
- Acquired firms keep their leaders: Leniolabs, MangoChango, InfraCloud, and Zigatta leadership all stayed under Improving's Enterprise Strategy.
- Culture is the stated differentiator and selection filter — Trinity Hunt cited Improving's culture as its advantage in hiring and retaining professionals ([Trinity Hunt](/sources/trinity-hunt-acquires-majority-interest-in-improving.md)).
