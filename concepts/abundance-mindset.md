# Abundance Mindset

An abundance mindset is the deliberate practice of seeing what is present rather than what is missing. This corpus pairs it with two observations: humans are wired to notice absence first, and the popular framing of work versus life is a false dichotomy that only feeds the scarcity it claims to resolve.

## Key Claims

- We see the lack before we see the plenty; pattern-matching for absence blinds us to what we have.
- Naming and sharing gratitude starts a virtuous cycle of appreciation.
- You cannot balance something that is always in flux, and the balance framing creates anxiety and feelings of inadequacy.
- Work is part of life, so find the good in it rather than treating it as the enemy of life.
- An abundance mindset beats scarcity; be present wherever you are.
