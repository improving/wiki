# trust building

Trust building is the deliberate accumulation of confidence through consistency, follow-through, and accountability. Across this corpus trust is treated as something generated rather than conserved — a resource that grows when exercised and is revealed as counterfeit when it needs constant reassurance.

## Key Claims

- Successful leaders do consistently what others do occasionally; consistency builds trust, reliability, and predictability, and is the factor you control.
- Regular habits of expectation-setting and follow-through inspire others and keep focus on long-term goals amid distractions.
- A missed deadline is a failure of leadership, not just execution: own the miss and show how the future will be better.
- Delivering results is the best place to start building trust.
- Trust is built rather than depleted by use, like muscle through exercise.
- When the craving for reassurance is stifled, real trust happens; trust at the mercy of a response is counterfeit.
