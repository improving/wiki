# Operating model

An operating model is the designed structure of roles, decision rights, and workflows through which an organization turns capability into delivery. In an AI-native enterprise it is the third layer of the Intelligence Operating System: the layer that decides how humans and agents divide work, and it is the layer that resists being bought.

## Key Claims

- The Intelligence Operating System separates foundational concerns from application concerns, the way an operating system does.
- Layer 1, Hybrid Intelligence, is a designed integration of human judgment and AI execution.
- Layer 2, the Governance Engine, provides audit trails, boundary enforcement, and approval points.
- Layers 1 and 3 resist purchase; only parts of governance are buyable.
- AI productivity gains skew toward junior workers — roughly 35 percent versus 3 percent for experts — which compresses the performance distribution.
- As the floor rises, judgment and novel problem definition become relatively more valuable.
