# product backlog

The product backlog is the prioritized, refined list of work a product team draws from — a living set of Product Backlog Items aligned to a product goal. In this corpus it is treated less as a static requirements list and more as a set of hypotheses of value: items are enriched with [acceptance criteria](/concepts/acceptance-criteria.md), ordered by value, risk, and effort, and revisited often enough that learning is not delayed.

## Key Claims

- Improving's Product Backlog Creation Lab turns fuzzy ideas into aligned items through an agenda of vision and constraints, themes and outcomes, breaking big rocks into items, prioritization by value/risk/effort, and enrichment with acceptance criteria and dependencies ([Inside Our Product Backlog Creation Lab](/sources/inside-our-product-backlog-creation-lab.md)).
- After a one-day lab, a client's data science team with competing workstreams was aligned two months later, spending less time cherry-picking tasks and running its own refinement sessions ([Inside the Lab](/sources/inside-our-product-backlog-creation-lab.md)).
- A backlog is a set of hypotheses; releasing as often as possible shortens the feedback loop and stops investment in unvalidated ideas ([When Should We Release In Scrum?](/sources/when-should-we-release-in-scrum.md)).
- Urgent stakeholder requests are scope decisions: take them to the team, let those doing the work decide what to remove or shrink, and record the change in the Sprint Backlog with a reason comment so patterns emerge ([Struggling to Deliver Done Work, Part 2](/sources/struggling-to-deliver-done-work-every-sprint-part-2-stakeholder.md)).
- AI compresses refinement and prioritization, freeing the Product Owner to validate intent and impact rather than transcribe requirements ([Why AI Skills Are Now Part of the Product Owner's Playbook](/sources/ai-skills-product-owners-playbook.md)).
- The Product Owner manages the backlog and is accountable for its ordering ([Drucker's Blueprint Pt. 1](/sources/druckers-blueprint-product-owner-to-effective-executive-pt-1.md)).
