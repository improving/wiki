# rapamycin

Rapamycin is an mTOR inhibitor taken experimentally to mimic caloric restriction and trigger autophagy, one component of a personal healthspan protocol. The corpus documents a complete arc — adoption, protocol design, and discontinuation — which makes it a useful case study in individual response to intervention.

## Key Claims

- Rapamycin inhibits mTOR, mimicking caloric restriction and triggering autophagy.
- Weekly dosing on rest days avoids interfering with muscle building.
- Dr. Peter Attia's *Outlive* prompted the experiment.
- The protocol was later discontinued after a "hollowed-out" feeling, skin issues, and a higher resting heart rate.
- Symptoms returned at a half dose, confirming the protocol caused them; individual biology varies, so listen to your body.
