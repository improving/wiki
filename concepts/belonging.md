# belonging

Belonging is the felt inclusion that lets people contribute fully rather than merely show up. In this corpus it is repeatedly described as deliberately built rather than declared — through employee-led initiatives, company-wide gatherings, and moments like sponsoring a Pride march — and it is the connective tissue across a distributed, multi-country company.

## Key Claims

- Belonging "doesn't happen by accident": Improving actively builds it through events and everyday culture rather than assuming it.
- Women of Improving, an employee-led initiative envisioned by Chief of Staff Susan Fojtasek, runs four to five global virtual or hybrid events a year plus localized office gatherings, on topics like confidence, self-advocacy, and imposter syndrome.
- The 2025 Las Vegas retreat was the first to bring South America, Guatemala, and India together with Mexico, Canada, and the USA; its closing group photo captured colleagues from six regions who had "become friends, mentors, and even family."
- At Guadalajara Pride, the company covered travel for any employee who wanted to attend, and an attendee found a celebration that wanted to "celebrate diversity, not just tolerate it."
- Peer-nominated recognition and shared time together deepen belonging in a 1,500-person, three-country company.
- Flexible, remote structures make IT a more sustainable long-term career for people across life stages.
