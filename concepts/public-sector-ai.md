# public sector AI

Public sector AI applies machine learning and document automation to government agencies and public research institutions, where compliance, security clearances, and procurement process shape every design decision. The recurring pattern in this corpus is pairing a self-service cloud platform for researchers with Document AI for high-volume paperwork, all inside protected environments.

## Key Claims

- Public-sector research teams are bottlenecked by limited on-prem capacity, long provisioning cycles, and complex cost governance.
- A federal health agency's medical device adverse-event intake went from weeks to hours using ML, Document AI, and BigQuery on Google Cloud, clearing backlogs and freeing staff.
- Document AI also backed a Government of Canada department's backlog processing and ATIP data-loss-prevention workflows.
- YoppWorks, an Improving company, won Google Cloud's 2023 Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada for HPC/AI/ML research access, Document AI workflows, and cloud landing zones.
- A defense research agency's portal operated inside PBMM-compliant architectures with built-in budget approval and spend alerts.
- Lessons learned: prioritize security and compliance and budget time for it, write documentation accessible to non-technical stakeholders, and invest in understanding client organizational context.
- Regulation drives the work: approaching CMS interoperability deadlines require FHIR-based prior authorization decisions with public denial reasons.
