# outcome-based pricing

Outcome-based pricing charges for results delivered rather than hours worked. This corpus frames the shift as forced, not optional: as AI compresses production work, hourly billing stops being able to describe — or justify — what the work is worth.

## Key Claims

- [McKinsey & Company](/entities/mckinsey-company.md) tied 25% of global fees to outcomes, and 67% of consulting buyers now prefer fixed-fee over time-and-materials.
- Outcomes-priced firms grow at 11-12% while advisory-hour firms stall at 5.7%.
- [Bain](/entities/bain.md) reports 30% of work is already AI-enabled and heading to 50%; clients refuse to pay for hours AI compresses.
- Agentic AI makes the mismatch absurd: hourly billing cannot explain an agent doing a week of work in six minutes.
- The value shift is to judgment, not production — the part AI cannot compress.
- Outcome-based software pricing is climbing from 10% to 60% this decade; firms that adopt outcome-based delivery early will own the next decade.
