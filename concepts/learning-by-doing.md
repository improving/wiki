# Learning by Doing

The principle that real understanding comes from action — trying things, making things, and going deep — rather than from passively collecting knowledge or pursuing a title. Across this corpus it is the bridge between curiosity and competence.

## Key Claims

- Experience is the only true impartation of wisdom; knowledge untouched by action stays academic.
- Nearly everything becomes interesting if you go into it deeply enough — depth is a choice, not a lucky match.
- Speed and surface-level skimming make us miss the rich layers underneath.
- Focus on what you want to do, not what you want to be; the title is a poor proxy for the work.
- Trying things teaches us even when the thing turns out not to be "our thing."
