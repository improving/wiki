# Capability vs Activity

Capability versus activity is the distinction between what an organization can reliably do with AI and the activity it can point to — tools purchased, pilots launched, licenses deployed. Across this corpus it is the central diagnostic error in AI adoption: organizations measure activity, overestimate their maturity, and then buy interventions from the wrong stage.

## Key Claims

- Organizations buy Stage 5 solutions while operating at Stage 2 capability.
- Most organizations sit on rung two, Experimentation, and believe they are on rung three.
- Maturity should be measured by capability, not activity like tools deployed.
- Maturity is a distribution of capability levels across the organization, not a single number — Rogers' diffusion model segments that distribution.
- The diagnostic question is what the organization can reliably do with AI, or equivalently what happens when AI fails, not what tools it owns.
- Rung interventions differ, so misdiagnosis produces expensive failures.
