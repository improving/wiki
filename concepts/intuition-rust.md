# intuition rust

The slow decay of an expert's judgment when AI takes over the repetitive reps that used to build and maintain it. Output can improve while the underlying skill quietly corrodes — a paradox central to how this corpus views AI adoption in knowledge work.

## Key Claims

- A cancer-specialist study found AI improved output while eroding judgment — the effect named "intuition rust."
- 11 million gateway jobs disappeared in 18 months, removing the entry-level reps where skill is normally built.
- A 2026 coding study found heavy AI reliance cut manual-mastery scores by 17%.
- Consultants sell judgment, not information, and judgment requires ongoing reps to stay sharp.
- The information-access premium is already being compressed by AI, shifting value toward judgment.
- AI sharpens judgment when used as a sparring partner rather than an answer machine.
- "Adequate" and "good" differ, and closing that gap is what clients actually pay for.
