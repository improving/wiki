# revenue cycle management

Revenue cycle management (RCM) is the end-to-end management of healthcare billing and claims, from charge capture through denial and appeal. Providers run it under thin margins and rising administrative burden, which makes it a prime target for AI automation — provided the underlying data and governance are sound first.

## Key Claims

- Initial claim denials hit 11.8% in 2024, with nearly half arising from missing or inaccurate data.
- Error rates in claims work can drop from 46% to 32%, then 20%, then 10% as AI learns an organization's own workflows.
- Expecting AI to be right 100% on day one is impossible; the value compounds as it improves over time.
- On the payer side, 80% of prior authorization denials are overturned on review, and the AMA reports 60% of physicians believe AI tools cause unnecessary claim denials.
- Denial management is fundamentally a data problem: accurate, complete, well-governed data is the prerequisite before any tool is applied.
- Transparency mandates (the Transparency in Coverage Final Rule, CAA, and No Surprises Act) force insurers to publish machine-readable price files, retrofitting legacy systems and expanding third-party vendor responsibilities.
- Shadow AI is the top provider risk for 2026, so governance — policy, technology, training, and monitoring — frames any RCM automation.
