# managed service provider

A managed service provider (MSP) is a firm that runs a client's IT under contract, combining proactive operation with contractual service levels. It differs from [staff augmentation](/concepts/staff-augmentation.md), which adds people without an SLA, and from reactive break-fix support; in this corpus the model is defined by [managed services](/concepts/managed-services.md) under agreed [service level objectives](/concepts/service-level-objective.md), increasingly with AI as an operating layer.

## Key Claims

- The global managed services market is projected to grow from $437.3B in 2026 to $847.4B by 2033, with managed security services growing at a 16.4% CAGR ([Managed Services Partners](/sources/managed-services-partners.md)).
- Core services include 24/7 monitoring, patching, multi-layered security, strategic technology roadmaps, and automated backup and disaster recovery — shifting clients from break-fix to prevention ([From Reactive to Proactive](/sources/how-an-msp-can-help-prevent-problems-before-they-start.md)).
- Pricing is commonly per user, per device, a fixed monthly fee, or hourly for out-of-scope work, with emergency remediation costing more per hour ([How Improving Elevates MSP Solutions](/sources/how-improving-elevates-msp-solutions-for-smbs.md)).
- Higher tiers add a virtual CIO for investment and risk guidance plus quarterly architectural advisory calls ([How Improving Elevates MSP Solutions](/sources/how-improving-elevates-msp-solutions-for-smbs.md)).
- A Canada Post case reports a 95% reduction in unscheduled downtime and Mean Time to Acknowledgment cut from 10.3 to 5.11 minutes ([Managed Services Partners](/sources/managed-services-partners.md)).
- Onboarding is arguably the most important project an MSP runs, because it is where the client first tests the MSP's promises ([Why Customer Onboarding Is the Most Important Project](/sources/why-customer-onboarding-is-the-most-important-project-msps-run.md)).
- AI now sits at the center: predictive maintenance, real-time security response, task automation, and AI-assisted support ([The Role of AI in Modern Managed Services](/sources/the-role-of-ai-in-modern-managed-services.md)).
