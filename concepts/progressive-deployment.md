# progressive deployment

Progressive deployment is the strategy of embedding AI at each stage of the Plan-to-Monitor loop in turn, under governance and human judgment, instead of bolting it onto code completion alone. It matters because each stage holds a distinct leverage point, and defect prevention compounds when issues are caught earlier in the loop.

## Key Claims

- Limiting AI to code completion leaves most delivery leverage untouched; every stage of the Plan-to-Monitor loop has a distinct AI leverage point.
- Defect prevention compounds: catching issues earlier costs less.
- Start with the stage holding the most quality problems or risk.
- Each ALM stage becomes a station with quality gates between stations.
- Humans decide at irreversible points: release, deployment, and live-data remediation.
- The factory is monitored as a system, not managed station by station.
