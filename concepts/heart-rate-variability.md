# Heart Rate Variability

Heart rate variability (HRV) is the measured variation in time between successive heartbeats. In this corpus it is the signal at the center of wearable-based health tracking: [WHOOP](/entities/whoop.md) uses it to estimate strain and recovery, turning an abstract physiological measure into a daily readiness number.

## Key Claims

- WHOOP has been the foundation of the author's health efforts since May 2019, with HRV as its key measure.
- Continuous wear enables nearly continuous monitoring, which is what makes the trend useful.
- Recovery is not the same thing as [sleep](/concepts/sleep-tracking.md) — the two are measured and discussed separately.
- Thirty minutes of pre-bed reading noticeably raised HRV and boosted recovery.
- Experimentation with sleep inputs yields measurable gains, so habits can be tested against the signal.
- The reading habit is a win-win: more reading and better recovery.
- Competition with brothers on strain scores drives engagement, a social layer over the data.
