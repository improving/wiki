# company culture

Company culture is the behaviors that emerge where an organization's people meet its values. This corpus treats it as a deliberately chosen artifact rather than an accident: culture happens by default or by decision, and hollow values that leadership does not live produce a hollow, even toxic, culture.

## Key Claims

- Culture emerges from behaviors produced by the intersection of people and values; misalignment between the two is what produces toxicity.
- Leaders must embody stated values to create safety for their people — people are too intuitive to be fooled by faked values.
- Culture happens by default or decision and needs deliberate attention; growing yourself is described as the best way to grow your business.
- Investing in maximizing people is framed as the best ROI a business can get; Improvers reportedly return to the office willingly to share work and connect.
- We spend too much of life at work for it to be terrible, so leaders should invest in relationships and families, not just output.
- Conscious capitalism extends the stakeholder model beyond shareholders to partners and community, assuming profit but focusing on how it is spent.
