# Learning From Failure

The discipline of treating mistakes as raw material for growth: owning them openly, finding the root cause, correcting course, and reflecting. Across this corpus it is framed as both a leadership behavior and a component of resilience.

## Key Claims

- Owning errors builds team trust and models honesty; deflecting blame and making excuses wreck credibility and morale.
- Root-cause the mistake using feedback and data before attempting to correct it.
- A corrective plan plus deliberate reflection is what turns a mistake into growth.
- Resilience is more than bouncing back — it embraces adversity as a source of growth.
- A growth mindset reframes challenges as opportunities to learn rather than as verdicts on ability.
- Resilient leaders create cohesion and stability during turbulent times, and supportive cultures that permit calculated risk build resilient teams.
