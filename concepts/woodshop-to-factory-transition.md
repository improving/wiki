# Woodshop-to-Factory Transition

The woodshop-to-factory transition is the shift from AI-assisted work, where a person is the quality gate, to autonomous agents that need explicit operating infrastructure. This corpus frames it as a category change rather than a bigger step: Stage 3 is not "Stage 2 with better tools," and scaling agents before building their safety scaffolding damages confidence in autonomous AI.

## Key Claims

- Stage 2 AI use is a woodshop where the developer is the quality gate.
- Stage 3 autonomous agents are factory machinery needing explicit operating infrastructure.
- Agent boundaries, quality gates, [audit trails](/concepts/audit-trail.md), and error handling must exist before production.
- Scaling before building safety infrastructure damages confidence in autonomous AI.
- Stage numbers and named failure mechanisms give strategy a precise shared language; broad terms like "AI adoption" convey almost no information.
