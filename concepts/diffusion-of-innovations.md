# Diffusion of Innovations

Diffusion of Innovations is [Everett Rogers'](/entities/everett-rogers.md) model of how new practices spread through a population, segmenting adopters into Innovators, Early Adopters, Early and Late Majority, and Laggards. Across this corpus it is used less as a history of adoption than as a diagnostic: where an organization actually sits relative to the market, and therefore what strategy is viable.

## Key Claims

- The curve describes populations at a moment, not timelines; it is a position, not a schedule.
- Organizations buy Stage 5 solutions while operating at Stage 2 capability — measuring activity instead of capability.
- Maturity is a distribution of capability levels, not a single number.
- In 2025, AI workflows sit at Early Adopter and agents at Innovator.
- Mapping your adoption against the market reveals strategic options.
- Applying an Innovator strategy as a Late Majority organization is the common mistake.
