# Virtuous Cycle

A virtuous cycle is a self-reinforcing loop: one good action creates the conditions that make the next one more likely. In this corpus the idea appears twice — as the reward for deliberately practicing [gratitude](/concepts/gratitude.md), and as the way passion for work spreads when someone models it instead of describing it.

## Key Claims

- We see the lack before we see the plenty; pattern-matching for absence blinds us to what we already have.
- Naming and sharing gratitude starts a virtuous cycle of appreciation.
- Modeling a behavior makes others want to imitate it, so the cycle propagates through example.
- Speaking of your own effort, even to inform, lessens its inspirational effect and invites suspicion that you seek recognition.
- Passion for the work spreads through a virtuous cycle rather than through announcement.
