# Independent Verification

Evidence that a party with no stake in the claim has checked, as opposed to self-reported numbers or monitoring run by the vendor. It matters because internal monitoring and external audit are different evidence statuses, and governance claims cost nothing until an outsider can test them.

## Key Claims

- [IBM](/entities/ibm.md)'s 45 percent productivity claim is self-reported and unreproduced outside the company.
- [Demis Hassabis](/entities/demis-hassabis.md) proposed a FINRA-style body to review frontier models before release; praise for such a self-regulatory body costs supporters nothing before it has teeth.
- An audit log caught nine Georgia officers running improper searches, but [audit trails](/concepts/audit-trail.md) are passive governance checked after the fact on the vendor's cadence.
- [Data governance](/concepts/data-governance.md) fixed the analogous problem by enforcing policy before a disallowed query executes; vendor-run logs stop exactly where the vendor decided logging should stop.
- Real oversight needs [runtime enforcement](/concepts/runtime-policy-enforcement.md) before the action plus verification by an independent party.
