# Skill Leveling Effect

The skill leveling effect describes how AI productivity gains skew toward less-experienced workers, compressing the gap between top and average performers. Because the tools raise everyone's execution floor roughly equally, the differentiator shifts away from tool access toward judgment, context, and integration speed.

## Key Claims

- Junior and mid-level workers gain roughly 35 percent versus 3 percent for experts.
- AI democratizes access to knowledge that once took a career to build.
- The performance floor rises and the distribution tightens.
- Judgment and novel problem definition become relatively more valuable.
- Equal gains across a market compress prices and reset expectations.
- Labor compression splits into demand-limited and collapsable tasks.
- Advantage concentrates in context access, integration speed, and judgment at the margin.
