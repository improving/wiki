# meal ordering

A nutrition tactic: eating foods in a specific order — vegetables, then protein, then starch — to reduce post-meal glucose spikes. It surfaced in this corpus through personal [continuous glucose monitoring](/concepts/continuous-glucose-monitoring.md), where the effect was visible in real time.

## Key Claims

- Eating vegetables, then protein, then starch reduces glucose spikes relative to eating the same foods in other orders.
- Eating protein before carbs improved glucose response and, together with cutting late alcohol, also improved sleep.
- Continuous monitoring makes the effect legible: the Dexcom G6 syncs continuously, unlike the Freestyle Libre.
- Creative work itself caused a 90-point glucose spike that a walk brought back down — meal order is not the only lever.
- The broader protocol pairs ordering with a daily stack spanning AG1, IDLife, and NAD, plus peptides for joints, longevity, and sleep.
- Consistent unwinding habits raised nightly sleep from 5 to 6.5 hours.
