**I just got done reading *Being Geek* by O'Reilly Media. It is awesome. I would recommend it to any geek in the business of IT.**

**Product:** Being Geek by *O'Reilly Media* — Awesome Handbook

**Pros:** Easy to understand, Accurate, Enjoyable, Concise, Well-written

**Best Uses:** Novice, Expert, Student, Intermediate

**Best for:** Designers, Educators, Developers, Makers, Sys Admins — really anyone working in software who cares about the career side of being a professional geek.

This is going to be a book that I have dog-eared, worn-covered, and close at hand. The interview personality section and the offer negotiation sections were incredibly helpful. The conversational style of the writing makes it a very easy read. I found that I was enjoying it and laughing at things like the bridge game analogy. I picked this book up while writing another review and I have to say it is an awesome find.
