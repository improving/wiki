We are thrilled to announce that Improving has landed a spot on the prestigious Inc. 5000 list for the **15th time! **The annual Inc. 5000 list recognizes the fastest-growing private companies throughout the United States.

According to the Inc 5000 website, “Companies on the 2024 Inc. 5000 are ranked according to percentage revenue growth from 2020 through 2023."

During the virtual "Make the Most of Making the List" streaming event for Inc. 5000 company leaders, our CEO Curtis Hite shared insights and offered valuable advice to fellow honorees on how to celebrate this achievement with stakeholders.

There will be Improving representatives at the Inc. 5000 Conference and Gala on October 16th through 18th in Palm Desert, California. For more information on the conference, visit [https://events.inc.com/2024IncConference](https://events.inc.com/2024IncConference)

This award and our 15 years on the list are a testament to everyone who has contributed to our incredible journey over the years. We couldn't have achieved this without our employee’s passion and commitment to excellence. Find out more about Inc. 5000 and see who else made the list here: [https://www.inc.com/inc5000](https://www.inc.com/inc5000)
