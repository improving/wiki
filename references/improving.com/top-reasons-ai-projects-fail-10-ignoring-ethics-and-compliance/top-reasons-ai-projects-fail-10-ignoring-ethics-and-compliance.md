[AI projects](https://www.improving.com/expertise/ai/) rarely fail because of weak algorithms. They fail because of oversight. In the tenth and final part of our [*Top Reasons AI Projects Fail*](https://www.improving.com/thoughts/top-10-reasons-ai-projects-fail/) series, we examine a risk that quietly undermines even the most advanced initiatives: ignoring ethics, compliance, and regulation. When governance is treated as an afterthought, “ship fast and break things” can quickly become “ship fast and break trust.”
