As 2025 draws to a close, Improvers across every enterprise can look back on a year defined by countless moments that reminded us of what it truly means to *Come Together*. This year’s initiative was more dynamic, global, and heartfelt than ever before. It was an evolution that we could feel in every virtual talk, in‑person gathering, and challenge completed around the world.

From the very first Health & Fitness kickoff in January to the final acts of generosity in December, Improvers showed up with energy, enthusiasm, and an unwavering sense of community. The initiative continued its mission of uniting teams across offices, time zones, and continents through shared themes and experiences.

## **A Year of Themes, Talks & Togetherness**

2025 launched strong with January’s **Health & Fitness Month**, complete with the energizing *Power Pair Challenge* and a lineup of virtual talks that set the tone for an impactful year ahead. Improvers teamed up to support one another’s goals, embodying the spirit of building habits and relationships simultaneously.

Month after month, the momentum continued. Virtual talks remained a cornerstone of the program, drawing hundreds of attendees and creating global touchpoints for learning and inspiration. In-person events, from themed gatherings to cultural celebrations, brought Improvers together locally.

Each theme added something new to the year, but together they painted a larger picture: 2025 was a year where our culture became more vibrant and inclusive.

## **Celebrating Growth Across the Globe**

One of the standout elements of this year’s Come Together initiative was how it reflected Improving’s growth. This year, we celebrated our expanding global family. Improvers embraced cross-cultural monthly themes such as Argentina and Chile, bringing global flavors into our celebrations and highlighting just how interconnected our community has become.

## **A Spotlight on Impact & Generosity**

No wrap-up of 2025 would be complete without highlighting one of our most meaningful traditions: the **Generosity Advocate Pitch Competition**. This year’s event showcased heartfelt presentations from representatives across every enterprise, each nominating a charitable organization doing life-changing work in their communities.

With inspiring stories, emotional moments, and spirited advocacy, Improvers came together to vote and direct donations toward causes around the world. This year we suprisingly had a tie for the winners: Special Spaces and Mesón AC, Fundación de la Hemofilia. Abuelitos de Laderos was our third-place winner. However, every organization mentioned in the competition received donations.

## **Looking Back and Moving Forward**

Come Together 2025 was a resounding success. It wasn’t just a series of events, but a yearlong reminder that connection fuels progress, culture thrives when shared, and every Improver plays a role in building the experience we all get to enjoy. As echoed in our closing virtual events, this initiative is far more than tradition. It’s a commitment to each other, to learning, and to building a workplace where everyone feels they belong.

With 2026 just around the corner, we’re ready to deepen this impact, explore new themes, host new conversations, and welcome even more Improvers into the growing network of people who make Come Together what it is.
