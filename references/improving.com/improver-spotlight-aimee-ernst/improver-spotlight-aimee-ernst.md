Meet Aimee Ernst, the Industry Solutions Delivery (ISD – formerly Microsoft Consulting Services) Program Manager for Improving. Aimee started working for Improving in 2017 as part of the ProSource Solutions acquisition. She shares her time with the Ohio Enterprise as a Business Development Manager and Improving Corporate as part of the newly formed Microsoft Services Team.

“I’ve worked with Microsoft Consulting services for over 10 years and have truly enjoyed building the business partnership and many friendships within the Microsoft teams. This is an opportunity to focus on expanding our reach within Industry Solutions Delivery.”

![Aimee Ernst](images/AimeeErnst.jpeg)

Aimee’s role will continue to work with the various Industry Solutions Delivery teams to support their client’s initiatives across Microsoft technologies. The goal is to ensure Improving is well aligned as a trusted, go-to Partner for Microsoft ISD.

“It has been exciting to watch the growth within Improving and I am very excited to bring Microsoft talent from many of our enterprises to work on the Industry Solutions Delivery projects," Aimee says. "With over 1200 consultants, many with strong Microsoft skills, I believe we will expand our relationships within the Microsoft ISD and continue to be a trusted partner.

Improving feels fortunate to have Aimee in this position as she has spent 10 years working with the ISD teams and has built great relationships with many of the Microsoft managers over this time.

![Ernst Family](images/Ernst_Family.webp)

During Aimee’s time off, you can find her spending time boating on Lake Erie or on Kelleys Island with her family and friends. Aimee currently resides in Bay Village, Ohio with her husband Steve, son Liam and two Australian Shepherds Lucie and Charlie.
