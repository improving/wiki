Wisdom is the right application of knowledge in a context. We gain wisdom through the wrong application of knowledge in a context. Funny how that works...

We can bring others along in our own learning and in doing so allow them to grow and even learn from our mistakes. But we can only learn from mistakes we own. How are you including those that need to grow that allows them to gain wisdom by you owning your mistakes?
