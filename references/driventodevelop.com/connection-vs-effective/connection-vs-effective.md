In interacting with those around you, look to be connected not just effective. Connection can bridge the rough spots that inevitably will show up. Focusing on being effective means you'll hit those bumps - hard! It can jar loose whatever you’ve worked to build and sometimes you’ll even blow past the fact that you’ve had an impact without realizing it. It’s hard to recover unless you have the grace that connection typically provides.

In what ways have you worked to connect to those around you today?
