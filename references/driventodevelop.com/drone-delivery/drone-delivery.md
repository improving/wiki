[Frisco](https://www.friscotexas.gov/?ref=driventodevelop.com) has been a fantastic place to live. There schools are great. Plenty to do. And there's been some amazing growth in the 13 years that we've lived here. Even beeter, they like to try cool things! We are a test-bed for the new, [Wing Delivery](https://wing.com/dfw/?ref=driventodevelop.com), so we decided to have them drop us a little test package. The footage from the balcony was cool enough I figured I'd share.
