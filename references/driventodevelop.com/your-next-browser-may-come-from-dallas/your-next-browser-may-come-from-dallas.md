![Your Next Browser May Come from Dallas](images/photo-1580536793208-117fdb20ffc1)

Island raised $400 million last week. The valuation is $6.4 billion. They build enterprise browsers. That sentence is still not getting the attention it deserves.

I have been watching the DFW tech story for years, and the dominant narrative has always been infrastructure. Data centers in the Metroplex. Semiconductor fabs in Sherman. Grid policy fights in Austin. That story is real and it matters. But Island is something different, and I think the funding round is underselling what the company actually is.

An enterprise browser sounds like a narrow product. It is not. Island controls what agents and employees can access at the application layer. In a world where AI agents are spinning up and hitting your SaaS stack, your email, your file systems, and your compliance-sensitive data, the browser is where governance actually has to live. Island is not a browser company. It is an agent governance company that happens to sit in the browser. That distinction matters a lot right now, because every IT leader is asking the same question: how do we control what agents can see and do?

My teams are in that conversation with clients constantly. The answer Island represents is one of the clearest I have seen, and it was built here in North Texas.

DFW's Q3 2026 was the region's best VC quarter on record: $962.5 million across 52 deals. The Texas Stock Exchange launched this year. Venture Dallas is October 22. These are not scattered data points. They describe a region that has quietly become the place where enterprise AI problems get built, funded, and governed. [I wrote earlier this year about DFW becoming a serious AI capital market](https://driventodevelop.com/dfw-is-now-4-and-the-capital-layer-is-the-real-story/). That thesis is moving faster than I expected.

The enterprise software solving the hardest governance problems of this AI era is coming out of North Texas. If you are in DFW and you are not at Venture Dallas next week, what is keeping you away?
