I heard a quote today, "Your character is the sum total of your choices.", and it reminded me of something Rick Voirin, Chairman of [Stagen Leadership Academy](stagen.com) said, "With awareness comes choice. Without awareness only habit". So you make your choices; then your choices make you. But what if you're not actually making choices but only living thru habit? Where might you be living in habit today?
