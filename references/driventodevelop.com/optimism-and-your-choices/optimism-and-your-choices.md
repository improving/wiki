“Unless you believe that the future can be better, you are unlikely to step up and take responsibility for making it so.” — Noam Chomsky

Optimism isn't optional, it's a requirement for living and for making a better world for our children. Change is Good! Fight the good fight and lean into change — no matter the pain or the effort. In the end, it's worth it.
