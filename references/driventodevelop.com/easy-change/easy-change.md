People talk about wanting change, embracing change, change being good etc. until that moment of impact - when the collision of the pain of change intersects with the comfortable now…and there’s no predicting how you’ll react. I believe the Mike Tyson quote is that, *"Everyone has a plan until they get punched in the mouth."* The need, or even desire, must far outweigh the magnitude of the pain in order for progress to be worth our while.

## What are you changing in yourself that is worth the pain?
