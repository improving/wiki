# Improving the Future: Reflections on Our AI Summer Camp — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-the-future-reflections-on-our-ai-summer-camp/improving-the-future-reflections-on-our-ai-summer-camp.md`

## Summary

Claudio Lassala reflects on AI Summer Camp, a three-part Improving series at the Houston office that taught high school students and their parents to understand and use AI responsibly. Sessions progressed through Wake-Up Mode (what AI is and isn't), Power-Up Mode (AI as a thought partner on essays, slides, and projects), and Genius Mode (using AI to study smarter). Parents attended their own sessions on the ethics, practicalities, and motivation of AI, and the post frames the camp as living out Improving's community values.

## Key Claims

- The camp ran as three Saturday sessions named Wake-Up Mode, Power-Up Mode, and Genius Mode, each building on the last.
- Wake-Up Mode taught students what AI is and isn't, where it hallucinates, and why not to let it think for them.
- Power-Up Mode used AI as a thought partner on real tasks — essays, slides, and projects — rather than a replacement.
- Genius Mode addressed common struggles like "I don't get this" and "I don't know where to start," leaving students with study strategies.
- Parents joined separate sessions exploring the ethical, practical, and motivational sides of AI.
- The camp was designed collaboratively by Improvers, with activities built for both extroverts and introverts and discussions on learning styles, motivation, and ethics.
- Improving framed the effort around its values of excellence, involvement, and community impact.
