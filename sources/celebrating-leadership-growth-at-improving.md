# Celebrating Leadership Growth at Improving — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/celebrating-leadership-growth-at-improving/celebrating-leadership-growth-at-improving.md`

## Summary

Improving announced a series of internal leadership promotions, emphasizing that every new role was filled by an existing Improver. The appointments span Texas, Houston, and Mexico, and each leader has a long tenure — 10 to 18 years. The post pairs each promotion with the leader's vision, collectively underscoring a strategy of growing leaders from within and scaling global delivery.

## Key Claims

- Every new leadership role was filled by an existing Improver, reflecting a grow-from-within approach.
- Devlin Liles (15-year Improver) became Chief Consulting Officer, with a vision of Improving as a "global challenger brand" and a hub of continuous learning.
- David O'Hara (18 years) became Regional Director, Texas, overseeing a unified "One Texas" strategy.
- Don McGreal (18 years, Professional Scrum Trainer) became President, Improving Dallas.
- Robyn Fuentes (10 years) became President, Improving Houston and Austin, with a 2026 theme of "excellence in the fundamentals."
- David Sandoval (14 years) became President, Improving Mexico, positioning Mexico as a nearshore delivery engine.
- Guillermo Ortega (15 years) became Managing Director of Global Delivery, extending a strategy begun with Mexico in 2020 to teams across LATAM, India, the USA, and Canada.
