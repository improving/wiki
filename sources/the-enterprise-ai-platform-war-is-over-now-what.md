# The Enterprise AI Platform War Is Over. Now What? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-enterprise-ai-platform-war-is-over-now-what/the-enterprise-ai-platform-war-is-over-now-what.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that the era of consulting firms hedging by running workloads on every model and calling themselves platform-agnostic is ending because the market has effectively made a choice. He cites Anthropic passing OpenAI in business AI subscription spending while OpenAI pivots toward an IPO and government contracts, and observes that the two companies diverged not just in strategy but in who their customer is. The interesting question, he says, is not which platform won but what that win changes for clients now asking which AI vendor to trust for multi-year production systems.

## Key Claims

- Anthropic passed OpenAI in business AI subscription spending, 41% to 39.5%, and the gap is growing.
- OpenAI is offering the U.S. government a 5% equity stake as it pivots toward an IPO and government contracts — a different customer than Anthropic's.
- Platform selection has shifted from a technology decision you could defer to a strategic one you cannot afford to skip.
- Cost compression is so strong that signing multi-year agreements risks overpaying.
- Clients are really asking who they can build on without blowing up procurement, security review, budget, or board risk appetite; firms with a clear, defensible position will close more work than those still saying "it depends."
