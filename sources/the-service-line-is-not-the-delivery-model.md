# The Service Line Is Not the Delivery Model — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-service-line-is-not-the-delivery-model/the-service-line-is-not-the-delivery-model.md`

## Summary

Distinguishes having an AI service line from having an AI-structured delivery model. Seventy percent of consulting firms now have an AI service line, graduate hiring is down 30%, yet analyst reports call organizational structures "remarkably stable." The firms winning the next phase redesign how work actually gets done; most settle for new branding, a new practice lead, and a new deck.

## Key Claims

- 70% of consulting firms now have an AI service line.
- Graduate hiring is down 30% — a signal about entry-level work, not a cost-cutting story.
- Multiple analyst reports describe organizational structures as "remarkably stable."
- An AI service line is a marketing decision; AI-structured delivery is an operational commitment.
- Buyers should ask a partner to describe a structural delivery-model change from the last 12 months, not a new offering.
