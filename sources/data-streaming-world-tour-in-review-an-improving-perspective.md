# Data Streaming World Tour in Review: An Improving Perspective — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/data-streaming-world-tour-in-review-an-improving-perspective/data-streaming-world-tour-in-review-an-improving-perspective.md`

## Summary

A recap of Improving's sponsorship of Confluent's 2025 Data Streaming World Tour, which reached over 1,500 attendees across North America. It highlights the Medtronic streaming case, strong attendee interest in Apache Flink, and a hands-on Flink workshop run as a follow-up to each stop.

## Key Claims

- Improving is a Confluent Elite Systems Integrator and Premier Partner, developing streaming reference architectures on Confluent Platform and Confluent Cloud.
- The 2025 tour kicked off in Mexico City in March — the first time Improving's teams joined the North American leg — and met over 1,500 attendees across industries, states, and time zones.
- The Medtronic story resonated: Improving built real-time pipelines on managed Kafka to ingest IoT sensor and Manufacturing Execution System data, feeding streaming applications for actionable insight.
- Attendees consistently asked for more hands-on sessions, and Apache Flink (bundled with Confluent Cloud) drew particular interest for building reusable data streams and a shift-left approach to integration.
- Improving responded with a hands-on Flink workshop after each stop, led by VP of Technology Nick Larson.
- The post notes AI's ubiquity and interest in how streaming augments GenAI and LLM applications, and looks ahead to sponsoring again in 2026.
