# How ImprovingU Helps You — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-improvingu-helps-improve-you/how-improvingu-helps-improve-you.md`

## Summary

ImprovingU is Improving's internal training program, through which any employee can teach a class on a topic they know well. The program rests on the belief that "everyone is the smartest person in the room" when their specialty comes up, and that teaching sharpens the teacher as much as it informs the class. Courses range well beyond technology, and the goal is a culture of continuous, community-shared learning.

## Key Claims

- ImprovingU gives employees the chance to learn from an in-house expert, while the expert learns to coach others.
- Everyone is treated as better than their peers at something, so anyone can contribute a course.
- Courses are not limited to technology — past sessions covered networking tips, wine tastings, and maple syrup tapping at the Minneapolis office.
- The program is one of several ways Improving gives back to its community, alongside user-group events, volunteering, conference speaking, and open-source contributions.
- The stated goal is to push people outside their comfort zone and spread knowledge that carries into daily work.
