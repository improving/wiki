# How CAMH Transformed Research Computing with Improving's Cloud Research Accelerator Portal on Google Cloud — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-camh-transformed-research-computing-with-improvings-cloud-research/how-camh-transformed-research-computing-with-improvings-cloud-research.md`

## Summary

CAMH, Canada's largest mental health teaching hospital, faced bursty, exploratory research workloads and AI/ML hardware needs its on-prem HPC cluster could not meet. Improving and Google Cloud turned an early portal concept into a production-grade Research Accelerator Portal that extends, rather than replaces, CAMH's on-prem environment. The result cuts estimated costs by about 40% and frames the future of research computing as hybrid by design.

## Key Claims

- On-prem HPC suffers high CapEx, underutilization, limited elasticity, and lengthy provisioning cycles.
- CAMH's Krembil Centre for Neuroinformatics needed short-term access to large compute and hardware not available on-prem, without cloud expertise.
- Rather than replacing its HPC cluster, CAMH sought to extend it with a governed cloud option.
- Improving hardened Google Cloud's prototype portal for daily research use and simplified it so non-expert users could launch compute via a web interface.
- Provisioning that once required manual intervention now runs rapidly through a guided web interface.
- CAMH realized an estimated 40% cost reduction versus equivalent on-prem infrastructure.
- The post argues leading institutions combine on-prem for steady-state workloads with cloud for elasticity and AI acceleration.
