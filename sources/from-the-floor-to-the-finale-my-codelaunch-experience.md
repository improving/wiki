# From the Floor to the Finale: My CodeLaunch Experience — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/from-the-floor-to-the-finale-my-codelaunch-experience/from-the-floor-to-the-finale-my-codelaunch-experience.md`

## Summary

David Sandoval recounts attending the CodeLaunch World Championship, a startup pitch event he describes as part rock concert and part esports championship. CodeLaunch is a free, zero-equity, high-velocity accelerator that pairs early-stage founders with Professional Coder League teams — all Improvers — who donate two days of engineering to build real MVP features for every finalist. Improving produces the event, and the post celebrates the founders, the volunteer engineers, and the finale in which Well Water Finders swept the awards.

## Key Claims

- CodeLaunch is a free, zero-equity, high-velocity startup accelerator pairing founders with professional software teams.
- Professional Coder League (PCL) teams donate two full days of engineering, building real MVPs, prototypes, and features at no cost to founders.
- Improving does not just support CodeLaunch — it produces it, with the PCL roster made up entirely of Improvers.
- PCL teams include seasoned engineers, architects, designers, and technical leaders who volunteer for two days of high-intensity development.
- The event is staged like a live show, with motion graphics, paced performances, audience participation, and judges questioning founders.
- Well Water Finders won every award — Judges Champion, Investors Champion, and Launch Kings Audience World Champion — taking home $50,000.
