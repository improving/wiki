# My Hero — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/shaun/shaun.md`

## Summary

O'Hara profiles his younger brother as his hero, tracing a life of setbacks: growing up in an older brother's shadow, being labeled clumsy, struggling in school, and being told by a coach he would never amount to anything. Driven by a single love of football, his brother walked on at Rutgers, repeatedly struck scholarship deals that collapsed when coaches were replaced, and eventually earned his scholarship and success as an offensive lineman. Undrafted by the Cleveland Browns, he won a Super Bowl and reached three Pro Bowls with the New York Giants. The lesson O'Hara draws is that hard work and determination get you what you want.

## Key Claims

- His brother faced consistent setbacks yet kept pursuing the one thing he loved.
- He walked on at Rutgers rather than accept Division 2 scholarships, striking a scholarship deal with the coach.
- That deal collapsed twice when head coaches were replaced; he still earned the scholarship.
- He went undrafted to the Cleveland Browns, then won a Super Bowl and made three Pro Bowls with the New York Giants.
- The moral: determination, focus, and willpower carry you past others' doubts.
