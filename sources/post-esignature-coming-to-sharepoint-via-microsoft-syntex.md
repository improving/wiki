# eSignature Coming to SharePoint via Microsoft Syntex — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/post-esignature-coming-to-sharepoint-via-microsoft-syntex/post-esignature-coming-to-sharepoint-via-microsoft-syntex.md`

## Summary

Kyle Thorburn notes that clients have long wanted electronic signatures in SharePoint for sign-off policies, compliance training, purchase orders, and formal policies — historically handled with Adobe Acrobat Sign or DocuSign plus SharePoint, requiring two licenses and logins. Microsoft announced Syntex eSignature at Build 2023, letting users send signature requests while content stays in M365. It won't replace DocuSign or Adobe for external parties but streamlines internal signatures; he advises refreshing information architecture and adding metadata to signed copies.

## Key Claims

- Common e-signature cases: sign-off policies, compliance training proof, purchase orders, and other formal policies.
- The traditional approach paired Adobe Acrobat Sign or DocuSign with SharePoint, requiring two separate licenses and two sign-ins.
- Microsoft announced Syntex eSignature at Microsoft Build 2023; it lets users send signature requests while content stays in Microsoft 365.
- Syntex eSignature will not remove the need for DocuSign or Adobe Sign for external parties, but is a good option for internal signatures.
- Syntex eSignature entered public preview in July 2023.
- Adopters should update their information architecture and consider metadata such as signer, signed date, and document type to make signed documents findable.
