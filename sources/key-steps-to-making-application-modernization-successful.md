# Key Steps to Making Application Modernization Successful — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/key-steps-to-making-application-modernization-successful/key-steps-to-making-application-modernization-successful.md`

## Summary

Maryna Boryshkevych's second post lays out how to start modernization: engage stakeholders to align goals and constraints, audit applications to find candidates by business criticality and technical debt, and match strategies to objectives. It walks through the seven R strategies — retain, retire, repurchase, rehost, replatform, refactor/rearchitect, and rebuild — and stresses that modernization demands a cultural shift eased by training and involvement. A modernized base also lays groundwork for AI, streaming, IoT, and analytics, and improves security and compliance.

## Key Claims

- Start by engaging stakeholders and evaluating constraints like personnel, budget, and process.
- Audit applications for business criticality and technical debt before choosing candidates.
- The seven R strategies range from retain and retire to rehost, replatform, refactor, and rebuild.
- Modernization often requires a cultural shift; training and involving employees ease the transition.
- A modernized base enables AI, streaming data, IoT, and data analytics.
- Modernization improves security posture and regulatory compliance.
