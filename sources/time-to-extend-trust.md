# It's Time to Extend Trust — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/time-to-extend-trust/time-to-extend-trust.md`

## Summary

After practicing the other trust behaviors, the next step is to turn trust from a noun into a verb: Extend Trust. A trusted person becomes a trusted leader by giving someone else the opportunity, support, and honest feedback to earn trust in turn — a transition the author says can be life-changing.

## Key Claims

- Too much of a good thing can backfire: excess transparency or blunt straight talk can erode trust, requiring Right Wrongs.
- Candidates are often team members already achieving but never given the spotlight, or held back by low confidence.
- Opportunity: start with a team discussion and choose a real event — big (a leadership presentation) or small (a pair-programming session where the candidate presents findings).
- Support: agree who is needed, what skills to include, and when help is available, so the candidate feels backed rain or shine.
- Feedback: give accolades for high points and empathy for missteps, praising attempts made with good intent.
- The practice extends beyond work — to a PTA, school board, church, or volunteer group.
