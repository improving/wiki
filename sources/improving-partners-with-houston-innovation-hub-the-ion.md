# Improving Partners with Houston Innovation Hub, The Ion — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-partners-with-houston-innovation-hub-the-ion/improving-partners-with-houston-innovation-hub-the-ion.md`

## Summary

Improving partnered with The Ion, Houston's innovation hub, to deliver "Ion Startup University powered by Improving" — a free, open-to-the-public, six-month curriculum on business topics for entrepreneurs. Sessions run bi-weekly, each with 45 minutes of expert-led content followed by hands-on workshops. The first three sessions cover product thinking, pitching, and networking.

## Key Claims

- The program is free, open to the public, and runs six months, twice a year, with new sessions added annually.
- First session "Product – Start Well" (Sept 29, 2022) covers MVP thinking: framing the problem, personas, vision and strategy, ideation, roadmap, and product backlog.
- "Pitch & Sell" (Oct 12) covers pitch content, structure, practice exercises, and common mistakes.
- "Why – Networking" (Oct 27) covers expanding your network and maintaining relationships.
- Improving's Devlin Liles and the Ion's Jan E. Odegard frame the partnership as widening access to startup resources in Houston.
- Attendees may take the full curriculum or individual sessions as needed.
