# Building Bonds Beyond Borders: Viva La Retreat — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/vegas-retreat-2023/vegas-retreat-2023.md`

## Summary

Armando Lopez recounts Improving's third corporate retreat in Las Vegas, three and a half years after the Mexican enterprises joined during the pandemic. With 400+ Improvers from different countries and offices, the three-day trip mixed activities, gratitude, and shared meals to strengthen cross-enterprise relationships.

## Key Claims

- Improving Mexico joined in June 2020 during the global pandemic, a difficult start marked by fear and worry about losing identity.
- The 2023 retreat was the third official corporate retreat, hosted in Las Vegas with 400+ Improvers staying at Planet Hollywood.
- Activities included the High Roller Ferris wheel, a sunrise Valley of Fire hike with 25 Improvers, a pool party, an inter-enterprise tournament, and the Amazing Race.
- A pre-happy-hour room gathering where a colleague shared personalized gratitude gifts was a personal highlight.
- The retreat coincided with the Sphere's opening and U2's residency, and included a 60-plus-person outing to see Tiesto.
- Lopez credits the trips with bonding Improvers across countries and argues the company invests in people and well-being as a source of its success.
