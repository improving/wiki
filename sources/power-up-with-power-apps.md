# Power Up with Power Apps — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/power-up-with-power-apps/power-up-with-power-apps.md`

## Summary

Power Apps shifts application work from IT to citizen developers, letting power users build low-code apps that read and write enterprise data. The post covers its modes, connectors, and integrations, then lists use cases and the limitations teams should weigh before adopting it.

## Key Claims

- Power Apps builds canvas apps for free-form design or model-driven apps from Dataverse entities, and can embed in Teams, SharePoint, and Dynamics 365.
- Logic uses an Excel-familiar formula language, with hundreds of connectors to Excel, SharePoint, SQL, and third-party services like Twitter, Workday, and ServiceNow.
- Authentication and access control ride on Microsoft 365, with the Power Apps Admin Center managing deployment and lifecycle.
- Use cases include airline cabin-crew maintenance reporting with object-recognition AI, a distributor's performance management app, and remote sales order portals.
- Limitations: weak for pixel-perfect UI, light source control that complicates parallel work, developer-minded resources still needed, and dependence on browser quality and caching.
