# Improving and Cognition Announce Strategic Partnership to Shape the Future of Autonomous AI Software Development — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-and-cognition-announce-strategic-partnership/improving-and-cognition-announce-strategic-partnership.md`

## Summary

Improving announced a strategic partnership with Cognition to advance autonomous AI software development, pairing Cognition's Devin and Windsurf with Improving's consulting expertise and 8-stage AI Maturity Model. The release frames adoption as a progression through trust, governance, and organizational readiness, with Windsurf as the on-ramp and Devin as the route to autonomous operations. Improving's core claim: autonomy without governance fails.

## Key Claims

- Announced February 18, 2026 (Dallas), deploying Devin, the autonomous AI software engineer, and Windsurf, the AI-enabled development environment.
- Improving's AI Maturity Model spans three waves and eight stages: Tools (1–2), Process (3–4), and Agentic Operations (5–8).
- Windsurf serves Stages 1–3 as the easy on-ramp; Devin provides enterprise-grade autonomy at Stages 4–6 and beyond.
- Autonomous AI projects achieve only 20% success without governance infrastructure, versus 45% with it.
- Human-plus-AI at Stages 5–6 achieves 94% quality, higher than fully manual (85%) or fully autonomous (73%).
- Improving brings a governance engine, multi-agent infrastructure, change management, and domain-specific compliance (HIPAA, PCI, SOC 2).
- An internal pilot deployed Devin on Engage, Improving's employee platform, validating progression from Stage 4 through Stage 5.
- Cognition also notes Windsurf is now branded Devin Desktop.
