# Media Room Riser with LED Lights - Home Project DIY — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/media-room-riser-with-led-lights/media-room-riser-with-led-lights.md`

## Summary

A departure from the author's usual posts: a detailed build log for a two-level media room riser he and his son constructed as a Saturday project. He walks through sizing, materials, framing each level, decking, and finishing, with photos and notes on what worked. The goal was a stable platform for three recliners with LED accent lighting and no creaks.

## Key Claims

- Sized the platform 103" × 70" to fit three recliners plus a walkway, and it happened to fit behind a decorative wall feature.
- Materials included 2x12s, 2x6s, OSB subfloor panels, angle brackets, subfloor adhesive, and R-21 fiberglass insulation.
- Two subfloor layers laid perpendicular with adhesive between them (plus screws) eliminated the creaky feel.
- Insulation plus an overhang (2" on level 2, 1.5" on the stair) provided the LED mounting surface.
- Lessons learned: a slanted step complicated the LED strip connectors, and undermount clearance was too tight—a corner mount would have been better.
