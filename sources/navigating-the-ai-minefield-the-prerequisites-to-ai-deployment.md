# Navigating the AI Minefield: The Prerequisites to AI Deployment — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/navigating-the-ai-minefield-the-prerequisites-to-ai-deployment/navigating-the-ai-minefield-the-prerequisites-to-ai-deployment.md`

## Summary

Devlin Liles urges executives to slow down before "doing AI things" and secure three prerequisites: a data strategy that establishes the source of truth, governance calibrated between lockdown and Wild West (watching PII, compliance, and IP), and a clear business value case with success measures and ROI. He describes Improving's sequence — executive workshops, business-case discovery, scoped governance, systems review, and a unified data strategy for experiments.

## Key Claims

- Three prerequisites to AI deployment: a data strategy and source of truth, governance, and a defined business value case.
- Governance should avoid both total lockdown and an unregulated "Wild West," and must consider PII, compliance, and sensitive IP.
- "You can't put the toothpaste back in the tube" — settle governance before letting data roam.
- Business value needs a real "R" in ROI: define what decisions a result would inform and how success is measured.
- Improving's approach starts with executive workshops for business-case discovery, then scopes governance to identified cases with a focus on privacy, security, and guidance.
- After governance, review the systems critical to each experiment, then produce a unified data strategy identifying impacted data sources.
