# Mexico's Tech Talent Revolution: Why Companies are Flocking South of the Border — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/mexicos-tech-talent-revolution-why-companies-are-flocking-south/mexicos-tech-talent-revolution-why-companies-are-flocking-south.md`

## Summary

Mexico has become a competitive IT outsourcing destination, offering skilled talent, cost advantages, and a favorable business environment. The post makes the case for nearshore outsourcing to Mexico on cost, proximity, cultural affinity, and government support, and profiles its main tech hubs.

## Key Claims

- IT outsourcing lets companies reduce downtime, effort, and cost by entrusting work to a third party with specialized expertise.
- Mexico offers lower labor cost than destinations like India or Eastern Europe while maintaining high quality.
- Proximity matters: most of Mexico is within a five-hour flight of the US, and major hubs have direct nonstop flights, enabling hands-on relationships.
- Strong cultural affinity with the US and English proficiency ease communication and collaboration.
- The Mexican government supports IT growth through technology parks, innovation centers, and digital-skills programs.
- Mexico graduates over 130,000 engineers annually and has a talent pool exceeding 700,000 skilled engineers.
- Key hubs include Guadalajara ("Silicon Valley of Mexico"), Mexico City, Monterrey, and Tijuana, each with distinct strengths from software development to manufacturing and logistics.

## Relevance

Makes the case for [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md) as an [IT outsourcing](/concepts/it-outsourcing.md) model, contrasting it with [offshore outsourcing](/concepts/offshore-outsourcing.md) to India and Eastern Europe.
