# A Weekend of Connection and Celebration: Improving Retreat 2024 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/a-weekend-of-connection-and-celebration-improving-retreat-2024/a-weekend-of-connection-and-celebration-improving-retreat-2024.md`

## Summary

A first-person account of Improving's annual corporate retreat, held in Las Vegas, where 400 Improvers from the US, Canada, and Mexico gathered for four days. The author describes the retreat as the company's best opportunity to connect to something bigger than oneself.

## Key Claims

- 400 Improvers from the US, Canada, and Mexico shared four days at Planet Hollywood in Las Vegas.
- Thursday night options included The Sphere Experience, a Blue Man Group show, The High Roller, a Hofbrauhaus excursion, or fine dining.
- "ImprovingU @ Retreat" and "Learning and Games" offers ran throughout, including a Delivery Excellence course.
- Programming included CEO Curtis Hite's "Ask Me Anything," a Come Together / Conscious Capitalism Champions breakfast, and a Global Onboarding Strategy champions lunch.
- The Amazing Race on the Strip and enterprise contests with prizes were the closing events, ending with a group photo at the Hard Rock Café.

## Relevance

An on-the-ground illustration of [corporate retreats](/concepts/corporate-retreat.md) as culture-building, and of [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md), [employee engagement](/concepts/employee-engagement.md), and [conscious capitalism](/concepts/conscious-capitalism.md).
