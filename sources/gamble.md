# Gamble — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/gamble/gamble.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that a bet only pays off big when it carries real risk: if you always win when you're gambling, you aren't gambling at all. Growth happens in the stretch beyond comfort — you learn, you become more than you were, and you either get more back than expected or at least learn what doesn't work.

## Key Claims

- "If you always win when you're gambling, you're not gambling at all."
- True bets that pay off big need risk attached; without it you're not stretching.
- Growth happens in the stretching beyond comfort.
- Push into something bigger than you're comfortable with and you'll get more back than expected — or at least learn what doesn't work.

## Relevance

Informs [comfort zone](/concepts/comfort-zone.md), [Risk Tolerance](/concepts/risk-tolerance.md), and [Learning From Failure](/concepts/learning-from-failure.md) — deliberate risk-taking is framed as the mechanism of growth.
