# The Value of Values — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/the-value-of-values/the-value-of-values.md`

## Summary

Barbara Schultz traces how she discovered her own values through coaching, then argues that Agile and Lean tools only bring value to organizations that also live by the values behind them. Tools without values, she writes, are unsatisfying and unfulfilling.

## Key Claims

- Her first exposure to values came in coaching: if you are upset, someone has probably stepped on one of your values.
- Patterns from her own life surfaced values like contributing, being recognized, open feedback, transparency with compassion, and learning from mistakes.
- She spent 15 years applying Lean without ever seeing the 14 Toyota Principles, then noticed how closely they align with the Agile Manifesto's 4 values and 12 principles.
- Tools and techniques are more likely to bring value to organizations that also live by the values.
- Organizations that don't understand how they deliver value are less in tune with changing customer needs.
- Failing to live the values and appreciate employees drives good people out or into mediocrity.

Informs [company culture](/concepts/company-culture.md), [Lean](/concepts/lean.md), and [agile transformation](/concepts/agile-transformation.md), where values precede tools.
