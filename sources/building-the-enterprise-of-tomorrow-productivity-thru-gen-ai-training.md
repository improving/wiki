# Building the Enterprise of Tomorrow: Productivity Thru Gen-AI Training — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/building-the-enterprise-of-tomorrow-productivity-thru-gen-ai-training/building-the-enterprise-of-tomorrow-productivity-thru-gen-ai-training.md`

## Summary

The post argues generative AI is already delivering large productivity gains and that enterprises should adopt it through a structured framework rather than ad hoc pilots. It cites Klarna and Amazon as proof points, insists on keeping humans in the loop, and lays out six adoption steps from discovery through training.

## Key Claims

- Klarna's OpenAI-based assistant handled 2.3 million conversations, replacing 700 full-time agents, cutting repeat inquiries 25% and average resolution time from 11 minutes to two, across 35 languages and 23 markets, adding $40 million to profit.
- Amazon's Q saved $260 million and 4,500 developer years; 79% of AI-generated code reviews needed no human intervention, and Java upgrades fell from 50 developer days to a few hours.
- Human oversight remains essential — framed as piano versus violin: AI handles mechanical complexity, humans supply nuance and expertise.
- A six-step adoption framework: Discovery, Strategy, Roadmap, Policy Development, Pilot Program, and Training and Education.
- Training matters: AI hackathons and hands-on sessions build understanding, and cited workshops raised productivity by over 30%.
- Tools like Improving Echo and Microsoft Copilot sharply reduced time to retrieve information and generate content.
