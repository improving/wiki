# Fast and Furious IV — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/fast-and-furious-iv/fast-and-furious-iv.md`

## Summary

Improving Vancouver finally checked go-karting off its bucket list, heading to TBC Racing to kick off a weekend of friendly competition. The short post recaps the racing, the fast karts, and the burgers afterward, and looks forward to the next outing.

## Key Claims

- Go-karting had been on the Vancouver office's bucket list for a long time before this trip.
- The team raced at TBC Racing, which boasts some of Metro Vancouver's fastest karts, reaching up to 75 km/hr.
- The outing was framed as playing as hard as the office works, with competitive energy and drifting around the curves.
- The evening ended with burgers and debate over who bumped whom and who had the fastest lap.
- The team plans to repeat the event.
