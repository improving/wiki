# Unlocking the Power of Conscious Capitalism's Stakeholder Orientation Within Your Life — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/unlocking-the-power-of-conscious-capitalisms-stakeholder/unlocking-the-power-of-conscious-capitalisms-stakeholder.md`

## Summary

The second post in Mark Kovacevich's series treats stakeholder orientation as how a person activates their higher purpose. He lists four attributes of a personal stakeholder model — specificity in groups, measurable impact, impactfulness, and actionability — and stresses reciprocity, including letting go of relationships that consistently diminish you.

## Key Claims

- Stakeholder orientation is driven by higher purpose and treats stakeholders as the ecosystem a business exists within.
- Stakeholders include employees, vendors, customers, industry members, and local government.
- Personally, stakeholder orientation is how you activate your higher purpose.
- Four attributes: specificity in groups, measurable impact, impactfulness, and actionability.
- Impact should be measured, for example through intentional time spent with friends and family.
- Relationships should be reciprocal and lift you up; letting go of harmful ones is not selfish.
- Stakeholder models evolve across life stages even as higher purpose stays more permanent.
- The author's charity tailgate for Ohio State football illustrates a "triple-win" across nonprofits, friends, and family.
