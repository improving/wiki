# Viva Engage: A New Era of Employee Engagement — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/viva-engage-a-new-era-of-employee-engagement/viva-engage-a-new-era-of-employee-engagement.md`

## Summary

Michael D'Onofrio argues Viva Engage is more than a Yammer rebrand: it is a platform for community building, knowledge sharing, and culture in hybrid and remote workplaces. He contrasts the social, community-oriented Viva Engage with Microsoft Teams' project-based collaboration, and claims the platform strengthens culture, engagement, knowledge sharing, decision-making, and retention.

## Key Claims

- Viva Engage aims to recreate spontaneous office interactions and foster belonging across locations.
- It supports discussions, questions, praise, polls, and topics in a social-media-like feed.
- Teams focuses on task management, file sharing, and real-time communication; Viva Engage focuses on community and culture.
- Viva Engage reaches employees not tied to specific projects, while Teams serves focused project groups.
- Claimed benefits include stronger culture, higher engagement, better knowledge sharing and decisions, and improved satisfaction and retention.
