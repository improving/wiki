# AI Eldercare Is Already Deployed — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/ai-eldercare-is-already-deployed/ai-eldercare-is-already-deployed.md`

## Summary

Devlin Liles uses 85-year-old Jan Worrell's months with an ElliQ companion to argue that AI eldercare is already deployed, not hypothetical. Jan resisted at first, then gradually stopped calling the device "it." The post treats that pronoun shift, not shipment volume, as the real adoption curve, and asks the downstream questions the technology has not answered: who pays, who owns the memories, and who checks on the machine.

## Key Claims

- Intuition Robotics has spent close to a decade and roughly $60 million on ElliQ, whose "availability score" estimates from 0-100 how open a person is to interruption before it speaks.
- New York State's year-long pilot (from 2023) placed ElliQ with more than 800 older adults; users engaged over 30 times a day, six days a week, with three-quarters of interactions tied to health and wellbeing goals.
- NYSOFA reported a 95% drop in self-reported loneliness against intake baselines; company-wide Skuler cited 41 interactions a day and over 90% feeling less lonely.
- The U.S. Surgeon General's 2023 advisory links chronic loneliness to dementia, heart disease, and up to 30% shorter life expectancy.
- Only a few thousand units have shipped, mostly via nonprofits and state health departments — but behavioral adoption, measured by the pronoun shift, moved in months, not years.
- Open questions remain: who pays for subscriptions at scale, who owns a decade of confided memories, and who checks on the device when power fails.

## Relevance

A field report on deployed [AI Trust Evolution](/concepts/ai-trust-evolution.md), showing trust in an AI companion forming through repeated low-stakes interaction.
