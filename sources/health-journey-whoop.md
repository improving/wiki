# Health Journey: WHOOP — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-whoop/health-journey-whoop.md`

## Summary

O'Hara calls the [WHOOP](/entities/whoop.md) band the foundation of his health efforts, using it since May 2019 to study his sleep. He values its focus on [Heart Rate Variability](/concepts/heart-rate-variability.md) as a measure of strain and recovery, and because it never needs to come off for charging it provides nearly continuous monitoring. He highlights journal-based sleep correlation, a wake-up feature, competition with his brothers, and skin temperature tracking. He notes the band sometimes misses casual workouts or misreads chores as workouts, but rates it highly overall.

## Key Claims

- WHOOP's emphasis on HRV as a strain/recovery measure is central to its value for him.
- Because it charges without removal, monitoring is nearly continuous.
- Journal logging plus correlation to sleep outcomes helps him predict how well he'll sleep; he is still not a consistent "green" sleeper.
- Skin temperature and HRV together give his most reliable correlation to sleep quality.
- Shortcomings: it misses some casual workouts and occasionally counts making dinner as a workout.
