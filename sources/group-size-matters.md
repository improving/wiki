# Group Size Matters - be sure your audience matches your intent — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/group-size-matters/group-size-matters.md`

## Summary

Prompted by an article on digital life and community building, O'Hara endorses being intentional about our "third place" for mental and emotional health, then focuses on the article's 8-18-1800 rule: 8 people for a good conversation, 18 for good ideas, and 1,800 to rally for a cause. He applies it to his own communication, admitting he sometimes starts without the right goal and aims too broadly or expects too much from a large group. Having a good conversation with a massive audience is unrealistic, while relying only on a close circle risks an echo chamber.

## Key Claims

- The 8-18-1800 rule: 8 people for a good conversation, 18 for good ideas, 1,800 to rally for a cause.
- A "good conversation" with a massive audience is not realistic.
- Relying solely on your close circle for fresh ideas limits outcomes via the echo chamber effect.
- People should be intentional about their third place for mental and emotional health.
- Before initiating an interaction, ask whether it is an 8, 18, or 1800 situation.

## Relevance

Informs [deep connection](/concepts/deep-connection.md) and intentional community building — choosing audience size so communication matches its purpose.
