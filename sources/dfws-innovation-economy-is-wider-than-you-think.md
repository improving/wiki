# DFW's Innovation Economy Is Wider Than You Think — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/dfws-innovation-economy-is-wider-than-you-think/dfws-innovation-economy-is-wider-than-you-think.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues the DFW story that dominates the news is a capex story — relocations, hyperscale data centers, chip fabs — while the more interesting story is what is being created locally. He points to a statewide innovation week, a new Fort Worth forum linking startups to investors and labs, and strong talent formation numbers. His conclusion is that durable innovation economies are built on startup infrastructure, and DFW's is finally catching up to its anchor-tenant layer.

## Key Claims

- More than 15 Texas cities hosted simultaneous innovation events in a single week.
- TechFW and UNT Health launched an Innovation Forum linking startups with Cowtown Angels investors and campus lab resources.
- DFW ranked third nationally for economic development project volume.
- The metro added 37,230 tech workers in 2022-2025; AI-specific workers grew 45% year over year to nearly 30,000.
- Cities that build durable innovation economies win on startup infrastructure, not facility announcements.
