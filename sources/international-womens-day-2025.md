# International Women's Day 2025 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/international-womens-day-2025/international-womens-day-2025.md`

## Summary

For International Women's Day 2025, Improving profiles its women employees — including Kimberly Roberts, Susan Fojtasek, Victoria Ochoa Landeros, Sunelle Pink, Daniela Medrano, and Paloma Olivera — on what being a woman means in a male-dominated industry, how they stay motivated, and what advice they would give women starting tech careers. Recurring themes are owning your strengths and voice, setting and celebrating goals, building networks and mentors, and improving things for the next generation.

## Key Claims

- Contributors describe their unique perspective as a source of value and a reason to own their strengths rather than hide them.
- Several frame women in tech as challenging stereotypes, breaking barriers, and acting as catalysts for change.
- Motivation comes from weekly, monthly, and yearly goals, continuous learning, passion for the work, relationships, and family.
- Advice includes identifying passions, matching them to roles, and building strong networks and mentors.
- Contributors stress celebrating wins to build confidence instead of fixating on development areas.
- Several cite responsibility to make things better for the next generation and to support women-in-STEM initiatives.
