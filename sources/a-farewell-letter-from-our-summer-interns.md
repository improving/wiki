# A Farewell Letter From Our Summer Interns — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/a-farewell-letter-from-our-summer-interns/a-farewell-letter-from-our-summer-interns.md`

## Summary

Five 2023 Improving interns recap their eight-week program, rotating through Recruiting, Marketing, Sales, and Delivery while building skills in sourcing, branding, lead generation, project management, and communication. They credit weekly "Trust Pods" covering the 13 Trust Behaviors and their relationships with Improvers as the program's highlight, and close with advice to future interns to stay open-minded, ask for help, and take initiative.

## Key Claims

- The 2023 internship was "8 weeks, 5 interns, 4 departments, 3 books, 1 company."
- Interns rotated through Recruiting, Marketing, Sales, and Delivery, learning skills applicable beyond any single field.
- Weekly Trust Pods with a current Improver taught the 13 Trust Behaviors and tied them to everyday life.
- The interns unanimously named their connections with Improvers as the favorite part, over the tasks themselves.
- Advice for future interns: be open-minded, learn from everyone, ask for help, take initiative, and reflect on what you're learning.
- Improving's stated mission includes positively changing the perception of the IT profession.
