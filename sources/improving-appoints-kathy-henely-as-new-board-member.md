# Improving Appoints Kathy Henely as New Board Member — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-appoints-kathy-henely-as-new-board-member/improving-appoints-kathy-henely-as-new-board-member.md`

## Summary

Improving added Kathy Henely to its Board of Directors on 1 August 2021, making her the company's first independent board member. The post frames the appointment around her experience scaling a global IT services firm and her alignment with Improving's culture and Conscious Capitalism focus.

## Key Claims

- Improving added Kathy Henely to its Board of Directors on 1 August 2021.
- Henely spent nearly 20 years in Perficient's senior leadership, mostly as COO, helping scale it past 3,000 employees through organic and acquisition growth.
- She is Improving's first independent board member and serves actively, advising both the board and the CEO.
- CEO Curtis Hite cites her global IT services operating experience and cultural alignment with Conscious Capitalism.
- Henely was also named one of the Most Influential Businesswomen in St. Louis, Missouri.
