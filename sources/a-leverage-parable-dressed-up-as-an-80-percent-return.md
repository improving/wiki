# A Leverage Parable Dressed Up as an 80 Percent Return — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/a-leverage-parable-dressed-up-as-an-80-percent-return/a-leverage-parable-dressed-up-as-an-80-percent-return.md`

## Summary

Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness fund closed the year up roughly 80 percent, and the post argues that headline flattens two very different investments. The fund's public AI and semiconductor book grew to about $45 billion on 4x leverage; a 25 percent decline would erase the capital base, and chip stocks sold off through late July. On July 30 the fund liquidated its entire book to [Citadel](/entities/citadel.md) in a single pre-market block trade. What saved the year was a separate, illiquid stake in [Anthropic](/entities/anthropic.md) up 620 percent.

## Key Claims

- At 4x leverage, a 25 percent decline in the underlying book erases the entire capital base.
- The fund liquidated its full public book, longs and shorts together, to Citadel before the July 30 open, a sign it had run out of room to maneuver.
- A concentrated, illiquid [Anthropic](/entities/anthropic.md) position up 620 percent absorbed the forced-liquidation loss and left the fund net positive.
- Reporting a leveraged trading book and a long-term concentrated position as one annual return figure flattens a distinction that matters.
- The next fund margin-called on an AI trade likely will not have a position up 620 percent sitting beside it to cover the gap.
