# Contentment — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/contentment/contentment.md`

## Summary

A one-paragraph reflection. Contentment cannot be given to us or bestowed by another person or by things — it is learned. O'Hara's mechanism is contrast plus practice: we learn contentment only through the experience of discontentment, and by practising gratitude after having lacked contentment, we are able to find it.

## Key Claims

- Contentment is learned, not bestowed by other people or by possessions.
- Discontentment is the experience through which contentment is learned.
- Gratitude is the practice that turns that experience into contentment.
