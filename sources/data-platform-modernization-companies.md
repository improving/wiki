# Leading Data Platform Modernization Companies: What to Look for in a Partner? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/data-platform-modernization-companies/data-platform-modernization-companies.md`

## Summary

A buyer's guide by Shribha Kalia comparing nine data platform modernization providers. It argues modernization means re-engineering pipelines, governance, and cost models onto cloud-native platforms rather than a lift-and-shift, and that partners should be judged on platform certifications, delivery bench depth, governance design, and quantified case studies.

## Key Claims

- The global data integration market is projected to grow from $17.58B (2025) to $33.24B (2030), and poor data quality costs organizations an average $12.9M per year (IBM).
- Modernization approaches include lift-and-shift, re-platforming, data mesh, and hybrid modernization of high-value domains first.
- Selection criteria: platform-specific certifications, verified bench depth (38% report a data talent shortage), industry pattern libraries, cloud-native cost management, governance and lineage design, and quantified case studies.
- Improving's case study: Integra Connect migrated from a legacy SQL Server warehouse to Snowflake with consumption-based compute, cutting processing time from days to minutes.
- Improving cites NPS 90+, a 7.5-year average partnership length, 21 offices in 7 countries with 2,500+ consultants, and a named Confluent real-time streaming partnership.
- The guide profiles Accenture, Capgemini, Improving, TCS, IBM, DATAFOREST, ScienceSoft, Simform, and N-iX.

## Relevance

A partner-selection view of [data platform modernization](/concepts/data-platform-modernization.md), grounded in [data readiness](/concepts/data-readiness.md) and [data governance](/concepts/data-governance.md), and aimed at [lakehouse](/concepts/lakehouse.md)-style architectures and [real-time data streaming](/concepts/real-time-data-streaming.md).
