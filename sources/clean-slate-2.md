# Clean Slate — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/clean-slate-2/clean-slate-2.md`

## Summary

A short "blog bankruptcy" announcement from the start of the blog's archive. O'Hara says recent life changes shifted his perspective on how he spends his time, declares the old blog finished (its posts remain archived at blog.davidohara.net), and relaunches on a new domain built with Octopress. He commits to posting at least once a week, keeping posts short as a way to build follow-through.

## Key Claims

- The previous blog is retired; old posts stay archived at blog.davidohara.net.
- The new site is built on Octopress, which he says impressed him so far.
- Intent: blog at least once a week, deliberately short, to encourage follow-through.
- Motivation: life is short and not worth running through if you're not enjoying it.

## Relevance

The archive's earliest post; it sets the blog's intent and an early consistency theme (weekly output as a discipline).

## Quality

Practitioner blog post by David O'Hara. A personal announcement rather than analysis; its value is historical context for the blog.
