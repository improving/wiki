# Four Pivotal Generative AI Trends That Impact Your Business in 2024 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/four-pivotal-generative-ai-trends-that-impact-your-business-in-2024/four-pivotal-generative-ai-trends-that-impact-your-business-in-2024.md`

## Summary

Kevin Jourdain frames 2024's AI landscape against post-ZIRP economic tightening and picks four trends: strategic moves by incumbents like Microsoft, a startup ecosystem that drew nearly $50B in 2023, corporate experimentation centered on RAG, and the rise of text-to-video. He predicts RAG systems will become a budget priority.

## Key Claims

- 2023 closed with 3.3% GDP growth, but the end of the Zero Interest Rate Policy tightened the environment for AI development.
- Microsoft partnered with OpenAI and Mistral, unveiled Azure Maia 100 and Cobalt 100 chips, and previewed Copilot for Fabric.
- Investors put nearly $50B into AI startups in 2023; Mistral AI and Aleph Alpha each raised about $500M.
- Groq's Language Processing Unit chip, from the designer of the TPU, is cited as a standout startup breakthrough.
- Corporate generative AI use benefits individual contributors most; enterprise solutions remain immature, but RAG is emerging.
- RAG lets users query their own IP without fine-tuning, and is predicted to proliferate in 2024 budgets.
- Tyler Perry paused an $880M studio expansion after seeing OpenAI's Sora, an early high-profile text-to-video disruption.
- Nvidia shares rose close to 240% in 12 months, with AMD's MI300X emerging as competition.
