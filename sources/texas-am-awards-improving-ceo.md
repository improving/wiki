# Texas A&M Awards Improving CEO, Curtis Hite — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/texas-am-awards-improving-ceo/texas-am-awards-improving-ceo.md`

## Summary

Texas A&M University honored Curtis Hite, CEO and Chairman of Improving, with the Dwight Look College of Engineering's Early Professional Achievement Award. Hite was the first distinguished alumnus and the youngest recipient of the award, recognized for business achievement and his commitment to the global software engineering community.

## Key Claims

- Curtis Hite received the Dwight Look College of Engineering's Early Professional Achievement Award.
- He was the first distinguished alumnus and youngest recipient of the award.
- He was recognized for business success as an Aggie entrepreneur and for service to the global software engineering community.
- Under Hite's leadership, Improving was honored four consecutive years on the Aggie 100 fastest-growing list (highest rank #5).
- Hite also received the College of Engineering Distinguished Alumni Award in 2009.
- He is an active donor and scholarship supporter and serves on several university Advisory Committees.
- Hite '91 holds bachelor's and master's degrees in Computer Science from A&M and served as commanding officer in the Aggie Corps of Cadets.
