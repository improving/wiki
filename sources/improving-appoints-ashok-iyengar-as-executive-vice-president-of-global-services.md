# Improving Appoints Ashok Iyengar as Executive Vice President of Global Services — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-appoints-ashok-iyengar-as-executive-vice-president-of-global-services/improving-appoints-ashok-iyengar-as-executive-vice-president-of-global-services.md`

## Summary

Improving announced the appointment of Ashok Iyengar as Executive Vice President of Global Services, a newly created role leading the company's global services sales strategy across 21 offices in North America, South America, and India. The announcement ties the hire to Improving's expanding global delivery model and its 10-Year Vision of becoming the premier challenger brand in global digital services.

## Key Claims

- Iyengar brings a 34-year record of scaling technology services businesses across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.
- Most recently he was SVP and Head of the Hitachi Business Unit at GlobalLogic (a Hitachi Group company), one of three founding leaders of the post-acquisition synergy organization, where he scaled the unit to over $275 million across Energy, Mobility, Rail, and Connected Industries.
- In the new role he will lead Global Services sales strategy across 21 offices in North America, South America, and India and drive expansion of the enterprise client portfolio.
- The appointment comes as Improving expands its global delivery model with recent additions across Latin America and India.
- Curtis Hite (CEO) cited Iyengar's combination of global scale experience and hands-on delivery leadership.
- Iyengar will unify the global sales model, strengthen strategic partnerships, and expand the global sales team, working with the Managing Director of Global Services and enterprise presidents.
- Improving has partnered with private equity firm Trinity Hunt Partners since 2018 and operates 21 offices across the Americas and India.
