# Overheard today...sharing something I heard today because I think it's impactful — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/overheard-today/overheard-today.md`

## Summary

The author shares a newly heard acronym he found striking: LIE, for Limiting Idea Entertained. Reflecting on it, he notes that everyone tends to believe lies from time to time — lies others have fed us about our capabilities, and the stories we tell ourselves about our experiences and desires. Referencing an earlier post on "Dream Thieves," he makes the point that these lies are everywhere but need not be entertained: you get to decide whether they limit you. He closes with a question to the reader about which limiting idea they are entertaining.

## Key Claims

- "LIE" stands for Limiting Idea Entertained — a memorable framing the author adopts.
- Everyone believes lies at times, whether from others about their capabilities or self-authored stories about their experiences and desires.
- These lies are pervasive, but entertaining them is a choice rather than a requirement.
- Deciding not to be limited by them is framed as genuinely empowering.
- The post ends by asking readers which limiting idea is keeping them from their full potential.
