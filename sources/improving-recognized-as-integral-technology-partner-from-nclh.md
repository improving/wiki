# Improving Recognized As An Integral Technology Partner In Client Testimonial From NCLH’s Emerging Technologies & Innovation Team — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-recognized-as-integral-technology-partner-from-nclh/improving-recognized-as-integral-technology-partner-from-nclh.md`

## Summary

Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings' Emerging Technologies and Innovation team publicly credits Improving Ottawa as an integral technology partner in developing and delivering a modernized reservation system. The testimonial frames a multi-year relationship built on resources, strategic consultancy, and managed services support.

## Key Claims

- Improving Ottawa and NCLH's innovation team have worked together on a modern reservation system since 2019.
- The engagement involved more than 40 developers, coaches, and managed services consultants.
- The platform affects over 2 million passengers aboard NCLH's 32 ships annually.
- NCLH previously relied on a fragmented legacy system; Improving helped it move to a unified, scalable, service-oriented platform.
- Fidel Perez, NCLH VP of Emerging Technologies and Innovation, cites dedication, expertise, flexible solutions, and proactive support.
- Jack Gulas, Improving Managing Director, calls being named a key partner a significant milestone.
