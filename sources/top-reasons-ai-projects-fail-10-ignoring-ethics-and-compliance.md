# Top Reasons AI Projects Fail #10: Ignoring Ethics & Compliance — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/top-reasons-ai-projects-fail-10-ignoring-ethics-and-compliance/top-reasons-ai-projects-fail-10-ignoring-ethics-and-compliance.md`

## Summary

The tenth and final part of Improving's "Top Reasons AI Projects Fail" series argues that AI projects rarely fail because of weak algorithms — they fail because of oversight. When ethics, compliance, and regulation are treated as afterthoughts, the authors warn, "ship fast and break things" becomes "ship fast and break trust." The published excerpt is a short introduction to the theme rather than a full treatment.

## Key Claims

- AI projects rarely fail because of weak algorithms; they fail because of oversight.
- Ignoring ethics, compliance, and regulation quietly undermines even the most advanced initiatives.
- Treating governance as an afterthought turns "ship fast and break things" into "ship fast and break trust."
- This is the final entry in the ten-part Top Reasons AI Projects Fail series.
