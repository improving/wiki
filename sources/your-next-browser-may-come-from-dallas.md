# Your Next Browser May Come from Dallas — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/your-next-browser-may-come-from-dallas/your-next-browser-may-come-from-dallas.md`

## Summary

David O'Hara reads Island's $400 million raise at a $6.4 billion valuation as under-appreciated evidence that Dallas-Fort Worth is producing serious enterprise AI software. He argues Island is not a browser company but an agent governance company that happens to sit in the browser, since the application layer is where control over what agents and employees can access must live. The funding round, a record DFW VC quarter, and the new Texas Stock Exchange together describe a region becoming a capital market for enterprise AI.

## Key Claims

- Island raised $400 million at a $6.4 billion valuation for enterprise browsers
- The enterprise browser is where governance of what agents can access actually has to live—making Island an agent governance company
- DFW's Q3 2026 was its best VC quarter on record: $962.5 million across 52 deals
- The Texas Stock Exchange launched in 2026, and Venture Dallas runs October 22
- These data points show DFW has quietly become where enterprise AI problems get built, funded, and governed
