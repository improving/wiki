# Vegas Retreat 2021 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/vegas-retreat-2021/vegas-retreat-2021.md`

## Summary

Improvers from every US, Mexico, and Canada office gathered in Las Vegas in October 2021 for the company retreat, with more than 100 first-time attendees. The post frames the event as space to build authentic, lasting relationships across offices rather than as a purely social trip.

## Key Claims

- Improvers from all US, Mexico, and Canada offices attended the October 2021 Las Vegas retreat.
- More than 100 people attended for the first time, including the Nearshore family attending since the 2020 iTexico merger.
- Houston President Devlin Liles describes the retreat as space to build long-term, authentic relationships.
- Activities included the High Roller, the Amazing Race, beer pong, team-building games, and self-planned outings.
- Organizers judge success by the friendships that outlast the event, not the scheduled activities.
