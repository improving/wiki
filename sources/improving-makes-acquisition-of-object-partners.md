# Improving Announces its Newest Acquisition of Object Partners — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-makes-acquisition-of-object-partners/improving-makes-acquisition-of-object-partners.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired Object Partners, a custom software development firm based in Minneapolis with a secondary Omaha office, merging it into Improving – Minneapolis to serve the Twin Cities region. The transaction was expected to raise Improving's annualized revenue by more than 25% in 2020, to over $150 million, and deepen its Midwest reach and healthcare vertical.

## Key Claims

- Object Partners is headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, with a second office in Omaha, Nebraska.
- The deal was expected to grow Improving's annualized revenue by over 25% in 2020, exceeding $150 million collectively.
- The acquisition continues Improving's "Enterprise Strategy" of merging service firms that share commitments to trust, excellence, and culture.
- Object Partners' leadership team stays intact and keeps operating the business while gaining access to Improving's broader services.
- Curtis Hite cited Object Partners' custom software expertise and extensive healthcare experience as strengthening Improving's strategic North American healthcare vertical.
- Object Partners President and CEO Chris Spurgat framed the fit around becoming a trusted partner to all customers.
