# The Difference Between Managing the Process and Managing the Goals — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/difference-between-managing-process-and-managing-goals/difference-between-managing-process-and-managing-goals.md`

## Summary

Emily Stickle argues that leaders and Scrum Masters struggle because they mix managing goals with managing process, and that each role should own a different one — leaders the goal, Scrum Masters the process that facilitates it.

## Key Claims

- A rental-car story shows the confusion of prescribing process ("leave at 10, get gas far away, use the back entrance") instead of stating the goal (return by 5 with a full tank).
- Teams get asked for features by being told how to build them, or hear process when they need the goal, which leaves them frustrated.
- Leaders should facilitate goal setting, build the vision, and hand it to the team without caveat; managing process makes them micromanagers and kills creativity.
- Scrum Masters should ensure the process facilitates the team's goals, removing blockers rather than managing the goals themselves.
- Reframe questions from "how does this follow the process" to "how will that decision impact our goal".
