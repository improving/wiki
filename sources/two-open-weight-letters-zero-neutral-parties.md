# Two Open-Weight Letters, Zero Neutral Parties — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/two-open-weight-letters-zero-neutral-parties/two-open-weight-letters-zero-neutral-parties.md`

## Summary

Liles compares two July 2026 letters on open-weight AI: Nvidia's "Open Weights and American AI Leadership," urging Washington to avoid premature restrictions, and "Pacing the Frontier," signed by 1,200+ lab employees asking government to build slowdown mechanisms in advance. He argues neither letter has a signatory who would lose money if its ask became policy, so readers should discount each argument by how directly the signer's revenue depends on the outcome — while still treating both underlying risks as real.

## Key Claims

- Nvidia's letter (July 24) argues closed models are not inherently safer and asks Washington to avoid "premature restrictions"; it drew 25 signers on day one, roughly 50 within 24 hours.
- OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google sat out the Nvidia letter — closed-model vendors whose silence is itself informative.
- "Pacing the Frontier" (July 28) came from 1,200+ employees across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Meta, asking for standby slowdown infrastructure, not an active pause now.
- Media inflated both: one podcast framed the worker letter as "SLOW DOWN AI," and an unverified report put Nvidia signers above 230 versus ~50 documented.
- The fight mirrors the 1990s Crypto Wars over encryption export controls.
- The two letters describe distinct risks: premature restriction strangling a technology, and no restriction mechanism existing when harm appears.
