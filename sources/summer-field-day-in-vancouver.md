# Summer Field Day in Vancouver — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/summer-field-day-in-vancouver/summer-field-day-in-vancouver.md`

## Summary

Improving Vancouver held a Picnic & Games event at Fraser Foreshore Park, then a family day at Playland and the PNE, as a way to build relationships and welcome new team members.

## Key Claims

- A Friday afternoon of group games: badminton, spike ball, frisbee, slacklining, cornhole, table tennis, bocci ball, and Jenga.
- A Steamworks food truck provided lunch and treats through the day.
- The event doubled as a welcome for several new team members.
- The next day, Bit Quillians and their loved ones of all ages went to Playland and the PNE.
