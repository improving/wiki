# Nearshore Outsourcing Explained — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/unlocking-the-benefits-of-nearshore-outsourcing/unlocking-the-benefits-of-nearshore-outsourcing.md`

## Summary

Nearshoring combines the reliability of onsite talent with the affordability of outsourcing by hiring teams in geographically close, time-zone-aligned countries. The post contrasts onshore, nearshore, and offshore models, enumerates nine benefits of nearshoring, and surveys the leading LATAM locations, arguing proximity yields better collaboration and control than distant offshore arrangements.

## Key Claims

- 87% of IT organizations have considered nearshore outsourcing to cut costs (Deloitte).
- Cost savings can reach 40–70% versus onshore (DevsData), while avoiding full-time benefits, training, and office-space costs.
- Proximity reduces communication and cultural barriers and makes visits and face-to-face meetings practical.
- A one-hour increase in time-zone distance reduces synchronous communication by about 11% (Organization Science), which hurts productivity.
- 437 universities across Latin America produce over 220,000 STEM graduates a year, feeding a pool of more than 2 million developers.
- Mexico, Chile, Guatemala, and Argentina have strong IP laws similar to the US, and take data protection seriously.
- Other benefits: easier project tracking, freeing the main team for strategy, and faster response to disruption.
- Key LATAM locations include Aguascalientes and Guadalajara in Mexico, Guatemala City, Buenos Aires, and Santiago.
