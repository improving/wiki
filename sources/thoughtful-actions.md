# Thoughtful Actions — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/thoughtful-actions/thoughtful-actions.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that the common advice to "think before you speak" should extend to thinking before you act. He credits his father's saying — "never be dictated to by expediency" — and says it has proven correct in his experience. He grants that life sometimes demands snap decisions, but insists there are many situations where reflection is affordable. The gap he identifies is that nobody ever taught him what to actually do with that reflection time, so he built a template to structure it. He shares it at journal.driventodevelop.com, describing it as simple and self-explanatory (Twitter authentication only), and reports that using it brought recent situations to significantly better conclusions than he expected.

## Key Claims

- Thinking before acting is as important as thinking before speaking.
- "Never be dictated to by expediency" has held up as guidance in his own life.
- Snap decisions are sometimes unavoidable, but deliberate reflection is often possible.
- No one teaches what to do with reflection time, so the thinking stays unstructured.
- His own written template produced better outcomes than he had hoped for at the outset.
