# How Artificial Intelligence Is Transforming Marketing — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-artificial-intelligence-is-transforming-marketing/how-artificial-intelligence-is-transforming-marketing.md`

## Summary

Cynthia Lupercio argues that AI and data science help marketers analyze customer data at a personal level to build hyper-focused, personalized campaigns. She lists personalization forms and focus areas, and cites rising marketing adoption of AI as evidence the shift is mainstream.

## Key Claims

- Citing Forbes, top-performing companies are more than twice as likely to use AI for marketing (28% vs 12%).
- AI platforms can analyze massive amounts of customer data at a personal level to anticipate customers' next moves and improve the customer journey.
- Personalization forms include behavior-based product recommendations, demographic-based promotions, customized push notifications, and automated outreach.
- Reported benefits include more impulse purchases, increased revenue, fewer returns, and higher customer loyalty.
- Suggested focus areas: know your customers, personalize interactions, engage across the journey, and make data-driven decisions.
- Citing Salesforce, the share of marketing leaders using AI rose from 29% (2018) to 84% (2020).
