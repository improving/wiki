# Improver Spotlight - Kevin Baker — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improver-spotlight-kevin-baker/improver-spotlight-kevin-baker.md`

## Summary

A profile of Kevin Baker, VP of Consulting and User Experience in Improving's Dallas office, who has spent seven years "making great technology feel simple." The piece centers on his habit of asking "why," his definition of user-centered design as meeting user and business needs in the middle, and his role in driving company-wide adoption of Conscious Capitalism.

## Key Claims

- Baker frames user-centered design as learning what the user needs and what the business needs, then meeting in the middle — he makes a habit of asking why.
- He joined Improving and quickly recognized he was among the smartest people and in the right place to learn and grow.
- He was a major mover behind company-wide adoption of Conscious Capitalism, attending nearly every Dallas chapter meeting since 2016.
- He values that business should do more than make money and that everyone and everything it touches matters.
- Purpose is a recurring theme for him: work that looks like progress without a greater purpose lacks meaning.
- He is known in the office for his daughter's annual Girl Scout cookie deliveries.

## Relevance

Ties [human-centered design](/concepts/human-centered-design.md) to [conscious capitalism](/concepts/conscious-capitalism.md) and a [stakeholder orientation](/concepts/stakeholder-orientation.md), showing how UX values and company culture reinforce each other.
