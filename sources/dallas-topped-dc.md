# Dallas Just Topped D.C. Here's What That Actually Means — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/dallas-topped-dc/dallas-topped-dc.md`

## Summary

O'Hara reports that Site Selection Magazine ranked Dallas the number one tech hub in North America, ahead of Washington D.C., alongside a large Wistron investment and record tech-job growth. He argues DFW is no longer a market catching up to the coasts but one where talent, demand, infrastructure, and capital converge at scale. The practical message is that firms should treat DFW as a primary market and move quickly.

## Key Claims

- Site Selection Magazine named Dallas the number one tech hub in North America, ahead of Washington D.C.
- Wistron is putting $761 million into AI supercomputers in Fort Worth.
- DFW added more tech jobs than any U.S. metro between 2022 and 2024.
- Fortune 500 headquarters supply the demand signal; the talent pipeline supplies the build side; data centers, manufacturing, and semiconductor investment are the foundation.
- The window to establish position in the region is shorter than it appears.
