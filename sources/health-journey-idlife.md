# Health Journey: IDLife Supplements — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-idlife/health-journey-idlife.md`

## Summary

O'Hara explains why IDLife became his supplement provider: quality and bio-availability matter more than any single vitamin debate, and IDLife starts customers with a DNA test to personalize their stack. He describes tailoring his subscription as he adds other supplements, and switching from Liquid IV to IDLife Hydrate after continuous glucose monitoring revealed hidden-sugar spikes.

## Key Claims

- There is little disagreement in the literature that supplement quality is not optional; a trusted, bio-available product matters or you are "flushing money down the drain"
- He rates IDLife as reputable, reasonably priced, and something he can feel when taking versus not — while admitting possible placebo effect
- IDLife starts you with data: a DNA test feeding into a personalized first order
- He previously used Liquid IV, but his CGM showed it caused spikes, which he attributes to hidden sugars
- Switching to IDLife Hydrate ended the spikes and coincided with an uptick in sleep he thinks relates to hydration (more testing to come)
