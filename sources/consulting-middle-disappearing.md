# The Consulting Middle Is Disappearing. Which Side Are You On? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/consulting-middle-disappearing/consulting-middle-disappearing.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that AI-driven consulting spend is rising — from 0.8% to 1.7% of client revenues — but the money is not spreading evenly. It flows to two poles: scaled ecosystem integrators running nine-figure transformation programs, and deep specialists owning a narrow lane. The generalist mid-market firm is being squeezed, because clients now demand measurable operational outcomes and route to whoever shows the tightest expertise-to-outcome connection. He positions Improving as a "global boutique" blending large-firm scale with boutique connectedness.

## Key Claims

- AI is set to double client consulting spend from 0.8% to 1.7% of revenues this year.
- Money concentrates in scaled integrators and narrow deep specialists (cybersecurity, pricing analytics, vertical AI in healthcare or financial services).
- The generalist mid-market firm — too big to be nimble, not big enough to compete on scale — is shrinking.
- Clients demand measurable operational outcomes and route to the tightest expertise-to-outcome connection.
- "AI consulting" is not a differentiator; "AI delivery in the mid-market with a track record" starts to be one.
