# How AI is Enabling the Agile Values — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-ai-is-enabling-the-agile-values/how-ai-is-enabling-the-agile-values.md`

## Summary

AI is reshaping Agile practice, but its best current use is augmenting familiar work: summarizing, categorizing, analyzing data, generating ideas, and filling blind spots. With well-crafted prompts — goal, role, context, task, and output format — AI can draft a product vision, customer personas, product backlog items, and a focused sprint goal. Those drafts give the Scrum Team and stakeholders a better starting point for refinement, and validation loops plus human review keep outputs ethical, unbiased, and accurate.

## Key Claims

- AI's best current application is augmenting things you already know how to do, rather than replacing them.
- A good prompt specifies the goal, the AI's role (with details), context and aspects of the ask, the task, and the expected output format.
- AI can generate a product vision reflecting company goals, customer needs, and strategic direction.
- AI can create detailed customer personas covering goals, challenges, and needs.
- AI can generate product backlog items from the vision and personas, and suggest items the team had not considered.
- Post-release data (reviews, usage logs, surveys, purchase history, social media, support tickets) can inform priorities by pain-point frequency and severity.
- Iterative validation, feedback loops, and human review are needed for ethical, unbiased, accurate results.
