# Improving Acquires Zigatta — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-zigatta/improving-acquires-zigatta.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired Zigatta, a Frisco, Texas software development and consulting firm with offices in Guadalajara, Mexico. The deal pushes Improving's annualized revenue past $275 million and extends its reach across the US and Latin America.

## Key Claims

- Zigatta specializes in high-end data, cloud, software engineering, and architecture.
- Under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy" — merging technology service companies that share a commitment to trust, excellence, and culture — the Zigatta leadership team stays intact and continues to run and grow the business.
- CEO Curtis Hite cites clear alignment between the two companies and Zigatta's customer relationships and culture.
- Zigatta CEO Nahid Giga cites shared values and community giving, plus access to Improving's broader capabilities.
- Founded in 2015, Zigatta grew its Latin America presence through two acquisitions in Guadalajara, Mexico.
