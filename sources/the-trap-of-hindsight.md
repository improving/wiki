# The Trap of Hindsight — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-trap-of-hindsight/the-trap-of-hindsight.md`

## Summary

A short reflection on hindsight bias: we judge our past selves through the understanding and information we hold now, then conclude we always could have done more, different, or better. In reality we chose what we could see at the time. Hindsight is useful for learning, but turned into a device for self-flagellation it stops helping, and it easily slides into comparison.

## Key Claims

- We judge past-you through the lens of current-you's understanding and information.
- The trap is the sickly sweet belief that you always could have done more, different, or better.
- In the moment you didn't see it or know it; had you, you would have chosen differently.
- Hindsight is valuable as a learning and growth tool, not as a self-flagellation device.
- Give past-you grace, and don't let the reflection become comparison.
