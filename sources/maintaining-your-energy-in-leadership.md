# Maintaining Your Energy in Leadership — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/maintaining-your-energy-in-leadership/maintaining-your-energy-in-leadership.md`

## Summary

The post argues that managing personal energy is the core skill for leading teams through turbulent times. The author offers three practices: ruthless prioritization, self-renewal through scheduled breaks, and fostering a culture of openness and empathy. Together they protect the leader from burnout, set an example for the team, and build the trust needed for people to take risks.

## Key Claims

- When a launch deadline collides with a flood of support requests, prioritize only launch-critical work and delegate or triage the rest rather than doing both and burning out.
- Schedule non-negotiable "power breaks" (a 15-minute walk, a short meditation) and protect time for joy and family outside work.
- Self-care replenishes energy and models healthy behavior for the team.
- Meet a team member's critical mistake with empathy and a solution-oriented mindset, not blame or punishment.
- Frame the conversation around "how things could be different in the future" to avoid shame, reinforce learning from failure, and grow a growth mindset.
