# Character and Choices — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/character-and-choices/character-and-choices.md`

## Summary

The post opens with a quote — "Your character is the sum total of your choices." — and pairs it with Rick Voirin's line that awareness precedes choice, while habit is what remains without it. O'Hara draws a loop from the two: you make your choices, then your choices make you. He closes by asking the reader where they are living by habit rather than by deliberate choice.

## Key Claims

- Character is the accumulated sum of a person's choices.
- "With awareness comes choice. Without awareness only habit" (Rick Voirin, Stagen Leadership Academy).
- Choices shape the chooser as much as the chooser shapes the choices.
- Habit can substitute for choice without the person noticing it.

## Relevance

Informs [self-awareness](/concepts/self-awareness.md) as the precondition for deliberate choice, and the reflective/leadership themes running through the blog.
