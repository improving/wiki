# Microsoft StaffHub Merged Into Teams — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/microsoft-staffhub-merged-into-teams/microsoft-staffhub-merged-into-teams.md`

## Summary

Microsoft announced at Ignite 2018 that StaffHub, its Office 365 shift-and-schedule management app for front-line workers, would retire on October 1, 2019, with its features folded into Microsoft Teams. The post explains the migration, the two surfaces the features move onto, and what IT admins should do to prepare.

## Key Claims

- StaffHub gave organizations a digital platform for managing shifts and schedules, letting supervisors push schedules and staff request shift changes or swaps.
- StaffHub is retired as of October 1, 2019; anyone opening it afterward is directed to download Microsoft Teams.
- Retiring StaffHub reduces the spread of overlapping solutions across Office 365 and cuts application switching for users.
- Features merge onto two surfaces in Teams: a Home screen (day's info, clock in/out, alerts, who else is on shift) and a Shifts tab for supervisors to create, edit, and push schedules.
- A Microsoft tutorial covers creating/publishing schedules, requesting time off, swapping shifts, clocking in/out, and pinning Shifts on mobile.
- IT admins are notified via the Office 365 Message Center and should communicate the change and update internal documentation.
- Users with a license that includes Teams retain their data and StaffHub's core functionality after transition.
