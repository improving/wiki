# We've Expanded Into Canada — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/weve-expanded-into-canada/weve-expanded-into-canada.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired Quadrus Development Inc., a leading IT services and solutions firm headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, expanding beyond the United States into Canada and expected to raise annualized revenue by more than 20%. Quadrus was a five-time Inc500/5000 recipient with a 20-year track record and closely aligned Agile, Microsoft (.NET), and Java services.

## Key Claims

- Improving acquired Quadrus Development Inc., headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.
- Quadrus was a five-time Inc500/5000 recipient (2009–2013 Honor Roll).
- The transaction was expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue by more than 20%.
- It expanded Improving's reach beyond the United States and into Canada.
- Quadrus had a 20-year track record and services aligned on Agile, Microsoft (.NET), and Java application development.
- The deal strengthened consulting, business intelligence and data services, and training offerings across local and international markets.
- Quadrus brought development tools including the Quadrus Estimator Suite.
- Brian Donaldson was co-founder and Chief Technical Director of Quadrus.

## Relevance

Improving's first geographic expansion outside the US, exemplifying [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) and growing its [IT outsourcing](/concepts/it-outsourcing.md) delivery into [Canada](/entities/canada.md).
