# My Improving 100 Experience — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/my-improving-100-experience/my-improving-100-experience.md`

## Summary

Armando Lopez recounts being named an Improving 100 recipient for 2023 and the all-expenses-paid four-day trip to Cancún's Secrets: The Vine resort with 62 honorees and their guests. He describes the welcome reception hosted by Scott McMichael and Curtis Hite, group dinners, an excursion day, and a closing celebration and dance-off. The post's point is that peer-nominated recognition and shared time together deepen belonging in a 1,500-person, three-country company.

## Key Claims

- Improving 100 recognizes excellence in Community Impact, Business Growth, Delivery Excellence, and Technical Innovation.
- Employees nominate their peers; leadership makes the final selection.
- The prize is an all-inclusive trip for the recipient and a plus-one; 62 honorees attended.
- The 2024 trip ran June 13–16 at Secrets: The Vine in Cancún.
- Excursion options included a catamaran adventure, Mexican cooking class, ATV, and cenote/adventure park.
