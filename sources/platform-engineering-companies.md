# Platform Engineering Companies to Know Before You Sign a Contract — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/platform-engineering-companies/platform-engineering-companies.md`

## Summary

Choosing a platform engineering partner is hard because the discipline spans cloud architecture, Kubernetes operations, developer experience, and governance, and few vendors are strong across all four. The guide profiles nine companies and lays out criteria — Kubernetes/cloud-native depth, multi-cloud fluency, internal developer platform experience, regulated-environment track record, delivery model, and long-term viability — to separate a real platform practice from a rebranded staffing arrangement.

## Key Claims

- Gartner projects 80% of large software engineering organizations will have dedicated platform teams by the end of 2026, up from 45% in 2022; 88% of technology executives call platform engineering crucial to their objectives.
- The platform engineering and IDP market is projected to grow from $10.44B in 2026 to $31.57B by 2031 (Mordor Intelligence).
- Look for certified engineers (CKA, CKS, CKAD) with production experience, multi-cloud delivery across Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud, and quantified outcomes, not logo agreements.
- Improving claims 74 certified Kubernetes engineers (51 CKA, 4 CKS, 19 CKAD), 2025 Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year, NPS 90+, and a 7.5-year average partnership length.
- McKesson case: automated deployment of its Drug Serialization Repository across 29 distribution centers with GitHub Actions, Kubernetes, Confluent Kafka, and MongoDB, cutting deployment time per location from 4–6 hours to 33 minutes and meeting the DSCSA deadline.
- Thoughtworks originated the "platform as a product" framing in 2017; the other profiles include EPAM Systems, Endava, Persistent Systems, Netris, Stackgenie, XenonStack, and RX-M.
- A golden path is an opinionated, pre-approved workflow that makes the secure and compliant route the fastest one, chosen because it saves time rather than because it is mandated.
