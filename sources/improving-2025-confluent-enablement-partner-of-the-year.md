# Improving is recognized as the Winner of the 2025 Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year - AMER — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-2025-confluent-enablement-partner-of-the-year/improving-2025-confluent-enablement-partner-of-the-year.md`

## Summary

A newsroom press release announcing that Improving was named the 2025 Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year – AMER, honored alongside other global Confluent partners. The award specifically recognizes a partner that advanced the "Data in Motion" vision and the "Data Streaming Engineer" category through collaboration, and that completed the highest number of Confluent certifications. The release includes a company boilerplate describing Improving's focus and scale.

## Key Claims

- Improving was recognized as the 2025 Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year – AMER, announced October 28, 2025, in Dallas.
- The award recognizes exceptional dedication to advancing the "Data in Motion" vision and creating the "Data Streaming Engineer" category.
- The winning partner completed the highest number of certifications through Confluent and invested in continued professional development.
- The AMER awards span 11 regional categories across systems integrators, cloud service providers, and technology partners.
- Ehren Seim, Global Alliances Lead, cited clients in healthcare, manufacturing, financial services, and energy.
- Improving describes itself as operating internationally with 21 offices across the Americas and India, partnered with Trinity Hunt Partners since 2019.
