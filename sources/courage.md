# Courage — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/courage/courage.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues courage is a virtue to be cultivated, not an innate quality — like a muscle, it grows through deliberate practice and effort. Cultivation starts with self-awareness and a willingness to face fears, through small steps, challenging the comfort zone, or seeking support. The moment fear grips you and you choose your next action is where courage grows or withers. Courage is also not solitary: leading by example motivates others, and sharing your fears is an act of vulnerability that builds connection.

## Key Claims

- Courage is not innate; it is a virtue that must be actively cultivated and nurtured.
- "Fear is a feeling; courage is a choice" — cultivation begins with self-awareness and facing doubts.
- Small steps, challenging the comfort zone, and seeking support grow courage over time.
- The choice point when fear grips is where courage grows or withers, and life offers repeated chances.
- Leading by example and sharing fears inspire others and deepen connection.
