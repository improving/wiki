# Improving Dallas New Location — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-dallas-new-location/improving-dallas-new-location.md`

## Summary

Improving moved its Dallas office from Addison to Plano, Texas, to accommodate growth, holding a grand opening and ribbon cutting on January 31. In ten years the company had grown to five enterprise locations and six offices across the United States and Canada, and the Plano event drew close to 175 guests.

## Key Claims

- Dallas office relocated from Addison to Plano, Texas.
- In ten years Improving grew to five enterprise locations with six offices across the US and Canada.
- Grand opening and ribbon-cutting ceremony held January 31.
- Members of the Plano Chamber of Commerce attended to welcome the company.
- New open-concept space includes three classrooms for software and process training and a large project area.
- Close to 175 guests attended.
- Curtis Hite framed the move as continued commitment to building trust with clients, suppliers, and community.
