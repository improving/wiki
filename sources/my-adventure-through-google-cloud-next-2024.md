# My Adventure Through Google Cloud Next 2024 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/my-adventure-through-google-cloud-next-2024/my-adventure-through-google-cloud-next-2024.md`

## Summary

Maryna Boryshkevych recounts Improving's attendance as a Google Partner at Google Cloud Next 2024. The post catalogs the announcements — six kinds of AI agents, the Gemini for Google Cloud suite, AI-powered Workspace, Gemini-infused databases and BigQuery/Looker, and AI-optimized infrastructure from TPU v5p to the AI Hypercomputer and Distributed Cloud.

## Key Claims

- Google announced six kinds of AI agents spanning customer service, employee empowerment, creative ideation and production, data analysis, code creation, and cybersecurity.
- Gemini for Google Cloud includes Gemini Code Assist and Gemini Cloud Assist.
- Workspace AI added "take notes for me" transcription and automatic classification/protection of sensitive files using privacy-preserving models and DLP controls.
- Gemini capabilities were added to Bigtable, Spanner, Memorystore for Redis, Firestore, CloudSQL for MySQL, and AlloyDB, plus BigQuery and Looker improvements.
- AI infrastructure spans TPU v5p GA and the AI Hypercomputer, with Google Distributed Cloud and rebranded Anthos (on GKE) for hybrid and multi-cloud.
- Sessions covered "AI in Action," "Serverless Revolution," and "Data Lakes and Beyond," with hands-on workshops on model deployment, MLOps, and data engineering.
