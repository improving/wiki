# Improving 100: A Wild Adventure of Gratitude and Connection — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-100-a-wild-adventure-of-gratitude-and-connection/improving-100-a-wild-adventure-of-gratitude-and-connection.md`

## Summary

A first-person account by a Leniolabs/Improving South America employee of being named to the 2024 Improving 100, the company's recognition program for people who go the extra mile. Winners and guests got an all-expenses-paid weekend at Maroma Beach Resort in Cancún, and the post argues the trip's real value is the cross-border connection it creates.

## Key Claims

- Improving 100 recognizes Improvers who embody company values through dedication, impact, and spirit; recipients are nominated by teammates.
- The 2024 winners and their chosen guests were invited to an all-expenses-paid weekend at Maroma Beach Resort in Cancún from June 27–29.
- The author joined seven months after Leniolabs became Improving South America and describes the integration's warmth and sense of belonging.
- The weekend included excursions (ATV and cenote), a VIP dinner hosted by leadership in small groups, and a celebration at Oceana Terrace.
- CEO Curtis Hite and CCO Devlin Liles joined an impromptu pool gathering; North American Managing Director Scott McMichael spoke at the finale.
- The post frames recognition as community — feeling seen and appreciated — rather than trophies.
