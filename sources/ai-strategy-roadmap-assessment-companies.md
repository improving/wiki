# AI Strategy & Roadmap Assessment: Choosing a Partner Who Can Actually Execute — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/ai-strategy-roadmap-assessment-companies/ai-strategy-roadmap-assessment-companies.md`

## Summary

A buyer's guide by Shribha Kalia profiling ten firms that offer AI strategy and roadmap assessments. It argues a credible assessment evaluates business priorities, data readiness, technology foundations, and governance maturity, then produces a phased, executable plan — and that enterprises should favor partners who pair strategy with in-house engineering delivery over slideware-only advisors.

## Key Claims

- 88% of AI proofs of concept never reach production (CIO); the failure is usually strategy built for a demo rather than for data quality, governance review, and budget scrutiny.
- A credible assessment covers discovery, readiness evaluation, governance framework design, phased roadmap development, and change enablement, and typically runs four to twelve weeks.
- Cost ranges from roughly $25k–$40k for short discovery engagements to $40k–$500k+ for comprehensive enterprise-wide assessments.
- Two questions separate real partners: what percentage of your recommended solutions reach production, and can you share a case where you recommended against AI.
- Improving's assessment maps to its 8-stage AI Maturity Model, citing 250+ AI projects, $4.4B in ROI, 400+ practitioners, and a Lakeshore Learning agentic deployment delivered in 90 days.
- The guide profiles McKinsey, Improving, BCG, Accenture, IBM, Deloitte, Appinventiv, LeewayHertz, SoftServe, and Cleveroad, contrasting strategy-only firms with strategy-plus-build firms.

## Relevance

Informs [AI strategy](/concepts/ai-strategy.md) and [AI readiness](/concepts/ai-readiness.md); readiness depends on [data readiness](/concepts/data-readiness.md), and the pilot-to-production gap is [pilot purgatory](/concepts/pilot-purgatory.md). The evaluation criteria extend [partner selection](/concepts/partner-selection.md).
