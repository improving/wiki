# The Audit Your Clients Haven't Done (And Are About to Need) — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/audit-clients-havent-done/audit-clients-havent-done.md`

## Summary

The post argues that enterprise AI has a widening gap between deployment speed and operational control: teams ship agents into production, then scramble to answer what the agent can access, who owns it when something breaks, and what data it touches. The EU AI Act's August enforcement deadline does not create that problem, it just puts a due date on a reckoning organizations have been deferring. An AI system inventory is the unglamorous prerequisite for governing anything, and firms that build the audit engagement model now will earn their clients' trust for the next wave.

## Key Claims

- 79% of organizations face challenges adopting AI, and 36% have no formal plan for supervising their agents.
- The EU AI Act enforcement clock "just started ticking," with an August deadline.
- You can't govern what you haven't mapped; an AI system inventory makes everything else possible.
- Firms that inventory AI now, for operational excellence rather than pure compliance, are the ones clients will trust next.
- The open question is whether consultants have an engagement model ready when the client finally asks.
