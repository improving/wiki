# Finding Home at Guadalajara Pride — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/finding-home-at-guadalajara-pride/finding-home-at-guadalajara-pride.md`

## Summary

An Improving employee describes attending Guadalajara Pride after the company offered to cover travel for anyone who wanted to go. Growing up in a small, conservative city, she expected judgmental glances and quota-filling corporate attendance, but found a genuine celebration that felt like home. She argues that this sense of belonging does not happen by accident — Improving actively builds it through such moments and everyday culture.

## Key Claims

- Improving covered travel expenses for any employee who wanted to attend Guadalajara Pride.
- The author grew up in Mérida, where change is slow and she learned to move cautiously through certain spaces.
- She expected at least a few judgmental looks, murmurs, or employees attending "out of obligation" — and found none.
- What she found was a celebration that wanted to "celebrate diversity, not just tolerate it."
- Belonging "doesn't happen by accident"; Improving builds it through the Pride march and the everyday culture around it.
- She returned home with hope and lasting connection to fellow Improvers.

## Relevance

Illustrates the lived side of [inclusion and diversity](/concepts/inclusion-and-diversity.md) and [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md) as culture that is deliberately built rather than declared.
