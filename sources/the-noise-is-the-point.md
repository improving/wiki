# The Noise Is the Point — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-noise-is-the-point/the-noise-is-the-point.md`

## Summary

O'Hara revisits Texas's posture on AI infrastructure after Governor Greg Abbott paused data center approvals on August 3 to audit energy usage. He argues the reversal is less substantive than it looks — vague, with company exemptions announced within weeks — and that the opposition is partly manufactured, citing documented Chinese-agent social media campaigns. He counters common objections, noting modern facilities recycle water and that data centers have lowered electricity rates in many areas, and warns that restricting compute will push builds to other states and countries.

## Key Claims

- Abbott paused data center approvals on August 3 to audit energy usage; the order is vague and specific exemptions followed within weeks.
- Axios documented Chinese agents seeding anti-data center social media content — some opposition is not organic.
- Water-use objections are largely debunked for modern facilities, which recycle water and have moved away from water-intensive cooling.
- Marketplace reported in July that data centers have lowered rates in many areas by spreading fixed grid costs across more customers.
- Compute access is foundational to the next generation of businesses; restricting it shifts the build elsewhere and hurts those who could benefit most.
