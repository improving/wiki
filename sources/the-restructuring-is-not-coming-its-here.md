# The Restructuring Is Not Coming. It's Here. — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-restructuring-is-not-coming-its-here/the-restructuring-is-not-coming-its-here.md`

## Summary

Argues that the consulting talent model shift has moved from a future planning item to a present restructuring. Accenture announced 11,000 layoffs in an $865 million AI restructuring, KPMG cut 400 US advisory positions, and Deloitte is restructuring select practices. The work being eliminated — high-volume, structured, document-heavy — is exactly the first- and second-year analyst workload, which AI now absorbs at a fraction of the cost.

## Key Claims

- Accenture announced 11,000 layoffs as part of an $865 million AI restructuring; KPMG cut 400 US advisory roles.
- PwC data showed AI-exposed junior roles were seven times more likely to require advanced human skills going forward.
- The replacement is not pure automation but fewer, more senior people operating with AI augmentation.
- Accenture expects overall headcount to grow in fiscal 2026, but the growth is in AI-aligned roles.
- Organizations must ask what year one looks like for a new hire today and whether it prepares them for year three.
