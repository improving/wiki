# Improving Acquires Sophic Group — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-sophic-group/improving-acquires-sophic-group.md`

## Summary

On January 14, 2011, Improving finalized its acquisition of The Sophic Group, Inc. in Columbus, Ohio, launching its "Enterprise Model" and expanding into the Midwest with annual revenue expected to rise more than 15%. Because both firms focused on Agile, Microsoft (.NET), and Java application development, the union extended the service offerings available to the Columbus office.

## Key Claims

- Improving finalized an agreement to acquire The Sophic Group, Inc. in Columbus, Ohio, on January 14, 2011.
- Annual revenue was expected to increase by more than 15%.
- The deal expanded Improving's national reach into the Midwestern United States.
- It launched Improving's "Enterprise Model" of merging like-minded service companies under a single brand.
- Sophic and Improving offered similar services, both focused on Agile, Microsoft (.NET), and Java application development.
- The union extended the number of service offerings available to the Columbus office.
- Curtis Hite said the model's secret is preserving local ownership behavior and accountability.
