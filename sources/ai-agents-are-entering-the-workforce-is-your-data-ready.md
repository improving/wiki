# AI Agents Are Entering the Workforce. Is Your Data Ready? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/ai-agents-are-entering-the-workforce-is-your-data-ready/ai-agents-are-entering-the-workforce-is-your-data-ready.md`

## Summary

A new generation of AI agents can act, not just help, working across systems end to end — retrieving, updating, validating, and passing work along. Janine Jeanson argues that because agents follow your lead, messy, siloed, or untrustworthy data produces messy, siloed, or untrustworthy AI. The winners will be organizations that treat AI as an operating model and redesign data flows, systems, and governance rather than bolting tools onto broken processes.

## Key Claims

- AI agents differ from prior AI by acting across systems rather than answering a single prompt.
- "If your data is messy, your AI will be messy" — agents follow the standards of the data they're fed.
- Data readiness does not mean having lots of data; it means clean, governed, consistent data, integrated systems, clear process logic, and accessible secure infrastructure.
- Agents work end to end: processing invoices, updating CRM records, reconciling data, and running customer workflows.
- Company A treats AI as a feature and bolts tools onto broken processes; Company B treats AI as an operating model and redesigns data flows, systems, and governance.
- Agents need permissions structured, audit trails, and guardrails to protect the business.
- AI agents won't replace teams; they take on work humans shouldn't be doing and free people to innovate.
