# Improving Announces Creation of New Microsoft Team — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-announces-creation-of-new-microsoft-team/improving-announces-creation-of-new-microsoft-team.md`

## Summary

Improving announced a new corporate Microsoft Services Team to focus on its Microsoft partnership, supporting each enterprise and helping clients get more from their Microsoft investments. CEO Curtis Hite appointed Lowell Messner, former president of Improving Cleveland, to lead the team.

## Key Claims

- Improving is a modern digital services company with a history as a System Integrator and indirect Cloud Solution Provider in the Microsoft Partner ecosystem.
- The new team will work with each Improving enterprise to maximize the value of the Microsoft partnership and help clients do the same.
- CEO Curtis Hite appointed former Improving Cleveland president Lowell Messner to lead the team.
- The merger of the Columbus and Cleveland enterprises into Improving Ohio freed a leader with deep Microsoft expertise for the corporate role.
- As a Microsoft Managed Partner, Improving supports M365, Azure, and D365 across account segments and industries.
- Continued success in this service area is expected to lead to expansion of the team.
