# 9 Years on Inc. 5000 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/9-years-on-inc-5000/9-years-on-inc-5000.md`

## Summary

Improving appeared on the Inc. 5000 list of America's fastest-growing private companies for the ninth consecutive year in 2017. The company ranked #4461 and was one of only 81 other firms to have made the list nine years running. Executives planned to attend the Inc. 5000 Conference in Palm Desert that October.

## Key Claims

- 2017 marked Improving's ninth year on the Inc. 5000.
- Improving ranked #4461 for 2017.
- It was among only 81 other companies with nine consecutive years on the list.
- A delegate attended the Inc. 5000 Conference in Palm Desert in October 2017.
- Speakers included Daymond John of Shark Tank and Alan Mulally, former Ford CEO.
- The conference closed with a black-tie gala for all honorees.
