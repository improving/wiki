# Consistency — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/consistency/consistency.md`

## Summary

Starting from a Craig Groeschel quote — "Consistency not luck is the differentiator. Successful leaders do consistently what other leaders do occasionally" — O'Hara argues consistency is the success factor a leader actually controls. He lays out three payoffs: it builds trust and reliability plus predictability and stability for the team; it builds the discipline and accountability needed to hit goals, and modelling it may inspire others; and it keeps attention on the big picture so leaders can course-correct and progress on the right things.

## Key Claims

- Consistency, not luck, is the differentiator; successful leaders do consistently what others do occasionally (Groeschel).
- Leaders decide what they focus on and spend time on, so consistency is within their control.
- Consistent delivery earns the team's trust and respect and creates predictability and stability.
- Consistency builds discipline and accountability, and a leader living it out may help others do the same.
- Consistency keeps focus on long-term goals despite challenges and distractions.
