# Leading with empathy and building a better culture — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/leading-with-empathy/leading-with-empathy.md`

## Summary

O'Hara reflects on [empathy](/concepts/empathy.md) as the force behind meaningful connections and effective leadership, transcending conventional leadership approaches with a human-centered perspective that also strengthens productivity. His approach centers on understanding team members' diverse perspectives and emotions through [active listening](/concepts/active-listening.md), naming feelings (his own and others'), and showing genuine concern — which he says cultivates trust and [psychological safety](/concepts/psychological-safety.md). He describes concrete rituals like "coffee chats" and "Wellness Wednesdays" that create space for openness and connection.

## Key Claims

- Empathy is not a buzzword but the driving force behind meaningful connections and effective leadership.
- Understanding perspectives and emotions, active listening, naming feelings, and genuine concern cultivate trust and psychological safety.
- Regular informal gatherings ("coffee chats") give team members a safe space to discuss experiences, challenges, and aspirations, fostering belonging.
- Initiatives like "Wellness Wednesdays" and mindfulness sessions prioritize mental health and reinforce a culture that values well-being.
- Prioritizing empathy creates a ripple effect, producing a culture where understanding, compassion, and support thrive.

## Relevance

Connects [empathy](/concepts/empathy.md), [active listening](/concepts/active-listening.md), and [psychological safety](/concepts/psychological-safety.md) into a practitioner's model of culture-building leadership.
