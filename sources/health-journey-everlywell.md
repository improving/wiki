# Health Journey: Everlywell — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-everlywell/health-journey-everlywell.md`

## Summary

A friend on his own health journey recommended Everlywell, so O'Hara takes the Food Sensitivity Comprehensive Test to see what might be tuned — motivated partly by how much he sweats. The test flags one significant reactivity and about thirty mild ones. He decides to eliminate everything scoring above a threshold, then reintroduce foods to find culprits, and notes the raw data was hard to export.

## Key Claims

- Only one significant issue: chickpeas ("bu-bye hummus")
- Mildly reactive to about 30 things
- He eliminates everything with a reactivity score above 20, excluding butter and garlic as low-reactivity and essential to palatability
- Plan: give it 30 days, then start adding things back to see what makes him feel bad
- The test provides raw numbers, but they were not easy to export or download, so he manually copied them into Excel
