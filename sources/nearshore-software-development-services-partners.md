# Nearshore Software Development Partners — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/nearshore-software-development-services-partners/nearshore-software-development-services-partners.md`

## Summary

Nearshore development in Latin America has become a strategic lever rather than a cost tactic, balancing cost efficiency, technical expertise, and real-time collaboration with teams in nearby time zones. The guide frames when nearshore beats onshore or offshore, lists the criteria for comparing vendors, and profiles twelve leading providers, with Improving ranked first.

## Key Claims

- Nearshore teams maintain a 6–8 hour overlap with US business hours, enabling live stand-ups, sprint reviews, and faster feedback loops.
- Nearshore services typically cut costs 20–40% versus onshore without sacrificing quality, and savings can be reinvested in innovation.
- Statista projects the nearshore market to grow at a CAGR above 12% over five years; Everest Group reports North American organizations increasingly prioritize agile delivery, real-time collaboration, and proximity.
- Evaluation areas include domain and industry experience, cultural fit, communication, scalability, technical expertise and partnerships, security and compliance (ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA), past projects, pricing flexibility, and delivery methodology.
- Top providers listed: Improving, TCS, Accenture, Wipro, ThoughtWorks, EPAM Systems, Persistent Systems, Slalom, Globant, Endava, SoftServe, and Nagarro.
- Improving is credited with 600+ nearshore developers, 500+ outsourcing projects, 30+ industries, and a 94% client satisfaction score, with LATAM centers in Mexico, Guatemala, Chile, and Argentina.
- Improving's LATAM footprint was deepened through the iTexico, Leniolabs, Zigatta, and MangoChango acquisitions.
