# DragonSpears Acquisition — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/dragonspears-acquisition/dragonspears-acquisition.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired DragonSpears, a Chicago software development firm specializing in cloud adoption, agile coaching, and DevOps, pushing Improving's annualized revenue beyond $285 million and strengthening its Midwest footprint. The DragonSpears leadership stays in place under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy," and DragonSpears is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant. Both CEOs frame the deal around shared values of trust, excellence, and community.

## Key Claims

- DragonSpears focuses on cloud adoption, agile coaching, DevOps, and data management, and was founded in 2015.
- The acquisition increases Improving's annualized revenue beyond $285 million.
- DragonSpears' leadership team remains in place and continues to lead and grow the business.
- DragonSpears holds SOC 2 Type 2 compliance for protecting client data.
- DragonSpears ranked on the Inc. 5000 four years in a row and was named a Best and Brightest Place to Work in Chicago eight consecutive years.
- Improving operates 20 offices across North and South America and India, partnered with private equity firm Trinity Hunt Partners.
