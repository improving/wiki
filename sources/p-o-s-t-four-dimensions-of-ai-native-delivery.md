# P.O.S.T.: Four Dimensions of AI-Native Delivery — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/p-o-s-t-four-dimensions-of-ai-native-delivery/p-o-s-t-four-dimensions-of-ai-native-delivery.md`

## Summary

P.O.S.T. reformulates the Agile Manifesto's four trade-off values as dimensions: Productivity (Individuals & Interactions × Processes & Tools), Outcomes (Working Software × Comprehensive Documentation), Satisfaction (Customer Collaboration × Contract Negotiation), and Time to Market (Responding to Change × Following a Plan). AI-native processes and tools make both sides of each historical trade-off available at once. A workflow that still requires trade-offs on any dimension is AI-assisted at best.

## Key Claims

- The Manifesto's values were corrections for scarcity; AI-native tooling dissolves each trade-off (self-maintaining builds, generated documentation, cheap adaptive planning).
- A trade-off tells a team what to sacrifice; a dimension tells it what to measure — a substantive shift, not repackaging.
- Goodhart's law is an argument for the framework's shape: gaming one dimension shows up as degradation in another, visible in a four-dimensional view.
- Diagnostic use: if a team still operates in trade-off mode on a dimension, that is where AI-native investment should focus.
- Design use: the four dimensions serve as requirements for new AI-native workflows.
