# How Technology is Holding Back Business Value — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-technology-is-holding-back-business-value/how-technology-is-holding-back-business-value.md`

## Summary

This webinar recap argues that technology is both enabler and hindrance — the "technology paradox" — and that rushed, misaligned adoption creates cost and risk. It prescribes aligning technology with business goals, investing incrementally, and treating AI as software that needs ongoing upkeep.

## Key Claims

- Misalignment is common: companies build their own ERP systems and discover too late that the cost outweighs the benefits versus affordable, scalable SaaS.
- Chasing trends like AI without a clear business need or ROI understanding wastes time and resources.
- Unmaintained systems become liabilities — a "technology maintenance overhang" that eventually forces massive overhaul costs.
- Remedies: prioritize business objectives before selecting technology, invest incrementally, and focus on real-world practicality and integration.
- AI has passed its hype phase but should be treated like any other software, requiring ongoing updates and robust data and infrastructure.
