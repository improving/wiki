# Improving Tech Week 2022 | Thoughts — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/tech-week-2022/tech-week-2022.md`

## Summary

Improving Nearshore hosted a free, week-long series of talks from May 16–20, 2022 aimed at anyone starting an IT career, held at its Guadalajara and Aguascalientes offices and available virtually. The program invited 28 Mexican universities and drew on Improving consultants as speakers.

## Key Claims

- Tech Week 2022 ran May 16–20, 2022, with free sessions at Guadalajara and Aguascalientes plus a virtual option.
- The audience was students and young adults interested in starting an IT career; 28 universities across Mexico were invited.
- Topics included cloud, Java, QA, machine learning, and soft skills such as leadership and trust.
- Sessions were delivered by current, successful Improving consultants.
- Most talks were in Spanish, but practicing English was an explicit goal for future careers.
