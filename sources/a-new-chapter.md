# A New Chapter — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/a-new-chapter/a-new-chapter.md`

## Summary

A short personal announcement. After the company grew past 90 consultants, it created additional VP of consulting positions and O'Hara was promoted into one, giving him more involvement in the business and in helping people chart their careers. Then president Barry Rogers chose to semi-retire in pursuit of a different work-life ratio (documented at We Be Balanced), so O'Hara would take over as president beginning in 2013. He is excited to shape the company's direction but expects to prioritize differently and to code late at night less often.

## Key Claims

- The company grew to over 90 consultants, prompting new VP of consulting positions; O'Hara was promoted into one.
- He values helping people see their career paths and the future-oriented side of the business.
- President Barry Rogers decided to semi-retire to change his work-life ratio, writing about it at We Be Balanced.
- O'Hara becomes president beginning in 2013.
- He will re-prioritize and expects his late-night coding sessions to become less frequent—not to disappear.
