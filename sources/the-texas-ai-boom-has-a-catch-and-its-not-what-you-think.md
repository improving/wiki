# The Texas AI Boom Has a Catch. And It's Not What You Think — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-texas-ai-boom-has-a-catch-and-its-not-what-you-think/the-texas-ai-boom-has-a-catch-and-its-not-what-you-think.md`

## Summary

Notes that Texas leads the nation in proposed AI data centers and in proposed natural gas plants to fuel them, and that the backlash has begun. Governor Abbott told utilities to charge data centers for their own infrastructure costs and named AI regulation a 2027 legislative priority. Infrastructure booms generate backlash when they externalize costs, so Texas's deregulation-based advantage faces a stress test.

## Key Claims

- Texas leads the US in proposed AI data centers and in proposed natural gas plants to power them.
- 248 data centers are in the pipeline across the state.
- Wistron operates at AllianceTexas; Lambda has a $700 million facility in Plano.
- Governor Abbott directed utilities to charge data centers for their own infrastructure costs.
- AI regulation was announced as a 2027 legislative priority, shortening the frictionless-expansion window.
