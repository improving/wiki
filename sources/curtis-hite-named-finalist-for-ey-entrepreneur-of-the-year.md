# Curtis Hite Named EY Entrepreneur Of The Year Finalist — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/curtis-hite-named-finalist-for-ey-entrepreneur-of-the-year/curtis-hite-named-finalist-for-ey-entrepreneur-of-the-year.md`

## Summary

Improving announced that CEO Curtis Hite was named a finalist for the EY Entrepreneur Of The Year 2025 Southwest Award, which recognizes game-changing business leaders. The Southwest region covers North Texas, Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Wichita, Kansas. Hite credited the whole team, and the post notes Improving's expansion across North and South America and India, nine years on the Inc. 5000, and repeated Best Places to Work recognition.

## Key Claims

- Curtis Hite was named a finalist for the EY Entrepreneur Of The Year 2025 Southwest Award.
- Finalists are judged by an independent panel on entrepreneurial spirit, purpose, growth, and impact.
- The Southwest region covers North Texas, Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Wichita, Kansas.
- Winners were to be announced June 21, 2025, at Gilley's Dallas, then advance to the national competition.
- Under Hite, Improving expanded across North and South America and India.
- Improving appeared on the Inc. 5000 for nine years and earned national and state Best Places to Work accolades.
- Hite framed the recognition as a reflection of the entire team over 18 years.
