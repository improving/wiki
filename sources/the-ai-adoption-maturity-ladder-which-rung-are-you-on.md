# The AI Adoption Maturity Ladder: Which Rung Are You On? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/the-ai-adoption-maturity-ladder-which-rung-are-you-on/the-ai-adoption-maturity-ladder-which-rung-are-you-on.md`

## Summary

The post argues organizations consistently overestimate their AI maturity, running experiments and calling it strategy, and offers a four-rung ladder — Ad-hoc, Experimentation, Strategic, Transformational. Most organizations are on rung two and believe they are on rung three, because they measure activity rather than capability. The diagnostic is what happens when AI fails: at Ad-hoc nothing organizational changes; at Transformational governance catches it at a quality gate.

## Key Claims

- Rungs: Ad-hoc (uncoordinated individual use), Experimentation (structured pilots with hypotheses and metrics), Strategic (AI in business planning with ROI accountability), Transformational (AI embedded in the operating model).
- Most organizations sit at Experimentation while believing they are Strategic.
- Overestimation comes from measuring activity (tools deployed, champions named) instead of capability (reliable value creation).
- The diagnostic for actual rung is organizational response when AI fails, from individual frustration to governance catching failures at a quality gate.
- Interventions differ by stage; applying Stage 4 thinking to a Stage 2 organization produces expensive failures, and skipping rungs relocates their cost into later failures.
