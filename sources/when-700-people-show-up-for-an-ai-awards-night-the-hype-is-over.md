# When 700 People Show Up for an AI Awards Night, the Hype Is Over — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/when-700-people-show-up-for-an-ai-awards-night-the-hype-is-over/when-700-people-show-up-for-an-ai-awards-night-the-hype-is-over.md`

## Summary

The author reads a standing-room crowd of 700 at the Dallas Regional Chamber's 2026 AI 75 awards night as a maturity signal rather than a hype signal. The list is not venture founders or academics but operators and institutions — the CTIO of DFW Airport, which moves 90 million passengers a year, and EY's innovation lead advising Dallas and Fort Worth on governance. Markets mature, he argues, when the infrastructure class (operators, policymakers, institutions) shows up to absorb what builders make. The conversation has moved from "should we do AI" to "who's doing it well."

## Key Claims

- Seven hundred people filled an awards event — not a pitch night or demo day — for the regional AI ecosystem.
- The AI 75 roster skews to operators and institutions, not venture-backed founders or researchers.
- Markets mature when the infrastructure class shows up, not only the builders.
- The DFW conversation has shifted from "should we do AI" to "who's doing it well and what can we learn."
- Clients now benchmark against peers already deploying AI; not being in relationship with those on these lists means missing the room where buying decisions are shaped.
