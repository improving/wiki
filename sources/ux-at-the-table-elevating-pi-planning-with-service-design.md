# UX at the Table: Elevating PI Planning with Service Design — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/ux-at-the-table-elevating-pi-planning-with-service-design/ux-at-the-table-elevating-pi-planning-with-service-design.md`

## Summary

Inviting UX into Product Increment (PI) Planning does more than provide design support: it shapes the experience early and aligns the team with decision-makers. In a recent session, the author facilitated a Service Design Blueprint around the quarterly theme "Full Install Workflow," mapping everything from pre-installation tasks to early in-app steps and exposing gaps the team had not seen.

## Key Claims

- Being present in PI Planning let UX hear requirements from Product, constraints from Development, and priorities early, keeping designs both intuitive and realistic.
- The install workflow spanned other systems, hardware, installation steps, and human touchpoints long before a user opened the application, so a Service Design Blueprint was the right tool to visualize that complexity.
- The session brought Development, Product, UX, and client stakeholders together, with each perspective adding a layer to the blueprint.
- Mapping the full journey revealed workflow gaps and cross-system dependencies, and showed how much time and effort the pre-installation tasks required.
- A client stakeholder shared the blueprint with company leadership as a quarterly highlight, opening the door to mapping and designing the pre-planning phase.
- Advice for UX practitioners new to PI Planning: connect with team leads beforehand, arrive with early concepts to spark discussion, and advocate for UX activities in the session.
