# Health Journey: Function Health — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-function-health/health-journey-function-health.md`

## Summary

O'Hara learns about Function Health through Dr. Mark Hyman and signs up for the beta, excited by its goal of making health-focused testing more accessible and affordable. He outlines the pricing, the volume of biomarkers tested, and the functional-medicine orientation he expects from the people behind the company. The post is a first-impressions account of joining the beta.

## Key Claims

- $499 per year (current beta pricing) buys two rounds of testing
- Function Health partners with labs for the draws (his was at a Quest Labs)
- The first round provides 100+ biomarkers; the 6-month follow-up provides 60+
- Recommendations are expected to be focused and functional-medicine oriented given the company's founders
- Joining the beta came with three sign-up slots, two of which he and his wife used, with one shared out
