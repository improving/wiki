# The Stack You Chose Last Year Just Became the Right Answer — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-stack-you-chose-last-year-just-became-the-right-answer/the-stack-you-chose-last-year-just-became-the-right-answer.md`

## Summary

Argues that converging market signals have effectively answered the AI platform selection question, and that the real cost was the time spent deliberating. Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation, Claude holds 42–54% of enterprise code generation share, and Claude edged past GPT in business AI spend. Teams that committed to a stack a year earlier are a year ahead.

## Key Claims

- Anthropic raised $65 billion in June at a $965 billion valuation.
- Claude holds somewhere between 42% and 54% of global enterprise code generation market share, depending on the study.
- Ramp's May 2026 AI Index shows Claude passing GPT in business AI spend for the first time.
- Platform lock-in cuts both ways: choosing early costs optionality, but waiting costs time.
- Teams that committed twelve months ago have production patterns, institutional knowledge, and an iteration lead.
