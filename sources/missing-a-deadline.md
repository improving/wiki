# Missing a Deadline — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/missing-a-deadline/missing-a-deadline.md`

## Summary

The post asserts that a missed deadline is a failure of leadership, not solely of execution. Leaders who want the role must accept that accountability, own what did and didn't happen, and show how the future will be better—rather than pointing at who contributed to the miss. Drawing on the Speed of Trust framework, the author argues that "Deliver Results" is the best starting point for building trust, and delivering means not missing deadlines.

## Key Claims

- "Missed deadlines is a failure of leadership and not solely of execution."
- It is a leader's job to be accountable for what did and didn't happen.
- The right response is to own the miss and explain how things will go right, not to assign blame.
- Among the 13 Trust Behaviors, "Deliver Results" is the strongest foundation for building trust.
- Delivering results means not missing deadlines.
