# How Improving Champions a Collaborative Environment Throughout a Global Company — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-improving-champions-a-collaborative-environment-throughout-a-global/how-improving-champions-a-collaborative-environment-throughout-a-global.md`

## Summary

Improving describes four internal programs that keep a 19-office global company collaborative: Come Together for connection and belonging, PATH for mentoring, ImprovingU for peer-taught learning, and an annual Las Vegas retreat. The through-line is that connection across geographies is deliberate and pays back in engagement and retention.

## Key Claims

- Come Together is the core collaboration initiative, bridging 19 global offices through virtual gatherings, cross-office projects, and team building; 1,356 Improvers participated at least once in 2023.
- PATH (Professional Advancement Through Helping) encourages Improvers to mentor, be mentored, and learn from each other, improving retention and capability.
- ImprovingU delivers virtual training, workshops, and courses, drawing on perspectives from all 19 offices rather than job roles or tech stacks.
- The annual Las Vegas retreat brings Improvers together for team-building, workshops, and social events.
- The post argues that when employees feel connected they care more about their work and colleagues, producing better team outcomes.
