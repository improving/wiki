# PATH Success Story: Intrinsic Motivation — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/path-success-story-intrinsic-motivation/path-success-story-intrinsic-motivation.md`

## Summary

Mark Runyon tells how an Improver used Improving's PATH program to pursue Professional Scrum Master I training, working with a coach to set 90-day commitments and SMART goals broken into daily practices. Reaching the goal generated unexpected intrinsic motivation, and he went on to apply PATH practices to sprint work, everyday tasks, and a further Azure Fundamentals certification.

## Key Claims

- The Improver's biggest historical obstacle was getting started; PATH supplied structure and a timetable.
- Coaching translated a goal into 90-day commitments and SMART goals with minute-level deliberate daily practices.
- Seeing incremental weekly progress produced motivation he had not anticipated — the closer he got, the stronger the desire to finish.
- Regular coach conversations sustained focus and recharged enthusiasm.
- He passed the PSM certification and then applied PATH to daily sprint execution and other areas of life.
- PATH remains his development method; he is now working toward Azure Fundamentals certification.
