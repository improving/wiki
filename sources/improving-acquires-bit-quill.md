# Improving Further Expands into Canada with Acquisition of Bit Quill — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-bit-quill/improving-acquires-bit-quill.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired Bit Quill Technologies, a Vancouver-based software consultancy specializing in data and cloud development. The deal is expected to push Improving's annualized revenue past $250 million and expand its footprint in Canada. Under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy," Bit Quill's leadership stays in place and continues to run and grow the business while gaining access to Improving's broader offerings.

## Key Claims

- Bit Quill is a data and cloud software development firm based in Vancouver, founded in 2017.
- The acquisition is expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue above $250 million.
- Improving's "Enterprise Strategy" merges technology service companies that share a commitment to trust, delivery excellence, and culture.
- The Bit Quill leadership team remains intact and continues to operate the business.
- CEO Curtis Hite and Bit Quill CEO/co-founder Kyle Porter both frame the deal around shared core values and cultural alignment.
