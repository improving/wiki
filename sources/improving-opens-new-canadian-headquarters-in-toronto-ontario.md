# Improving Opens New Canadian Headquarters in Toronto, Ontario — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-opens-new-canadian-headquarters-in-toronto-ontario/improving-opens-new-canadian-headquarters-in-toronto-ontario.md`

## Summary

Improving announced the grand opening of its Canadian headquarters in Toronto, describing it as a milestone in a rapid three-year expansion of its Canadian footprint. The office will host 100 employees and anchors further investment in platform engineering and data & AI.

## Key Claims

- The new headquarters is at 171 East Liberty in Toronto and will be home to 100 employees.
- Over three years, Improving grew its Canadian presence from 49 employees in Calgary to 400 across Vancouver, Toronto, Ottawa, and Winnipeg, giving coverage across major time zones.
- Ontario's Minister of Economic Development cites the region's 70,000 annual STEM graduates and 420,000 tech workers.
- Improving supported the local tech community through CodeLaunch, which held its inaugural Canadian show in Toronto in August.
- CEO Curtis Hite says the office helps serve Canadian customers and tap Toronto's talent pool.
- Future Canadian investments are planned in platform engineering and data & AI.
