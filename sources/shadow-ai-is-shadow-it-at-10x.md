# Shadow AI Is Shadow IT at 10x — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/shadow-ai-is-shadow-it-at-10x/shadow-ai-is-shadow-it-at-10x.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that unapproved AI tool use is the shadow IT story repeating at far greater speed and scale, and that approval queues do not stop adoption so much as push it underground. Because the governance gap is a design problem rather than a discipline problem, the winning response is to make the sanctioned option competitive with the unsanctioned one: when the compliant tool is also the best tool, shadow adoption declines naturally. He suggests governing by reversibility rather than restriction.

## Key Claims

- 78% of AI users at work bring their own tools outside IT approval.
- One CIO calls shadow AI "shadow IT at 10x"; a real number, not a metaphor.
- IBM's breach research found unsanctioned AI tools inside 43% of security incidents, more than doubling in one year.
- 65% of AI users fear falling behind if they do not adopt AI now.
- Restriction-based programs only make the AI inventory harder to find, and you cannot govern what you cannot see.
