# The Safe Bet Isn't Safe Anymore — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-safe-bet-isnt-safe-anymore/the-safe-bet-isnt-safe-anymore.md`

## Summary

Revisits the author's long-standing guidance that the Microsoft ecosystem was the lowest-risk enterprise AI play, arguing the landscape has shifted. Microsoft is reportedly training salespeople to pitch against OpenAI and Anthropic, replacing them in Word and Excel, while rivals build a counter-protocol. The "safe bet" was never a vendor — it was an architectural approach that survives vendor consolidation and model commoditization.

## Key Claims

- Microsoft invested $13 billion in OpenAI yet is reportedly training sales to compete against it.
- Microsoft is developing competing models and swapping OpenAI and Anthropic out of Word and Excel.
- Google, Salesforce, Snowflake, and ServiceNow are building a counter-protocol to the Anthropic/OpenAI model layer.
- When a dominant vendor starts competing with its own partners, the integration tax rises and flexibility falls.
- The safe bet is open standards, API-first integration, and workloads not locked to a single model or platform.
