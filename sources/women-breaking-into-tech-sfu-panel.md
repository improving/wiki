# Women Breaking into Tech - SFU Panel — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/women-breaking-into-tech-sfu-panel/women-breaking-into-tech-sfu-panel.md`

## Summary

On June 7th, several Improving team members held a speaking panel at Simon Fraser University's Burnaby campus, sharing personal journeys into the tech industry. Panelists spanned Software Development, Account Management, Talent Acquisition, and People and Development, making the case that thriving in tech does not require being a developer. Discussion covered career advancement, mentorship, and the challenges facing people early in their careers.

## Key Claims

- The panel took place on June 7th, hosted at Simon Fraser University's Burnaby campus.
- Panelists represented Software Development, Account Management, Talent Acquisition, and People and Development — roles beyond coding.
- The core message: "You don't have to be a developer to thrive in tech."
- Every part of the team plays a role in achieving shared goals, not just developers.
- Audience questions focused on career advancement, the importance of mentorship, and challenges for early-career individuals.
- Team members' images were blurred at their request to protect their privacy.
