# Building the Tech Backbone of Guadalajara's World Cup Future — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/building-the-tech-backbone-of-guadalajaras-world-cup-future/building-the-tech-backbone-of-guadalajaras-world-cup-future.md`

## Summary

When FIFA named Guadalajara a 2026 World Cup host city, the region needed mobility, security, tourism, and city-experience solutions fast. CodeCup 2026 was created as a multi-sector innovation program to accelerate startup solutions against FIFA-identified challenges and leave a lasting digital legacy. Improving was the only technology company directly supporting startups at the Guadalajara hackathon, deploying 24 engineers to mentor 24 startups one-to-one.

## Key Claims

- CodeCup 2026 spanned three phases — onboarding, mentorship, and development — culminating in a Grand Final on May 19 at the Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara.
- Over a two-day sprint on April 20–21, Improving paired 24 engineers with 24 startups for focused real-time technical guidance.
- Startup challenges were drawn from FIFA-identified priorities: mobility, security, tourism, and city experience.
- Institutional partners included the Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara, the Government of Zapopan, the U.S. Consulate, and Jalisco's Secretary for Innovation, Science and Technology as primary financial sponsor.
- Improving's Sandy Hermosilla, David Sandoval, and Mario Mendez sat on the Grand Final judging panel.
- The stated goal was not just one event but a durable digital legacy for the region.
