# Improving Achieves Outstanding Customer Satisfaction with an NPS of 78% — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-achieves-outstanding-customer-satisfaction-with-an-nps-of-78/improving-achieves-outstanding-customer-satisfaction-with-an-nps-of-78.md`

## Summary

Improving announced a Net Promoter Score of 78% and an average market study score of 9.1 out of 10, based on 32 detailed client interviews. The company frames the result as evidence of a loyal client base and high-quality delivery, while acknowledging feedback on pricing, resource management, and strategic partnerships as areas to improve.

## Key Claims

- Improving reported an NPS of 78% and an average market study score of 9.1 on a 10-point scale.
- The figures came from 32 detailed interviews focused on how Improving could grow in line with market trends, core values, and client needs.
- 81% of interviewees scored Improving a 9 or higher, counting as NPS "promoters."
- CEO Curtis Hite attributed the score to a focus on client value through IT consulting, software development, and agile training.
- Clients also surfaced opportunities around pricing, resource management, and strategic partnerships.
- Hite said the feedback would be used to keep improving and to build trust as a strategic partner.
