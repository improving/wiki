# Closing the SAP Data Gap: How CEOs Get Clear Business Insight — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/closing-the-sap-data-gap-how-ceos-get-clear-business-insight/closing-the-sap-data-gap-how-ceos-get-clear-business-insight.md`

## Summary

When a CEO asks a question about costing or margin, the answer usually arrives days later as a deck built by someone merging spreadsheets by hand. The post argues the real problem is access to data: the needed answer is SAP financial data blended with operational data, and those sets are usually siloed. Getting a plain-language answer grounded in real SAP data requires the data to be trusted, accessible, and unified with the right semantics; otherwise AI returns hallucinations. The fix is staging and joining data in SAP Business Data Cloud, with Joule querying a structured dataset, and starting from one use case that matters.

## Key Claims

- The typical pattern: a question routes to a team, an individual merges data in Excel, and three days later the CEO gets a partial report.
- Once data is assembled, building a story about what is driving a change or anomaly is hard, and institutional preconceptions often go unchecked by the data.
- AI needs unified data with the right semantics; without that foundation you get hallucinations or irrelevant information.
- Staging and joining data using Business Data Cloud makes it queryable as a specific, structured dataset rather than wide-open unstructured data.
- Joule, SAP's AI, is trained to point to the logical dataset, enabling natural-language querying against a verifiable source.
- Example: a CEO can ask for margin over the past eight quarters for a business unit and get a visualized chart, then drill deeper before going to the team.
- The right start is small: pick one use case where better data would change a decision, build and structure the dataset, then layer in AI and test whether it adds value.
