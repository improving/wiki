# YoppWorks: Improving From the Start — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/yoppworks-improving-from-the-start/yoppworks-improving-from-the-start.md`

## Summary

Shervin Joseph traces the name of Improving's newest addition, YoppWorks, to Dr. Seuss's Horton Hears a Who! In the story, a quiet Who named JoJo shouts "Yopp!" and saves Whoville by making every voice heard. Joseph reads the name as a statement that every voice matters, and welcomes the firm to the Improving family.

## Key Claims

- "Yopp" comes from Dr. Seuss's Horton Hears a Who!, which the author's family knows well.
- In the story, the residents of Nool cannot hear the Whos and believe Horton is mad, trying to destroy the clover.
- JoJo, a quiet Who, speaks his first word — "Yopp!" — which breaks the sound barrier and lets all the Whos be heard, saving Whoville.
- Joseph reads the name as meaning every voice matters, and notes it echoes Improving's name, which "simply means to make it better."
- He closes with a Seuss quote — "for every voice counts" — and welcomes YoppWorks to Improving.
