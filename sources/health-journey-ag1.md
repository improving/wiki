# Health Journey: Athletic Greens — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-ag1/health-journey-ag1.md`

## Summary

After hearing it recommended on The Doctor's Farmacy podcast, O'Hara tries AG1 (Athletic Greens) despite long-standing skepticism toward greens drinks. He adds it to his existing IDLife regimen and reports a noticeable difference in energy and focus within a couple of days — a difference he noticed mainly by its absence on a three-day trip. He rates the production quality and dissolvability highly but criticizes the travel packets' awkward pouring.

## Key Claims

- Skeptical at first, but AG1 straight into water "is actually pretty good for a greens drink"
- Difference was noticeable within the first couple of days, taken alongside IDLife supplements
- He could tell he had less energy and focus during a three-day trip without it, though he allows for placebo
- Reviewing its ingredients let him adjust his IDLife subscription to target specific gaps
- Travel packets are hard to pour into a narrow bottle; the main pouch must be refrigerated once opened

## Relevance

A data point for [supplement stacking](/concepts/supplement-stacking.md), showing how one product's ingredient list reshaped an existing [AG1](/entities/ag1.md)/[IDLife](/entities/idlife.md) routine.

## Quality

Practitioner blog post by David O'Hara. Self-reported, single-case, and openly acknowledges possible placebo effect; useful as an experience log rather than evidence.
