# Improving Acquires Georgia-Based Innovative Architects — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-innovative-architects/improving-acquires-innovative-architects.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired Innovative Architects, headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia with satellite offices in Washington D.C. and Florida. The transaction was expected to raise Improving's annualized revenue by more than 30% in 2019, bringing collective revenue close to $100 million, and expanded Improving into the Southeastern United States as its seventh enterprise location.

## Key Claims

- Innovative Architects is based in Atlanta, Georgia, with satellite offices in Washington D.C. and Florida.
- The deal was expected to grow annualized revenue by over 30% in 2019, reaching close to $100 million collectively.
- It was Improving's seventh enterprise location and its entry into the Southeastern US.
- The acquisition continued Improving's "Enterprise Strategy," pairing local ownership and incentives with geographically leveraged services.
- Innovative Architects is a Microsoft Gold-Certified Partner focused on cloud computing, enterprise integration, and data-driven IT solutions.
- Its leadership team, including President Scott McMichael, stayed on to keep operating and growing the business.
- Curtis Hite cited the firm's Microsoft and cloud expertise and strong culture as accelerating Improving's regional goals.
