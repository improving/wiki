# So busy! — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/so-busy/so-busy.md`

## Summary

Responding to Omid Safi's post "The Disease of Being Busy," O'Hara confesses to answering "How are you doing?" with "I'm so busy!" and argues that busyness has wrongly become a badge of honor. We need to stop doing so much that we cannot simply be. He cites a slide from Improving's Integral Business Program: "What is something you should do less efficiently so that you can do it more thoroughly?" For him the answer is connecting — asking about the state of people's being rather than their to-do lists. He has started with his family, swapping "How are you?" for "What are you feeling right now?" and challenges readers to ask someone, "How is the state of your heart today?"

## Key Claims

- "I'm so busy" has become a near-universal and wrongly prized answer.
- Busyness has become a badge of honor rather than a genuine accomplishment.
- The IBP slide asks what you should do less efficiently in order to do more thoroughly.
- People readily share activities and distractions but rarely their feelings or fears.
- Asking about the state of someone's heart yields deeper connection than asking about their doing.
