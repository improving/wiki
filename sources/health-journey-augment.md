# Health Journey: Augment — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-augment/health-journey-augment.md`

## Summary

O'Hara abandons plans for a DIY cold plunge when a recovery and wellness studio, Augment, opens within walking distance of his home. Lowering friction lets him and his wife plunge nearly daily. He details his evolving sauna/cold protocol, reports large blood-pressure drops and better energy, and describes how a DEXA scan contradicted his subjective sense of getting leaner — traced to plunging immediately after weight training.

## Key Claims

- Protocol evolved to 3-minute cold plunge, 10-minute sauna, 2-minute cold, 10-minute sauna, 1-minute cold
- Morning blood pressure readings dropped by 15-20 points
- DEXA scan showed body fat percentage had *increased* and muscle mass had *decreased*, contradicting his subjective "leaner" feeling
- Cause: plunging immediately after weight training, since cold can blunt the inflammation needed for muscle recovery and growth
- Shaking for 40-45 minutes afterward is the body doing the beneficial work; he avoids fighting it with towels, clothes, or hot liquids
- Frames the practice as hormesis and a daily exercise in choosing discomfort
