# The Project Manager's Evolution, Stage 2: A Faster Status Report — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/the-project-managers-evolution-stage-2-a-faster-status-report/the-project-managers-evolution-stage-2-a-faster-status-report.md`

## Summary

Opens a series on how the delivery project manager's role changes up an eight-stage AI maturity model shared with the engineering and product-team series. Stage 2 is where most delivery teams sit: the PM uses AI for action items, status reports, schedules, risk registers, and stakeholder emails, but every draft still passes through their judgment. The assistant is confidently wrong often enough to matter — inventing action items, sequencing around unseen dependencies, and reading upbeat wording as on-track. The role's competency floor is knowing whether a plan, status, or risk register reflects reality, and that floor becomes the job rather than fading.

## Key Claims

- The clerical layer of project management compresses, but the role broadens onto a higher floor of competence, not a lower one.
- The assistant will invent an action item nobody committed to, sequence a schedule around a dependency it cannot see, and call a status on track from upbeat wording.
- The PM catches these because they were in the room and understand the work; the assistant drafts, but the PM owns whether the draft is true.
- Every Stage 2 gain is personal and ephemeral — two PMs produce status reports of two qualities and neither standard survives the week.
- The real risk is quiet drift: a well-written status that misstates reality flows to a steering committee with only a busy human watching.
