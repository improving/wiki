# ESG in Mining — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/esg-in-mining/esg-in-mining.md`

## Summary

ESG reporting exists so organizations look beyond financial metrics toward long-term stakeholder value, but the profusion of frameworks (ESG, SASB, GRI, TCFD, CDSB) makes accurate reporting a burden for site managers. The post describes an Improving solution that puts a Power Apps front end over framework-specific metrics and feeds agile dashboards.

## Key Claims

- ESG reporting grew from the need to measure more than financial performance, centered on stakeholder value and KPIs.
- The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI), created in 1997, was the first framework for reporting economic, environmental, and social impacts.
- SASB segments 77 industries and picks the most operationally impactful metrics from each.
- Site managers find understanding and reporting those metrics a burden.
- Improving deploys a Power Apps front end where managers select their framework and enter metrics with comments.
- A mining analytics layer organizes metrics by framework and KPI into dashboards for COO or public reporting.
