# The Intelligence Operating System: Three Layers — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/the-intelligence-operating-system-three-layers/the-intelligence-operating-system-three-layers.md`

## Summary

Analogizes the Intelligence Operating System to a computer OS: it does not do the work of the AI applications but provides the infrastructure that makes them reliable and trustworthy. Most organizations build AI applications without the OS, which works in simple cases and fails in complex ones. Three layers: Hybrid Intelligence (a designed integration of human judgment and AI execution), the Governance Engine (audit trails, boundary enforcement, quality gates, approval points, access control), and the Operating Model (coordination protocols, meta-agent supervision, human-AI communication, performance monitoring). It concludes the IOS is organizational architecture no vendor sells complete.

## Key Claims

- Historical parallel: batch monitors and OS/360 absorbed per-application concerns because shared foundational infrastructure beats heroic per-application effort as systems grow.
- Layer 1 is a design question: which decisions require human judgment, which AI outputs require review, and where each informs the other.
- The Governance Engine is load-bearing at AI trust evolution Stages 7 and 8, where multiple agents run concurrently and some supervise others.
- The Operating Model becomes necessary at ten or more concurrent agents handling distinct workflow components.
- Parts of Layer 2 are purchasable, but Layers 1 and 3 resist purchase — the ERP era showed process redesign is the project, not the software.
