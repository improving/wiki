# What Improvers Are Thankful For This Year — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/thanksgiving-2023/thanksgiving-2023.md`

## Summary

Improving gathered reflections from employees on what they are thankful for in 2023, both professionally and personally. The responses repeatedly credit the company's culture, the PATH coaching program, colleagues and mentors, and personal anchors such as family, community service, and a marathon finish.

## Key Claims

- Multiple Improvers cite Improving's culture — being surrounded by people who want the best for you — as their professional highlight.
- The PATH coaching initiative is named as a source of support, trust, and valued contribution, and as a path to coaching skills and certifications like PSM I/II and PSPO.
- The Come Together initiative and the Houston team feature in one employee's gratitude for community and human-experience work.
- Personal gratitude spans family support networks, running an election site in Houston, volunteering for the Cenotes Urbanos aquifer project, and completing a fourth marathon.
- One responder ties professional growth to leadership opportunities and a supportive manager during market volatility.
- Gratitude extends to colleagues and consultants worked with during the year.
