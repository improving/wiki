# Maximizing ROI in AI: The Case for Leveraging Off-the-Shelf Solutions — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/maximizing-roi-in-ai-the-case-for-leveraging-off-the-shelf-solutions/maximizing-roi-in-ai-the-case-for-leveraging-off-the-shelf-solutions.md`

## Summary

This piece lays out Improving's thesis that most companies maximize AI ROI by using off-the-shelf solutions as part of a solution rather than building custom models. It catalogs the costs of custom development — data acquisition, training, maintenance, plus hidden talent, infrastructure, and time-to-market costs — and argues the key question is not whether you can build custom but whether you truly need to. Rules of thumb follow: don't build outside your core differentiators, treat ROI as the primary measure, and define the problem space first. Off-the-shelf platforms (Vertex AI, AWS Bedrock, Azure Promptflow, Copilot Studio) offer cost efficiency and speed, and Improving recommends a 40/40/20 split of AI investment time.

## Key Claims

- Custom AI development carries visible costs (data, training, maintenance) and hidden ones (talent, infrastructure, time to market).
- The decisive question is whether a custom solution is truly needed, not whether it is possible.
- Don't build custom outside your core business or differentiators; ROI is the primary measure of success.
- Off-the-shelf platforms can be tailored and deliver speed to value across retail, healthcare, and media.
- Improving recommends a 40/40/20 blend: 40% personal productivity, 40% team effectiveness and operations, and the remainder elsewhere.

## Relevance

Informs buy-versus-build AI investment strategy and [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s positioning on pragmatic, ROI-first adoption.
