# Prepare vs Position - what position do you find yourself in — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/prepare-vs-position/prepare-vs-position.md`

## Summary

Borrowing and expanding on Shane Parrish's *Clear Thinking*, O'Hara draws a distinction between preparation and positioning. When you know what's coming — a pitch, a lunch, a bill — you can prepare concretely. When the future is uncertain, you can't prepare, but you can position: spend less than you earn, exercise and eat well, and be the kind of friend others can count on. Being well-positioned lets you come out ahead regardless of what the unknown brings, and that position is built from small, daily, mundane habits rather than big decisions.

## Key Claims

- Known events call for preparation; uncertain futures call for positioning.
- Examples of preparation: rehearse a pitch, allow drive time, save for a known bill.
- Examples of positioning: save more than you spend, exercise and eat well, invest in friendships.
- Being well-positioned enables coming out ahead no matter what obstacles appear.
- Position comes from small daily habits, not big decisions.
