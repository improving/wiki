# 8 Years on Inc. 5000 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/8-years-on-inc-5000/8-years-on-inc-5000.md`

## Summary

Improving was named to the Inc. 5000 list of America's fastest-growing private companies for the eighth consecutive year in 2016, ranking #3,934. It was among only 116 other companies with eight straight years on the list, and a small delegation of executives attended the conference in San Antonio.

## Key Claims

- 2016 marked Improving's eighth year on the Inc. 5000.
- Improving ranked #3,934 for 2016.
- It was among only 116 other companies with eight consecutive years on the list.
- The 2016 conference took place in October in San Antonio, Texas.
- Speakers included Tony Robbins and founders of several large companies.
- The Inc. 500/5000 ranks by percentage growth comparing 2013 to 2015.
- Qualifying companies had to be US-based, privately held, for-profit, and independent.
- Ric DeAnda, SVP of Operations, called the conference and celebration motivating.
