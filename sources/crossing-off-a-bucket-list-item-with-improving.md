# Crossing Off A Bucket List Item with Improving — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/crossing-off-a-bucket-list-item-with-improving/crossing-off-a-bucket-list-item-with-improving.md`

## Summary

When two Houston Improvers happened to be dive masters and instructors, they turned that expertise into an ImprovingU SCUBA class. Seven Improvers and their significant others spent two weekends learning to dive — a pool weekend for skills and a second in open water at Mammoth Lake in Lake Jackson, TX. All came away with SSI Open Water Certifications.

## Key Claims

- The class was run through ImprovingU, Improving's internal training program for personal and professional growth.
- The first weekend covered classroom skills, gear, and physics in a controlled pool environment.
- The second weekend repeated those skills in open water at Mammoth Lake, descending to about 25 feet for buoyancy, equipment-failure response, and rescue drills.
- Mammoth Lake is a purpose-built SCUBA park with sunken boats, sculptures, vehicles, a submarine, and a C130 cargo plane; the group explored for about 45 minutes.
- All participants earned SCUBA Schools International (SSI) Open Water Certifications and immediately planned their next dives.
- The group's next step is deep-water certification.
