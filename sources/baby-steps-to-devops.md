# Baby Steps to DevOps — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/baby-steps-to-devops/baby-steps-to-devops.md`

## Summary

Businesses hesitate to change how work is done the way a toddler hesitates to let go of the couch, and DevOps is no exception. The post gives each of three personas — individual contributor, team lead, and manager — a concrete first step, then warns that the initial push will hit a wall.

## Key Claims

- DevOps merges Development and Operations so they collaborate to increase delivered business value, borrowing principles from physical manufacturing.
- Individual contributors should start by automating a consistent, repeatable, production-like development environment.
- Team leads should map the steps from commit to customer use, how long each takes, and how many people each requires.
- Managers should clarify how a DevOps transformation affects people on a product, which builds psychological safety.
- Initial DevOps pushes often stall on inexperience or political headwinds, so teams must push through the frustration.
- Sustained improvement requires learning the real customer need and having a higher purpose beyond profit.
