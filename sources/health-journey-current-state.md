# Health Journey: Current State — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-current-state/health-journey-current-state.md`

## Summary

O'Hara summarizes his current health and longevity protocols across supplements, peptides, exercise, eating, and sleep. He explicitly frames it as his own experience, not a recommendation, urging readers to find what fits their biology. The post is a structured inventory of a daily stack plus lifestyle rules he has refined over time.

## Key Claims

- Morning supplements: AG1, IDLife Personalized Supplements and Collagen, resveratrol, PQQ, NAD, and nitric oxide support; evening: IDLife and sublingual vitamin D & K
- Peptides: BPC-157 & TB-500 as needed for joints, epithalon every 6 months, DSIP on a 3-month cycle (with diminishing returns)
- Exercise: minimum 3× weekly HIIT at Camp Gladiator, hot yoga (60 min), Peloton in between
- Eating: at least 160g protein daily; no grains, refined sugars, or high-GI fruit; meals eaten protein, veggie, then carb — a key learning from Levels
- Cold shower at least 5 minutes, pivoting to daily cold plunge starting Feb 15
- Sleep average raised from under 5 hours to about 6.5 hours; room at 67-68°F (his side 66 via BedJet)
