# 12 Years on the Inc. 5000 List — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/12-years-on-inc-5000/12-years-on-inc-5000.md`

## Summary

Improving made the Inc. 5000 list of fastest-growing US companies for the twelfth consecutive year in 2021, ranking No. 2,790. The post highlights how rare that streak is and ties it to succeeding through the pandemic's disruption.

## Key Claims

- Improving made the Inc. 5000 for the twelfth year in a row in 2021.
- Only fifteen companies have made the list twelve times, and Improving is one of only two IT companies to do so.
- Improving ranked No. 2,790 this year.
- Inc. 5000 ranks companies by overall revenue growth over a three-year period.
- The ranking came despite the disruption COVID-19 caused to the business world.
