# Health Journey: Healthspan and Rapamycin — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-healthspan/health-journey-healthspan.md`

## Summary

O'Hara walks through why rapamycin has become a longevity contender: it inhibits mTOR, mimics some effects of caloric restriction, and stimulates autophagy. He credits Peter Attia's videos and book *Outlive* for sparking his interest, then describes starting a weekly rapamycin protocol through HealthSpan, a virtual longevity provider, and the temporary side effect he noticed on dose days.

## Key Claims

- Rapamycin targets mTOR, a pathway influencing cellular growth and metabolism
- Inhibiting mTOR mimics caloric restriction and stimulates autophagy, the cellular cleanup process
- Rapamycin is also thought to influence inflammation and stem cell function
- HealthSpan's onboarding: answer health/lifestyle questions, meet a team member and doctor, then begin a personalized protocol
- He doses weekly on Sundays, choosing rest days because mTOR inhibition can interfere with muscle-building
- Initial effect: a temporary "hollowed-out" feeling on dose days, short-lived due to rapamycin's brief half-life
- He notes more research is needed and urges readers to do their own
