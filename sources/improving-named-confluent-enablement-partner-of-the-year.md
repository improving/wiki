# Improving Named Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year for AMER — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-named-confluent-enablement-partner-of-the-year/improving-named-confluent-enablement-partner-of-the-year.md`

## Summary

Improving announced its recognition as the 2025 Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year – AMER, crediting work in event-driven architectures and real-time pipelines built on Confluent Cloud and Flink. The post recaps a year of Data Streaming World Tour events, hands-on Flink and "shift left" workshops, and an AI-powered migration service offering. It frames the award as validation of a deepening partnership rather than a one-off honor.

## Key Claims

- Improving is a Confluent Elite Systems Integrator partner, and its teams reached over 1,500 attendees across the 2025 Data Streaming World Tour from Mexico City to Toronto.
- It developed a migration offering that moves organizations off AWS MSK to Confluent Cloud, using its AI accelerator Code Explorer.
- Code Explorer performs layered analysis of producers and consumers, maps dependencies, generates tailored migration plans, and runs entirely inside the client environment.
- VP of Technology Nick Larson led hands-on Flink and shift-left data integration workshops, with Alex Brekken joining upcoming MSK-to-Confluent Cloud sessions.
- At Current 2025, Devlin Liles and Nick Larson showcased work in AI Alley while Mark Soule presented at the Confluent booth.
- Ehren Seim, Global Alliances Lead, tied the work to healthcare, manufacturing, financial services, and energy clients.
