# Health Journey: HealthSpan - Update — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-healthspan-update/health-journey-healthspan-update.md`

## Summary

A short follow-up to his rapamycin post: O'Hara has stopped taking rapamycin because of side effects, though he still backs HealthSpan for longevity protocols generally. To test whether the symptoms were caused by the protocol, he paused it for a few weeks and restarted at half the dose — the symptoms returned, so he stopped for good. He closes by urging readers to research and listen to their own bodies.

## Key Claims

- He has quit rapamycin due to side effects
- Beyond the "hollowed-out" feeling, he experienced skin issues and a noticeable increase in resting heart rate
- To validate causation he stopped for a few weeks, then reintroduced at a 1/2 dose; the symptoms came back "with a vengeance"
- He still supports HealthSpan for protocols even though he stopped this one
- His takeaway: biologies differ, so do your research and listen to your body

## Relevance
