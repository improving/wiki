# Improving Announces Three New C-Suite Executives — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-announces-three-new-c-suite-positions/improving-announces-three-new-c-suite-positions.md`

## Summary

Improving announced three new C-suite positions plus several other leadership changes as the company grew. Chris Spurgat moved from Minnesota president to Chief Information Officer, Jorge Pliego joined as Chief Financial Officer, and Devlin Liles took on Chief Consulting Officer alongside his Houston president role. Additional moves reshaped integrations, the North America region, sales, recruiting, and two enterprise presidencies.

## Key Claims

- Chris Spurgat, former President of the Minnesota enterprise, became Chief Information Officer, focused on maturing processes and technologies.
- Jorge Pliego was hired as CFO with over 30 years of FMCG experience, responsible for financial strategy and growth initiatives.
- Devlin Liles added the Chief Consulting Officer role, providing consultant growth and career-planning programs plus tools, templates, and processes.
- Mark Kovacevich became EVP of Integrations, helping Chief Acquisition Officer Ric DeAnda integrate acquired companies.
- Scott McMichael took over as Managing Director of the Northern region and Managing Director of North America.
- Matt Russell became SVP of Sales; Clara Sponitz became VP of Centralized Recruiting; Jay Bailey (Atlanta) and Ehren Seim (Minneapolis) became enterprise Presidents.
