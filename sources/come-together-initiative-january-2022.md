# Come Together Initiative: Wellbeing in the Workplace — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/come-together-initiative-january-2022/come-together-initiative-january-2022.md`

## Summary

Shelby Sansone Stephens describes Improving's Come Together Initiative, launched to keep Improvers connected as work shifted to remote and hybrid settings. Each month of 2022 gets a theme and a slate of activities; January's theme was wellbeing, with company-wide virtual talks and a distinct event at each enterprise office.

## Key Claims

- The initiative assigns a theme to each month of 2022 to build bonds and share knowledge across locations.
- January's wellbeing theme included company-wide virtual talks on mental health, finances, and life balance, delivered in English and Spanish.
- Dallas held an in-person/virtual restorative yoga and sound bath; Minnesota ran a customized virtual yoga class.
- Atlanta, Ohio, Guadalajara, and Aguascalientes hosted Smoothie Days focused on nutrition.
- Chicago ran a mindfulness session; Calgary brought in The Chain Reaction for emotional intelligence and leadership through mindfulness.
- Houston trained with its Improving MS 150 cycling team for the year's first ride.
