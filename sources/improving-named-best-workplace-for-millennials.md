# Improving Named Best Workplace for Millennials — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-named-best-workplace-for-millennials/improving-named-best-workplace-for-millennials.md`

## Summary

Improving announced it was named one of the 2024 Best Workplaces for Millennials by Great Place to Work and Fortune, ranking 28th. Employee interviews describe a culture of learning, community, and recognition that the company attributes to its 700 millennial employees' experience.

## Key Claims

- Improving ranked number 28 on the 2024 Best Workplaces for Millennials list from Great Place to Work and Fortune.
- Rankings are based on employee feedback about workplace experience, which the organizations say predicts retention, agility, and business success.
- Improving has 700 millennial employees across its enterprises.
- Employees cite abundant learning opportunities (public speaking, book clubs, meetups, training), the annual Vegas retreat, the monthly "Come Together" initiative, and hybrid in-office days.
- One employee participated in CodeLaunch MN 2024, helping startup StoryForge with UX and architecture, and praised the community give-back.
