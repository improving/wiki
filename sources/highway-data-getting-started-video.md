# Highway.Data Getting Started Video — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/highway-data-getting-started-video/highway-data-getting-started-video.md`

## Summary

A short announcement post for the Highway.Data web tutorial series. Liles links a getting-started video that answers most "new to Highway" questions, provides a timestamped table of contents, and points readers to a supporting C# and LINQ primer by Curtis Schlak.

## Key Claims

- The video covers Patterns (0:00), Getting Started (3:16), Queries (7:01), Scalars (10:21), Commands (14:22), Advanced Queries including SQL-based queries and projections (16:58), Eager Loading (20:56), What is IDomain? (22:22), and Testing with Highway (27:17).
- The series was produced after many requests, with help from Highway Framework contributors.
- Curtis Schlak's "C# lambdas and LINQ Methods" primer is offered as additional material.

## Relevance

Documents the onboarding material for [Highway.Data](/entities/highway-data.md) and its treatment of the [repository pattern](/concepts/repository-pattern.md), [specification pattern](/concepts/specification-pattern.md), and [unit of work](/concepts/unit-of-work.md).
