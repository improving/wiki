# Unlocking the Potential of Power Pages — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/unlocking-the-potential-of-power-pages/unlocking-the-potential-of-power-pages.md`

## Summary

Power Pages, an extension of Power Apps, builds externally visible websites that sit between simple site builders and fully custom-coded solutions. It combines a drag-and-drop designer with direct HTML/CSS/JavaScript editing, integrates with SharePoint, Power BI, and Dynamics 365, supports authentication via Azure AD, Gmail, and Facebook plus custom domains with SSL, and uses CDNs and progressive web apps for performance. Copilot helps design sites and handle routine tasks. Use cases include customer-facing sites, employee portals, community hubs, and public-sector portals.

## Key Claims

- Power Pages extends Power Apps to build internal and external websites with a low barrier to entry.
- It offers a drag-and-drop UI designer plus direct HTML, CSS, and JavaScript customization, with custom domains and SSL.
- It integrates with SharePoint, Power BI, and Dynamics 365, and supports authentication through Azure AD, Gmail, and Facebook.
- CDNs improve performance and progressive web app support enables a seamless mobile experience.
- Copilot provides a starting point and handles straightforward tasks; keyword search and auto summaries are additional AI features.
- Sites can be created blank, from templates, or via Copilot; governance uses web roles and table and page permissions.
- Case studies: Intralux built a technician portal for work orders and inspection reports; Akuma extended customer data to distributors.
