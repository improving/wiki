# Using Low/No-Code AI to Revolutionize Knowledge Management — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/using-low-no-code-ai-to-revolutionize-knowledge-management/using-low-no-code-ai-to-revolutionize-knowledge-management.md`

## Summary

Traditional knowledge management — browsing, navigating, and searching — breaks down as collections grow, so the post argues organizations should let people ask questions in natural language instead. It pitches Microsoft's Copilot Studio as a low/no-code way for power users to build AI "copilots" over corporate knowledge, then deploy them where employees already work, such as Teams and SharePoint.

## Key Claims

- As information volume grows, conventional search becomes slow and inefficient; natural-language Q&A over the collective knowledge base is more intuitive and faster.
- Copilot Studio builds on Power Virtual Agents and lets power users create copilots via templates or from scratch, without deep technical skills.
- A practical application: a copilot over corporate procedures that employees query in Teams and SharePoint, returning answers with links to detail.
- Cited benefits are rapid development, a power-user-friendly interface, integration with existing platforms, and customization to specific workflows.
- Caveats: the platform is still emerging, language and regional support may be limited, and high-volume cost structure is not yet fully clear.
