# Improver Spotlight - Lowell Messner — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improver-spotlight-lowell-messner/improver-spotlight-lowell-messner.md`

## Summary

A spotlight on Lowell Messner, SVP of the Microsoft Services Team, who has been with ProSource since 2006 and continued after Improving acquired it in 2017. With the Cleveland and Columbus locations combined under Josh Harrison, Messner now extends his Microsoft relationships across all Improving enterprises.

## Key Claims

- Messner joined ProSource (pre-Improving) in 2006 and continued as president after Improving acquired ProSource in 2017.
- Earlier in 2022, Cleveland and Columbus combined under Josh Harrison's leadership, freeing Messner to lead the new Microsoft Services Team.
- As a Microsoft Managed Partner, Improving has relationships with Microsoft executives, sellers, and specialists and covers M365, Azure, and D365.
- Messner previously held leadership roles at National City Bank (now PNC), Fabri-Centers of America (now Joann), CCAi, and University Hospitals of Cleveland.
- He cites Improving's US offices plus Canadian and Mexico resources as a competitive advantage few Microsoft partners offer.
