# Simplify Stream Processing With Serverless Apache Flink® From Confluent & Improving — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/simplify-stream-processing-with-serverless-apache-flink/simplify-stream-processing-with-serverless-apache-flink.md`

## Summary

A partnership announcement: Confluent's cloud-native, serverless Apache Flink service reached general availability across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud, running alongside its managed Kafka offering. The post positions Flink as the de facto stream-processing standard and the combined platform as a way to filter, join, and enrich streams without infrastructure management.

## Key Claims

- Confluent announced GA of what it calls the industry's only cloud-native, serverless Apache Flink service.
- The service runs on AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud alongside Confluent's cloud-native Kafka.
- Apache Flink is a unified stream *and* batch processing framework and a top-five Apache project.
- Flink powers stream processing at Uber, Netflix, LinkedIn, ING, Goldman Sachs, and Comcast.
- Combined Kafka + Flink enables filtering, joining, and enriching streams with integrated monitoring, security, and governance.
- Confluent backs the managed service with a 99.99% uptime SLA and multicloud deployment flexibility.
