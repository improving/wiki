# Looking Ahead: 2023 Technology Predictions — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/2023-technology-predictions/2023-technology-predictions.md`

## Summary

A short predictions post in which Devlin Liles names 5G, artificial intelligence, and augmented reality as the technologies he expects to advance in 2023.

## Key Claims

- 5G offers speeds up to 100 times faster than 4G, starting with field data in mining, energy, and weather, then moving to appliances and vehicles.
- 5G could also improve consumer applications like streaming and mobile gaming by using limited cellular data more efficiently.
- AI, aided by advances in machine learning, natural language processing, and deep learning, will become an essential part of daily life.
- AR holds more near-term promise than VR because it needs no bulky hardware and can run on the phones people already own.
- Better motion tracking, GPS, and camera technology will keep improving AR fidelity, with wide and varied business applications.
