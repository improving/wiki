# Transforming Financial Planning: From SAP BPC to SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/transforming-financial-planning-from-sap-bpc-to-sap-analytics-cloud-sac/transforming-financial-planning-from-sap-bpc-to-sap-analytics-cloud-sac.md`

## Summary

The post argues that many companies still lack a dedicated financial planning and budgeting solution — often relying on Excel — and that legacy SAP BPC brings rigid workflows, manual processes, and an end-of-support horizon. Moving to SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC) delivers continuous updates, subscription-based real-time access, and business-user control that reduces reliance on IT. Improving's xi.Planning packages, offered at four tiers, extend SAC for planning across opex, capex, workforce, revenue, and cash flow.

## Key Claims

- BARC research cited: 50% of companies lack a specialized financial planning and budget system, citing cost, complexity, and reliance on Excel.
- SAP BPC pain points: rigid workflows, manual processes, limited reporting, Excel macro and validation errors, and an expiration date for support and updates.
- SAC benefits: innovation, lower time and TCO, better connectivity and user experience, reusable existing developments, and continuous updates.
- xi.Planning packages come in four tiers — Essential, Silver, Gold, and Platinum — covering opex, capex, labor, revenue, production costs, cash flow, financial statements, and custom modules.
- North River Midstream, a leading energy company, moved from BPC to SAC with xi.Planning, improving forecasting, collaboration, and decision-making.
- SAC was chosen for integration with Employee Central and GL-level planning detail, with extensive training to ensure adoption.
