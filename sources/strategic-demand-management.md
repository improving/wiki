# Strategic Demand Management: What's the Rush? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/strategic-demand-management/strategic-demand-management.md`

## Summary

Strategic demand management is about balancing what the market wants against the fixed capacity of the teams delivering it. The post argues that companies stuck operating as they always have take on more risk than they admit, and that systems thinking, early viability testing, and WIP limits keep investment aligned to real value.

## Key Claims

- Strategic planning gives the whole organization a sense of purpose and direction, not just leadership.
- Demand management works two ways at once: aligning products to the market and aligning team capacity to the work.
- Fixed headcount forces "fussy" prioritization and simplifies decisions about what to focus on and when.
- Validate "big bets" with small experiments and MVP-style viability checks before spending heavily.
- Managing WIP improves quality, morale, trust, and forecasting, and prevents burnout.
- Restaurants balance demand to capacity live, putting customers on a waitlist when the dinner rush exceeds servers.
