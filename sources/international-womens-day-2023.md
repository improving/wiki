# International Women's Day 2023 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/international-womens-day-2023/international-womens-day-2023.md`

## Summary

A Q&A collection in which several Improving women answer questions about what International Women's Day means to them, the best part of their work, why inclusion and diversity matter in tech, and what they would tell their former selves.

## Key Claims

- Lisa Lueck argues a diverse workforce increases creativity, broadens solution options, and reduces the bias baked into technology solutions.
- Nicole Hilland stresses that women should lift each other up rather than compete, and that progress toward gender equality is unfinished.
- Africa Soto urges young women not to be intimidated — their ideas matter — and to aim high.
- Clara Sponitz links diversity to accelerated innovation and calls for encouraging girls into STEM programs.
- Diana Stevens advises judging yourself against your own potential rather than others, living with positivity, and starting bucket-list goals young.
- Several respondents credit Improving's leaders and teammates with investing in their growth and creating a supportive culture.
