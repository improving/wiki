# Modern Ways of Working: From Complacency to Innovation — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/modern-ways-of-working/modern-ways-of-working.md`

## Summary

Terms like Agile and Business Agility carry baggage, so the post prefers "modern ways of working" to describe a mindset of continuous learning and improvement. It lays out seven elements — systems thinking, lean thinking, OKRs, lean portfolio management, and PMO transformation among them — for staying adaptable.

## Key Claims

- Agile and Business Agility terminology has been misunderstood and can trigger resistance, so framing matters.
- Systems thinking means understanding the whole concept-to-cash flow and the flow of value from executives to teams.
- Lean thinking removes bottlenecks and waste so limited resources go to what customers actually value.
- OKRs bridge strategy and execution, shifting the organization from outputs to outcomes and customer-centricity.
- Lean portfolio management connects agile delivery to business strategy through a bi-directional feedback loop measuring what customers use and ignore.
- PMOs should drive the mindset by empowering lean and agile practices and customer-centricity.
