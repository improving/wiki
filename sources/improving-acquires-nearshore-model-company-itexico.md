# Improving Acquires Nearshore Model Company iTexico — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-nearshore-model-company-itexico/improving-acquires-nearshore-model-company-itexico.md`

## Summary

Improving merged with iTexico, an Austin-headquartered firm with nearshore offices in Guadalajara and Aguascalientes, Mexico. The merger added more than 300 professionals and combined annualized revenue in excess of $115 million, expanding Improving into the nearshore market and giving it a local presence in Austin.

## Key Claims

- iTexico is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with nearshore offices in Guadalajara and Aguascalientes, Mexico, and sales offices in Dallas, Chicago, and Northern and Southern California.
- The merger added over 300 professionals and combined annualized revenue above $115 million.
- It expands Improving into the nearshore market via Mexico teams and adds a local presence in fast-growing Austin.
- iTexico's offerings span Agile Product Engineering, UI/UX & Design, Mobile, Cloud and AI, delivered nearshore.
- iTexico had appeared on the Inc. 5000 list five times in the prior six years.
- Curtis Hite cited unusually aligned values and iTexico's commitment to an inclusive culture and delivery excellence.
- iTexico leadership, including CEO and co-founder Anurag Kumar, continued operating and growing the business.
