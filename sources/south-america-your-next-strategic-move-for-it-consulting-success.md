# South America: Your Next Strategic Move for IT Consulting Success — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/south-america-your-next-strategic-move-for-it-consulting-success/south-america-your-next-strategic-move-for-it-consulting-success.md`

## Summary

With an uncertain global economy and AI reshaping operations, the post argues South America is a compelling nearshore destination and lists five reasons: proximity and shared time zones with the US and Canada, a deep skilled IT workforce in hubs like Buenos Aires and Santiago, cultural affinity and English proficiency, rapidly growing tech ecosystems, and a strategic location that keeps travel and oversight cheap. Improving expanded its outsourcing footprint in the region through the LenioLabs acquisition, which brought team members in Argentina, Chile, and beyond.

## Key Claims

- Shared time zones with the US and Canada enable real-time collaboration without the delays of Asia and Europe.
- Argentina and Chile have invested heavily in STEM education, producing talent in software development, AI, data analytics, and cybersecurity.
- Buenos Aires is cited as a thriving hub for web development, software engineering, and digital marketing; Santiago for IT consulting and cloud solutions.
- Cultural similarity to North America and Europe plus strong English proficiency reduce collaboration friction.
- Proximity lowers travel cost and time, making it easier for clients to visit teams and collaborate on complex projects.
- Improving expanded its South American presence through the LenioLabs acquisition, with teams in Buenos Aires, Santiago, and other regions.
