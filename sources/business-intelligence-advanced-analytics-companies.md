# Best Business Intelligence and Advanced Analytics Companies — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/business-intelligence-advanced-analytics-companies/business-intelligence-advanced-analytics-companies.md`

## Summary

Business intelligence and advanced analytics turn raw operational data into dashboards, reports, and predictive models, and the wrong partner can stall a reporting modernization for years. The guide profiles ten companies and lays out selection criteria — platform depth, data governance and lineage maturity, industry experience, delivery model, and quantified outcomes — while separating descriptive BI from predictive advanced analytics and custom data science.

## Key Claims

- The global BI market was valued at $34.82B in 2025 and is projected to reach $72.21B by 2034 (Fortune Business Insights).
- Poor data quality costs organizations an average of at least $12.9M per year (Gartner); companies applying analytics broadly are about twice as likely to generate above-average profits (McKinsey); insights-driven organizations are 8.5 times more likely to report 20%+ revenue growth (Forrester).
- Most programs run a governed data layer (Snowflake, Databricks, or Microsoft Fabric) feeding self-service dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, or Looker), with governance deciding whether insights are trusted.
- Maturity sequence: a governed BI foundation first, advanced analytics layered on top, custom data science reserved for problems generic models cannot solve.
- Criteria include governance and lineage maturity, industry-specific experience, delivery-model fit, adoption/change support, and total cost of ownership.
- Improving case: Integra Connect moved from a legacy SQL Server platform to Snowflake with dbt, Azure Data Factory, and Power BI, cutting data processing times from several days to minutes.
- Profiles Deloitte, Improving, PwC, KPMG, Accenture, Kanerika, Oxagile, Ciklum, DataForest, and XenonStack.
