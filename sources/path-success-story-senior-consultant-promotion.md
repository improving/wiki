# PATH Success Story: Senior Consultant Promotion — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/path-success-story-senior-consultant-promotion/path-success-story-senior-consultant-promotion.md`

## Summary

A case study of an Improver who used PATH, Improving's internal coaching program, to plan and reach a promotion to senior consultant. Working with a PATH Coach through SMART goals and 90-day commitments, he built visibility, client presence, and subject-matter expertise.

## Key Claims

- The coach walked him through senior-consultant expectations and they co-authored a development plan.
- To grow visibility, he committed to a lightning talk to the internal app dev community in 90 days, then an hour-long enterprise talk, delivering two in six months.
- To lead an API development team, he worked on client interaction and trust while the coach — also his account lead — helped train and position him for the transition off the outgoing senior developer.
- The client, initially hesitant, became comfortable with him leading the API development team.
- He expanded Azure expertise through the Azure Developer CertSquad, which also advanced Improving's Microsoft partnership goals.
- The program provided a baseline, a customized plan, and ongoing encouragement and accountability.
