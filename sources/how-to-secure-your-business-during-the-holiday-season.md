# How to Secure Your Business During the Holiday Season — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-to-secure-your-business-during-the-holiday-season/how-to-secure-your-business-during-the-holiday-season.md`

## Summary

The holiday season is a prime time for cyber threats, so the post lays out foundational practices to keep a business secure through the festive period and beyond. The advice clusters around hygiene — patching, MFA, secure remote access — plus vigilance against phishing, network monitoring, backups, and a rehearsed incident response plan.

## Key Claims

- Keep all software, including operating systems and security software, updated, since criminals target outdated-system vulnerabilities.
- Implement multi-factor authentication so a password alone is not enough to log in.
- Secure remote work with VPNs and secure channels, protecting sensitive data as remote access grows.
- Remind employees to verify the legitimacy of holiday-themed or urgent emails before clicking links or opening attachments.
- Monitor networks for deviations from normal behavior as a signal of a possible breach, and keep secure, recent backups.
- Review and restrict access permissions so only those who need sensitive information have it.
- Encourage safe online shopping on work devices, avoiding public Wi-Fi for transactions, and develop and communicate a clear incident response plan.
