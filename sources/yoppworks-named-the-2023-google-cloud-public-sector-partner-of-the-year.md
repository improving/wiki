# YoppWorks Named the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/yoppworks-named-the-2023-google-cloud-public-sector-partner-of-the-year/yoppworks-named-the-2023-google-cloud-public-sector-partner-of-the-year.md`

## Summary

A press-release announcement that YoppWorks, an Improving company, received the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year Award for Canada. It quotes a Google Cloud ecosystem executive and YoppWorks's CEO, names the public sector clients served, and cites HPC and AI/ML research access, Document AI document workflows, and cloud landing zones as the recognized work.

## Key Claims

- YoppWorks received the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year Award for Canada, announced from Ottawa on August 29, 2023.
- Kevin Ichhpurani (Corporate VP, Global Ecosystem and Channels, Google Cloud) and Jack Gulas (Founder & CEO, YoppWorks) are quoted.
- Named clients include National Defence Canada, Health Canada, and the governments of Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, and New Brunswick.
- Recognized work: HPC and AI/ML resources for scientific research, Document AI document workflows, and cloud landing zones.
- YoppWorks describes itself as an application and infrastructure modernization leader using Reactive Architecture, Domain-Driven Design, and Agile.
- Improving is described as a global digital services company offering IT consulting, software development, and agile training.
