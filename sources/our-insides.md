# Our Insides — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/our-insides/our-insides.md`

## Summary

A brief reflection post built around two quotes the author encountered in a single week. The first, from *The Boy, the mole, the fox and the Horse*, observes that we can only see our outsides while nearly everything happens on the inside; the second, from Erwin McManus, holds that all limitations in you are created by you. Together they push the author to add an extra reflection timeslot to his existing daily journaling and weekly performance journaling, devoted to one question: what limitations am I currently experiencing?

## Key Claims

- Nearly everything that matters happens on the inside, though we can only see our outsides.
- Limitations are self-created, per the McManus quote.
- The author already journals daily and performance-journals weekly; the quotes prompted an additional, dedicated reflection slot.
- The new practice centers on the question "What are the current limitations that I'm experiencing?"

## Relevance

Touches [self-awareness](/concepts/self-awareness.md) and [Limiting Beliefs](/concepts/limiting-beliefs.md) as things surfaced deliberately through structured reflection.
