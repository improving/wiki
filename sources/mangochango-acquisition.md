# Improving Acquires MangoChango, a Guatemala-Based Software Company — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/mangochango-acquisition/mangochango-acquisition.md`

## Summary

A newsroom press release announcing that Improving acquired MangoChango, a Dallas-based technology consulting and custom software firm whose primary developer workforce is in Guatemala. The deal expands Improving's LATAM presence and is expected to push annualized revenue above $300 million. MangoChango's leadership stays in place and continues to lead the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Key Claims

- Improving acquired MangoChango on September 9, 2025; terms were not disclosed.
- MangoChango is Dallas-based for sales and client operations, with its developer workforce based in Guatemala, founded in 2019.
- The transaction is expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue to exceed $300 million.
- The acquisition expands Improving's LATAM development capabilities and nearshore delivery capacity.
- MangoChango's leadership team stays in place, continuing to lead and grow the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."
- CEO Curtis Hite cited MangoChango's technical expertise and agile delivery model as a fit for global reach; founder Rene Larrave framed the deal as expanding impact.

A case of [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) and [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md), extending Improving's presence in [Guatemala](/entities/guatemala.md) and [Latin America](/entities/latin-america.md).
