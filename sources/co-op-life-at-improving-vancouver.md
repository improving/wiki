# Co-Op Life at Improving Vancouver — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/co-op-life-at-improving-vancouver/co-op-life-at-improving-vancouver.md`

## Summary

Improving Vancouver runs a paid co-op program that gives students real project experience, with some co-ops returning as permanent employees and rising to senior roles.

## Key Claims

- By the end of a term, a co-op can code and test parts of a feature, configure environments and set up test automation and frameworks, and author developer and end-user documentation.
- Co-ops gain domain knowledge in databases and cloud and work on problems for industry-leading and unicorn clients.
- Karen went from co-op to senior developer, crediting the support and encouragement of teammates and supervisors.
- Avneet, also now a senior developer, was scrum master for her team, ran meetings, and led smaller sub-projects, and aspires to gain management experience.
- Co-ops may come back as permanent employees after graduating.
