# Short Term versus Long Term — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/short-term-versus-long-term/short-term-versus-long-term.md`

## Summary

O'Hara contrasts short-term efficiency with long-term optimal, warning that a singular focus on immediate gains neglects durable outcomes. Cutting corners to get somewhere faster can produce missed opportunities and negative consequences, while patience, persistence, and strategic planning enable sustainable growth. He recommends a mindful approach: resist immediate gratification, analyze consequences, run a cost-benefit analysis weighing short-term gains against long-term impact, and seek mentors or peers for perspective, which also buys time to consider ramifications.

## Key Claims

- Short-term efficiency often flies in the face of long-term optimal.
- Sacrificing quality for immediate gains risks missed opportunities and negative consequences.
- We rarely make our best decisions in haste or under a timer.
- A cost-benefit analysis helps weigh short-term gains against long-term impacts.
- Advice from mentors or peers adds perspective and space to think about ramifications.

## Relevance

Speaks to long-term thinking and patience as a decision discipline; a personal-development reflection rather than a technical argument.
