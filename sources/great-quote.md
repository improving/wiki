# Great Quote... — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/great-quote/great-quote.md`

## Summary

A short reflection built around a quoted line: "Maybe rounds down to a 'No' when you're talking about culture." O'Hara argues it is far too easy to make excuses rather than do the hard thing — say no — especially when hiring people or making a change that feels needed. He closes with a challenge to the reader: how are you working to do the right thing, not just the easy thing, today?

## Key Claims

- Excuses "round down to a No" when the subject is culture.
- It is far easier to make excuses than to do the hard thing and say no.
- The temptation is sharpest when hiring people or making a change that feels needed.
- Doing the right thing rather than the easy thing is a daily practice, not a one-time decision.

## Relevance

Informs [company culture](/concepts/company-culture.md) — culture is shaped by the hard refusals leaders make when hiring and changing direction.

Practitioner blog post by David O'Hara. Very short, aphoristic, and essentially a prompt for reflection; the opening quote is unattributed, so treat it as opinion rather than sourced argument.
