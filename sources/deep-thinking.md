# Deep Thinking — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/deep-thinking/deep-thinking.md`

## Summary

O'Hara admits he is a skimmer by default, reading quickly for salient points rather than thinking deeply. He describes trying Obsidian with a reading-notes workflow, which lets him keep highlighting on his Kindle while giving him a vehicle to integrate those notes. The goal is intentional time to think deeply, supported by the materials that make dot-connecting possible.

## Key Claims

- Skimming is fast and can surface useful nuggets, but it does not produce deep understanding.
- Obsidian provides a vehicle to integrate reading notes and thinking notes into one place.
- Intentional time to think is the goal; having the source materials on hand is the enabler.
- Connecting previously unconnected dots is the payoff, even when a connection turns out to be wrong.
- The post frames a committed space and workflow for deep thinking as the reader's own open question.
