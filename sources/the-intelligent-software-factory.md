# The Intelligent Software Factory — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/the-intelligent-software-factory/the-intelligent-software-factory.md`

## Summary

Recasts the ALM infinity loop as a factory floor where AI is embedded at every stage, governed by the Intelligence Operating System and the Hybrid Intelligence architecture. Each stage — planning, development, build, test, release, deploy, operate and monitor — is a station that receives input, transforms it, and passes output on, with quality gates between stations. The Governance Engine supplies audit trails, boundary enforcement, and human approval points; at Stage 7-8 the factory is monitored as a system, not station by station. The human role is elevation, not absence: the orchestrator produces a better factory.

## Key Claims

- Planning station: AI flags requirement ambiguity as a flag, not a decision — the product owner and developer decide.
- Deploy station: progressive deployment with AI-monitored rollout; anomaly detection triggers rollback faster than human monitoring, applied to the canary first.
- Audit trails document what AI contributed at each station so a production defect can be traced to where it was introduced and which gate failed.
- Boundary enforcement keeps station agents within their required data and systems; development agents cannot modify production infrastructure.
- Human approval points sit where output meets irreversible consequences: release decisions, production deployments, and incident remediations on live data.
- Factory bottlenecks are found by monitoring which station's output produces more downstream failures than expected.
