## Summary

A brief, enthusiastic post about living in Frisco, Texas, which serves as a test bed for Wing's drone delivery service. O'Hara has the service drop a small test package and shares video captured from his balcony. He notes the delivery took a little longer than the initial estimate, but treats the autonomous delivery as a fun demonstration that "technology is awesome."

## Key Claims

- Frisco has been a good place to live: strong schools, plenty to do, and notable growth over the 13 years the author has lived there.
- Frisco is a test bed for Wing Delivery (DFW).
- The drone delivery took longer than initially estimated.
- The author tracked the drone's approach and captured video of the autonomous delivery from his balcony.
