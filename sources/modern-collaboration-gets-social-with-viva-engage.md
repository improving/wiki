# Modern Collaboration Gets Social with Viva Engage — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/modern-collaboration-gets-social-with-viva-engage/modern-collaboration-gets-social-with-viva-engage.md`

## Summary

Email is no longer enough to maintain workplace relationships, especially with remote work. Viva Engage — the evolution of Yammer, acquired by Microsoft in 2012 and refined within the Viva suite after the pandemic — builds online communities that connect people across departments and companies, foster shared-interest groups, amplify company culture, and ease knowledge exchange. It integrates with Microsoft Teams, adds social features like trending topics and praise, and needs a clear purpose and community managers to avoid abandonment. Copilot supports content creation.

## Key Claims

- Traditional tools like email cannot maintain relationships, particularly in a remote-work environment.
- Viva Engage evolved from Yammer (acquired by Microsoft in 2012) and was refined as part of the Viva suite after the pandemic exposed the need for robust digital platforms.
- It builds social capital by connecting people across departments and organizations.
- It supports interest-based communities, leadership engagement, culture amplification, and knowledge sharing.
- It integrates with Microsoft Teams: Teams handles work, documents, and project groups while Viva Engage drives broader open interaction.
- Community success requires a clear purpose, adoption goals, engagement strategy, content planning, and active community managers to prevent abandonment.
- Copilot provides AI support for content creation.
