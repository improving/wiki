# The Pros and Cons of SharePoint Lookbook — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/pros-and-cons-of-sharepoint-lookbook/pros-and-cons-of-sharepoint-lookbook.md`

## Summary

Microsoft's SharePoint Lookbook offers ready-made intranet templates that show "the art of the possible" and can be applied directly to a tenant, making it a useful entry point for teams new to SharePoint. The post argues the templates are best used as design inspiration during UI/UX discussions rather than deployed wholesale, because they create new site collections and leave the hard information-architecture work untouched.

## Key Claims

- Lookbook provides detailed web parts and configurations that can be applied to a tenant without custom code.
- Biggest advantage: templates serve as a starting point for UI/UX design discussions and let newcomers see SharePoint's capabilities without deployment.
- Deploying a template requires Global Administrator access.
- Templates cannot be applied to existing site collections — provisioning creates a new one.
- Even after provisioning, teams must still invest in information architecture, permissions, navigation, and site structure.
- The fastest route is often to take inspiration from one or more templates and design a bespoke site rather than deploy and redesign.
- Improving cites hundreds of SharePoint intranet projects over 10+ years as its credential.
