# Improving CEO is Honored as Finalist for Entrepreneur of the Year — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-ceo-honored-as-finalist-for-entrepreneur-of-the-year/improving-ceo-honored-as-finalist-for-entrepreneur-of-the-year.md`

## Summary

Improving CEO Curtis Hite was honored as a finalist for the Entrepreneur Of The Year 2018 Award in the Southwest region, selected from 900 nominees for North Texas, Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Kansas-Wichita. The post frames the recognition around Hite's commitment to culture and his purpose-driven, beyond-profit approach to running the company.

## Key Claims

- Hite was named a finalist for the Entrepreneur Of The Year 2018 Award in the Southwest region, in the award's 32nd year across more than 60 countries.
- He was selected out of 900 nominees for the North Texas, Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Kansas-Wichita area.
- Finalists are chosen on innovation, financial performance, and personal commitment to businesses and communities.
- Ric DeAnda, VP of Operations, said Improving attracts talent because it is a purpose-driven company that thinks beyond profit.
- In his own words, Hite called the company's culture and being a great place to work his proudest achievement.
- Hite's next aim: take the company's promise of trust with more impact to customers, suppliers, and all stakeholders.
- Finalists celebrated at the Hilton Anatole in Dallas on June 23, 2018.
