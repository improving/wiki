# Resilience as a leadership skill — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/resiliency-as-a-leader/resiliency-as-a-leader.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that resilience is central to leadership and is not merely bouncing back from setbacks but embracing adversity as an opportunity for growth. Resilient leaders adapt quickly, recover, and draw lessons from trials, which in turn makes their teams more cohesive and better at problem-solving. Crucially, resilience is a skill that can be deliberately built, not a fixed trait. He outlines three levers: fostering a growth mindset, practicing self-care, and creating a supportive culture that treats mistakes as learning.

## Key Claims

- Resilience is "not merely about bouncing back from setbacks but embracing adversities as opportunities for growth and learning."
- Teams led by resilient leaders show greater cohesion, enhanced problem-solving, and an ability to weather storms, supporting organizational stability in turbulent times.
- A growth mindset — viewing challenges as opportunities — is a primary way to bolster resilience.
- Self-care practices such as reflection, regular exercise, and mindfulness strengthen resilience.
- A culture that encourages innovation and experimentation, with a safety net for calculated risks, helps teams see mistakes as learning rather than failure.
