# 2025 Come Together: A Year of Connection and Collective Impact — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/2025-come-together-a-year-of-connection-and-collective-impact/2025-come-together-a-year-of-connection-and-collective-impact.md`

## Summary

Improving's year-in-review of its Come Together initiative describes a 2025 defined by connection and community across every enterprise. The program ran monthly themes — starting with January's Health & Fitness Month and the Power Pair Challenge — alongside virtual talks that drew hundreds of attendees and local in-person gatherings. The post highlights cross-cultural themes like Argentina and Chile, and the Generosity Advocate Pitch Competition that directed donations to charitable organizations worldwide.

## Key Claims

- Come Together unites teams across offices, time zones, and continents through shared monthly themes and experiences.
- 2025 opened with January's Health & Fitness Month, including the Power Pair Challenge and a lineup of virtual talks.
- Virtual talks remained a cornerstone, drawing hundreds of attendees as global touchpoints for learning and inspiration.
- In-person events, from themed gatherings to cultural celebrations, brought Improvers together locally.
- Cross-cultural monthly themes such as Argentina and Chile reflected Improving's expanding global family.
- The Generosity Advocate Pitch Competition had representatives nominate charitable organizations, with Improvers voting to direct donations.
- The competition produced a tie between Special Spaces and Mesón AC, Fundación de la Hemofilia, with Abuelitos de Laderos third; every nominated organization received donations.
- The post frames Come Together as a commitment to belonging, learning, and a workplace where everyone feels included.
