# Speaking in Romania — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/speaking-in-romania/speaking-in-romania.md`

## Summary

O'Hara recounts speaking at the *Leaders of Tomorrow* conference in Bucharest, an invitation from a friend he had deferred for years. He describes Romania's post-Communist context — rule until 1989, a transition toward democracy, election protests, and visible legacies such as Soviet-style housing and the People's House — and reflects on how easily Americans take their birth circumstances for granted. The talk itself covered conscious capitalism, building a strong company culture, and using the balanced scorecard, aimed at business and entrepreneurship students from the Bucharest University of Economic Studies.

## Key Claims

- Romania was under Communist rule until 1989 and is still transitioning toward democracy and capitalism.
- The conference targeted business/entrepreneurship students at the Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE).
- O'Hara spoke on conscious capitalism, company culture, and the balanced scorecard.
- Engagement varied: first-years were "green," while third-years were highly inquisitive.
- Residual communal mindset, high taxes, and resistance to innovative thinking persist from the Communist era.
