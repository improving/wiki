# Navigating Strategic Risk-Taking - balancing ambition and caution — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/navigating-strategic-risk-taking/navigating-strategic-risk-taking.md`

## Summary

The post argues that strategic decision-making requires balancing ambition with caution, knowing when to push boundaries and when to be prudent. Blind ambition invites disaster, while complacency is equally fatal. The author uses well-known corporate cases to illustrate both, and recommends risk assessment, data, and a list of assumptions and unknowns—rather than a pros-and-cons list—to reveal where risk is hiding without freezing decision-making.

## Key Claims

- Ambition without risk assessment can lead to disastrous outcomes.
- Blockbuster's aggressive expansion overlooked the rise of streaming (dismissing Netflix as a minority player) and ended in bankruptcy.
- Bold moves backed by research can succeed, as with Apple's iPhone launch.
- Complacency and lack of foresight are equally costly, as Nokia's failure to adapt to smartphones shows.
- Data supports decisions but must not cause paralysis; listing assumptions and unknowns, rather than pros and cons, exposes hidden risk.

## Relevance

Informs [risk tolerance](/concepts/risk-tolerance.md) and the discipline of strategic decision-making under uncertainty.

## Quality

Practitioner blog post by David O'Hara. Clear structure and familiar cases, but the examples are illustrative rather than evidenced, and the argument is opinion.
