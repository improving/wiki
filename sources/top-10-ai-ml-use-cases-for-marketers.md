# Top 10 AI & ML Uses Cases for Marketers — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/top-10-ai-ml-use-cases-for-marketers/top-10-ai-ml-use-cases-for-marketers.md`

## Summary

AI marketing uses data collection and analysis to optimize automated decision-making across the customer journey. The post walks through ten use cases, from real-time personalized recommendations and lookalike audience targeting to ROI measurement and SEO content optimization, with named brand examples.

## Key Claims

- AI tools learn from customer profiles to serve personalized messages at the right time without marketer intervention.
- Google uses search and post-search behavior to build lookalike audience clusters for targeted advertising.
- Measuring ROI by channel, campaign, and overall is critical to securing resources for data science and ML projects.
- Netflix classifies content by genre, mood, and popularity, applying AI with a product-first, business-needs approach.
- Starbucks uses its Rewards Program and mobile app data to time and target recommendations.
- Predictive analytics (Power BI), personalization via Salesforce Einstein, and SEO keyword and topic clusters round out the list.
