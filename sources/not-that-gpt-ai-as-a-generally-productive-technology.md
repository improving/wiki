# Not ChatGPT — AI as a General Purpose Technology, Like Electricity or the Internet — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/not-that-gpt-ai-as-a-generally-productive-technology/not-that-gpt-ai-as-a-generally-productive-technology.md`

## Summary

The post reclaims "GPT" from the chatbot product and argues AI is a general purpose technology — pervasive, continuously improving, and spawning complementary innovation — in the lineage of electricity and the internet. Using [Paul David](/entities/paul-david.md)'s electrification history, it argues the value comes from redesigning work around the technology rather than bolting it onto existing processes. Treating AI as a product yields a tool list; treating it as a GPT yields a strategy.

## Key Claims

- A GPT is pervasive, improves continuously, and spawns complementary innovation; AI meets all three tests.
- Edison's Pearl Street station began central power in 1882, but measurable manufacturing productivity took roughly forty years, because early adopters bolted motors onto existing shaft-and-pulley layouts instead of redesigning around the unit drive.
- AI dissolves the constraint that organized knowledge work — human execution capacity — and firms that only run existing processes faster are wiring motors to the line shaft.
- Counterarguments: GPT diffusion has historically been slow; the "normal technology" view holds deployment is gated by organizations and trust; Robert Gordon doubts modern technology rivals 1870–1970 inventions.
- The author counters that AI's distribution layer already exists, the retooling is organizational, and the Early Adopter advantage window is months to low single-digit years.

## Relevance

Frames AI adoption through [productivity paradox](/concepts/productivity-paradox.md) and [Woodshop-to-Factory Transition](/concepts/woodshop-to-factory-transition.md) lenses, with [Diffusion of Innovations](/concepts/diffusion-of-innovations.md) governing who captures the gains.
