# Focus to Rise — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/focus-to-rise/focus-to-rise.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that leaders distinguish themselves by rising above their context rather than being absorbed by it. They refuse to add to the chaos, and instead guide themselves and others through emotion toward a higher level. Focusing on outcomes, he says, is a practical way to make that shift.

## Key Claims

- Leaders rise above context rather than merely living in it.
- Good leaders don't contribute to the chaos around them.
- It's the leader's job to navigate themselves and others while in the emotion.
- Leaders must show a path through the emotion to a higher level.
- Focusing on outcomes is an effective way to do that.

## Relevance

Informs [emotional regulation](/concepts/emotional-regulation.md) and [Intentional Focus](/concepts/intentional-focus.md) — staying oriented to outcomes lets leaders lead through, not into, emotional turbulence.
