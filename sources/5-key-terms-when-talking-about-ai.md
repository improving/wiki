# 5 Key Terms When Talking About AI — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/5-key-terms-when-talking-about-ai/5-key-terms-when-talking-about-ai.md`

## Summary

Olivier Aubin untangles five frequently conflated AI terms — artificial intelligence, data science, big data, advanced analytics, and machine learning — and adds neural nets and deep learning as bonuses. The goal is to give business readers enough vocabulary to engage their technical teams directly.

## Key Claims

- Artificial Intelligence is an umbrella term for any system that gives the appearance of intelligence, not a specific implementation or technique.
- Data Science is an empirical, hypothesis-driven discipline that overlaps with AI but often produces interpretations that are not AI.
- Big Data is about volume plus disparate sources, varied formats, and minimally interpreted data that resists relational storage — not just row counts.
- Advanced Analytics goes beyond traditional business intelligence to predictive methods; Data Analytics is the narrower practice of extracting meaning from data.
- Machine Learning is an approach where the developer chooses an algorithm, sets hyperparameters, defines an accuracy metric, and trains on many examples to produce a model.
- Neural Nets are brain-inspired layered algorithms; Deep Learning is any neural net with more than three layers (at least two hidden layers).
