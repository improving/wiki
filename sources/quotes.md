# Quotes — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/quotes/quotes.md`

## Summary

O'Hara gathers several quotations on trust and reflects on what they imply for leaders. The central idea is that trust is generative: like muscle, it is built rather than depleted by use, and it grows when it is deliberately exercised. He pairs this with two quotes from *Ragamuffin Gospel* defining trust as what remains when the craving for reassurance is stifled, and warning that trust held hostage to the response it receives is counterfeit.

## Key Claims

- Trust is "a peculiar resource; it is built rather than depleted by use" (quoted, attributed to Unknown).
- Building trust resembles working out: it requires intentional repetition, yet we often skip it out of laziness or mood.
- "When the craving for reassurances is stifled, trust happens" — needing confirmation or following up is a litmus test that trust is absent.
- "Trust at the mercy of the response it receives is a bogus trust"; asking someone to confirm what you said you trusted can negate the deposit.
- Trust is a critical component of effective leadership, to be wielded and built in oneself and others.
