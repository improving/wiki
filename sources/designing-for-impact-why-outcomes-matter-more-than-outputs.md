# Designing for Impact: Why Outcomes Matter More Than Outputs — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/designing-for-impact-why-outcomes-matter-more-than-outputs/designing-for-impact-why-outcomes-matter-more-than-outputs.md`

## Summary

David Belcher argues that design teams are too often celebrated for the artifacts they produce — wireframes, prototypes, journey maps, design systems — which show what was made, not what changed. Outputs are a means to an end; the real measure is the outcomes they enable, such as higher conversion, reduced support volume, or better retention. Focusing on outcomes aligns teams, reduces risk, builds executive trust, and elevates design from production service to strategic partner.

## Key Claims

- Outputs are tangible deliverables (journey maps, interaction models, prototypes, usability findings, design system components) that represent progress but not success.
- Outcomes reflect real-world change: higher conversion, reduced support volume, increased task completion, faster onboarding, better retention.
- "Outputs say, 'Here's what we made.' Outcomes say, 'Here's why it mattered.'"
- A cart-abandonment example: an output-focused team redesigns checkout; an outcome-driven team targets a 20% reduction and guides decisions with research, experimentation, and A/B tests.
- Outcome focus creates clarity and alignment, reduces risk by testing assumptions early, builds executive trust, and elevates design's role.
- Outputs still matter — discovery, ideation, prototyping, and validation remain essential.
- Becoming outcome-driven means defining success via user and business metrics, linking every artifact to an intended outcome, instrumenting experiences, and shifting governance from feature roadmaps to outcome roadmaps.

## Relevance

A direct case for [outcomes over outputs](/concepts/outcomes-over-outputs.md) in [user experience](/concepts/user-experience.md), using [design thinking](/concepts/design-thinking.md) practices like design sprints to keep user and business results central.
