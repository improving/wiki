# 7 Ways to Safeguard Remote Working Teams with Cybersecurity — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/7-ways-to-safeguard-remote-working-teams-with-cybersecurity/7-ways-to-safeguard-remote-working-teams-with-cybersecurity.md`

## Summary

Shelby Sansone Stephens argues remote work expands the attack surface and walks through seven safeguards: recognizing heightened vulnerability, protecting sensitive data with encryption and access controls, endpoint security for personal devices, phishing and social-engineering training, secure remote access via MFA and VPNs, hardened collaboration tools, and an incident response and backup plan. Cybersecurity is framed as pivotal to keeping distributed teams and their data safe.

## Key Claims

- Scattered teams connecting from varied networks raise the risk of data breaches, unauthorized access, and malware infections.
- Sensitive company and client data needs strong encryption, secure file transfer protocols, and access controls to stay confidential and available.
- Personal laptops, phones, and tablets are entry points; firewalls, antivirus, and regular updates are the baseline defense.
- Phishing and social engineering exploit remote-work distractions, so training people to recognize and respond is critical.
- MFA and VPNs secure access to company networks and encrypt data transmissions.
- Even with strong controls breaches happen, so a tested incident response plan plus encrypted backups enables fast recovery and minimized downtime.
