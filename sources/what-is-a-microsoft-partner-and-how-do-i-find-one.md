# What is a Microsoft Partner and How Do I Find One? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/what-is-a-microsoft-partner-and-how-do-i-find-one/what-is-a-microsoft-partner-and-how-do-i-find-one.md`

## Summary

Janine Jeanson defines a Microsoft Partner as an entity officially recognized by Microsoft that offers Microsoft-related products and services such as application development, SharePoint consulting, intranet development, and data analytics. Partners keep pace with a fast-moving platform and help clients adopt efficient processes while meeting compliance needs. The post then walks through Microsoft's partner-finding directory and closes by promoting Imaginet/Improving as a long-standing partner.

## Key Claims

- Microsoft Partners are officially recognized by Microsoft and offer Microsoft products and services.
- Typical offerings include application development, SharePoint consulting, intranet development, and data analytics.
- Becoming a partner is selective, and partners gain access to services available only within the partner network.
- Microsoft's "find a partner" page lists partners and lets you filter by country, products, services, and size, or search a known name directly.
- Partners help organizations adopt better processes and workflows and adhere to regulatory compliance.
- Imaginet has delivered professional services for over 25 years and was one of the earliest Microsoft Partners in Canada; Improving covers all Microsoft Solution Areas.
