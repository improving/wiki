# Sandcastle Days — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/sandcastle-days/sandcastle-days.md`

## Summary

O'Hara shares a phrase he recently encountered, "sandcastle days," and the imagery behind it. If we don't spend time at the end of the day reflecting and generating feedback for ourselves, we have little chance to reinforce what we accomplished and learned that day. Sleep and time are like the tide: they wash away the day's progress as a tide erases a sandcastle. He likes the image and poses a personal question — how do I capture my day's castle before it washes away?

## Key Claims

- End-of-day reflection is what reinforces the day's accomplishments and learning.
- Without that reflection, the day's progress is lost to "the tides of sleep and time."
- The sandcastle metaphor frames daily effort as something valuable but fragile and temporary.
- The open question the author is working on: how to capture the day's castle before it washes away.

## Relevance

A short reflection on personal learning habits; it touches the practice of consolidating experience but names no established concept.
