# The 8-Stage AI Trust Evolution: A Map for the Journey — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/the-8-stage-ai-trust-evolution-a-map-for-the-journey/the-8-stage-ai-trust-evolution-a-map-for-the-journey.md`

## Summary

The post frames AI maturity not as how good your tools are but as how much you can responsibly hand off, mapping eight stages from Zero AI to Orchestrate. It argues the industry is moving from a woodshop model, where a craftsperson watches every cut, to a factory model that depends on quality gates, audit trails, and defined roles — and that most organizations sit at Stage 2 while believing they are at Stage 4.

## Key Claims

- Maturity is measured by responsible delegation and error-catching, not tool sophistication; adoption as a binary state produces bad decisions.
- Stage 3 (Task Agents) is the autonomy inflection point and the hardest transition; it needs agent boundaries, quality gates, and audit trails built first.
- Refinement estimates: ~18 hours for a stable Stage 3 agent, ~172 hours for Stage 4, 1,000–1,200 hours for Stage 5, and 6,000+ hours for Stage 6.
- Stage 4 requires deterministic validation, human punch-out points, adversarial controls, and an audit trail; building it on unreliable Stage 3 agents produces a "slop cannon."
- The diagnostic: with no quality gates and a human still watching continuously, you are at Stage 2 regardless of agent sophistication.
