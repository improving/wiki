# Cultivating Conscious Culture Within Your Personal Life — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/cultivating-conscious-culture-within-your-personal-life/cultivating-conscious-culture-within-your-personal-life.md`

## Summary

Mark Kovacevich continues the series by translating Conscious Culture — a business's values, principles, and practices — into a personal philosophy. He suggests choosing a few of Conscious Capitalism's ten culture qualities (he picks trust, caring, and learning) and aligning them with one's higher purpose, while rejecting the fixed mindset that people cannot change.

## Key Claims

- Conscious Culture is the values, principles, and practices forming a business's underlying social fabric.
- Conscious Capitalism offers ten qualities of a conscious culture; individuals can adopt a focused few.
- The author chose trust, caring, and learning to steer his personal culture.
- People can choose their reactions and outlook; the belief that "you are who you are" is a fixed mindset.
- Three reflection questions: alignment with higher purpose, engagement of stakeholders, and visibility of one's culture to others.
- Conscious culture is intangible but should be visible and felt, requiring awareness, deliberateness, and intentionality.
