# Improving's Acquisition of InfraCloud Boosts Development Capabilities in Asia — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/infracloud-acquisition/infracloud-acquisition.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired InfraCloud, an AI cloud services and application modernization firm with its primary workforce in Pune, India, extending Improving's development services into Asia and lifting annualized revenue beyond $270 million. InfraCloud specializes in cloud-native and open-source technologies and was an early Kubernetes partner. Its leadership stays in place under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Key Claims

- InfraCloud focuses on AI cloud services and application modernization using cloud-native and open-source technologies.
- Its primary workforce is based in Pune, India, giving Improving an offshore team.
- The transaction raises Improving's annualized revenue above $270 million.
- InfraCloud was founded in 2016 and was one of the first Kubernetes partners.
- The InfraCloud leadership team remains in place under Improving's Enterprise Strategy.
- The deal strengthens Improving's expertise in Kubernetes and AI-driven infrastructure.
- Improving operates 19 offices across North and South America and India, partnered with Trinity Hunt Partners.
