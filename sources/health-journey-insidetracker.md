# Health Journey: InsideTracker — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-insidetracker/health-journey-insidetracker.md`

## Summary

O'Hara chooses InsideTracker because he wants more than standard check-ups test for, and because it offers repeated testing and an InnerAge feature estimating biological age. Sign-up and local-lab scheduling were easy, and he could upload prior bloodwork to build a health profile. He found the app's daily achievements and journal less useful and mostly stopped after a week, and expects to switch services after his second round of testing.

## Key Claims

- Key selling points: multiple tests ("one data point does not a trend make") and the InnerAge biological-age estimate
- Sign-up was easy and bloodwork could be scheduled at a local lab
- Uploaded prior bloodwork surfaced trends (cholesterol, vitamin D) even though older panels lacked some markers
- DNA is offered via their test or by uploading another provider's data for a fee — untried by him
- The app generates daily/weekly achievement goals based on biomarkers plus a journal; he mostly quit it after about a week
- Overall a solid offering, but he questions its added value versus Function Health and plans to switch
