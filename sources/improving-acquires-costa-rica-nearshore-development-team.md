# Improving Accelerates LATAM Expansion with Addition of Costa Rica Nearshore Development Team — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-costa-rica-nearshore-development-team/improving-acquires-costa-rica-nearshore-development-team.md`

## Summary

Improving announced it expanded its Latin American footprint into Costa Rica through an M&A transaction with Oceans Code Experts, a nearshore technology services firm it already had a trusted client relationship with. The Costa Rica team becomes a new nearshore hub collaborating with onshore and global teams on modern application development and digital transformation. The post frames the move as continued investment in LATAM as a key growth region, citing Costa Rica's STEM education, high English proficiency, and alignment with U.S. business practices.

## Key Claims

- The transaction follows a long-standing client relationship with Oceans Code Experts.
- The acquired team delivers application, data, and AI-driven solutions and becomes a nearshore hub in Improving's global delivery model.
- Costa Rica is described as one of the premier technology hubs in the Americas, with strong STEM education and high English proficiency.
- CEO Curtis Hite says the team's expertise complements Improving's focus on practical solutions and creative execution.
- Keylor Arroyo Villegas, president of the new Costa Rican organization, says joining Improving extends the team's impact beyond what it could do alone.
- The team will support enterprise clients alongside onshore and global teams.
