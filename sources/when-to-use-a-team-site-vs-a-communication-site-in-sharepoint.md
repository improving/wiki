# When to use a Team Site vs. a Communication Site in SharePoint — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/when-to-use-a-team-site-vs-a-communication-site-in-sharepoint/when-to-use-a-team-site-vs-a-communication-site-in-sharepoint.md`

## Summary

Janine Jeanson explains that the first decision when creating a SharePoint site is the site type, and that the answer follows from intended use. Team sites suit groups that collaborate and co-author on projects; communication sites suit broadcasting news, policies, and services to many read-only viewers. She recommends a site collection per team because sharing and permission settings are scoped at that level.

## Key Claims

- A team site is for collaboration: all members are content authors who create and edit content together.
- A communication site is for "show and tell": content is view-only for most, with editing limited to a small number of authors.
- Team site examples include project teams, departments, event committees, and partner extranets; communication site examples include training, policies, governance, and announcements.
- Governance decisions such as sharing and permissions are scoped to the site collection, so each team should have its own for maximum flexibility.
- A team site created in Office 365 is automatically connected to an Office 365 Group and created as a new site collection.
- The SharePoint home page plus Microsoft Graph surface followed, frequent, featured, and suggested sites so users can track many sites.
