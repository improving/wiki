# IT Outsourcing Trends to Watch Closely in 2023 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/it-outsourcing-trends-to-watch-closely-in-2023/it-outsourcing-trends-to-watch-closely-in-2023.md`

## Summary

Preeti Gupta surveys five IT outsourcing trends for 2023: AI/ML and automation, blockchain, IoT, hybrid work, and nearshoring. She cites market figures for each and argues the pandemic exposed the traditional outsourcing model's weaknesses, accelerating a shift toward nearshoring in Latin America and the Caribbean.

## Key Claims

- IT outsourcing spending was predicted at $519 billion in 2023, a 22% increase over 2019.
- The global AI and ML outsourcing market was $93.5 billion in 2021, forecast to grow at a 38.1% CAGR from 2022 to 2030.
- Blockchain's market was $5.92 billion in 2021, expected to grow at an 85.9% CAGR, driven by immutability, security, transparency, and decentralization.
- The global IoT market is expected to grow from $243 billion to $575 billion in 2027.
- Microsoft surveys: 70% of employees want hybrid work, 46% are considering switching jobs over remote options, and 66% of businesses plan to redesign office space.
- The IDB expects nearshoring to add $78 billion to Latin American and Caribbean exports after 2023; Bloomberg reported 80% of US companies were actively considering nearshoring.
