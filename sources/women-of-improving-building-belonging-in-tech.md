# Women of Improving: Building Belonging in Tech — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/women-of-improving-building-belonging-in-tech/women-of-improving-building-belonging-in-tech.md`

## Summary

Women of Improving is an employee-led initiative, envisioned by Chief of Staff Susan Fojtasek, to support women building long careers in IT. It runs global virtual and hybrid events plus localized office gatherings, focused on universal challenges like confidence, self-advocacy, and imposter syndrome. The post argues that investing in women — including as visible role models beyond the company — strengthens both the people and the work.

## Key Claims

- The initiative was envisioned by Susan Fojtasek, Improving's Chief of Staff, to help women build long, fulfilling IT careers.
- It hosts four to five global virtual or hybrid events a year across regions, time zones, and roles.
- Event topics center on career confidence, self-advocacy, imposter syndrome, work-life balance, and well-being, in emotionally safe spaces.
- Each office has a leader who localizes events and hosts in-person gatherings to build deeper local relationships.
- It encourages women to serve as visible role models beyond Improving through student talks and community volunteering.
- The post argues flexible, remote structures make IT a sustainable long-term career for women across life stages.
- Bethany Hilton, Director of Corporate Affairs, is quoted on the universal nature of these challenges.
