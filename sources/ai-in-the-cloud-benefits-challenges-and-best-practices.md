# AI in the Cloud: Benefits, Challenges, and Best Practices — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/ai-in-the-cloud-benefits-challenges-and-best-practices/ai-in-the-cloud-benefits-challenges-and-best-practices.md`

## Summary

AI and cloud computing converge: the cloud supplies on-demand compute and data at scale, while AI turns stored data into predictive insight. The post catalogs common applications, weighs benefits like pay-per-use cost savings and scalability against challenges like vendor lock-in, limited infrastructure control, and latency, and closes with ten best practices.

## Key Claims

- Five common cloud AI applications: IoT analytics, cognitive cloud computing, business intelligence, AI-as-a-Service, and chatbots.
- Benefits include pay-per-use cost savings, scalability, flexibility and remote access, improved performance, data availability, collaboration, automation, and integration with IoT and big-data tools.
- Challenges include data privacy and security, vendor lock-in, limited control over third-party infrastructure, and data-transfer latency.
- AI-as-a-Service lets companies experiment with ready-made models for a fraction of the cost of building in-house, yielding substantial capex savings.
- Best practices span understanding AI's limits, securing data, vetting the provider, training employees on ethics, monitoring performance, planning integration, explainability, evaluating ROI, tracking regulation, and planning for future advances.
