# The Forward Deployed Model Changes Everything — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-forward-deployed-model-changes-everything/the-forward-deployed-model-changes-everything.md`

## Summary

O'Hara reads the Accenture–ServiceNow partnership as giving a name to something that already works: forward deployed engineering, in which a team embeds inside the client's environment before the enterprise rollout is even approved, builds and proves the thing there, and only then talks about scale. He notes the announcement lacked the usual big-firm slides and steering committees, and that what was described resembles how the best work at Improving actually gets done. The nagging part is that Improving never named, packaged, or priced it as the product itself — it remained a side effect of "how we work."

## Key Claims

- Accenture and ServiceNow named forward deployed engineering as a formal delivery model.
- The pattern is to put the team inside the client environment before rollout approval, build it there, prove it there, then scale.
- The expertise pitch — AI transformation decks, maturity models, frameworks — is losing ground to firms that can have something running in production by next quarter, or the end of the week.
- This is a methodology question, not a staffing question, and most firms still answer it like a staffing question.
- Improving already practiced forward deployment but never claimed or sold it as the thing itself; clients burned by "a beautiful roadmap, a year of nothing shipped" are the natural audience.
