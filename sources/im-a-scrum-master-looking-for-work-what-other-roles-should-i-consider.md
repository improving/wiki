# I'm a Scrum Master Looking for Work, What Other Roles Should I Consider? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/im-a-scrum-master-looking-for-work-what-other-roles-should-i-consider/im-a-scrum-master-looking-for-work-what-other-roles-should-i-consider.md`

## Summary

With Agile roles under pressure from layoffs, the post argues Scrum Masters should reframe their skills for adjacent jobs. It names consulting, Business Relationship Manager, DevOps Engineer, and Organizational Change Manager as fits, and adds that Agile empiricism and empathy suit helping organizations adopt AI.

## Key Claims

- The Agile community faces layoffs and reduced opportunities, causing job insecurity and career stagnation.
- Scrum Masters' transferable strengths are communication, organizational change, facilitation, negotiation, leadership, metrics, and process improvement.
- Consulting fits because of process-improvement expertise and the ability to facilitate workshops and lead project teams.
- Business Relationship Managers bridge IT and business, aligning technology solutions to business goals.
- DevOps Engineer suits those with a technical background, implementing CI/CD and automation.
- Organizational Change Managers use Agile skills to manage people and process change as new tools and processes arrive.
- Agile empiricism and empathy make Scrum Masters and coaches well suited to support AI adoption, where much is still unknown.
