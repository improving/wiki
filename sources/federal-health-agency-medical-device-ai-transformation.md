# How AI Helped a Federal Health Agency Transform Medical Device Reporting — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/federal-health-agency-medical-device-ai-transformation/federal-health-agency-medical-device-ai-transformation.md`

## Summary

Sergey Vershinin describes how Improving's Google Practice helped a North American federal health agency automate the intake of medical device adverse event reports. Reports arriving in varied, non-standardized formats were manually triaged and entered, creating backlogs and delaying risk detection. Improving built an Automated Medical Device Reporting Solution on Google Cloud using ML, Document AI, and BigQuery, transforming a weeks-long workflow into hours. The post closes with lessons for public sector AI projects.

## Key Claims

- Manufacturers' adverse event reports arrived in varied formats to a central inbox and were manually reviewed, triaged, and entered, creating backlogs and error risk.
- The solution combined a front-end form app, back-end processing and storage, ML models for data extraction, Document AI for parsing non-standardized forms, BigQuery analytics, and API integration to internal systems.
- Processing time dropped from weeks to hours, clearing backlogs and freeing staff for higher-value tasks.
- Stated benefits included improved accuracy, scalability without added manual labor, cost savings from reduced temporary data entry, regulatory compliance, and structured data for pattern detection.
- Collaboration spanned Google Cloud's public sector engineering and Document AI teams, the agency's corporate IT and security teams, and procurement partners handling security clearances.
- Lessons learned: early access to representative datasets, clear scope management when data formats change, continuous client collaboration, agile iteration, patience with government processes, and non-negotiable compliance rigor.
