# Fort Worth Just Became a Tier-One AI City — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/fort-worth-just-became-a-tier-one-ai-city/fort-worth-just-became-a-tier-one-ai-city.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that Dallas-Fort Worth has crossed from an "emerging" AI market to load-bearing infrastructure for AI. Wistron, an Nvidia supplier, chose Fort Worth's AllianceTexas for its first U.S. AI supercomputing facilities — a $761 million, 1.1-million-square-foot, 800-job project. Combined with 248 planned Texas data centers and AI engineering topping DFW's fastest-growing jobs, he says local leaders can no longer defer AI strategy, because infrastructure, talent, and clients all sit within a 20-mile radius.

## Key Claims

- Wistron selected Fort Worth's AllianceTexas for a $761 million AI supercomputer project: 1.1 million square feet and 800 jobs.
- This is the compute layer, not a corporate relocation story — the machinery enterprise AI runs on.
- 248 data centers are planned across Texas, and AI engineering tops DFW's fastest-growing jobs.
- Client conversations have shifted from "Should we take AI seriously?" to "We're behind; what do we do now?"
- Proximity removes the excuse to defer: "Somebody down the street is already building it."
