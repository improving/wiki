# The Market Just Valued the Agent Security Gap at $1.1 Billion — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-market-just-valued-the-agent-security-gap-at-1-1-billion/the-market-just-valued-the-agent-security-gap-at-1-1-billion.md`

## Summary

Obsidian Security raised $85M at a $1.1 billion valuation to secure AI agents operating inside enterprise SaaS platforms, which O'Hara reads as the market putting a number on the agent security gap. He cites Okta's 2026 enterprise agent report: 88% of organizations confirmed or suspected a security incident involving AI agents this year, and 86% don't enforce access policies for AI identities. The governance conversation, he argues, has shifted from "something might go wrong" to a quantified, already-live problem touching liability, compliance, and the board.

## Key Claims

- Obsidian Security raised $85M at a $1.1B valuation for agent security inside enterprise SaaS.
- Obsidian has 100+ customers spending $100K annually and 14 spending over $1M — category validation.
- Okta's 2026 report: 88% of organizations confirmed or suspected an AI-agent security incident this year.
- 86% of organizations don't enforce access policies for AI identities.
- The right client conversation is no longer "should we think about AI governance" but what the gap means for liability and compliance.
