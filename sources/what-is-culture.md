# What is Culture? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/what-is-culture/what-is-culture.md`

## Summary

O'Hara defines culture as the intersection of people and values such that it produces behaviors — behaviors that can be beneficial or destructive, and that in turn shape the culture in a cyclical feedback loop. Leaders, he says, must ensure the right people and well-defined values so the culture is what they intend to produce. Misalignment — intentionality about people but vague, aspirational, or inaccurate values — is what he believes produces a toxic culture.

## Key Claims

- Culture is the intersection of people and values that produces behaviors.
- Those behaviors feed back and shape the culture cyclically.
- It is leaders' job to get the right people and clearly defined values.
- "Hollow values" — values leadership doesn't live — produce a hollow culture.
- People sense the incongruence between stated and lived values; leaders must embody them and create safety.
