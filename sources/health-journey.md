# Health Journey — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey/health-journey.md`

## Summary

O'Hara opens with the question that started his health journey — "Have you ever heard of functional medicine?" — noting he had long followed Robb Wolf, Dave Asprey, and Mark Sisson, plus epigenetics research (Fraga's paper on methylation, Feinberg's work, relevant to his family history of Alzheimer's), but had not read Dr. Mark Hyman until *Young Forever* and *The Doctor's Farmacy*. Approaching a significant age milestone, he began trying products to make his 60th birthday the midpoint of his life. This post serves as a hub: it lists supplements (IDLife, Athletic Greens AG1, HealthSpan) and services (InsideTracker, Levels, Function Health, Everlywell), plus a WHOOP device that he calls the foundation of his health hacking.

## Key Claims

- Functional medicine, epigenetics, and longevity research reignited his interest in health optimization.
- He is working toward making his 60th birthday the midpoint of his life.
- Supplements tried or researched: IDLife, Athletic Greens AG1, and HealthSpan (not yet used).
- Services used: InsideTracker, Levels, Function Health, and Everlywell.
- WHOOP is the foundation of his health hacking and warranted its own post.
- He intends to keep updating the post as he learns and tries new things.
