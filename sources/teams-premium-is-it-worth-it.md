# Teams Premium: Is It Worth It? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/teams-premium-is-it-worth-it/teams-premium-is-it-worth-it.md`

## Summary

A guide to whether Microsoft Teams Premium is worth adopting, aimed at power users and enterprises with heightened meeting-security needs. It catalogues the security, meeting, and webinar features Premium adds over standard Teams and notes the price.

## Key Claims

- Security features include watermarking, end-to-end encryption for meetings and calls, attendee recording/transcription controls, chat copy-paste protection, meeting sensitivity labels, and custom user policy packages.
- Meeting features include reusable meeting templates that enforce compliance, custom branding and lobbies, attendee view controls, and live translated captions.
- Webinar/Town Hall features include custom reminder emails, waitlists and registration approvals, double Town Hall capacity, more simultaneous Town Halls, 20,000-attendee Q&A, and event insights.
- Teams Premium enables customized Microsoft Mesh virtual-reality spaces.
- Teams Premium costs $10 per person per month.
