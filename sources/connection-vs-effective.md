# Connection vs Effective — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/connection-vs-effective/connection-vs-effective.md`

## Summary

A brief reflection urging readers to be connected rather than merely effective in their interactions. O'Hara argues connection is what bridges the rough spots that inevitably show up. An effectiveness-first focus hits those bumps hard, can jar loose whatever you've built, and may even blow past the impact you had without you noticing it. Recovery is hard unless you have the grace that connection typically provides.

## Key Claims

- Aim to be connected, not just effective, in how you interact with others.
- Connection bridges the rough spots that inevitably arise.
- Effectiveness-first approaches hit those bumps hard and can damage what you've built.
- Without connection's grace, recovering from those moments is difficult.
