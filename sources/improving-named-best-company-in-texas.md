# Improving Named Best Company in Texas — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-named-best-company-in-texas/improving-named-best-company-in-texas.md`

## Summary

Improving was named the #1 Best Company to Work for in Texas by Texas Monthly, the Texas Association of Business, Texas SHRM, and Best Companies Group, covering its Dallas, Houston, and Austin enterprises.

## Key Claims

- This is the first time at the top of the list and the 12th year on it, in the large-company category.
- The award is based on employee surveys, so it reflects every Improver who responded.
- Dallas President David O'Hara says growing the culture to other parts of the state is the real achievement.
- He credits Improving's guiding principles being lived daily rather than stated on a website, giving congruence between words and actions.
- Leadership is intentional about living the culture so it trickles down; consistency matters more than perfection.
- The gala was held in October at the JW Marriott in Austin.
