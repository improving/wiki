# Top 10 Reasons AI Projects Fail - #2: Undefined Success Metrics — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/undefined-success-metrics/undefined-success-metrics.md`

## Summary

Many AI teams launch without deciding what success actually means, so a live prototype is celebrated while no one can answer whether it worked. The post argues AI without measurement is a game without a scoreboard — lots of activity, no way to tell if you are winning — and that KPIs, baselines, and measurement cadence must be built into delivery like code or models.

## Key Claims

- Undefined metrics produce subjective claims like "promising signals" and "strong user interest" with no ROI behind them.
- Four causes: unclear business intent, no shared KPI ownership, manual and inconsistent evaluation, and fear of accountability.
- Lock KPIs upfront: identify the single most important business metric you are paying to move, plus guardrails for quality, risk, and compliance.
- Baseline the current state and set the target before development begins, so every sprint shows movement toward the goal.
- Instrument the workflow with telemetry so ROI is captured automatically instead of reported manually.
- Visualize impact on a simple dashboard showing metrics, owners, and cadence, and review it every sprint — "if we don't measure it, we don't build it."
- Example: a sales email assistant with the KPI of reply-rate uplift, plus secondary metrics for hallucination rate and legal compliance, with telemetry in every message to prove impact and expand confidently.
