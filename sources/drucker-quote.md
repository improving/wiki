# Drucker quote — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/drucker-quote/drucker-quote.md`

## Summary

A minimal post that shares a single line attributed to the management thinker Peter Drucker: "Only three things happen naturally in organizations: friction, confusion, and underperformance. Everything else requires leadership." The post adds no commentary — the quote stands alone as the author's message to readers.

## Key Claims

- Three things happen naturally in organizations: friction, confusion, and underperformance.
- Everything else in an organization requires leadership.
- Leadership is therefore the deliberate intervention that produces any outcome other than drift and dysfunction.
