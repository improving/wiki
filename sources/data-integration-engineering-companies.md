# The Complete Guide to Choosing a Data Integration & Engineering Partner — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/data-integration-engineering-companies/data-integration-engineering-companies.md`

## Summary

Data integration and engineering connects fragmented systems and moves data reliably into a form analytics, applications, and AI can use, replacing manual reconciliation with a trusted foundation. The guide profiles nine companies and lays out how to assess them on platform certifications, real-time and streaming capability, governance discipline, delivery-model fit, and a quantified track record.

## Key Claims

- IT teams spend 39% of their time building custom integrations (Salesforce); organizations using fully managed ELT pipelines are nearly twice as likely to exceed ROI targets on the reclaimed time (Fivetran); 73% of organizations run hybrid cloud (Flexera).
- Common models: batch ETL/ELT, real-time and streaming integration (Kafka, Confluent, Azure Event Hubs), API-led integration (REST, GraphQL, iPaaS), cloud warehouse and lakehouse modernization, and fully managed data engineering.
- Criteria: platform and hyperscaler certifications, production streaming experience (not just batch), named case studies with quantified outcomes, governance built in from day one, vendor transparency, and delivery-model and cultural fit.
- Improving is a Confluent Elite Systems Integrator and Premier Partner, certified across Azure, AWS, GCP, Databricks, and Snowflake, with 21 offices spanning onshore, nearshore, and offshore locations.
- Improving case: Integra Connect's legacy SQL Server warehouse migrated to Snowflake with dbt for transformation, Azure Data Factory for orchestration, and Power BI for reporting, cutting processing times from several days to minutes.
- Programs typically run 12 months or longer; global systems integrators bring bench strength and MDM practices while boutique firms move faster and specialize on Databricks or Snowflake.
- Profiles Capgemini, TCS, Wipro, HCLTech, N-iX, Itransition, STX Next, and InData Labs.
