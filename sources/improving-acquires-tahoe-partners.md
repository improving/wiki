# Improving Acquires Chicago-Based Tahoe Partners — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-tahoe-partners/improving-acquires-tahoe-partners.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired Tahoe Partners, a Chicago professional services firm specializing in cloud and Microsoft digital transformation. The deal lifts Improving's collective annualized revenue past $185 million and expands its Midwest reach, with Tahoe's leadership kept intact.

## Key Claims

- Improving acquired Tahoe Partners, headquartered in Chicago, Illinois.
- The transaction is expected to raise collective annualized revenue above $185 million and expand Improving's Midwest geographic reach.
- Tahoe's leadership team stays intact and continues to operate and grow the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."
- Tahoe has been a Microsoft Gold partner for over 10 years, specializing in digital strategy, experience design, project management, and technology implementation.
- Improving CEO Curtis Hite and Tahoe's Ray Conrad and Ralph Keipert frame the merger as alignment on culture, quality, and Microsoft capability.
