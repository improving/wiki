# A PATH Forward — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/path-program/path-program.md`

## Summary

Shelby Sansone Stephens profiles PATH, an Improving coaching and career-development program conceived by Dallas President David O'Hara. PATH helps employees set and pursue their own goals through a coach and repeating 90-day cycles, on the premise that Improving leaders see careers as a rock wall rather than a corporate ladder. VP of Consulting Kevin Baker, an early participant, describes how it works and why it succeeds.

## Key Claims

- PATH is a framework to help individuals set and achieve goals at the highest level, conceived by Dallas President David O'Hara.
- The first of three meetings covers StrengthsFinder and DISC results, work history, skills, and "dreaming" about long-term goals.
- The second meeting narrows goals, then starts a 90-day journey to meet them, with the coach available throughout.
- After 90 days, coach and participant review progress and can repeat the cycle.
- Kevin Baker's three success factors: writing goals down, owning your own goals, and having an accountability partner during the journey.
- PATH is bigger than mentorship and goal-setting: participants can pursue goals Improving doesn't currently offer, and the program sometimes creates new services or positions.
