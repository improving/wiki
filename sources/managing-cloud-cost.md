# Managing Cloud Cost — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/managing-cloud-cost/managing-cloud-cost.md`

## Summary

Devlin Liles reports that cloud cost has become the top challenge above security, with lift-and-shift migrations producing unexpected bills; Flexera puts overruns around 18% of budget, heading toward 20%. He offers short-term strategies — spending policies with automated monitoring and retirement, multi-cloud, right-sizing, reserved instances and savings plans, eliminating unused resources, and auto-scaling — plus long-term re-architecture to cloud-native microservices, containers, and serverless.

## Key Claims

- Cloud cost is the #1 challenge, above security, and concentrated in the enterprise: over 80% call it a top priority versus only 47% of SMBs.
- Flexera's latest report puts overruns around 18% of budget, expected to grow to 20% within 12 months.
- Based on some estimates that could mean as much as $97B of overspend against a roughly $483B global cloud market in 2022.
- Short-term controls: limit who can authorize cloud spend, automate usage monitoring, and automate resource retirement or de-scaling.
- Optimization: right-sizing instances, using reserved instances and savings plans, eliminating unused resources, and auto-scaling to demand.
- Long-term: re-architect cloud-native with microservices, containers, and serverless to gain scalability and cut cost.
