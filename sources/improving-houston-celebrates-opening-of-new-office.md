# Improving Houston Celebrates Opening of New Office — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-houston-celebrates-opening-of-new-office/improving-houston-celebrates-opening-of-new-office.md`

## Summary

Improving's Houston office moved from its Bellaire location to a newly designed space in the Energy Corridor to accommodate growth, celebrating with a ribbon cutting on June 15. The open-concept office added two classrooms for software and process training plus a project area for onsite development teams, and now hosts community user groups.

## Key Claims

- Houston office relocated from Bellaire to the Energy Corridor.
- Ribbon cutting held June 15 with family, friends, clients, and the Houston West Chamber of Commerce.
- The space includes two classrooms for software and process training.
- It includes a large project area for onsite development teams.
- Improving now hosts several Houston-area community user groups in the classrooms.
- Over 140 guests attended the celebration.
- Devlin Liles was Houston President at the time.
