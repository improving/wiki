# The $200B Gap Is Yours If You Want It — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-200b-gap-is-yours-if-you-want-it/the-200b-gap-is-yours-if-you-want-it.md`

## Summary

David O'Hara reads BCG's projection of $200 billion in net new tech-services demand from agentic AI as an opportunity that the Big 4 consulting firms are deliberately declining. Those firms are moving upmarket — Fortune 500 accounts, hyperscaler partnerships, platform plays — and pricing themselves out of mid-market work by design, since a $200,000 engagement cannot move a firm built to close eight figures. O'Hara argues boutique firms like his can fill that gap, because mid-market buyers care more about proximity, trust, and delivery speed than brand name.

## Key Claims

- BCG estimates $200 billion in net new tech-services demand from agentic AI, and the Big 4 are walking away from most of it on purpose.
- Large firms are not ignoring the mid-market; they are pricing themselves out of reach by design, because small engagements don't move their needle.
- 75% of mid-market enterprises say they want a partner to help them build and implement agentic AI, and most have no one to call.
- At the mid-market tier, proximity, trust, and delivery speed outweigh the name on the slide deck.
- Responsiveness when a bigger firm isn't is itself the differentiator that BCG's figure quantifies.
