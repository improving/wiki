# International Women's Day 2022 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/international-womens-day-2022/international-womens-day-2022.md`

## Summary

For International Women's Day 2022, Improving collected short Q&A responses from several of its women — including Africa Soto Flores, Allana Luterman, Cynthia Turpin, Gabriela Garza-Ramos, Kris Wickett, and Robyn Fuentes. Asked what being a woman means, what assumptions they would change, their greatest strengths, and what advice they would give women entering the workforce, the respondents emphasize tenacity, balance, vulnerability as strength, and believing in your own abilities.

## Key Claims

- Africa Soto Flores: tenacity and commitment matter more than fitting a stereotype; "stop worrying and start doing."
- Allana Luterman: confidence lands the meeting and often the job; experience on paper is irrelevant next to work ethic and thinking differently.
- Cynthia Turpin: vulnerability should be seen as positive, and imposter syndrome is common among women in IT — "you are not hired because you know all the things."
- Gabriela Garza-Ramos: find a mentor and surround yourself with people who respect you.
- Kris Wickett: leadership that seeks win/win solutions is wrongly seen as weakness; the opposite is true.
- Robyn Fuentes: interests and expertise are not tied to gender, and people should be their own biggest cheerleader.
