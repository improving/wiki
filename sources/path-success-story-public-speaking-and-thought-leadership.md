# PATH Success Story: Public Speaking and Thought Leadership — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/path-success-story-public-speaking-and-thought-leadership/path-success-story-public-speaking-and-thought-leadership.md`

## Summary

A PATH case study: an Improver aiming to grow into leadership used PATH coaching to set and refresh speaking goals, turning a COVID-disrupted 2020 into a launch pad for thought leadership.

## Key Claims

- The goal was to deliver one talk per quarter to a user group or conference and to develop three new presentations to improve selection odds.
- He used SMART goals and 90-day commitments to schedule the first user-group talk while applying to conferences.
- When COVID shut down in-person events in March 2020, his PATH Coach pushed him to get creative rather than wait a year.
- He applied to virtual conferences outside his market and landed many speaking engagements.
- He raised his target from one engagement a quarter to one a month and generated two more talks.
- The year of work served as a launch pad into thought leadership and helped him become a high performer in his enterprise.
