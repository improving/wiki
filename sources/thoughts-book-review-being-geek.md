# Book Review: Being Geek — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/thoughts-book-review-being-geek/thoughts-book-review-being-geek.md`

## Summary

Devlin Liles gives a brief, enthusiastic review of *Being Geek*, published by O'Reilly Media, recommending it to "any geek in the business of IT." He highlights the interview personality and offer negotiation sections as especially helpful and praises the book's conversational, easy-to-read style, citing a bridge-game analogy as a moment that made him laugh. He notes it will be a dog-eared reference kept close at hand.

## Key Claims

- The book is easy to understand, accurate, enjoyable, concise, and well-written.
- It suits a wide audience: novice through expert, students through professionals, including designers, developers, educators, makers, and sys admins.
- The interview personality and offer negotiation sections are the most helpful parts.
- The conversational style makes it a very easy, engaging read.
