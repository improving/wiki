# Rewriting a Talk the Day Before: AWESOME — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/rewriting-a-talk-the-day-before-awesome/rewriting-a-talk-the-day-before-awesome.md`

## Summary

A short personal post. Devlin Liles arrives at the office at 6:30, reads Scott Guthrie's blog announcement of Entity Framework Code-First CTP5, and realizes his Thursday talk on POCO separation and Entity Framework can be rebuilt around the newest bits. He frames the surprise as an opportunity and sets out to rewrite the talk in four to five hours.

## Key Claims

- The scheduled talk was on POCO separation and Entity Framework.
- Entity Framework Code-First CTP5 was announced that same morning on Scott Guthrie's blog.
- The author chose to rewrite the talk to use the latest CTP and its new features, estimating four to five hours of work.
- He treats the plan change as a positive: "Gotta love it when the plan gets blown away and replaced by a better plan."
