# How Improving Elevates MSP Solutions for SMBs and Mid-Market Companies — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-improving-elevates-msp-solutions-for-smbs/how-improving-elevates-msp-solutions-for-smbs.md`

## Summary

Small and mid-market businesses get the most from a managed service provider when it operates as a partner that prioritizes insight and oversight rather than a hands-off vendor. The post describes Improving's engagement model — monthly stakeholder meetings, a virtual CIO service, and quarterly architectural advisory calls — and lays out the pricing, SLA, and security considerations any buyer should weigh.

## Key Claims

- Regular cadence builds trust: monthly stakeholder meetings keep leaders informed on IT performance, plans, and risks.
- Premium clients get a virtual CIO (vCIO) for guidance on investments, budgeting, risk, and long-term innovation, plus quarterly hour-long architectural advisory calls.
- Common MSP pricing models are per user, per device, fixed monthly fee, and hourly for out-of-scope work; emergency remediation may cost more per hour than standard support.
- SLAs should be scrutinized for guaranteed response times, uptime, and performance standards.
- Security must be a top priority: real-time monitoring, threat detection, incident response, and regular vulnerability assessments.
- Named challenges of outsourcing include loss of direct control, service suitability, communication, and dependency — mitigated by frequent engagement and transparency.
