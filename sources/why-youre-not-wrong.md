# Why You&#x27;re Not Wrong...Just Different — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/why-youre-not-wrong/why-youre-not-wrong.md`

## Summary

The post opens with a paired inner monologue — "I'm logical and I arrived at my conclusion because it's right" versus "you arrived at a different conclusion; it's because you're wrong" — and challenges it. People start from different places: different contexts, backgrounds, and experiences that form the filters through which they process the world. Rather than reading disagreement as error, the author argues we should work to understand the other person's context and thought process, which both deepens our knowledge of them and strengthens our ability to explain our own position. His practical discipline is to ask more questions before arriving at conclusions.

## Key Claims

- Treating a different conclusion as proof of error is a common but flawed reflex.
- Context, background, and experience create different filters for interpreting the same events.
- Understanding another person's context improves the relationship and sharpens your own articulation.
- Practicing questions before conclusions is the concrete countermeasure.
- Closing question: when did you last explore someone else's understanding before deriving your own?
