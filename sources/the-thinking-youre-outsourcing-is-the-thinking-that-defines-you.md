# The Thinking You're Outsourcing Is the Thinking That Defines You — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/the-thinking-youre-outsourcing-is-the-thinking-that-defines-you/the-thinking-youre-outsourcing-is-the-thinking-that-defines-you.md`

## Summary

Takes up Sam Altman's warning about cognitive atrophy as an overlooked AI risk, agreeing that executives risk becoming editors rather than thinkers. The author blocks calendar time for "thinking work," sometimes using AI to outline what to work through, then closing the chat and reasoning independently. Using AI to decide what to think about is fine; outsourcing the thinking itself erodes capacity.

## Key Claims

- Sam Altman named cognitive atrophy as one of AI's most overlooked risks.
- The temptation is to start deliverables with AI and edit from there, which turns the executive from engine into editor.
- The author blocks protected time specifically for unstructured thinking work.
- AI can scaffold what to think about, but production and thinking must remain the person's own.
- Executives producing more while feeling less sharp are mistaking volume for the problem.
