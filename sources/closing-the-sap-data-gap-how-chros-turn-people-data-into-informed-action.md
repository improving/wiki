# Closing the SAP Data Gap: How CHROs Turn People Data into Informed Action — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/closing-the-sap-data-gap-how-chros-turn-people-data-into-informed-action/closing-the-sap-data-gap-how-chros-turn-people-data-into-informed-action.md`

## Summary

CHROs sit on valuable people data — headcount, performance, compensation, retention — but it rarely connects to financial data, so workforce investment cases reach the boardroom without numbers. In an SAP environment, HR master data lives in SuccessFactors while cost-center and project charging lives in finance, and HR has no visibility into it. The post argues that joining the two through SAP Integration Suite on BTP and Data Products in SAP Business Data Cloud lets HR and finance work from one model, so the CHRO can cost a strategic workforce plan and speak the CFO's language.

## Key Claims

- HR owns the HRIS and master data; finance owns financial information — both reasonable positions that leave neither side with the full picture.
- Without connected data, organizations cannot put a cost on upskilling, skill transfers, or attrition — for example, the true cost of hiring five replacements.
- SAP Integration Suite on BTP replicates employee master data from SuccessFactors into S/4HANA and keeps transactional systems in sync.
- Data Products in SAP Business Data Cloud bring SuccessFactors and S/4HANA Finance datasets together inside SAP Datasphere, with separate security spaces preserving access control.
- A unified model lets you report on skills, supply, demand, and the cost of upskilling or moving people, and standardizes metrics like headcount and FTE across both sides.
- The approach is lighter than expected: analytic use cases and dashboards can stand up in thirty to forty-five days.
