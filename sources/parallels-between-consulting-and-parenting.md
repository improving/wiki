# Parallels Between Consulting and Parenting — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/parallels-between-consulting-and-parenting/parallels-between-consulting-and-parenting.md`

## Summary

Emily Stickle argues that consulting and parenting share the same core practices — providing guidance rather than solving problems, creating a safe space for experimentation, working through problems by discussing consequences, and tolerating the repetition learning requires. She uses kitchen and household anecdotes to show that trust, patience, and productive struggle build capability in teams just as in children. The post reframes treating a client like a child as empowerment rather than condescension.

## Key Claims

- Choose guidance over solving the problem, even though letting people work it out is slower.
- Stepping in early can deny people the chance to try and set them up never to succeed.
- Celebrate lessons from minor failures to build psychological safety for experimentation.
- Discuss consequences and options rather than issuing firm noes.
- Education is not a checklist; repetition and patience are required, as in Marine drills or martial-arts kata.
