# Obsidian Obsessed — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/obsidian-obsessed/obsidian-obsessed.md`

## Summary

A short first-impressions post on adopting Obsidian, prompted by colleagues at Improving who are enthusiasts. The author likes the simplicity of Markdown and linking for capturing and connecting ideas because it matches how he thinks. He is less sold on the to-do functionality, which creates an additional inbox to process and a source of friction, and he still prefers a physical journal for morning quiet time — though he may push himself out of that habit to try digital journaling.

## Key Claims

- Markdown plus linking feels closer to how he naturally thinks about and connects ideas.
- Obsidian's to-do capability adds another "Inbox" to process, creating friction and a feeling of missing things.
- Writing in Obsidian keeps him close to his notes, which has helped him post more consistently.
- There are community workflows and plugins worth researching, plus an ImprovingU class he can draw on.
- He still prefers a physical journal for morning quiet time and may only move to digital journaling to stretch outside his comfort zone.

## Relevance
