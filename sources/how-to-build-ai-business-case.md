# How to Build an AI Business Case That Gets Executive Buy-In — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-to-build-ai-business-case/how-to-build-ai-business-case.md`

## Summary

Devlin Liles argues that AI pitches get rejected for trust reasons far more often than for weak ROI models: organizations run an informal social economy where every ask draws down credibility, and a champion vouching for their own work pays a self-interest tax. The fix is to shift weight from claim to evidence — working prototypes, peer-validated numbers, and named failure modes — and to price the pitch in each executive's own currency rather than addressing one generic audience.

## Key Claims

- Rejection usually means the room didn't trust the presenter, not that the ROI model was too weak; more modeling won't fix a trust gap.
- Evidence beats enthusiasm: a prototype, a peer's unprompted testimonial, or a number produced by someone other than the requester survives the room's discount.
- Each executive weighs different risk: the CFO wants a risk-adjusted range with a downside case, the general counsel wants named exposure, the CTO wants failure modes, and the CEO wants opportunity cost.
- Objections are signals, not obstacles: "we don't have the data" often means the use case is too broad, so narrow the ask and make the missing data a separate budget item.
- Credibility is earned before the pitch — through a small proof of value someone else describes, and a track record of naming risk honestly.
- The hype cycle rewards volume and confidence over evidence, so leaders should also give quieter, technically strong proposals a fair chance.

## Relevance

Frames the commercial and political side of [AI business case](/concepts/ai-business-case.md) work, where [AI ROI measurement](/concepts/ai-roi-measurement.md) only lands if the room trusts the presenter. The social-currency argument extends [corporate currency](/concepts/corporate-currency.md) to AI asks and connects stalled asks to [pilot purgatory](/concepts/pilot-purgatory.md).
