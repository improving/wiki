# Improving is Harnessing the Power of AI Through Cutting-Edge Technologies — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-is-harnessing-the-power-of-ai/improving-is-harnessing-the-power-of-ai.md`

## Summary

Improving claims it has delivered over 500,000 hours on AI/ML projects with client returns conservatively exceeding $3.5B. The post surveys its capabilities — machine learning, neural networks and deep learning, expert systems, robotics, computer vision, fuzzy logic, and NLP — with a drilling analytics case study as its concrete example.

## Key Claims

- Improving says it has delivered over 500,000 hours on AI/ML-related projects, producing returns it conservatively estimates at over $3.5B for clients.
- Machine learning work spans predictive models, recommendation systems, and anomaly detection for forecasting, personalization, and fraud detection.
- A Real Time Drilling Predictive Analysis for Anadarko (later acquired by Oxy) displayed KPIs within one minute of field data, saving $5,000 per well in connection time and $300,000 in drilling time per well.
- Neural networks and deep learning power speech recognition, translation, and content generation, with adoption rising in medical, agricultural, and commerce sectors.
- Expert systems encode human expertise for tasks such as pipeline analysis and insurance claims trends.
- Robotics work includes warehouse sorting, picking, and storage, plus robotic control systems in hyper-clean manufacturing environments.
- Computer vision identifies product defects and tracks workers for safety; fuzzy logic handles uncertain data for banking and logistics; NLP powers chatbots, sentiment analysis, and text mining.
