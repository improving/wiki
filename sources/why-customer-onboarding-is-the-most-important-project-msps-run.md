# Why Customer Onboarding Is the Most Important Project MSPs Run — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/why-customer-onboarding-is-the-most-important-project-msps-run/why-customer-onboarding-is-the-most-important-project-msps-run.md`

## Summary

Diane Gormley argues that customer onboarding is the most important project a managed service provider runs, because it is where a client first sees whether the MSP delivers on its sales promises. Done well it converts a new account into a long-term partnership; done poorly it creates friction that lingers for years. Improving MSP uses a repeatable onboarding methodology with a jointly agreed project plan.

## Key Claims

- Onboarding is the client's first experience of how the MSP delivers on its promises of reliability, responsiveness, and technical excellence.
- It is also when the MSP learns about the client: business processes, key users, existing technologies, and security postures vary widely.
- Improving MSP deploys a repeatable onboarding methodology, sharing a project plan with the customer as a joint agreement for how the relationship begins.
- Clients who feel heard, informed, and supported from the start are far more likely to stay and expand services later.
- The post frames onboarding as more than a checklist or kickoff — it determines how clients perceive value, how efficiently the team operates, and how long partnerships last.

A customer-facing view of [managed services](/concepts/managed-services.md) and the [managed service provider](/concepts/managed-service-provider.md) model, tied to [customer success](/concepts/customer-success.md) and retention.
