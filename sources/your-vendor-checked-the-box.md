# Your Vendor Checked the Box. You Still Haven't. — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/your-vendor-checked-the-box/your-vendor-checked-the-box.md`

## Summary

David O'Hara flags the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations, live as of August 2, 2026, and the gap most US teams have not internalized: vendor compliance protects the vendor, not the deployer. The Act follows the output rather than the office, so any AI system touching EU customers, employees, or data subjects is in scope regardless of headquarters. He argues the response is an audit, not a legal project.

## Key Claims

- Article 50 transparency obligations took effect August 2, 2026
- The Act distinguishes a GPAI provider (the vendor) from a deployer (you); deployer obligations are yours
- Scope follows the output: touching EU users, employees, or data subjects brings you in regardless of headquarters
- Chatbots must disclose "you are talking to an AI" in plain language at the start of an interaction
- Synthetic or deepfake content generated after August 2 must be labeled; earlier content is grandfathered
- Penalties reach up to €35 million or 7% of global annual revenue for prohibited-practice violations

## Relevance

A concrete case for [AI Governance](/concepts/ai-governance.md): it separates vendor certification from organizational accountability and treats compliance as continuous, not a checkbox.

## Quality

Practitioner blog post by David O'Hara. Specific on dates, obligations, and penalty figures, but reads as an urgent opinion piece; it does not cite the regulation text directly or a legal authority.
