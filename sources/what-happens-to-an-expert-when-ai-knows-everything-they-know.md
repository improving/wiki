# What Happens to an Expert When AI Knows Everything They Know — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/what-happens-to-an-expert-when-ai-knows-everything-they-know/what-happens-to-an-expert-when-ai-knows-everything-they-know.md`

## Summary

O'Hara describes watching a consultant shift over six months from making the call to merely approving the tool's output: faster, clients satisfied, and the judgment that made him good quietly unused. Researchers at CHI 2026 named this "intuition rust" — the gradual dulling of expert judgment when AI handles the analysis layer so consistently that the human stops doing the cognitive work that built it. Unlike outright "cognitive surrender," it feels like doing your job well, just not very often.

## Key Claims

- "Intuition rust" is the gradual dulling of expert judgment when AI consistently does the analysis.
- What consultants actually sell is judgment: making a call under ambiguity and being accountable for it.
- That capability requires reps, experience, and being wrong sometimes; ratifying tool output skips the rep.
- The information-access premium is already compressing, but judgment still earns the billing rate.
- AI sharpens judgment when used as a sparring partner to stress-test thinking, and creates unnamed blind spots when used only to go faster.
