# An Equation for Change: Dedication + Transformation = Illumination — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/an-equation-for-change/an-equation-for-change.md`

## Summary

O'Hara explores the formula "Dedication + Transformation = Illumination," extending his earlier writing on transformational leadership and growth mindset. Dedication is the commitment to a goal that keeps you moving through challenges; transformation is the deliberate effort to evolve—becoming aware of your challenges, stepping outside your comfort zone, and accepting the work change requires. Together they lead to illumination, which he glosses as wisdom: insight beyond mere knowledge, encompassing clarity of purpose, wisdom won from challenges, and resilience. He frames illumination as a steady presence in each step rather than a far-off destination, contributing to collective as well as personal progress.

## Key Claims

- Dedication is the commitment that sustains progress toward a goal, especially through challenges.
- Transformation requires awareness of challenges, stepping outside comfort zones, and accepting the effort to change.
- Dedication + Transformation = Illumination, where illumination means wisdom rather than mere knowledge.
- Illumination includes clarity of purpose, wisdom from challenges, and resilience.
- Growth is both personal and collective: each step contributes to a more enlightened collective.
- Many desire illumination, but few are willing to do the work.
