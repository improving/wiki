# Workforce Planning – SAP Analytics Cloud — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/workforce-planning-sap-analytics-cloud/workforce-planning-sap-analytics-cloud.md`

## Summary

SAP HXM Workforce Planning is a pre-configured, guided solution for operational workforce planning over a one- to two-year horizon. It links HR data to finance so organizations can plan cost and FTE, create positions, and administer absences, transfers, and terminations, then compare actuals against plan.

## Key Claims

- The solution covers cost and FTE planning, new positions, and administration of absences, transfers, and terminations.
- It integrates HR with finance: based mainly on SAP SuccessFactors, plus S/4 Finance for historical personnel costs and Fieldglass for external workforce.
- Two approaches — top-down aggregated planning and bottom-up detailed (employee-level) planning — can be used in sequence, in combination, or switched between.
- The process runs configuration/import of master and transactional data, then aggregated or detailed planning, then transfer of the financial plan.
- Deriving G/L accounts maps HR pay components to accounts, and personnel costs are copied to cost-center plans by company code, cost center, and G/L account.
- Four customizable reports ship with it: Progress Overview, Gender Analysis, External Workforce, and Budget Comparison, all Actuals vs Plan.
