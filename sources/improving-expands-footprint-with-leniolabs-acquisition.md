# Improving Boosts South American Development Services with Leniolabs Acquisition — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-expands-footprint-with-leniolabs-acquisition/improving-expands-footprint-with-leniolabs-acquisition.md`

## Summary

A newsroom announcement that Improving acquired Leniolabs, a nearshore software developer with talent in Chile, Argentina, Spain, and the US. The deal is expected to push Improving's annualized revenue past $280 million and establish a South American presence, with Leniolabs leadership staying in place under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Key Claims

- Improving acquired Leniolabs, a software development company leveraging nearshore talent in Chile, Argentina, Spain, and the US.
- The transaction is expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue to exceed $280 million and extend development services into South America.
- Leniolabs' leadership team stays intact and continues to manage and grow the business under Improving's Enterprise Strategy.
- Leniolabs (founded 2013) specializes in backend, frontend, and data technologies using agile methods, with a focus on AI projects using cloud services and language models.
- Improving is designated SOC 2 compliant, cited as a commitment to protecting client data.
- The announcement carries quotes from Curtis Hite (CEO, Improving) and Iago Rodriguez (Co-Founder and Partner, Leniolabs).

## Relevance

An instance of [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) that extends Improving's [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md) footprint into [South America](/entities/south-america.md).
