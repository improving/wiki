# You Cant Govern an Inventory You Dont Know You Have — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/you-cant-govern-an-inventory-you-dont-know-you-have/you-cant-govern-an-inventory-you-dont-know-you-have.md`

## Summary

David O'Hara argues that most organizations skip the step that precedes identity governance: inventory. Because agents were deployed at the team level—built by a department, pre-loaded by a vendor, or spun up by an automation tool—nobody registered them centrally. The result is widespread adoption with almost no visibility, leaving agents connected to systems with unaudited permissions and no accountable owner. The fix, he says, is a policy rather than a platform purchase.

## Key Claims

- An OutSystems survey of nearly 1,900 IT leaders found 96% of enterprises run agents, but only 12% have a centralized platform to manage them
- The 84-point visibility gap represents real agents with unaudited permissions, owned by nobody accountable
- The fix is a policy: no agent goes to production until it is registered, named, assigned an owner, and documented for what it can access
- The "Agentic Landing Zone" is a decision, not a tool—someone must answer what an agent does, what it can touch, and who is responsible when it fails
