# Six Tech Trends That Defined 2023 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/six-tech-trends-that-defined-2023/six-tech-trends-that-defined-2023.md`

## Summary

Improving's year-end reflection names six trends that defined 2023 for its technologists. Generative AI leads, but the list also covers rising privacy and security concerns, quantum computing progress, robotics in daily life, health-tech transformation, and momentum behind sustainable tech.

## Key Claims

- Generative models such as OpenAI's GPT-4 dominated 2023, with abilities in natural language, content creation, and problem solving that raised ethical and regulatory questions.
- Privacy and security concerns reached new heights as notable cyberattacks and data breaches prompted investment in encryption and decentralized identity, though always-listening devices remain a challenge.
- Quantum computing advanced toward more stable, scalable processors, bringing quantum supremacy and applications in cryptography, optimization, and drug discovery closer.
- Robotics entered daily life through delivery drones, autonomous vehicles, robot-assisted surgery, and smart-home devices, aided by AI and machine learning.
- Health tech accelerated: telemedicine became standard, wearables fed real-time data into personalized care, and AI-driven diagnostics and drug discovery showed promise.
- Sustainable tech gained momentum, from eco-friendly hardware to renewable-powered data centers, with consumers demanding environmentally responsible products.
