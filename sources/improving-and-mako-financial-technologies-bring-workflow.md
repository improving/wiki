# Improving and Mako Financial Technologies Bring Workflow Intelligence and Operational Transparency to Wealth Managers — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-and-mako-financial-technologies-bring-workflow/improving-and-mako-financial-technologies-bring-workflow.md`

## Summary

Improving and Mako Financial Technologies partnered to help wealth managers move past workflow automation toward operational insight. Mako supplies configurable digital workflows for onboarding, account maintenance, approvals, and regulatory processes that capture structured process data; Improving turns that data into reporting and dashboards showing how workflows actually perform. The aim is earlier detection of inefficiencies, reduced operational risk, and better accountability across operations and compliance.

## Key Claims

- Wealth management firms have invested heavily in digital workflows yet still struggle to see where time, risk, and friction accumulate.
- Mako provides configurable digital workflows for client onboarding, account maintenance, approvals, and regulatory processes.
- These workflows capture rich, structured process data across teams and roles.
- Improving transforms that data into reporting and dashboards revealing end-to-end cycle times, role-level bottlenecks, and performance trends.
- The result is earlier detection of inefficiencies, reduced operational risk, and improved accountability across operations and compliance.
- The goal is to help leaders scale without adding operational burden or increasing regulatory exposure.
- Improving aims to fit insights into existing operating models, supporting continuous improvement rather than disruptive change.
- The partnership serves shared clients across Canada and the United States, tailored to each firm's regulatory environment.
