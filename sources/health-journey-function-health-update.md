# Health Journey: Function Health [Update] — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/health-journey-function-health-update/health-journey-function-health-update.md`

## Summary

O'Hara revisits Function Health after several months in the beta, describing how the platform contextualizes lab results with causes, foods to eat or limit, and cited supplement suggestions. The headline is his biological age: it started slightly below his chronological age and then dropped sharply after six months of supplements, recovery tracking, and diet changes — with workouts largely unchanged.

## Key Claims

- The platform labels levels (abnormal, typical, optimized, low), suggests causes, foods, and supplements, and cites sources
- First blood test (June 2023): biological age 47.6 vs. chronological 48.4
- Most recent test (Dec 2023): biological age moved back by nearly 7.5 years in six months
- He attributes the change to supplementation (IDLife, AG1, Toniiq), recovery (WHOOP), and food (Levels, Everlywell)
- Workouts were mostly unchanged and he did no extreme diets

## Relevance

A concrete example of [biological age](/concepts/biological-age.md) tracking via [biomarker testing](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md), tying the series' supplement and diet threads together.
