# Trinity Hunt Acquires Majority Interest in New Partnership with Improving Holdings — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/trinity-hunt-acquires-majority-interest-in-improving/trinity-hunt-acquires-majority-interest-in-improving.md`

## Summary

Growth-oriented middle-market private equity firm Trinity Hunt Partners partnered with Improving, taking majority interest in the Dallas-based technology and custom software firm. Co-founder and CEO Curtis Hite and the management team remained significant shareholders, and the partnership was framed as accelerating growth through both organic initiatives and continued strategic acquisitions.

## Key Claims

- Trinity Hunt Partners, a growth-oriented middle-market private equity firm, announced its partnership with Dallas-based Improving.
- Improving was co-founded in 2007 by Curtis Hite and had expanded via acquisitions into Houston, Minneapolis, Calgary, Columbus, Cleveland, and College Station.
- Hite and management remained significant shareholders, partnering with Trinity Hunt to increase the growth trajectory.
- Growth was to come from both organic initiatives and continued strategic acquisitions, aiming to build a market-leading North American provider.
- Trinity Hunt partner Blake Apel cited Improving's culture as its true differentiator for hiring and retaining top professionals.
- Improving had been named to the Inc. 5000 list for 9 consecutive years and won Best Places to Work awards in Ohio, Minneapolis, and Texas.
