# Investing in Teams — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/investing-in-teams/investing-in-teams.md`

## Summary

Devlin Liles lays out two responses to the "do more with less" pressure of 2023: invest in existing teams via time-boxed innovation and modernization labs, and reach beyond the company through nearshoring. He argues labs let teams get better, while nearshoring to Mexico addresses the quality, turnover, time-zone, and cultural drawbacks of offshoring.

## Key Claims

- Innovation Labs are time-boxed, fully committed proofs of concept where time, not quality, is the constraint.
- Modernization Labs focus on system quality and refreshing practices, consolidating or eliminating baggage from past learning.
- Time and space for labs is hard to find under business pressure, but these investments pay larger dividends later.
- Offshoring drawbacks include lower quality, high turnover, over-billing from blocking issues and time-zone gaps, and weak local market understanding.
- Nearshoring is not a panacea but addresses many of these, requiring deeper investment and oversight and the right engagement structure.
- Mexico offers developed technical talent, cultural alignment, creative capacity, and significantly better time-zone overlap.
