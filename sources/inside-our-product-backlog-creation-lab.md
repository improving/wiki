# Inside Our Product Backlog Creation Lab — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/inside-our-product-backlog-creation-lab/inside-our-product-backlog-creation-lab.md`

## Summary

Lisa Lueck describes Improving's Product Backlog Creation Lab, a hands-on facilitated workshop that turns fuzzy ideas into a prioritized, well-understood set of Product Backlog Items aligned to a product goal. The lab walks teams through vision and constraints, themes and outcomes, breaking big rocks into items, prioritization by value/risk/effort, and enrichment with acceptance criteria. A data science client case shows the lab producing lasting alignment and self-run refinement sessions.

## Key Claims

- The lab agenda covers product vision, goal and constraints; themes and outcomes; breaking big rocks into backlog items; prioritization by value, risk, and effort; enrichment with acceptance criteria and dependencies; and next steps for refinement.
- Three stated benefits: accelerated clarity and alignment in one session, expert facilitation with proven tools, and tailoring to real-world team context.
- A client's data science team with multiple competing workstreams ran a one-day lab; two months later they were aligned, following the defined process, and spending less time cherry-picking tasks.
- The team was able to host its own refinement sessions, applying shared approaches for breaking down work, estimation, and prioritization.
- The lab is pitched as especially powerful for cross-functional teams forming around a new product or initiative.

## Relevance

A concrete practice for [product backlog](/concepts/product-backlog.md) creation and [product ownership](/concepts/product-ownership.md), using [acceptance criteria](/concepts/acceptance-criteria.md) and prioritization to align [cross-functional teams](/concepts/cross-functional-teams.md).
