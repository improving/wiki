# DFW Is Now the AI Talent Market — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/dfw-is-now-the-ai-talent-market/dfw-is-now-the-ai-talent-market.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that DFW's talent numbers now describe a settled market rather than a rising one. CBRE and CompTIA data place the region near the top for net new tech workers and AI-specific hiring, and firms like Pudu Robotics and Samsung are choosing DFW over the coasts. He contends infrastructure is the wrong frame for the talent side, because talent signals where a market is going.

## Key Claims

- CBRE's Scoring Tech Talent 2026 ranked DFW eighth overall in North America and second in net new tech workers, with 37,230 added between 2022 and 2025.
- CompTIA placed DFW fifth in the country for AI-specific hiring.
- Pudu Robotics moved its U.S. headquarters from Silicon Valley to Richardson, and Samsung is in Plano.
- Infrastructure explains the capital inflows but is the wrong frame for what is happening on the talent side.
- The question has shifted from whether to build AI capacity in DFW to whether an organization can capture or train the talent.
