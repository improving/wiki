# Work Life Balance — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/work-life-balance/work-life-balance.md`

## Summary

The author argues you cannot "balance" something that is always in flux, and that attempting it manufactures anxiety and a sense of inadequacy because the goal is unattainable. Work is not the zero-sum opponent of life: how we spend our time is our life, and work is part of that life. He recommends finding something good in the work and making that your measure of success, and adopting an abundance rather than scarcity mindset — scarcity locks you into focusing on the wrong things. Being integrative in how you think about your life helps you act that way, and failing to be present where you are creates anxiety too.

## Key Claims

- You can't balance something that is constantly changing; trying to produces anxiety and inadequacy.
- Work-life is not zero-sum — how we spend our time is our life, and work is part of that life.
- Find something good in your work and make that your measure of success.
- Abundance beats scarcity: a scarcity mindset locks you into focusing on the wrong things.
- Be integrative in how you think about your life; presence matters, and its absence creates anxiety.
