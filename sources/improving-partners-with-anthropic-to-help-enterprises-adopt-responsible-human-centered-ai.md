# Improving Partners with Anthropic to Help Enterprises Adopt Responsible, Human‑Centered AI — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-partners-with-anthropic-to-help-enterprises-adopt-responsible-human-centered-ai/improving-partners-with-anthropic-to-help-enterprises-adopt-responsible-human-centered-ai.md`

## Summary

Improving announced a strategic partnership with Anthropic to help organizations responsibly integrate large language models into enterprise software, digital platforms, and internal workflows. The announcement frames the work around Improving's Trust Evolution model, arguing that adoption depends less on model capability than on governance, oversight, and organizational readiness at each stage.

## Key Claims

- The partnership aims to help organizations move beyond experimentation to production-ready AI systems that are secure, transparent, and grounded in real business context.
- Improving positions its role as combining advanced AI capabilities with strength in software engineering and enterprise delivery.
- Curtis Hite (CEO): "AI is most effective when it's implemented with intention and discipline."
- The work aligns with Improving's Trust Evolution model, which holds that successful AI adoption requires growing trust through governance, oversight, and organizational readiness — not just better models.
- Devlin Liles (Chief AI Officer and Chief Consulting Officer) says the partnership helps organizations move from experimentation to real adoption in ways that support the people doing the work.
- The partnership builds on training, architecture guidance, and hands-on delivery across AI, cloud engineering, data platforms, and enterprise modernization.
- Anthropic is described as focused on reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems.
