# How To Choose the Best Offshore Software Development Partner? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/how-to-choose-best-offshore-software-development-partner/how-to-choose-best-offshore-software-development-partner.md`

## Summary

Faizan Fahim argues that choosing an offshore partner is a critical business decision requiring rigorous vetting, because the market is large and quality varies drastically. He lays out twelve evaluation criteria — from technical depth and delivery maturity to security, legal protections, political stability, retention, and scalability — and closes with a detailed evaluation questionnaire. The message is that the best partners behave as strategic contributors rather than order-takers.

## Key Claims

- The global offshore software development market is valued at over $150 billion, with thousands of vendors and widely varying quality.
- Common failure modes include "ghost firms" that vanish after deposits, vendors holding IP hostage, and teams secretly subcontracting to unqualified third parties.
- Around 70% of companies cite access to specialized skills as a top reason for offshoring.
- Delivery maturity and governance matter more than raw coding ability; mature partners offer direct access to project tracking tools like Jira or Azure DevOps.
- Hybrid or onshore leadership models reduce the "thrown over the wall" problem and improve alignment and feedback loops.
- About 53% of companies identify data security as a top concern; providers should comply with SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, or HIPAA where relevant.
- Approximately 54% of offshore projects experience cultural or communication challenges.
- Credibility signals include partner status (Microsoft Gold, AWS Advanced, Google Cloud), awards like the Inc. 5000 and Best Places to Work, and certifications like ISO 9001 and CMMI.
- Contracts should assign IP on creation, include indemnification, and specify dispute jurisdiction; a turnover rate below 15% is considered excellent.
- A long evaluation questionnaire spans company culture, delivery, technical expertise, security, legal terms, financial stability, and team fit.
