# Improving CEO, Curtis Hite, Joins Conscious Capitalism Board of Directors — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-ceo-joins-conscious-capitalism-board-of-directors/improving-ceo-joins-conscious-capitalism-board-of-directors.md`

## Summary

On February 7, 2018, Conscious Capitalism International announced new executive leaders joining its Board of Directors, including Improving co-founder and CEO Curtis Hite among eight newly appointed members. The post notes Hite joining figures such as Whole Foods' John Mackey and The Container Store's Kip Tindell, and positions Conscious Capitalism as the foundation and philosophy of Improving.

## Key Claims

- Curtis Hite was one of eight newly appointed members of the Conscious Capitalism International Board of Directors, announced February 7, 2018.
- He joined notable names including John Mackey, co-founder and CEO of Whole Foods Market, and Kip Tindell, co-founder and chairman of The Container Store.
- Board members are leaders in the movement, charged with setting the organization's strategic direction.
- Conscious Capitalism International's CEO, Alexander McCobin, called it potentially the defining social movement of the 21st century.
- Improving has been a longtime leader and sponsor of the Conscious Capitalism movement.
- The post states Conscious Capitalism is the foundation and philosophy of Improving.
