# Powering Modern Enterprises Part 5: Build the Platform First — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/powering-modern-enterprises-part-5-build-the-platform-first/powering-modern-enterprises-part-5-build-the-platform-first.md`

## Summary

Closing Improving's five-part Powering Modern Enterprises series, the post argues that AI, data, and application capabilities all depend on a platform foundation, and that organizations which invest in those capabilities before the platform get unreliable pipelines, struggling applications, and AI that never delivers consistent results. It lays out four platform areas and a McKesson case where a platform built first met a federal compliance deadline.

## Key Claims

- Improving was the 2025 Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year and holds more certified data streaming engineers in the Americas than any other partner.
- Many organizations invest in AI, data engineering, and application modernization before building the platform they depend on; as workloads grow, pipelines become unreliable, applications struggle, governance requirements grow, and AI fails to deliver.
- Four platform areas: cloud (Azure, AWS, Google Cloud), data and analytics (Snowflake, Databricks, Confluent, Microsoft Fabric), AI platform and infrastructure (MLOps pipelines, agent orchestration, runtime environments), and integration and application.
- Most AI projects demo well but struggle in production without reliable deployment pipelines, governance, observability, version control, and infrastructure; operational overhead grows with every new model or agent.
- Most enterprises get stuck after 12 to 24 months because the headline work is right but the foundation underneath is weak.
- Case: McKesson's Drug Serialization Repository had a federal compliance deadline across 29 distribution centers; automating deployment with GitHub Actions, Kubernetes, Confluent Kafka, and MongoDB reduced deployment to 33 minutes per location and achieved 100% DSCSA compliance before the deadline.
- The series' closing theme: lasting transformation comes from a foundation that lets AI, data, and applications evolve together, not from isolated investments.
