# The 2x2 AI Impact Matrix: What to Do Based on Where You Are — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/the-2x2-ai-impact-matrix-what-to-do-based-on-where-you-are/the-2x2-ai-impact-matrix-what-to-do-based-on-where-you-are.md`

## Summary

The post argues that AI strategy's hard question is not whether AI will affect an organization but what to do about it, and that the answer depends on two variables: time impact (how much AI compresses hours per unit of output) and demand limit (whether demand or execution capacity caps value creation). Crossing them gives four quadrants with distinct prescriptions — Wait, Scale by Headcount + Incremental Improvement, Reduce Headcount or Grow the Role, and Do More.

## Key Claims

- Time impact measures productivity amplification; demand limit asks whether demand or capacity is the binding constraint.
- Low time impact + high demand limit → monitor; do not over-invest.
- High time impact + high demand limit → redeploy freed capacity into demand generation rather than shrinking the team.
- High time impact + low demand limit → the hard quadrant; do honest capacity planning, growing the role or adjusting team size.
- Quadrant assignments expire as AI capability rises and cost falls; date them and re-run the analysis on a cadence.
