**Original source**: `references/improving.com/our-14th-year-on-inc-5000/our-14th-year-on-inc-5000.md`

## Summary

Improving announced its fourteenth year on the Inc. 5000 list of the fastest-growing US private companies, ranked No. 3820. Inc. ranks companies by overall revenue growth over a three-year period. The post quotes the Inc. 5000 spokesperson on the 2023 class's growth amid inflation, rising capital costs, and hiring challenges, and notes Improving representatives will attend the conference and gala in San Antonio.

## Key Claims

- Improving appeared on the Inc. 5000 for the fourteenth year, at rank No. 3820.
- Inc. 5000 ranks companies by overall revenue growth over a three-year period.
- The 2023 class's top 500 companies averaged a 2,238% three-year revenue growth rate.
- This year's Inc. 5000 companies added 1,187,266 jobs to the economy over the past three years.
- Improving will be represented at the Inc. 5000 Conference and Gala on October 31 to November 2 in San Antonio.
