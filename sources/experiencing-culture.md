# Experiencing Culture — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/experiencing-culture/experiencing-culture.md`

## Summary

O'Hara argues that how strongly an employee experiences a company's culture depends on how deeply their management chain (their "up-line") has internalized it. Culture must be lived to stay alive, which makes it hard to sell to newcomers and outsiders and impossible to fake, because people intuitively detect the gap between stated and practiced values. The open question is how to make culture real at every level of an organization.

## Key Claims

- "A person's ability to experience a culture is directly dependent on their up-line's internalization of it."
- Culture cannot be faked — people are too intuitive and will call out inauthenticity.
- A significant culture touches nearly every aspect of work, both individually and corporately.
- Living the values (or not living them) is real and visible, not a message you can control.
- The hard problem is making a strong culture real to all levels, especially newcomers and outsiders.
