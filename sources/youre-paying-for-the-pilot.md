# You're Paying for the Pilot. You're Not Getting the Production. — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/youre-paying-for-the-pilot/youre-paying-for-the-pilot.md`

## Summary

David O'Hara points to a wide gap between AI agent pilots and scaled production and argues the cause is environmental, not technological. The technology works; what fails is everything around it—ownership after the engagement, monitoring, real-system integration, domain training data, and output quality under real-world variance. His prescription is to treat AI deployment like software delivery, with ownership, monitoring, iteration, and accountability to outcomes.

## Key Claims

- A March 2026 survey of 650 enterprise technology leaders found 78% have at least one AI agent pilot; only 14% have scaled one to org-wide production—a 64-point gap
- The five consistent root causes of failed pilots: integration complexity, inconsistent output quality at scale, no monitoring tooling, unclear ownership after go-live, and insufficient domain training data
- None of those causes is a model limitation, so choosing a different model solves none of them
- Integration is often deferred to a "phase two" that never ships
- The winning AI consultancies will be those that close the pilot-to-production gap, a delivery problem

## Relevance

Connects [outcome accountability](/concepts/outcome-accountability.md) to agentic delivery; argues production readiness is an operating-model concern rather than a model-selection one.
