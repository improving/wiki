# Being PRIDEfully Me at Improving — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/being-pridefully-me-at-improving/being-pridefully-me-at-improving.md`

## Summary

An Improving Nearshore Talent Management Specialist describes living openly at work and why visibility matters: seeing someone else go first makes a hard experience easier for those who follow. He ties the personal story to Pride's history and to Improving's Come Together inclusion-and-diversity focus for June.

## Key Claims

- Visibility changes lives — the author is out and proud partly to show younger people they are not alone.
- Inclusivity at Improving extends beyond gender and sexual orientation to any group labeled "different."
- June is Pride month, and the Come Together Initiative focused on Inclusion and Diversity that month.
- Pride traces to the June 28, 1969 Stonewall Inn riots and the June 1970 Chicago Gay Liberation march.
- Pride means being proud of living openly and not apologizing, not pride in orientation itself.
- Speaking about Pride to 50+ colleagues at his site became a turning point that shifted the site's culture.
