# Introducing Improving Ohio — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-ohio-merger/improving-ohio-merger.md`

## Summary

Improving's Columbus and Cleveland offices merged into a single Improving Ohio enterprise on January 1, 2022. President Josh Harrison says the two offices have complementary strengths — Cleveland in infrastructure and Microsoft, Columbus in application development on Azure — and that combining them makes Improving Ohio a full-spectrum Azure destination rather than one that must borrow from other offices.

## Key Claims

- The two Ohio enterprises officially became one on January 1, 2022.
- Josh Harrison, previously Columbus President, continues as Improving Ohio President.
- Cleveland's strength is infrastructure and all things Microsoft; Columbus's is application development, especially on Microsoft Azure.
- Harrison's goal: become "the destination for everything that sits on the Azure platform," from infrastructure to AppDev and beyond.
- The merger lets Improving Ohio serve clients end to end instead of reaching out to other offices.
- Recruiting, sales, and delivery teams from both offices were already collaborating before the official date.
