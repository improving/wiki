# The Benefit of a Corporate Innovation Lab — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/innovation-lab-benefits/innovation-lab-benefits.md`

## Summary

Digital strategy now determines most firms' viability, yet IT budgets are still dominated by maintenance. The post argues that a corporate innovation lab signals and enforces a growth priority, but only if it runs a distinct execution model with genuine accountability.

## Key Claims

- The 2003 "IT Doesn't Matter" view proved partly true for base infrastructure, but software built on cloud platforms is where competitive advantage now lives.
- For North American companies, 70% of IT budgets go to maintaining existing infrastructure and operations, leaving only 30% for innovation, while 88% of CIOs say they are increasingly innovation-focused.
- Innovation is a discipline that works through a method, not suspended processes and measurements.
- Labs should focus on new product development across idea generation, implementation, and validation, run by cross-discipline teams organized around a product.
- Labs need tension with the parent: shared vision, purpose, and strategy, but flexible "how" and "what."
- New product sales as a percent of revenue and gross margins on those sales are the practical accountability metrics.
