# Thought Leadership: One Creators Journey — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/thought-leadership-one-creators-journey/thought-leadership-one-creators-journey.md`

## Summary

Tim Dickey traces his route into thought leadership, starting with an embarrassing March 2022 livestream interview that produced "word salad" answers. He turned the experience into a self-reflective video, then ran an experiment comparing video and written posts on LinkedIn. Finding written posts performed better and that LinkedIn masked unviewed videos, he moved to YouTube and began hosting an internal podcast. His stated mission is to inspire others to create, and he closes with five steps for beginners.

## Key Claims

- A livestream interview where he received questions in advance went badly because he lacked rapport with the interviewer.
- After nearly two months, his experiment showed written LinkedIn posts had better engagement than video posts.
- Posting videos directly to LinkedIn masked a lack of viewed videos compared to posting to YouTube.
- His YouTube format is deliberately minimal: one topic, 60–90 seconds, press record, light editing, for fast feedback.
- He launched an internal podcast where guests pick from 25 questions and talk for about 15 minutes, aiming to turn it into a reusable framework.
- Five starter steps: start simple, start small, enlist others already on the journey, ask your network for encouragement, and don't be afraid to embarrass yourself.
