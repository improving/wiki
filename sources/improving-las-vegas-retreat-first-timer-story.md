# Retreat No. 1 (of Many): A First-Timer's Vegas Story — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-las-vegas-retreat-first-timer-story/improving-las-vegas-retreat-first-timer-story.md`

## Summary

A first-person account of Improving's 20th company retreat in Las Vegas, written by a Dallas-based Improver under seven months into the job. The essay describes a planned-but-unstructured weekend built to mix people from different offices, and argues the culture let him show up as himself — a point he underscores with the moment founder Curtis Hite stopped to talk and dance with a newer face and remembered him all weekend.

## Key Claims

- It was the company's 20th retreat and the author's first; he was a little under seven months in and had never been to Vegas.
- Thursday opened with a welcome happy hour and an attendee-choice event; the author chose fine dining at Alexxa's in front of the Bellagio fountains.
- Friday's Enterprise Challenge took over the Hard Rock Café with a company-wide competition and a beer pong tournament, which the Mexico team turned into a dance party.
- Saturday's Amazing Race on Fremont Street deliberately mixed teams across locations so people could not stay in their comfort zone.
- Founder and CEO Curtis Hite stopped to talk and dance with the author in a large crowd, then remembered him through the weekend.
- The author's friends and family reacted with disbelief that a job would do all that.
