# Empowering IT Projects with Modern Delivery Frameworks — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/empowering-it-projects-with-modern-delivery-frameworks/empowering-it-projects-with-modern-delivery-frameworks.md`

## Summary

As GenAI and shifting buyer expectations complicate IT projects, unmanaged complexity causes poor communication, rising costs, and lower quality. The post cites PMI data that organizations with a consistent project-management approach report 38% more projects meeting original goals, matching Improving's experience across thousands of engagements. It introduces Improving's ACT (Anchor, Consistency, Transition) Framework — building trust and foundations, delivering value with disciplined practice, then transitioning for a smooth, measurable conclusion — now used internally and by other organizations.

## Key Claims

- Unmanaged complexity in IT projects leads to poor communication, rising costs, and lower quality.
- PMI reports organizations with a consistent project-management approach have 38% more projects meet their original goals.
- Consistency, not heavy process, matters: shared success measures, defined roles, regular communication, and visibility.
- ACT stands for Anchor, Consistency, Transition.
- Anchor builds trust and understanding of the vision and goals while putting foundational elements in place.
- Consistency delivers regular value with disciplined practice; Transition delivers measurable outcomes and equips the client for ongoing success.
- Improving supports adoption with a Launch Workshop, an Engagement HomeBase, and biweekly Calibration Cadence meetings.
