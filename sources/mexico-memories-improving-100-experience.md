# Mexico, Memories, and my Improving100 Experience — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/mexico-memories-improving-100-experience/mexico-memories-improving-100-experience.md`

## Summary

A first-person account of being selected for an Improving 100 award and attending the all-expenses-paid recognition trip to Cancún. The author argues that meaningful recognition goes beyond certificates and awards — it creates memories, builds connections across a remote, multi-country company, and leaves a lasting impact.

## Key Claims

- Improving 100 is Improving's employee recognition program, selecting and rewarding top performers across 22+ offices in four categories: Community Impact, Business Growth, Delivery Excellence, and Technical Innovation.
- Employees nominate their peers — anyone can nominate anyone — and leadership decides after due diligence, which the author says makes the recognition more special.
- Atulpriya Sharma was selected in the Community Impact category, the first winner from Improving India; the reward was an all-expenses-paid trip to Cancún.
- He was joined by fellow Improving India winners Bhavin Gandhi, Deeksha Sharma, Shehbaz Pathan, and Aman Juneja, and stopped in New York to facilitate a platform engineering round table at Kubernetes Community Days New York en route.
- Improving India works remotely, so the gathering was the first time many colleagues met in person and the first time many met Improvers from other countries.
- The welcome reception was hosted by US Managing Director Scott McMichael and CEO Curtis Hite; excursions included deep-sea fishing, sailing, golfing, and a Mexican cooking experience, and the trip closed with an award night.
- The author concludes that recognition that creates memories and builds connections leaves a more lasting impact than certificates alone.
