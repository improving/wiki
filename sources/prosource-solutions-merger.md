# ProSource Solutions Merger — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/prosource-solutions-merger/prosource-solutions-merger.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired ProSource Solutions, LLC, a management and IT consulting firm headquartered in Richfield, Ohio, expected to add more than $10,000,000 in annualized revenue in 2018 and extend Improving's reach into the Midwestern and Southeastern United States. The deal continued Improving's "Enterprise Model" of merging like-minded service firms, and ProSource became Improving's sixth enterprise location.

## Key Claims

- ProSource Solutions, LLC, headquartered in Richfield, Ohio, was acquired by Improving Holdings.
- The transaction was expected to increase annualized revenue by more than $10,000,000 in 2018.
- It expanded Improving's geographic reach in the Midwestern and Southeastern US.
- ProSource joined the Improving brand as its sixth enterprise location.
- ProSource was a preferred Microsoft Partner and won "Top U.S. Services Partner of the Year" from Microsoft in five separate years.
- ProSource's offerings spanned application development, cloud computing, collaboration, and infrastructure.
- Lowell Messner was President and CEO of ProSource; Curtis Hite was Improving's CEO.
