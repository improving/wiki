# Improver Spotlight - Aimee Ernst — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improver-spotlight-aimee-ernst/improver-spotlight-aimee-ernst.md`

## Summary

A spotlight on Aimee Ernst, the Industry Solutions Delivery (ISD, formerly Microsoft Consulting Services) Program Manager for Improving. She joined via the 2017 ProSource Solutions acquisition and now splits her time between the Ohio enterprise and the corporate Microsoft Services Team, focused on expanding Improving's reach within Microsoft ISD.

## Key Claims

- Ernst started at Improving in 2017 as part of the ProSource Solutions acquisition.
- She has worked with Microsoft Consulting Services for over 10 years and now focuses on expanding Improving's reach within Industry Solutions Delivery.
- Her goal is to keep Improving well aligned as a trusted, go-to partner for Microsoft ISD.
- She notes Improving has over 1,200 consultants, many with strong Microsoft skills, and can bring talent from many enterprises onto ISD projects.
- She resides in Bay Village, Ohio.
