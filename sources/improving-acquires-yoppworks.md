# Improving Acquires Canadian YoppWorks — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-yoppworks/improving-acquires-yoppworks.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired YoppWorks, an application-development consultancy headquartered in Ottawa, Canada, that specializes in digital transformation and cloud-native modernization. The deal is expected to push Improving's collective annualized revenue beyond $200 million and expand its Canadian presence. Under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy," YoppWorks leadership stays in place and continues to run the business with access to Improving's wider services.

## Key Claims

- YoppWorks is based in Ottawa, Ontario, founded in 2016, and specializes in Scala programming, Akka open source, cloud-native technologies, and Reactive Architecture.
- The acquisition lifts Improving's collective annualized revenue above $200 million.
- Improving's "Enterprise Strategy" merges technology service companies that share a commitment to trust, excellence, and culture.
- The YoppWorks leadership team remains intact and continues to operate and grow the business.
- CEO Curtis Hite cites alignment on business philosophy and company culture as the basis for the deal.
- YoppWorks CEO Jack Gulas calls it "1+1=3" for team members and customers, and notes partnerships with Lightbend and Google Cloud.
