# Back to Basics: The Value of Statistical Machine Learning Today — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/back-to-basics-the-value-of-statistical-machine-learning-today/back-to-basics-the-value-of-statistical-machine-learning-today.md`

## Summary

The post argues that classical statistical methods — linear and logistic regression, decision trees, and support vector machines — are unfairly overshadowed by deep learning and remain valuable for their simplicity, interpretability, and efficiency. It positions them as both practical tools for transparent domains and the rigor layer that validates and legitimizes advanced and generative AI.

## Key Claims

- Regression, decision trees, and SVMs are effective wherever transparency and speed matter, despite deep learning's prominence.
- Interpretability is their core advantage: healthcare, finance, and manufacturing need to understand the "why" behind a prediction, not just its accuracy.
- They provide a framework for testing hypotheses, evaluating model assumptions, and quantifying uncertainty — evidence for accountability and regulatory compliance.
- In generative AI, statistical methods help identify biases, ensure data distribution integrity, and supply quantitative metrics for output quality.
- They act as a first line of defense: robustness checks, bias assessment, and assumption validation before complex models are deployed, guarding against spurious correlations.
- Integrating statistical competencies keeps AI from being an untrustworthy black box and supports evidence-based decisions.
