# From Cop to Coach: When Governance Stopped Being About Control — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/from-cop-to-coach-when-governance-stopped-being-about-control/from-cop-to-coach-when-governance-stopped-being-about-control.md`

## Summary

Devlin Liles reads Gartner's August 2021 retirement of its Magic Quadrant for Metadata Management as a declaration about the discipline's direction. The old model treated a [data catalog](/concepts/data-catalog.md) as a passive inventory requiring manual curation; the new "active metadata" model captures metadata as a byproduct of system operation. He argues this shift reframes governance from control (a compliance mechanism) to enablement (an aid that makes the right thing the easy thing), and illustrates it with the DHCS behavioral health platform.

## Key Claims

- Passive metadata "relies on human effort to curate"; active metadata is a system that does not wait for humans to maintain it.
- Passive catalogs go stale because maintenance budgets are underfunded; [runtime lineage](/concepts/runtime-lineage.md) and quality metrics captured automatically eliminate that loop for a large class of metadata.
- When the cost of governance information drops toward zero, governance can be applied everywhere — every table, column-level policy, full-pipeline lineage.
- Gartner's 2027 prediction that 80% of governance initiatives will fail is rooted in the control-versus-enablement distinction: compliance exercises fail, outcome-tied programs have a chance.
