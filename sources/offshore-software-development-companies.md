# Best Offshore Software Development Companies — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/offshore-software-development-companies/offshore-software-development-companies.md`

## Summary

Offshore development has moved from cost arbitrage to a cornerstone of global digital strategy, giving organizations access to talent and follow-the-sun delivery — but distance creates latency, miscommunication, and jurisdictional risk. The guide profiles sixteen offshore partners and frames selection around technical depth, delivery maturity, security and compliance, and cultural fit, then compares the major hubs of Asia Pacific, Eastern Europe, and the Middle East & Africa.

## Key Claims

- Common engagement models are staff augmentation, dedicated teams/PODs, managed services, and end-to-end delivery.
- Benefits include cost efficiency via purchasing power parity, faster scaling, time-zone coverage, niche skills, freeing internal teams for strategy, and cultural exposure.
- Selection criteria: validate technology depth and domain fit, assess delivery maturity and governance, evaluate hybrid/onshore leadership, prioritize security and compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA), check cultural and communication fit, and focus on long-term strategic value.
- Cited stats: ~70% of companies cite access to specialized skills as a top reason to offshore; ~53% name data security as a top concern; ~54% experience cultural or communication challenges.
- Improving is profiled with 100+ clients, 170+ engineers, 2–4 week team launches, and an India presence expanded via the InfraCloud acquisition.
- Improving case results: Mercedes-Benz consolidated 1,000+ Azure and 400 AWS accounts and cut deployment times 80%; J.P. Morgan reduced deployment times from 20 to 5 minutes.
- Hidden costs can include onboarding, knowledge transfer, security tooling, and vendor transition.
