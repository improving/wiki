# Mining Center of Excellence — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/mining-center-of-excellence/mining-center-of-excellence.md`

## Summary

Improving announces a bi-annual, two-hour virtual conference series for the mining industry, pairing best mining practices with data insights and discovery. It also promotes its ESG "Play," part of its Analytics Playbook, as an industry-designed way to capture, document, store, analyze, and report on ESG frameworks beyond standard ones.

## Key Claims

- ESG reporting grew out of the need to go beyond financial metrics, centering on long-term stakeholder value tracked through KPIs.
- Improving's ESG Play aims to go beyond SASB, GRI, and TCFD and to make tracking thousands of metrics simple with customizable reporting frameworks.
- The Mining Center of Excellence sessions run bi-annually, each spotlighting one area of mining operations with a guest expert.
- Attendees share challenges and best practices around gathering and using data in open forum discussions.
- Presenters include Michael Matrick, Jeff Armstrong, and Jack Lobe of Improving, and Bethany Borody of New Gold Inc.
