# Data Is Not the Goal: The Knowledge-Insight-Action Chain — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/data-is-not-the-goal-the-knowledge-insight-action-chain/data-is-not-the-goal-the-knowledge-insight-action-chain.md`

## Summary

The post argues that data is raw material, not the objective — action is. It lays out a four-stage AI value chain (Data → Knowledge → Insight → Action), contending that most strategies neglect the transformations between stages, especially governance, which makes knowledge trustworthy enough to yield actionable insight. Generative AI's real contribution is compressing the insight-to-action gap.

## Key Claims

- "Data-driven" failures usually mean either no data use or data that isn't acted upon; the latter signals confusing collection with value.
- Data is valuable as gold ore is: useful only after cleaning, normalization, contextualization, and governance.
- Skipping governance produces untrustworthy insights — models with unassessable confidence and reports stakeholders read differently.
- Insight serves three cadences: scheduled summarization, exception summarization, and predictive analytics; organizations often invest in one and neglect the others.
- Gen AI compresses the insight-to-action chain from a three-day human relay to hours via drafted, reviewable actions; the value is the compression, not the AI.
