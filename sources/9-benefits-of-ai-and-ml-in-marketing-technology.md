# 9 Benefits of AI and Machine Learning in Marketing Technology — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/9-benefits-of-ai-and-ml-in-marketing-technology/9-benefits-of-ai-and-ml-in-marketing-technology.md`

## Summary

AI MarTech applies machine learning to replace one-size-fits-all marketing with predictions and personalization at scale. The post catalogs nine benefits, from higher revenue and lower costs to actionable insights and shorter sales cycles, drawing on Zigatta's client experience.

## Key Claims

- AI marketing pairs creative work with ML that predicts and optimizes experiences for each visitor.
- Personalization at scale raises revenue, order frequency, average order value, and customer satisfaction.
- The post cites DRIFT stats: 41% of surveyed B2B professionals use AI to accelerate revenue and a $5.9 trillion annual marketing/sales impact.
- AI is only as smart as the data fed to it, so the data an organization tracks is decisive.
- A Zigatta recommender game sped data gathering up to 5x and cut item returns by 11%.
- A Zigatta build combining a centralized CRM, BI, and Azure Machine Learning surfaced churn detection, up-sell recommendations, and onboarding insights.
