# Personal Commitment to Excellence — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/personal-commitment-to-excellence/personal-commitment-to-excellence.md`

## Summary

O'Hara shares a declaration a friend passed along that "made the hairs on my arms stand up." The passage asserts that a life, reputation, and influence are the mirror of one's own choices: if you are not everything you can be, it is because you have not chosen to be more. It commits to living in the present rather than the unchangeable past or the unguaranteed future, accepting that you cannot do everything well but can do some things well, and resolving that failure will not be a failure of nerve.

## Key Claims

- You are the sum of your genetics and experiences, and all of them are yours.
- Your life, reputation, and influence mirror the choices you have made.
- Live in the present "NOW" rather than the past you can't change or a future you can't guarantee.
- You cannot do everything well, but you can do some things well.
- You can't guarantee winning, but you can refuse to let losing become a habit.
