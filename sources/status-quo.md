# Status Quo - the way things are — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/status-quo/status-quo.md`

## Summary

A brief quote-driven post built on the quip that "status quo is Latin for 'the mess we are in'." O'Hara argues that we too often treat the current state of affairs as the easiest and most stable option even when it isn't. He calls for stepping back to assess the reality of the current state before deciding whether change is needed, and closes with an open question to the reader about how they push themselves to look at situations objectively.

## Key Claims

- "Status quo" can be read as shorthand for "the mess we are in."
- The present state is often assumed to be the easiest and most stable, though that may be false.
- People should step back and assess the reality of the current state objectively.
- Change is only warranted once that assessment shows it is needed.

## Relevance

A short reflection on questioning default assumptions; it gestures at objective self-assessment and change without developing a specific concept or framework.
