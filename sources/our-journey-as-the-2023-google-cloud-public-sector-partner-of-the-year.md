# Our Journey As The 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner Of The Year For Canada — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/our-journey-as-the-2023-google-cloud-public-sector-partner-of-the-year/our-journey-as-the-2023-google-cloud-public-sector-partner-of-the-year.md`

## Summary

Sarthak Routh describes how YoppWorks, an Improving company, won the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada. The team helped legacy on-prem public sector clients migrate, built a scientific computing portal that lets federal researchers provision HPC, AI, and ML resources on demand, and applied Document AI to a large Government of Canada department's backlog and ATIP workflows. It closes with lessons on security, documentation, and client context.

## Key Claims

- YoppWorks was named the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada.
- Public sector clients included a Canadian federal research organization, a Government of Canada department, and the governments of Ontario, Quebec, and New Brunswick.
- A scientific computing portal enabled automated provisioning of secure cloud scientific computing, project lifecycle management, and cost controls, lowering cost of ownership.
- Document AI work included a backlog processing solution, a Document AI Warehouse POC, and an ATIP data loss prevention solution.
- Lessons learned: prioritize security and compliance and budget time for it, write documentation accessible to non-technical stakeholders, and invest in understanding client organizational context.
- In 2022 the team earned Google Cloud expertise in Open Source technologies; the award coincided with Google Cloud Next '23.
