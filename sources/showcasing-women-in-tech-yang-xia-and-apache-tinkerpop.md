# Showcasing Women in Tech: Yang Xia & Apache TinkerPop — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/showcasing-women-in-tech-yang-xia-and-apache-tinkerpop/showcasing-women-in-tech-yang-xia-and-apache-tinkerpop.md`

## Summary

Improving announces that consultant Yang Xia is the company's first Apache Project Management Committee (PMC) member, recognized for her work on Apache TinkerPop. The post explains the PMC's governance role, describes TinkerPop's graph computing model and its Gremlin traversal language, and traces Yang's path from neuroscience and cancer-genetics research into software.

## Key Claims

- The PMC governs Apache Software Foundation projects, holds sole authority to vote on formal releases, and votes in new committers and PMC members.
- Apache TinkerPop is an open-source framework for graph databases (OLTP) and graph analytics (OLAP); graphs are vertices (objects) connected by edges (relationships).
- Gremlin is TinkerPop's graph traversal language, analogous to SQL for relational databases.
- TinkerPop lets developers add graph capabilities without building their own APIs, graph processing engines, or algorithms.
- Yang moved from a Gremlin-based JDBC driver to a Go implementation of Gremlin, drawn to serialization mechanics.
- She came to programming via a neuroscience/psychology degree and a master's in pathology and laboratory medicine researching cancer genetics.
