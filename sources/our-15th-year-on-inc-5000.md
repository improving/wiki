# Our 15th Year on Inc. 5000 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/our-15th-year-on-inc-5000/our-15th-year-on-inc-5000.md`

## Summary

Improving announced its 15th appearance on the Inc. 5000 list of fastest-growing private US companies. CEO Curtis Hite shared advice with fellow honorees during a virtual Inc. 5000 event, and Improving representatives attended the October conference and gala in Palm Desert, California. The post credits the sustained growth to employee passion and commitment.

## Key Claims

- Improving made the Inc. 5000 list for the 15th time.
- The 2024 rankings are based on percentage revenue growth from 2020 through 2023.
- Curtis Hite spoke at the "Make the Most of Making the List" virtual event for Inc. 5000 company leaders.
- Improving attended the Inc. 5000 Conference and Gala October 16–18 in Palm Desert, California.
- Improving attributes the run to employees' passion and commitment to excellence.
