# IBM Just Showed What Delivery Restructuring Actually Looks Like — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/ibm-just-showed-what-delivery-restructuring-actually-looks-like/ibm-just-showed-what-delivery-restructuring-actually-looks-like.md`

## Summary

O'Hara contrasts two years of consulting firms merely rebranding AI offerings with what [IBM](/entities/ibm.md) announced on August 13: GPT-5.6 embedded directly into the platform consultants use on every engagement, not a separate AI service line. He reads this as a new labor model — certified practitioners through an [OpenAI](/entities/openai.md) Practice (comparable to [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s Deep Learning Program) plus [forward-deployed engineers](/concepts/forward-deployed-engineering.md) stationed at client sites. His warning to mid-market consultancies: survivors will be those whose delivery infrastructure is genuinely rebuilt around AI, not decorated with it.

## Key Claims

- Most consulting AI announcements are rebranding: same org chart, same delivery motion, new deck.
- IBM embedded GPT-5.6 into IBM Consulting Advantage with no opt-in — every consultant uses it.
- IBM paired the platform with certified practitioners and forward-deployed engineers at client sites.
- The two questions firms avoid: what the delivery platform looks like with AI built in, and what labor model supports it.
- Survivors will be firms whose delivery infrastructure is rebuilt around AI, not decorated with it.

## Relevance

Informs the consulting delivery and AI-native operating model themes, especially [forward deployed engineering](/concepts/forward-deployed-engineering.md).
