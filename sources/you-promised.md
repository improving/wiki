# You Promised! — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/you-promised/you-promised.md`

## Summary

David O'Hara reflects on the implicit promises leaders make when they paint a vision. He personally avoids the word "promise," reserving it for a tiny circle, but insists on doing what he says. The tension he explores is what happens when a vision evolves—people grow, opportunities arise, understanding changes—and the expectations others attached to that vision feel broken. His answer is not to hide from it but to address it directly.

## Key Claims

- Followers treat a leader's vision as an implicit, sometimes explicit, promise
- Visions necessarily change as people grow, opportunities arise, and clarity is gained
- The response to broken implicit promises has three parts: be honest about what changed, be clear about the new direction, and be okay with some people opting out
- Keeping your word should be deliberate; "promise" should be reserved, not used casually

## Relevance

Informs leadership communication and [trust building](/concepts/trust-building.md), and pairs with [change management](/concepts/change-management.md) when direction shifts under a team that joined for the old vision.
