# The Magic of Belonging: Improving Las Vegas Retreat 2025 — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/the-magic-of-belonging-improving-las-vegas-retreat-2025/the-magic-of-belonging-improving-las-vegas-retreat-2025.md`

## Summary

Fernanda Rodriguez, a leader in the Trust & Culture workstream, recounts Improving's annual Las Vegas retreat, the first to include South America, Guatemala, and India alongside teams from Mexico, Canada, and the USA. She describes the leadership celebration, the Welcome Reception, activity options, learning and games rooms, a Women of Improving Authentic Relating workshop, and the Amazing Race finale. Her thesis is that the retreat's magic comes from the people, making Improving a place of belonging rather than just employment.

## Key Claims

- The 2025 retreat was the first to bring South America, Guatemala, and India together with teams from Mexico, Canada, and the USA.
- A Vegas Leadership Celebration recognized those "shaping the standard of excellence"; the official retreat began Thursday with welcome bags and a Blue Moon Bar reception.
- Learning & Games Rooms ran Thursday to Saturday, with AI and M&A discussions driving conversations about the future and global collaboration.
- Rodriguez co-hosted an Authentic Relating workshop for about 25 women with Bethany Hilton and Sandy Hermosillo, plus a requested dance class.
- Friday at the Hard Rock Café featured games and a "Global Delivery/New Continents" team uniting South America and Pune-India Improvers; CEO Curtis Hite spoke about upcoming company changes and new roles.
- Saturday included the Global Onboarding Strategy Champions (second year), a Come Together/Conscious Capitalism breakfast, a pool party, and The Amazing Race with about 500 Improvers.
- The closing global group photo captured colleagues from six regions who had "become friends, mentors, and even family."
