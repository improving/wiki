# Google Cloud Next ’26: Advancing Enterprise AI with Gemini, Agentic AI, and Strategic Partnerships — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/google-cloud-next-26-advancing-enterprise-ai-with-gemini-agentic-ai-and-strategic-partnerships/google-cloud-next-26-advancing-enterprise-ai-with-gemini-agentic-ai-and-strategic-partnerships.md`

## Summary

Improving's Google practice leaders, Sergey Vershinin and Ehren Seim, report that Google Cloud Next '26 signaled a pivot from AI experimentation to enterprise-grade agentic adoption at scale. The headline announcements were Gemini Enterprise and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (a rebrand of Vertex AI), with emphasis on governance, security, observability, agent identity, and auditability. The post argues the event validated market direction and positions Improving to scale its Google Cloud partnership around AI and data transformation.

## Key Claims

- Google's messaging moved from demonstrating AI capability to trust, governance, and operational readiness.
- Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform is a rebrand and evolution of Vertex AI, which Improving used across public sector, healthcare, and retail in Canada and the US.
- Keynote highlights included enterprise governance, agent identity frameworks, auditability and traceability, and integration across Google Cloud services.
- Google introduced 8th-generation TPUs, custom silicon for training and serving models.
- Public sector is framed as "enterprise with additional security and sovereignty requirements," where the platform's compliance and data sovereignty capabilities fit.
- Partners are central to Google's go-to-market: every digital transformation opportunity needs a partner attached.
- Engagements with Google and partners such as Confluent reinforced ecosystem opportunities in real-time streaming, AI analytics, and agent architectures.
- Next steps: refresh demos for Gemini Enterprise and agentic AI, advance go-to-market with Google, and convert pipeline into engagements.
