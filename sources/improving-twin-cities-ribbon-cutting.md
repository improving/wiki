# Improving Twin Cities Ribbon Cutting — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-twin-cities-ribbon-cutting/improving-twin-cities-ribbon-cutting.md`

## Summary

Improving's Minneapolis (Twin Cities) office moved to a new Bloomington space designed with an open atmosphere and a large classroom for process training and community user groups. The grand opening ribbon cutting on February 2 drew the Bloomington Chamber of Commerce, and after a foot of snow the celebration was extended to a second evening, with around 100 people attending across two days.

## Key Claims

- Minneapolis/Twin Cities office moved to a new Bloomington location.
- Space is open and includes a large classroom for process training and community user groups.
- Ribbon-cutting ceremony held February 2.
- Bloomington Chamber of Commerce members attended alongside Improvers, clients, family, and friends.
- A foot of snow prompted extending the celebration to a second evening.
- Around 100 people came through over the two days.
- Leroy Thydean was Twin Cities President.
