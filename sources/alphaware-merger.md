# Alphaware Merger — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/alphaware-merger/alphaware-merger.md`

## Summary

Improving agreed to acquire Alphaware Training Center in Houston, Texas, the third integration under its "Enterprise Model," expanding its presence in the Southwest United States. Combined revenues were expected to rise more than 20% in 2013, and Alphaware's training offerings complemented Improving's existing Training division.

## Key Claims

- Improving finalized an agreement to acquire Alphaware Training Center in Houston, Texas.
- It was the third successful integration under Improving's "Enterprise Model."
- Improving was already a four-time Inc 500/5000 recipient (2009 through 2012).
- Combined revenues were expected to increase by more than 20% in 2013.
- The deal expanded Improving's national presence in the Southwest United States.
- It brought the Houston office 100+ technology training classes, UX teams, and access to Improving's offshore alternative, Rural Sourcing.
- Peter Babirad was General Manager of Improving Houston.
