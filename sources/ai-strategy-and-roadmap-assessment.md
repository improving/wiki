# AI Strategy & Roadmap Assessment: How Enterprises Avoid 88% AI Failure — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/ai-strategy-and-roadmap-assessment/ai-strategy-and-roadmap-assessment.md`

## Summary

Shribha Kalia argues that enterprises rarely struggle to start AI pilots; they fail when those experiments must integrate into core systems and deliver measurable outcomes. Citing figures such as 88% of AI proof-of-concepts never reaching production and 95% of enterprise AI solutions failing on data issues, she frames the difference as execution discipline rather than ambition or budget. The post lays out what a structured AI strategy and roadmap assessment involves, two engagement models, readiness questions, KPIs, partner-vetting criteria, and common mistakes that waste millions.

## Key Claims

- IBM reports 42% of enterprise-scale companies already have AI in production, with another 40% piloting.
- 88% of AI proof-of-concepts never reach production, and 95% of enterprise AI solutions fail due to data issues, per MIT and IDC research.
- McKinsey research finds 77% of companies exploring AI but only 20% achieving significant ROI.
- 56% of organizations remain stuck in "pilot purgatory"; the average enterprise wastes 18–24 months on failed pilots costing $500K to over $3M each.
- Common causes: starting with technology, underestimating data quality, treating AI as an IT initiative, skipping MLOps, and hiring strategy firms that cannot build.
- Engagement models range from a 2–4 week AI/ML discovery ($25,000+) to a 4-week-per-department AI-driven role assessment.
- Success criteria span technical performance, business impact, operational feasibility, and scalability path, defined before starting.
- Best-practice targets include time-to-production of 6–9 months, payback under 12 months for operational AI, and ROI above 150% within 18–24 months.
- The post lists 29 questions to ask consultants, red flags to avoid, and 11 costly strategy mistakes.
- 2026 trends include agentic AI, EU AI Act enforcement, small/edge models, AI cost optimization, and platform engineering for AI.
