# Acquisition of The Willow Group — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/acquisition-of-the-willow-group/acquisition-of-the-willow-group.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired The Willow Group, Inc., a management and IT consulting firm headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, expected to add more than $10,000,000 in annualized revenue in 2015 and extend Improving's Midwestern reach. The deal was another execution of Improving's "Enterprise Model," and The Willow Group's financial-services and healthcare focus accelerated Improving's move toward vertical specialization.

## Key Claims

- The Willow Group, Inc., headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, was acquired by Improving.
- The Willow Group was a six-time Inc500/5000 recipient (2009–2014 Honor Roll).
- The transaction was expected to increase annualized revenue by more than $10,000,000 in 2015.
- It expanded Improving's geographic reach within the Midwestern United States.
- The Willow Group's service focus was two verticals: financial services and healthcare.
- The entire Willow Group leadership team continued operating the Minnesota business.
- Curtis Hite cited prior success in Columbus, Houston, and Calgary as evidence the model would work again.
- Leroy Thydean was President of The Willow Group.
