# Business Roles and the AI Split: What to Delegate and What to Own — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/business-roles-and-the-ai-split-what-to-delegate-and-what-to-own/business-roles-and-the-ai-split-what-to-delegate-and-what-to-own.md`

## Summary

Business roles show the judgment-bound/form-bound split most clearly because the client sees the output and can tell within minutes which layer got skipped. Liles works through business analysts, product managers, consultants, and strategists, naming which tasks belong in each category and the workflow that actually works. The pattern: interpretation, synthesis, and recommendation are human; structure, format, and documentation can be automated.

## Key Claims

- Requirements elicitation, prioritization, situation assessment, and strategic framing are judgment-bound; AI can organize notes but cannot do the interpretation.
- Story formatting, PRDs, release notes, proposal structure, and framework templates are form-bound and safe to automate.
- A PM who lets AI fill prioritization scores without interrogating each one has outsourced judgment to a formula.
- The failure mode is a finished-looking document with the form of insight but no reasoning underneath; experienced clients detect it quickly, damaging the author's credibility.
- Invest in form-bound tooling so human time concentrates on the judgment-bound work that justifies the engagement.
