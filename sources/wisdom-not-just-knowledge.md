# Wisdom Not Just Knowledge — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/wisdom-not-just-knowledge/wisdom-not-just-knowledge.md`

## Summary

A short 2013 post arguing that sites like Quora dispense "virtual wisdom" that is brain candy — enjoyable, but no substitute for a good meal. The author's complaint is that people prefer collecting knowledge and parading it around as wisdom to actually acting on it and discovering that knowledge's true nature. His conclusion is blunt: experience is the only true impartation of wisdom, and everything short of that is merely academic.

## Key Claims

- Knowledge-collection sites provide "virtual wisdom" that is entertaining but shallow.
- People tend to hoard collected knowledge and display it as wisdom rather than act on it.
- Acting on knowledge is how you discover its true nature.
- Experience is the only true impartation of wisdom.
- Everything short of experience is academic.

## Relevance

An early statement of the [experiential wisdom](/concepts/experiential-wisdom.md) theme, framing action as the only route from knowledge to wisdom via [learning by doing](/concepts/learning-by-doing.md).

## Quality

Practitioner blog post by David O'Hara. Very brief opinion piece built on an analogy (candy versus a meal); no evidence or examples.
