# The Three Eras of Agile: Constraint, Synergy, Intent — Summary

**Original source**: `references/devlinliles.com/the-three-eras-of-agile-constraint-synergy-intent/the-three-eras-of-agile-constraint-synergy-intent.md`

## Summary

Argues "Agile" has become nearly meaningless because its practices have changed across three eras, each defined by its binding constraint. Era 1 (Constraint) treats human execution capacity as scarce, so the Manifesto's "over" formulations manage scarcity. Era 2 (Synergy) sees AI remove the execution constraint, turning the trade-offs into multiplication. Era 3 (Intent) puts AI in execution and the human in direction, making intent the exponent on the whole equation. A diagnostic — what is the binding constraint on your team's ability to deliver value? — locates a team in the right era.

## Key Claims

- Constraint Era: the binding resource is human attention, and practices like time-boxing and minimal documentation are scarcity management.
- Synergy Era: AI drafts documentation, tests, and infrastructure, so "working software over documentation" becomes working software times documentation.
- Intent Era formula: `(Individuals & Interactions × Processes & Tools)^INTENT = Exponential Productivity`, where small improvements in intent change everything underneath.
- Brooks's essential vs. accidental complexity: AI strips accidental complexity at scale, leaving the essential specification work as hard as ever — which is why intent sits in the exponent.
- Era 3 is a trajectory claim anchored to Stage 7 and 8 deployments, and deserves looser confidence than the two observable eras.
