# My 18-Month Journey to PST Certification — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/my-18-month-journey-to-pst-certification/my-18-month-journey-to-pst-certification.md`

## Summary

Lisa Lueck describes an 18-month path to Scrum.org's Professional Scrum Trainer certification, pursued amid home renovations, a demanding career, and being among the few women in that role. She credits time management, determination, and support from family, colleagues, and mentors, framing the achievement as both a personal triumph and a statement for women in tech.

## Key Claims

- The journey to PST certification unfolded alongside Scrum.org changes to the PST path, self-managed home renovations, and a full career.
- Effective time management and an unwavering support network were central to navigating professional and personal responsibilities.
- The assessments were published as some of the most difficult in Agile, and the PSM III took two attempts to pass.
- Lueck notes the rarity of women pursuing this certification and treats each assessment as a demonstration of women's capability in challenging fields.
- She advises aspiring women professionals to embrace challenges, lean on their network, and let determination redefine expectations.
- Certification here is framed as a declaration that barriers are meant to be shattered, not just a measure of knowledge.
