# Improving Gives Back to Employees with Tax Savings — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-gives-back-to-employees-with-tax-savings/improving-gives-back-to-employees-with-tax-savings.md`

## Summary

After changes to national tax legislation, Improving shared the savings with employees as one-time bonuses of up to $1,000 for many US-based employees, starting with offices that had demonstrated significant, sustained profitability. The post ties this to Improving's "Involvement" core value and CEO Curtis Hite's belief that tax savings should be shared with the company.

## Key Claims

- Improving gave a one-time bonus of up to $1,000 to many US-based employees, funded by savings from the national tax legislation changes.
- Offices with significant and sustained years of profitability were included first, with plans to share similar bonuses with all offices as local benefits continue.
- The company frames the bonus as appreciation for the "Involvement" core value and for employees going above and beyond.
- CEO Curtis Hite said the savings were an opportunity to celebrate the company's collective success and involvement.
- Beyond bonuses, Improving planned to create more jobs at its Dallas headquarters, growing to 165 total employees.
- "Our success is a consequence of our collective involvement," per Hite.
