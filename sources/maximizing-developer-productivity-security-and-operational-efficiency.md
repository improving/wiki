# Maximizing Developer Productivity, Security and Operational Efficiency with Platform Engineering — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/maximizing-developer-productivity-security-and-operational-efficiency/maximizing-developer-productivity-security-and-operational-efficiency.md`

## Summary

This post positions platform engineering as the next evolution beyond DevOps and SRE, delivering internal developer platforms (IDPs) that give teams self-service, standardized environments with built-in automation, security, and compliance. It supports the case with a stack of industry statistics on the payoff.

## Key Claims

- Platform engineers build IDPs that abstract infrastructure, aligning with infrastructure as code, so developers can focus on coding and innovation.
- Gartner predicts 80% of enterprises will adopt platform engineering by 2025 to accelerate delivery.
- Puppet's State of DevOps Report: IDP users see 20-25% faster time to market and up to 75% fewer infrastructure-misconfiguration incidents.
- Humanitec (2023): 51% of CTOs are investing in platform engineering to combat rising development and operational costs.
- Cited gains: Forrester's 45% fewer manual-infrastructure incidents; Red Hat's 47% higher developer satisfaction and 62% higher productivity; Gartner's 35% less rework; an average ~50% shorter SDLC; 40% fewer security incidents.
- Cost and retention: IDC (2023) reports 25-30% cloud cost savings, and Puppet reports 15-20% lower developer turnover.
- Platform engineering supports modernizing legacy systems into microservices, containers, and serverless.
