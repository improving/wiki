# Sharing Content — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/sharing-content/sharing-content.md`

## Summary

A brief link-sharing note in which O'Hara recommends a video from an author whose books he enjoys and an excerpt from Peter Thiel's talk for *The New Criterion*. The video prompts him to reflect that we have discounted too much the impact of the past few years and current cultural focuses on the younger generation, and he asks what he can do, given his abilities and opportunities, to help navigate those difficult conversations. The Thiel excerpt has him pondering what diversity looks like in its healthiest form.

## Key Claims

- A video by an author he admires resonated and prompted journaling.

- He asks how he can help navigate these challenges given his own abilities and opportunities.
- Peter Thiel's *New Criterion* talk excerpt raises the question of what healthy diversity looks like.
