# Improving Announces Acquisition of Imaginet — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-imaginet/improving-acquires-imaginet.md`

## Summary

Kristin Johnson reports Improving's acquisition of Imaginet, a Microsoft consulting and implementation firm with offices in Winnipeg, Canada and Dallas, Texas. The deal is expected to push Improving's annualized revenue past $280 million and add a Winnipeg enterprise plus a stronger Dallas presence, while Imaginet's leadership stays in place under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy." Imaginet brings more than 25 years of Microsoft 365, application development, and data analytics experience.

## Key Claims

- Imaginet specializes in Microsoft consulting with offices in Winnipeg, Canada and Dallas, Texas.
- The transaction is expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue beyond $280 million.
- Imaginet leadership will remain unchanged and continue operating the business.
- Imaginet is a 20-time Microsoft Gold Partner and one of Canada's earliest Microsoft partners.
- The acquisition establishes an Improving enterprise in Winnipeg and strengthens its Dallas presence.
