# What is Application Modernization and Why Does Your Business Need It? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/what-is-application-modernization-and-why-does-your-business-need-it/what-is-application-modernization-and-why-does-your-business-need-it.md`

## Summary

Maryna Boryshkevych opens the modernization series by listing the symptoms of a legacy application — hard to maintain, hard to integrate, insecure, costly, unautomated — and defining modern applications by their use of modern languages, resilient modular architecture, streaming data, NoSQL, containers, microservices, and cloud with CI/CD. She argues modernization means enhancing existing systems rather than discarding them, and enumerates benefits including cost reduction, efficiency, customer experience, faster time-to-market, security, scalability, and compliance. Challenges like outdated code, data migration, and cultural resistance are met with an incremental, collaborative, automated approach.

## Key Claims

- Legacy applications are outdated but still in use, expensive to maintain, and often insecure.
- Modern applications use resilient modular designs, streaming data, containers, microservices, and cloud.
- Modernization enhances existing systems rather than replacing them outright.
- Benefits include lower cost, better efficiency, customer experience, time-to-market, security, and compliance.
- Common challenges: outdated codebases, data migration, cultural resistance, and integration complexity.
- Best practices: an incremental approach, collaboration, automation, and continuous improvement.
