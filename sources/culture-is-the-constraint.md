# Culture Is the Constraint — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/culture-is-the-constraint/culture-is-the-constraint.md`

## Summary

Drawing on new McKinsey survey data from 10,000 executives, O'Hara argues that culture, not technical readiness, is the constraint on AI value. Most organizations are deploying AI yet see no bottom-line impact, and the strongest predictor of success is whether senior leaders visibly change how they work. Pilots that succeed technically still stall when the surrounding culture does not believe the change is real.

## Key Claims

- 88% of organizations are deploying AI, but 81% report no bottom-line impact.
- Culture predicts success more than any technical readiness metric, by nearly a factor of two.
- Only 14% of respondents say their senior leaders consistently champion adoption — among organizations already deploying.
- The 19% seeing real impact are those whose leaders changed meetings, metrics, and decisions, not those who made announcements.
- AI adoption correlates with managers' own use; culture follows behavior, not comms plans.
