# Board Members — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/board-members/board-members.md`

## Summary

Newsroom announcement that Improving appointed Vasu Sarangapani and Raghu Chandra to its Board of Directors. The post frames the additions around Improving's 10-Year Vision to become the "premier challenger brand" in digital services, tying them to the company's expansion, Conscious Capitalism, and Best Place to Work positioning.

## Key Claims

- Improving appointed Vasu Sarangapani and Raghu Chandra to its Board of Directors.
- Vasu Sarangapani is a growth strategist with 30+ years of experience; as Chief Growth and Sales Officer at GlobalLogic he led 28 consecutive quarters of expansion, growing revenue from $250M to over $900M, and later unified three digital engineering firms as President & CEO of Relevance Lab.
- Raghu Chandra has 30+ years across product companies and global system integrators; he built global flight scheduling systems, led large-scale electronic health record platforms, and launched public cloud transformation businesses at Cognizant and Capgemini.
- CEO Curtis Hite ties the appointments to the 10-Year Vision and to delivering stakeholder value through trust, innovation, and excellence.
- The appointments come as Improving expands its global footprint and promotes initiatives like CodeLaunch.
- Improving has partnered with Trinity Hunt Partners since 2019 and operates 20 offices across North and South America and India.

## Relevance

A governance and growth story under [conscious capitalism](/concepts/conscious-capitalism.md), tied to Improving's [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) and [stakeholder orientation](/concepts/stakeholder-orientation.md).
