# Improving Acquires Jump Analytics — Summary

**Original source**: `references/improving.com/improving-acquires-jump-analytics/improving-acquires-jump-analytics.md`

## Summary

Improving acquired Jump Analytics, a Canadian technology firm specializing in advanced analytics and corporate performance management. The deal is expected to push Improving's annualized revenue above $290 million and extend its North American reach; Jump Analytics' leadership stays intact under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Key Claims

- Jump Analytics specializes in Microsoft Data Analytics and SAP Analytics, with offices in Toronto, Vancouver, and Calgary.
- The acquisition is expected to raise Improving's annualized revenue above $290 million.
- Jump Analytics' leadership team remains intact and continues to operate and grow the business.
- Jump Analytics has delivered solutions in over 20 industries, including utilities, mining, oil and gas, manufacturing, retail, and life sciences.
- CEO Curtis Hite frames the move as expanding capability and customer service.
- The deal follows Improving's "Enterprise Strategy" of merging like-minded technology services firms committed to trust, excellence, and culture.
