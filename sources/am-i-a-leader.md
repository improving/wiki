# Am I a Leader? — Summary

**Original source**: `references/driventodevelop.com/am-i-a-leader/am-i-a-leader.md`

## Summary

O'Hara addresses the common realization that people are following you before you ever thought of yourself as a leader. He argues leadership is widely misunderstood—what it means, how it happens, and why people do it. We are leaders not because there are followers, but because we are in pursuit of something greater and helping others aim at it. A leader holds a vision of something more than the present, can paint that picture, and can align perspectives so everyone travels the same direction—while being willing to bear the weight, cost, and sometimes ridicule of giving that image life.

## Key Claims

- People often discover they are leading only after others start following.
- Leadership comes from pursuing something greater and helping others aim at it, not from followers or a title.
- A leader holds a vision—an image of something more than what is right now.
- The leader's work is to paint that picture and align perspectives toward a shared direction.
- Leaders must be willing to bear the weight, cost, and sometimes ridicule of pursuing that image.

## Relevance

Informs leadership themes: vision, alignment, and the difference between title and influence.
