# FHIR

FHIR (Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources) is the healthcare data-exchange standard that defines how clinical and administrative data moves between systems. In this corpus it appears as the technical requirement behind US payer-interoperability rules — the standard health plans must build toward, not an optional integration.

## Where It Appears

- [CMS](/entities/cms.md) interoperability rules for 2026 require **80% of prior-authorization decisions to be made in real time using FHIR**, with denial reasons made public.
- Building to FHIR is part of the payer data work that accompanies [prior authorization](/concepts/prior-authorization.md) automation.
- FHIR is one piece of the broader healthcare [data integration](/concepts/data-integration.md) and governance challenge facing insurers under federal mandates.

Supporting evidence for this page is thin — FHIR recurs in only one summary in this corpus so far, and is likely to gain more sources as healthcare-AI coverage grows.
