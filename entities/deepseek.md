# DeepSeek

An open-weight model vendor that appears in the corpus twice: as the supplier of the reasoning-trace models behind a dual-thread agent design, and as the case that punctured the assumption of permanently falling token prices.

## Overview

On August 6, 2026 DeepSeek warned developers that API prices would rise significantly, ending roughly eighteen months of declining open-weight rates. The corpus reads that decline as a [model routing](/concepts/model-routing.md) subsidy that bought default-routing share until serving volume outran the price — a reminder that a vendor's rate is not a long-term constant.

## Key Claims

- Eighteen months of falling open-weight prices looked like physics but was a subsidy.
- Cheap tokens bought default-routing share until serving volume outran the price.
- Pricing a vendor's rate as a long-term constant is an unstated bet on their economics.
- The dual-thread agent design that held scores under time pressure worked only on open-weight models exposing reasoning traces.
