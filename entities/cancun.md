# Cancún

Cancún is the Mexican resort destination of [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s [Improving 100](/entities/improving-100.md) recognition trip, an all-expenses-paid getaway for top performers and their chosen guests. In this corpus the trip is the concrete form of Improving's [employee recognition](/concepts/employee-recognition.md) program and a deliberate builder of cross-border [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md).

## The Recognition Trip

- [Improving 100](/entities/improving-100.md) selects top performers across 22+ offices in four categories: Community Impact, Business Growth, Delivery Excellence, and Technical Innovation; peers nominate and leadership makes the final selection after due diligence.
- One honoree's 2023 award came with a four-day trip to Secrets: The Vine, attended by 62 honorees and their guests, with excursions including a catamaran adventure, a Mexican cooking class, an ATV ride, and a cenote/adventure park.
- The 2024 winners and their guests were invited to an all-expenses-paid weekend at Maroma Beach Resort from June 27–29.
- [Atulpriya Sharma](/entities/atulpriya-sharma.md) was selected in the Community Impact category — the first winner from Improving India — joined by fellow India winners; he stopped in New York to facilitate a platform engineering round table at Kubernetes Community Days en route.

## Why It Matters

- Welcome receptions were hosted by US Managing Director [Scott McMichael](/entities/scott-mcmichael.md) and CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md), with excursions, VIP dinners in small groups, and a closing award night.
- Because Improving India works remotely, the gathering was the first time many colleagues met in person and the first time many met Improvers from other countries.
- The recurring argument across all three accounts is that recognition which creates memories and connections leaves a more lasting impact than certificates alone.
