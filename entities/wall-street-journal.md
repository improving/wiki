# Wall Street Journal

The Wall Street Journal appears in the corpus as an outside authority the author reaches for when a claim needs grounding beyond personal experience — once for a sleep statistic, later for the enterprise pattern of AI bills that don't match business cases.

## Key Claims

- A WSJ article states that only about 1–3% of people can survive on five hours of sleep or less, used to argue most readers are not among them.
- The sleep post cites that figure to justify treating his own chronic short sleep as a problem rather than a trait.
- A WSJ report on corporate AI costs confirms the inside view that companies which said yes to everything AI now receive invoices that don't match their business cases.
- That reporting is the named evidence behind the Uber budget-burn and Microsoft license-cancellation cases.
