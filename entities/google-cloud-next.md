# Google Cloud Next

Google Cloud Next is [Google Cloud](/entities/google-cloud.md)'s annual customer and partner conference, where the platform unveils its AI, data, and infrastructure roadmap. Improving attends as a Google Partner and publishes year-by-year recaps, making the event a recurring lens on where enterprise AI is heading.

## Editions Covered Here

- **Next '24** ran April 9–11, 2024 in Las Vegas. Recaps highlighted JAX for accelerator computation, multimodal embeddings across databases, the Gemini 1.5 preview with up to 1M input tokens, and GCP Workflows connectors that call Generative AI APIs directly.
- **Next '24** also introduced six kinds of AI agents, the Gemini for Google Cloud suite (Gemini Code Assist and Cloud Assist), AI-powered Workspace, and infrastructure from TPU v5p to the AI Hypercomputer and Distributed Cloud.
- **Next '26** was reported as a pivot from AI experimentation to governed, agentic enterprise deployment: the headlines were Gemini Enterprise and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (a rebrand of [Vertex AI](/entities/vertex-ai.md)), plus 8th-generation TPUs.

## Themes

- The '26 edition foregrounded governance, security, observability, [agent identity](/concepts/agent-identity.md), and auditability — the move from demos to production.
- Public sector was framed as "enterprise with additional security and sovereignty requirements."
- Partners are central to Google's go-to-market; Improving's 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year award for Canada coincided with Next '23.
