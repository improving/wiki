# ServiceNow

ServiceNow appears in this corpus twice, from opposite directions: as half of the Accenture-ServiceNow partnership that put forward deployed engineering on the agenda, and as an example of an enterprise system opening itself to AI agents before the access controls caught up.

## Key Claims

- The Accenture-ServiceNow partnership names forward deployed engineering, a methodology consultancies must claim and sell rather than treat as a side effect ([forward deployed model](/sources/the-forward-deployed-model-changes-everything.md)).
- Forward deployment puts a team inside the client environment before enterprise rollout is approved, and winning firms ship something in production in a quarter, not a year ([forward deployed model](/sources/the-forward-deployed-model-changes-everything.md)).
- Expertise-deck pitches are losing ground to demonstrated delivery, and Improving already works this way but never packaged or priced it as the offering ([forward deployed model](/sources/the-forward-deployed-model-changes-everything.md)).
- ServiceNow opening its system to agents via MCP widens the attack surface ([agents have keys](/sources/your-agents-have-keys-nobody-changed-the-locks.md)).
- 92% of security leaders lack visibility into agent access and 86% enforce no policies; agents get no onboarding checklist, role-based access, or offboarding ([agents have keys](/sources/your-agents-have-keys-nobody-changed-the-locks.md)).
- The remedy is least privilege, regular audits, and revoke-on-change applied to agents as to employees ([agents have keys](/sources/your-agents-have-keys-nobody-changed-the-locks.md)).
