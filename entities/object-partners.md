# Object Partners

Object Partners is a custom software development firm based in Minneapolis that [Improving](/entities/improving.md) acquired in 2020, merging it into Improving – Minneapolis to serve the Twin Cities region. The deal deepened Improving's Midwest reach and its healthcare vertical.

## Company

- Headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota, with a second office in Omaha, Nebraska.
- Merged into Improving – Minneapolis after the acquisition.
- Leadership stayed intact and continued to operate the business while gaining access to Improving's broader services.
- President and CEO Chris Spurgat framed the fit around becoming a trusted partner to all customers.

## Deal

- The transaction was expected to grow Improving's annualized revenue by more than 25% in 2020, exceeding $150 million collectively.
- CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) cited Object Partners' custom software expertise and extensive healthcare experience as strengthening Improving's North American healthcare vertical.
- Continued Improving's "Enterprise Strategy" of merging service firms that share commitments to trust, excellence, and culture.
