# Google Cloud

Google's cloud platform and one of Improving's central delivery and partner ecosystems. This corpus follows Google Cloud through its annual [Google Cloud Next](/entities/google-cloud-next.md) conferences, a run of public-sector and healthcare case studies, and a data-engineering pipeline, showing a platform whose messaging shifted from demonstrating AI capability to governance, agent identity, and enterprise readiness.

## Platform and Partnership

- Improving's Google practice delivered a federal health agency's automated medical device reporting solution and a defense research agency's self-service HPC portal.
- YoppWorks, an Improving company, won the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada, recognized for HPC/AI/ML access, Document AI workflows, and cloud landing zones.
- Partners are central to Google's go-to-market; at Next '26 Google emphasized that every digital transformation opportunity needs a partner attached.

## Capability Arc

- Next '24 highlighted JAX, multimodal embeddings available across databases, Gemini 1.5 with up to 1M input tokens, and GCP Workflows connectors that call Generative AI APIs directly.
- Next '24 also announced six kinds of AI agents and Gemini for Google Cloud (Code Assist, Cloud Assist), with Gemini infused into Bigtable, Spanner, Firestore, AlloyDB, BigQuery, and Looker.
- Next '26 pivoted to enterprise-grade agentic adoption, headlining Gemini Enterprise and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (a rebrand of [Vertex AI](/entities/vertex-ai.md)) with governance, agent identity, and auditability.
- Google introduced 8th-generation TPUs and frames public sector as "enterprise with additional security and sovereignty requirements."

## Data and Analytics

- [BigQuery](/entities/bigquery.md) is the analytics layer for GCP workloads, including a 10TB/day serverless ETL pipeline that lands data with roughly 600ms end-to-end latency.
- Vertex AI, Jupyter, Gemini, and Terraform underpin the Research Accelerator Portal's elastic, governed research compute.
