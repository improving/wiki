# Stephen M.R. Covey

Stephen M.R. Covey is the author of *The Speed of Trust*, whose thirteen "trust behaviors" are the framework behind a long-running Improving essay series. Different writers each take one behavior — extending trust, creating transparency, keeping commitments, listening first, delivering results — and translate it into workplace and consulting practice.

## The Framework in This Corpus

- Trust is treated as learnable behavior rather than a fixed trait: specific actions build or tax credibility.
- **Extend Trust** — a trusted person becomes a trusted leader by giving others the opportunity, support, and honest feedback to earn trust in turn.
- **Create Transparency** — tell the truth in ways people can verify; raise blockers early rather than at the deadline; own failures quickly, within real confidentiality boundaries.
- **Keep Commitments** — Covey's "Big Kahuna"; if you have any doubt you can keep a promise, don't make it, because a broken commitment does lasting damage.
- **Listen First** — a deliberate practice of seeking understanding while withholding judgment, which supports the other behaviors.
- **Deliver Results** — consistency over years, not a single peak, is what builds career capital.
