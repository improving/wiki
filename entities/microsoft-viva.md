# Microsoft Viva

Microsoft Viva is Microsoft's employee-experience suite, and Viva Engage — the successor to Yammer — is its social layer for community building, knowledge sharing, and culture. Improving's writing positions it as the complement to [Microsoft Teams](/entities/microsoft-teams.md): Teams handles work, while Viva Engage rebuilds the spontaneous connection that remote and hybrid work erodes.

## Key Claims

- Viva Engage evolved from Yammer, which Microsoft acquired in 2012, and was refined within the Viva suite after the pandemic exposed the need for robust digital platforms.
- It supports discussions, questions, praise, polls, and topics in a social-media-like feed, and builds social capital by connecting people across departments.
- Teams focuses on task management, file sharing, and real-time communication; Viva Engage focuses on community and culture, and reaches employees not tied to specific projects.
- Claimed benefits include stronger culture, higher engagement, better knowledge sharing and decisions, and improved satisfaction and retention.
- Community success requires a clear purpose, adoption goals, engagement strategy, content planning, and active community managers to prevent abandonment.
- [Microsoft Copilot](/entities/microsoft-copilot.md) provides AI support for content creation.
