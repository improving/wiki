# Outlive

*Outlive* is Dr. Peter Attia's book on longevity and healthspan, and it is the stated trigger for the rapamycin experiment documented in this corpus. It matters here as the bridge between the longevity literature and a personal, self-tested protocol.

## Key Claims

- Dr. Peter Attia's *Outlive* prompted the rapamycin experiment ([healthspan and rapamycin](/sources/health-journey-healthspan.md)).
- Rapamycin inhibits mTOR, mimicking caloric restriction and triggering autophagy ([healthspan and rapamycin](/sources/health-journey-healthspan.md)).
- Weekly dosing on rest days avoids interfering with muscle building ([healthspan and rapamycin](/sources/health-journey-healthspan.md)).
- The later update argues biology is too individual for one-size-fits-all health advice, and that one-size-fits-all diets and superfoods are usually selling something ([health journey update](/sources/health-journey-update.md)).
- Data should guide health decisions over subjective feeling, using Function Health six-month baselines plus intermediate testing ([health journey update](/sources/health-journey-update.md)).
- A Levels CGM showed different glucose responses to identical meals, evidence for the individual-protocol stance ([health journey update](/sources/health-journey-update.md)).
