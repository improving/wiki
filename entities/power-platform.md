# Microsoft Power Platform

Microsoft Power Platform is Microsoft's low-code suite — [Power Apps](/entities/power-apps.md), [Power Automate](/entities/power-automate.md), [Power BI](/entities/power-bi.md), and [Power Pages](/entities/power-pages.md), with Copilot Studio for AI copilots. It shifts application work from IT to citizen developers while keeping authentication and administration on Microsoft 365.

## Key Claims

- The suite's logic uses an Excel-familiar formula language, with hundreds of connectors to Excel, SharePoint, SQL, and third-party services like Workday and ServiceNow.
- Power Platform powers real Improving deliveries: a mine "Shutdown App" for maintenance planning, an ESG reporting front end, and a contract lifecycle management system.
- Copilot Studio, built on Power Virtual Agents, lets power users build AI copilots over corporate knowledge and deploy them where employees already work, such as Teams and SharePoint.
- It also served as the Canvas Apps front end for a secure internal RAG assistant built on Azure AI for a global paint brand.
- Microsoft Planner is built on the Power Platform, integrating with Teams and Power BI.
- Weak spots surfaced in the writing include light source control and the continued need for developer-minded resources.
