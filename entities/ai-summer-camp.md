# AI Summer Camp

AI Summer Camp is a three-part [Improving](/entities/improving.md) series at its [Houston](/entities/houston.md) office that taught high school students and their parents to understand and use AI responsibly. Written up by [Claudio Lassala](/entities/claudio-lassala.md), it is an applied case of [AI literacy](/concepts/ai-literacy.md) and [community giving](/concepts/community-giving.md).

## Format

- Three Saturday sessions, each building on the last: Wake-Up Mode, Power-Up Mode, and Genius Mode.
- Wake-Up Mode taught students what AI is and isn't, where it hallucinates, and why not to let it think for them.
- Power-Up Mode used AI as a thought partner on real tasks — essays, slides, and projects — rather than a replacement.
- Genius Mode addressed common struggles like "I don't get this," leaving students with study strategies.
- Parents attended separate sessions on the ethics, practicalities, and motivation of AI.

## Approach

- Sessions were designed collaboratively by Improvers, with activities built for both extroverts and introverts.
- Discussions covered learning styles, motivation, and ethics.
- Improving framed the camp around its values of excellence, involvement, and community impact, positioning AI as [augmentation](/concepts/ai-augmentation.md) rather than replacement.
