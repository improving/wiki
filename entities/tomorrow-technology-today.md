# Tomorrow Technology. Today

Tomorrow Technology. Today is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s webinar series and Improving Talks program, where consultants and industry experts present on enterprise technology trends. Its sessions are widely adapted into written recaps across AI, data, platform engineering, and cloud architecture, making it a recurring front door for the firm's thought leadership.

## Recurring Themes

- **AI and data strategy** — AI roadmaps, maturity stages, and use-case selection, plus repairing data foundations before scaling; AI treated as software needing ongoing upkeep rather than a passing trend.
- **Real-time data** — event-driven architecture, Apache Kafka and Confluent, with governance, observability, and developer experience as four pillars of a real-time platform.
- **Data platforms** — [Microsoft Fabric](/entities/microsoft-fabric.md), OneLake, the [lakehouse](/concepts/lakehouse.md), and consolidating fragmented analytics stacks.
- **Platform engineering** — self-service platforms resting on [CI/CD](/concepts/ci-cd.md), [infrastructure as code](/concepts/infrastructure-as-code.md), and governance.
- **Architecture** — [hybrid cloud](/concepts/hybrid-cloud.md) and [multi-cloud](/concepts/multi-cloud.md) trade-offs of resilience, lock-in, regulation, and cost.

## Format

- Sessions are presented by Improving consultants and later published as recap posts, often authored by the presenter.
- The series is promoted as "Improving Talks," with a dozen or more sessions covering AI, data ecosystems, and platform engineering.
