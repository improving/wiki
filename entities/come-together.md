# Come Together

Come Together is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s wellbeing and community initiative, launched to keep Improvers connected as work shifted to remote and hybrid settings. It assigns a monthly theme and a slate of activities spanning every enterprise, from company-wide virtual talks to local in-person events.

## How It Runs

- Each month gets a **theme**. January 2022's was wellbeing: company-wide virtual talks on mental health, finances, and life balance (delivered in English and Spanish) plus a distinct event at each enterprise office.
- Local events have ranged from Dallas restorative yoga and sound baths to Guadalajara Smoothie Days, Chicago mindfulness, and a Calgary emotional-intelligence workshop.
- June focused on **Inclusion and Diversity**, tied to Pride month and to LGBTQ+ visibility at work.
- In 2025 the program opened with Health & Fitness Month and the Power Pair Challenge, kept virtual talks drawing hundreds of attendees, and ran a **Generosity Advocate Pitch Competition** that directed donations to charities worldwide.

## Reach and Purpose

- **1,356 Improvers participated at least once in 2023** across 19 global offices.
- Cross-cultural monthly themes (such as Argentina and Chile) reflect Improving's expanding global footprint.
- Come Together is one of three internal programs — with [PATH](/entities/path-program.md) and [ImprovingU](/entities/improvingu.md) — that Improving credits for connection, engagement, and retention across geographies.
- The stated aim is a workplace where everyone feels included, connected, and able to learn from each other.
