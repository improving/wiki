# Sarthak Routh

- Wrote the Data Streaming World Tour recap and the [Medtronic](/entities/medtronic.md) streaming case.
- Authored an ETL modernization piece on migrating from Informatica to [Confluent](/entities/confluent.md) and [Databricks](/entities/databricks.md).
