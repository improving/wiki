# Ottawa

Ottawa is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s Canadian public-sector hub, the home of the acquired consultancy [YoppWorks](/entities/yoppworks.md) and the base for the firm's award-winning government work on Google Cloud. It appears in this corpus as the delivery center for federal and provincial clients.

## Improving's Presence

- YoppWorks, headquartered in Ottawa and founded in 2016, specialized in Scala, Akka open source, cloud-native technologies, and Reactive Architecture before Improving acquired it.
- Improving Ottawa has worked with Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings' innovation team on a modern reservation system since 2019, involving more than 40 developers, coaches, and managed-services consultants.
- Managing Director Jack Gulas called being named a key partner a significant milestone.

## Public-Sector Work

- YoppWorks was named the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada.
- Clients included a Canadian federal research organization, a Government of Canada department, and the governments of Ontario, Quebec, and New Brunswick.
- The team built a scientific computing portal for on-demand HPC, AI, and ML provisioning and applied Document AI to a large federal department's backlog and ATIP workflows.
- Lessons learned: prioritize security and compliance, document for non-technical stakeholders, and invest in understanding client organizational context.
