# Shelby Sansone Stephens

Shelby Sansone Stephens is one of [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s most prolific writers, authoring culture, inclusion, data, and company-news pieces. Her work ranges from International Women's Day profiles and community stories to data-strategy explainers and award announcements.

## Work

- Authored the [Women of Improving](/entities/women-of-improving.md) feature and the 2022, 2023, and 2025 International Women's Day Q&As.
- Wrote the [CodeCup](/entities/code-cup.md) community story from [Guadalajara](/entities/guadalajara.md) and the Come Together year-in-review.
- Produced nearshore thought leadership such as "Beyond Cost Savings: How Mexico Talent Drives Innovation."
- Authored the "Powering Modern Enterprises" series on data, modern applications, and building the platform first.
- Covered internal leadership promotions, Inc. 5000 appearances, and the EY Entrepreneur Of The Year finalist news for [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md).

## Themes

- Recurring subjects: [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md), [inclusion and diversity](/concepts/inclusion-and-diversity.md), [employee engagement](/concepts/employee-engagement.md), and [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md).
- Her data pieces argue for [business intelligence](/concepts/business-intelligence.md) and data-driven decision-making in broad, accessible terms.
