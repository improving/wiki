# David Belcher

An Improving UX leader and writer who treats design as a strategic capability rather than a production service. His posts argue that design's value shows up in changed behavior and business results, and that maturity grows through leadership commitment, collaboration, and evidence.

## Topics and Work

- Argues [outcomes over outputs](/concepts/outcomes-over-outputs.md): design teams are too often celebrated for artifacts (wireframes, prototypes, design systems) rather than the change they enable, illustrated with a cart-abandonment example targeting a 20% reduction.
- Defines design maturity as how deeply design is embedded in culture and decisions, citing the Nielsen Norman Group's six-stage [UX maturity](/concepts/ux-maturity.md) model from "Absent" to "Integrated and Optimizing."
- Documents facilitating a Service Design Blueprint during [PI planning](/concepts/pi-planning.md) to map a full install workflow, surfacing gaps and cross-system dependencies the team had not seen.
- Reframes AI's design value as learning faster — accelerating research synthesis, widening ideation, and enabling early prototyping — while conversations with users and the judgment calls stay human.
- Lays out a five-phase project-kickoff recipe (discovery, scope, strategy, collaboration, deliverables), citing a redesign that lifted user interaction 30% after goals were tied to engagement metrics.
