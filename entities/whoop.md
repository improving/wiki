# WHOOP

A screenless wearable strap that measures [heart rate variability](/concepts/heart-rate-variability.md), strain, and sleep to produce a daily recovery score. Across this corpus it is the foundation of a personal [health journey](/sources/health-journey.md), the instrument that turns vague wellness intentions into measurable daily feedback.

## Overview

WHOOP has anchored the author's health efforts since May 2019, worn continuously so monitoring is nearly uninterrupted. Its battery-pack charging lets the strap stay on during recharge, supporting near-100% uptime for [sleep tracking](/concepts/sleep-tracking.md). Competition with siblings over strain scores is credited with driving engagement.

## Key Claims

- Continuous wear enables nearly continuous monitoring of strain and recovery.
- [Heart rate variability](/concepts/heart-rate-variability.md) is the key signal for measuring strain and recovery.
- A 2019 trial of the device followed failed attempts at polyphasic sleep, medications, and a 73-hour wake reset — the hope was more data, not a magic device.
- Thirty minutes of pre-bed reading measurably raised HRV and recovery.
- Recovery is not the same thing as sleep; the two move independently.
