# Frisco, Texas

Frisco is a city in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex that shows up in this corpus in two very different roles: as a proving ground for new consumer technology, and as the everyday setting for a personal fitness experiment.

## Overview

Frisco's appearance as a drone-delivery testbed connects it to the wider [Regional AI Economy](/concepts/regional-ai-economy.md) story, in which DFW is described as a place where new enterprise and consumer technology gets built and tried. The fitness camp post uses the same city as an ordinary backdrop for a lesson about habit and community.

## Key Claims

- Frisco serves as a testbed for new delivery technology ([Drone Delivery](/sources/drone-delivery.md)).
- In that test, Wing's autonomous delivery landed a package on a residential balcony; the trip took longer than estimated but the experience was impressive.
- A group fitness camp in the area showed that the people and banter, more than the workout, keep members returning ([Dragging me to a camp](/sources/dragging-me-to-a-workout.md)).
- That post argues comfortable routine can be mistaken for growth, and that mixing up workouts benefits the body and breaks stale patterns — a [comfort zone](/concepts/comfort-zone.md) argument.
