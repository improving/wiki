# HIPAA

HIPAA is the US law governing the privacy and security of protected health information. In this corpus it is not a paper compliance checkbox but an architectural driver: it shapes data-platform design, agent governance, and how organizations vet technology partners.

## In Data Platforms and Governance

- A regional health plan enforced column-level access control and automated lineage on every dataset feeding a production agent, producing HIPAA audit entries as a byproduct of access.
- At BCBSM, Unity Catalog governed assets across eleven domains and 88-plus suppliers with column-level PHI/PII control and HIPAA-satisfying audit logs, across six security approvals.
- HIPAA is one of the regimes a continuously-enforced responsible-AI program must satisfy, alongside GDPR and the EU AI Act.

## In Healthcare AI and Partnering

- Payer-AI recommendations include zero-trust architecture, micro-segmentation, and HIPAA-compliant AWS built in from day one.
- HIPAA appears among the compliance criteria in partner-vetting and outsourcing selection (with SOC 2, ISO 27001, and GDPR).
- Improving cites HIPAA, PCI, and SOC 2 as domain-specific compliance it brings to its Cognition partnership.
