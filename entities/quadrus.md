# Quadrus Development

Quadrus Development Inc. is a Calgary, Alberta IT services and solutions firm whose acquisition was [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s first expansion beyond the United States and into Canada.

## Company

- Headquartered in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.
- Had a 20-year track record with services aligned on Agile, Microsoft (.NET), and Java application development.
- A five-time Inc500/5000 recipient (2009–2013 Honor Roll).
- Brought development tools including the Quadrus Estimator Suite; Brian Donaldson was co-founder and Chief Technical Director.

## Deal

- Expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue by more than 20%.
- Expanded Improving's reach beyond the United States and into [Canada](/entities/canada.md).
- Strengthened consulting, business intelligence and data services, and training offerings across local and international markets.
