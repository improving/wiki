# Costa Rica

Costa Rica is a Latin American [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md) destination that Improving added to its global delivery network through an M&A transaction with Oceans Code Experts, a firm it already had a trusted client relationship with. It appears in this corpus as part of Improving's continued investment in [Latin America](/entities/latin-america.md) as a growth region.

## Improving's Presence

- The acquired Oceans Code Experts team became a nearshore hub delivering application, data, and AI-driven solutions alongside onshore and global teams.
- CEO Curtis Hite framed the team's expertise as complementing Improving's focus on practical solutions and creative execution.
- Keylor Arroyo Villegas, president of the new Costa Rican organization, said joining Improving extends the team's impact beyond what it could do alone.
- The move continues Improving's [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) and extends its [IT outsourcing](/concepts/it-outsourcing.md) delivery model.

## Why Costa Rica

- Described as one of the premier technology hubs in the Americas, with strong STEM education and high English proficiency.
- Ranked among high-English nearshore destinations in Improving's buyer guides, alongside Mexico, Guatemala, Argentina, and Brazil.
- Costa Rica has strong IP protections similar to the US and takes data protection seriously.
