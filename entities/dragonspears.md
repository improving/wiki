# DragonSpears

DragonSpears is a Chicago software development firm specializing in cloud adoption, agile coaching, and DevOps, acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) to strengthen its Midwest footprint and push annualized revenue beyond $285 million.

## Company

- Founded in 2015; focuses on cloud adoption, agile coaching, DevOps, and data management.
- Holds [SOC 2 Type II](/entities/soc-2-type-ii.md) compliance for protecting client data.
- Ranked on the [Inc. 5000](/entities/inc-5000.md) four years in a row and named a Best and Brightest Place to Work in Chicago eight consecutive years.
- Leadership stayed in place and continued to lead and grow the business.

## Deal

- Increased Improving's annualized revenue beyond $285 million.
- Both CEOs framed the deal around shared values of trust, excellence, and community.
- Cited alongside [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s roughly 20 offices across North and South America and India, and its private equity partnership with [Trinity Hunt Partners](/entities/trinity-hunt-partners.md).
