# Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings

Norwegian Cruise Line Holdings (NCLH) is a cruise operator that appears in this corpus as a long-running Improving client. Its Emerging Technologies and Innovation team publicly credited Improving Ottawa as an integral partner in a multi-year modernization of its reservation platform.

## Reservation Platform Modernization

- Improving Ottawa and NCLH's innovation team have worked together on a modern reservation system since 2019.
- The engagement involved more than 40 developers, coaches, and managed-services consultants, and the platform affects over 2 million passengers aboard NCLH's 32 ships annually.
- NCLH previously relied on a fragmented legacy system; Improving helped it move to a unified, scalable, service-oriented platform.
- Improving's buyer's guide cites the same case: NCLH rebuilt its reservation platform using domain-driven design and reactive architecture with 40+ Improving engineers on a multi-year engagement.
