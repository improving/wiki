# Bain

Bain & Company is a global strategy consultancy. In this corpus it appears as one of the firms whose two core assets — the billable hour and the junior pyramid — are being reworked as AI compresses production work.

## Key Claims

- Bain says 30% of its work is already AI-enabled and is heading toward 50% ([the billing model just broke](/sources/the-billing-model-just-broke.md))
- It is one of three firms, with [McKinsey & Company](/entities/mckinsey-company.md) and [BCG](/entities/bcg.md), cutting jobs in the same quarter, targeting junior research and back-office roles where AI compressed the workflow ([the premium you charged](/sources/the-premium-you-charged-was-for-knowing-things.md))
- Clients refuse to pay for hours AI compresses, pushing fees toward [outcome-based pricing](/concepts/outcome-based-pricing.md)
- The value shift is toward judgment rather than production, because judgment is what AI cannot compress
- The cuts force the question of what share of a firm's revenue is knowledge versus judgment
