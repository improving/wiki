# Google

Google, through Alphabet and its DeepMind and Cloud units, is a recurring actor in this corpus — as the author of federated discovery conventions, as the site of a revealing AI leadership reshuffle, and as the origin of causal-measurement tooling.

## Overview

On discovery, Google's Agentic Resource Discovery uses an `ai-catalog.json` crawled from domain-owned files. Federation answers cross-boundary discovery but is not a registry: crawled catalogs are static, self-reported metadata needing enrichment, and Google ships a proprietary governance layer the open spec omits.

On organization, Google split its top AI research job into two titles on August 5, 2026. Demis Hassabis kept prestige as Alphabet chief scientist while Koray Kavukcuoglu gained budget and roadmap authority. Jeff Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat left within days, suggesting talent follows authority — and that once an org outgrows one person doing both jobs, research authority becomes advisory.

On agents, Google introduced persistent coworker agents with their own Workspace identities, turning agent governance into an identity architecture problem: you cannot audit what you cannot attribute or revoke access you never granted. And as Microsoft competes directly with OpenAI and Anthropic, Google is one of the firms building a counter-protocol alongside Salesforce, Snowflake, and ServiceNow — evidence that the durable bet is open standards and API-first integration rather than any single vendor.

## Key Claims

- Agentic Resource Discovery crawls `ai-catalog.json` from domain-owned files.
