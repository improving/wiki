# Atlanta

Atlanta is the Southeastern US market Improving entered by acquiring Innovative Architects, headquartered in Georgia with satellite offices in Washington D.C. and Florida. It appears in this corpus as an [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) case and as a recurring host for the firm's events and culture programs.

## Entry and Growth

- Improving acquired [Innovative Architects](/entities/innovative-architects.md), an Atlanta Microsoft Gold-Certified partner, as its seventh enterprise location and its entry into the Southeastern US.
- The deal was expected to grow annualized revenue by over 30% in 2019, bringing collective revenue close to $100 million.
- Innovative Architects President [Scott McMichael](/entities/scott-mcmichael.md) stayed on; he later became Managing Director of the Northern region and of North America.
- Jay Bailey became President of the Atlanta enterprise in a subsequent leadership slate.
- The acquisition continued Improving's "Enterprise Strategy," pairing local ownership with geographically leveraged services.

## Programs and Presence

- Atlanta hosted a stop on Confluent's 2025 Data Streaming World Tour and features in the Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year recap.
- Atlanta's Come Together activities included a Smoothie Day focused on nutrition.
