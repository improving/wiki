# CodeCup

CodeCup 2026 is a multi-sector innovation program in [Guadalajara](/entities/guadalajara.md), created after FIFA named the city a 2026 World Cup host, to accelerate startup solutions against FIFA-identified challenges. [Improving](/entities/improving.md) was the only technology company directly supporting startups at the hackathon, deploying 24 engineers to mentor 24 teams.

## Program

- Spanned three phases — onboarding, mentorship, and development — culminating in a Grand Final on May 19 at the Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara.
- Over a two-day sprint on April 20–21, Improving paired 24 engineers with 24 startups for focused real-time technical guidance.
- Startup challenges were drawn from FIFA-identified priorities: mobility, security, tourism, and city experience.
- Institutional partners included the Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara, the Government of Zapopan, the U.S. Consulate, and Jalisco's Secretary for Innovation, Science and Technology as primary financial sponsor.
- Improving's Sandy Hermosilla, David Sandoval, and Mario Mendez sat on the Grand Final judging panel.

## Intent

- The stated goal was not a single event but a durable digital legacy for the region.
- It is an example of [community giving](/concepts/community-giving.md) rooted in Improving's Mexican presence.
