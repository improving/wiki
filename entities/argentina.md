# Argentina

Argentina is a South American nearshore destination whose capital, Buenos Aires, is cited as a thriving hub for web development, software engineering, and digital marketing. It matters to this corpus because Argentina was one of the talent locations of [Leniolabs](/entities/leniolabs.md), the firm [Improving](/entities/improving.md) acquired to establish its [South America](/entities/south-america.md) presence.

## Role as a Nearshore Hub

- Buenos Aires is named alongside Santiago as a skilled IT market, with Argentina and Chile having invested heavily in STEM education to produce talent in software development, AI, data analytics, and cybersecurity.
- Latin America overall offers a 4–8 hour overlap with US and Canadian hours, plus cultural affinity — though rates are higher than Asia or Africa and some economies are volatile.
- Argentina, like Mexico, Chile, and Guatemala, is credited with strong IP laws similar to the US and a serious approach to data protection.

## At Improving

- The [Leniolabs acquisition](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) brought talent in Chile, Argentina, Spain, and the US, pushing Improving's annualized revenue past $280 million.
- Argentina is listed as one of Improving's LATAM delivery centers — alongside Mexico, Guatemala, and Chile — supporting 600+ nearshore developers.
- Leniolabs (founded 2013, remote-first since that year) became "Improving South America"; the post argues culture and communication, not proximity alone, make such teams work.
- Argentina and Chile were featured as a cross-cultural monthly theme in Improving's 2025 Come Together year.
