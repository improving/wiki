# Mark Runyon

An Improving writer on trust, leadership, and career development. He documents [PATH](/entities/path-program.md), Improving's internal coaching program, through success stories, and writes on Stephen M.R. Covey's [trust behaviors](/concepts/trust-behaviors.md) and on what employees actually want from leaders.

## Topics and Work

- Explores "Create Transparency," Covey's third trust behavior: err on the side of telling too much, disclose blockers early, and own failures rather than shifting blame — while respecting genuine confidentiality.
- Chronicles PATH success stories: a senior-consultant promotion built through talks, client presence, and Azure study; a PSM certification unlocked by 90-day commitments and SMART goals; and a public-speaking practice that survived COVID by pivoting to virtual conferences.
- Emphasizes that PATH coaching supplies structure, accountability, and encouragement, and that seeing incremental progress produces unexpected intrinsic motivation.
- Adapts Gary Chapman's *The 5 Love Languages* into five leadership needs — recognition, professional growth, autonomy, financial fairness, and feedback — citing surveys that recognition drives great work and 94% of employees would stay longer at a company investing in their growth.
