# Ward Cunningham

Programmer widely credited with the technical-debt metaphor and with inventing the wiki. He anchors several arguments in this corpus about debt, manifesto inheritance, and precise vocabulary.

## Overview

Cunningham functions here as an origin point rather than a subject: his coinages supply the vocabulary that later posts extend, contest, and push back onto the industry.

## Key Claims

- The technical-debt metaphor is invoked to argue that debt is a chronic condition — the compounding result of continually trading craftsmanship for velocity — not a single decision, and that "pay it back later" rarely happens.
- Cunningham's wiki invention underlies the corpus's [shared vocabulary](/concepts/shared-vocabulary.md) argument: naming a recurring pattern makes it discussable, actionable, and reusable shorthand.
- As a signatory of the [Agile Manifesto](/entities/agile-manifesto.md), he is connected to the claim that its 2001 trade-offs hardened into defaults, producing inherited "Manifesto debt."
