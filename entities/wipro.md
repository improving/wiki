# Wipro

Wipro is a global IT services firm that recurs across several of Improving's partner buyer's guides, appearing in nearshore, offshore, legacy modernization, managed services, and data integration comparisons. It represents the large incumbent systems-integrator profile — broad bench, enterprise delivery at scale, and Latin American delivery presence.

## In the Partner Guides

- Listed among the twelve leading nearshore providers, with a nearshore network in Mexico and Brazil.
- Profiled in the offshore guide for cost-effective enterprise delivery.
- Profiled in the legacy modernization guide for a mainframe and cloud modernization profile.
- Profiled in the managed services guide for infrastructure and security outsourcing.
- Profiled in the data integration guide for a legacy-to-lakehouse migration profile.
