# Harvard Business Review

Harvard Business Review (HBR) is a management publication. In this corpus it is the venue for two related arguments about organizational AI: that AI must be treated as foundation rather than feature, and that automating routine work removes the reps that once built judgment.

## Overview

- The foundation argument holds that a feature model caps improvement at 10–30% on existing processes, while a foundation redesigns processes around where human judgment matters.
- Most digital transformation is described as renovation mislabeled as transformation.
- Foundation layers are model-agnostic and survive model churn.
- The judgment argument holds that activity was a proxy converting reps into judgment, and AI removes the routine work that served as apprenticeship.
- The proposed remedy is deliberate design: curated failure libraries, intent-specification drills, evaluation exercises, and protected reflection time.
