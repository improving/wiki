# Tata Consultancy Services

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is a global IT services firm that appears throughout Improving's vendor buyer's guides as a benchmark for scale, cost-efficient delivery, and large enterprise engagements across offshore, nearshore, and data-platform work.

## Position in the Guides

- Profiled among sixteen offshore delivery partners as a "global IT services giant" for large-scale enterprise delivery.
- Listed among twelve leading nearshore providers for its global reach and Latin America centers, in a market where nearshore teams keep a 6–8 hour overlap with US business hours.
- Named among nine data platform modernization providers for cost-efficient delivery at scale.
- Cited in the AI strategy buyer's guide as a representative of global services scale.

## Context

- The guides' selection criteria stress technical depth, delivery maturity, governance, security and compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA), and cultural fit over brand.
- TCS is one of several global-scale firms — with [Accenture](/entities/accenture.md), [Wipro](/entities/wipro.md), and [EPAM Systems](/entities/epam-systems.md) — that Improving positions itself against.
