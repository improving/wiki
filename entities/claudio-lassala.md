# Claudio Lassala

Claudio Lassala is an [Improving](/entities/improving.md) writer and practitioner focused on legacy modernization, testing, and the practical use of AI in daily development. His posts favor evolutionary, test-protected change over rewrites and frame AI as a tool that changes the developer's role rather than removing it.

## Work

- Authored "Stop Treating Legacy Systems as the Enemy," arguing for evolutionary maintenance and treating the codebase as archaeology.
- Wrote "Respecting and Rewriting Our Legacy," on replacing legacy systems by business value rather than line-by-line replication.
- Authored "Context-Based Testing," on splitting bloated test fixtures into readable, scenario-organized specs.
- Wrote "Hidden Cost of Over-Engineering Enterprise Applications," on the "linguistic tax" and the reversed problem-tool sequence.
- Authored "Embedding AI Into Daily Development," moving from vibe coding to "Vibe Solving" with auditable AI workflows.
- Wrote up the [AI Summer Camp](/entities/ai-summer-camp.md) community program.

## Themes

- Recurring ideas: [legacy modernization](/concepts/legacy-modernization.md), [test automation](/concepts/test-automation.md), [over-engineering](/concepts/over-engineering.md), and [context engineering](/concepts/context-engineering.md).
- He consistently pairs AI leverage with verification, framing oversight as non-negotiable.
