# Scott Poulin

An Improving technology leader and writer on AI in healthcare and on the data foundations that make AI trustworthy. His Q&As and posts cover payer and provider AI, AI roadmaps and maturity, and a defense of classical statistical machine learning alongside deep learning.

## Topics and Work

- Argues health plans facing a trust crisis over AI-driven claim denials need better [AI governance](/concepts/ai-governance.md), not less AI — observability, bias testing, risk thresholds, and human-in-the-loop review — amid CMS interoperability deadlines and state bans on fully automated denials.
- On the provider side, covers [revenue cycle management](/concepts/revenue-cycle-management.md) (initial denials at 11.8% in 2024), clinical documentation as a "second set of eyes," and [shadow AI](/concepts/shadow-ai.md) as the #1 2026 risk, addressed with a policy/technology/training/monitoring framework.
- Frames [AI strategy](/concepts/ai-strategy.md) as a staged [AI maturity model](/concepts/ai-maturity-model.md) — Ad Hoc, Strategic, Transformational — with use cases chosen on alignment, impact, feasibility, ROI, and time to value, and data foundations fixed first.
- Defends [statistical machine learning](/concepts/statistical-machine-learning.md) — regression, trees, SVMs — for interpretability, speed, and as a rigor layer that validates and legitimizes generative AI.
- Writes on combining Databricks and Confluent for governed, real-time analytics, illustrated with [Thrivent](/entities/thrivent.md).
