# McKesson

McKesson is a healthcare distributor that appears in this corpus as a recurring case for building the platform foundation before higher-level AI and data initiatives. Its Drug Serialization Repository is used to show what a platform built first can achieve under a hard regulatory deadline.

## Drug Serialization Repository

- McKesson needed to automate deployment of its Drug Serialization Repository across 29 distribution centers to meet a federal compliance deadline.
- Using GitHub Actions, Kubernetes, Confluent Kafka, and MongoDB, the work cut deployment time per location from 4–6 hours to 33 minutes.
- It achieved 100% DSCSA compliance before the deadline.

## Platform-First Argument

- The case anchors Improving's argument that AI, data, and application capabilities all depend on a platform foundation, and that organizations investing in those first get unreliable pipelines and AI that never delivers consistent results.
- Most enterprises get stuck after 12 to 24 months because the headline work is right but the foundation underneath is weak.
- McKesson also appears in Improving's platform engineering buyer's guide as a deployment-automation case study.
