# PATH

PATH (Professional Advancement Through Helping) is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s internal coaching and career-development program, conceived by Dallas President [David O'Hara](/entities/david-o-hara.md). It pairs each employee with a coach to set and pursue their own goals through repeating 90-day cycles, on the premise that careers are a rock wall rather than a corporate ladder.

## How It Works

- The **first of three meetings** covers StrengthsFinder and DISC results, work history, skills, and "dreaming" about long-term goals.
- The **second meeting** narrows goals and begins a 90-day journey, with the coach available throughout.
- After 90 days, coach and participant review progress and may repeat the cycle.
- Early participant Kevin Baker names three success factors: **write goals down, own your own goals, and have an accountability partner**.

## Outcomes and Reach

- Documented success stories include a promotion to senior consultant, a Professional Scrum Master I certification that unlocked intrinsic motivation, and a public-speaking practice that grew from quarterly to monthly engagements.
- PATH is bigger than mentorship and goal-setting: participants can pursue goals Improving doesn't currently offer, and the program sometimes creates new services or positions.
- It is one of three internal programs — with [Come Together](/entities/come-together.md) and [ImprovingU](/entities/improvingu.md) — that Improving credits for keeping its 19 global offices collaborative and improving retention.
