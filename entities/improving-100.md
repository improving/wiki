# Improving 100

Improving 100 is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s employee recognition program, honoring top performers across its offices and rewarding recipients with an all-expenses-paid trip for themselves and a guest. It is the company's flagship expression of [employee recognition](/concepts/employee-recognition.md), designed to build [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md) across a remote, multi-country workforce.

## How It Works

- Winners are nominated by peers — anyone can nominate anyone — with leadership making the final selection after due diligence.
- Recognition spans four categories: Community Impact, Business Growth, Delivery Excellence, and Technical Innovation.
- The prize is an all-inclusive trip; recent editions went to Cancún resorts including Secrets: The Vine and Maroma Beach.
- Honorees and their plus-ones attend group dinners, excursions (deep-sea fishing, sailing, golf, ATV, cenote, cooking classes), and a closing award night.
- The welcome reception is typically hosted by US Managing Director Scott McMichael and CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md).

## Notable Editions

- The 2023 edition drew 62 honorees; the first winner from Improving India, [Atulpriya Sharma](/entities/atulpriya-sharma.md), was selected in the Community Impact category.
- A 2024 winner joined only seven months after [Leniolabs](/entities/leniolabs.md) became Improving South America, framing the trip as cross-border integration.
- The essays repeatedly argue the lasting value is memory and connection, not trophies or certificates.
