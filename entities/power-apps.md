# Power Apps

Power Apps is Microsoft's low-code application builder, letting power users create apps that read and write enterprise data with minimal hand-written code. It is the most-written-about member of [Microsoft Power Platform](/entities/power-platform.md) in this corpus, appearing both as a capability tour and as the front end of concrete client solutions.

## Key Claims

- Power Apps builds canvas apps for free-form design or model-driven apps from Dataverse entities, and can embed in [Microsoft Teams](/entities/microsoft-teams.md), SharePoint, and Dynamics 365.
- Logic uses an Excel-familiar formula language, with hundreds of connectors; authentication and access control ride on Microsoft 365, managed through the Power Apps Admin Center.
- Design matters: of 60+ available controls, a quality app should use fewer than ten grouped into components, with global styling and components to cut duplicate work.
- A client's 8-week traditional build became a one-week bare-bones Power App; adding design resources extended it to three weeks for a far better product.
- Use cases include airline cabin-crew maintenance reporting with object-recognition AI, a distributor's performance management app, remote sales order portals, ESG metric entry, and mine shutdown planning.
- Limitations: weak for pixel-perfect UI, light source control that complicates parallel work, dependence on browser quality and caching, and the continued need for developer-minded resources.
