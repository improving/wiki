# Conscious Capitalism International

Conscious Capitalism International is the organization behind the conscious-capitalism movement, which holds that business should serve a higher purpose and all stakeholders while still assuming profit. It appears in this corpus as the stated foundation and philosophy of [Improving](/entities/improving.md), whose CEO joined its board.

## Organization

- On February 7, 2018, it announced eight newly appointed Board of Directors members, including Improving co-founder and CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md).
- Hite joined figures such as Whole Foods' [John Mackey](/entities/john-mackey.md) and The Container Store's Kip Tindell.
- Board members are leaders in the movement, charged with setting the organization's strategic direction.
- Its CEO, Alexander McCobin, called it potentially the defining social movement of the 21st century.

## Philosophy

- Conscious capitalism holds that business exists for a higher purpose while still requiring profit to survive; the question is how profit is spent and where value is delivered.
- Its stakeholder orientation widens focus beyond shareholders to employees, vendors, customers, industry, and community.
- Its framework drives the personal and organizational pillars discussed in this corpus — [stakeholder orientation](/concepts/stakeholder-orientation.md), [higher purpose](/concepts/higher-purpose.md), and [conscious leadership](/concepts/conscious-leadership.md).
