# Lisa Lueck

An Improving writer and Professional Scrum Trainer who works at the intersection of learning strategy and product practice. Her posts give practical frameworks for aligning training to business outcomes, and she writes candidly about her own 18-month path to [Scrum.org](/entities/scrum-org.md)'s Professional Scrum Trainer certification.

## Topics and Work

- Argues a [learning strategy](/concepts/learning-strategy.md) must align training to business objectives and measure ROI in organizational results — not headcount trained or pass/fail rates — starting from a needs assessment and blending modalities.
- Lays out a three-tier measurement stack for learning: activity metrics (completion, retention), performance metrics (behavior, engagement, productivity), and business outcomes (ROI, client satisfaction, project success).
- Describes her 18-month PST certification journey — pursued amid home renovations and a full career, with the PSM III taking two attempts — crediting time management, resilience, and a support network, and framing it as a statement for women in tech.
- Documents Improving's Product Backlog Creation Lab, a one-day workshop taking a data science team from fuzzy ideas to a prioritized backlog, and reports the team running its own refinement sessions two months later.
