# Las Vegas

Las Vegas is the recurring host city of [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s annual company retreat — a [corporate retreat](/concepts/corporate-retreat.md) that this corpus treats as the main mechanism for building [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md) and relationships across a distributed, multi-country company.

## The Annual Retreat

- Improvers from every US, Mexico, and Canada office gathered in Las Vegas for the October 2021 retreat, with more than 100 first-time attendees including the Nearshore team from the 2020 [iTexico](/entities/itexico.md) merger.
- The 2023 retreat drew 400+ Improvers to Planet Hollywood; the 2024 retreat brought 400 Improvers from the US, Canada, and Mexico together for four days.
- One account describes roughly 1,500 Improvers across three countries and sixteen sites; the 2025 retreat was the company's 20th.
- The 2025 retreat was the first to bring [South America](/entities/south-america.md), Guatemala, and India together with teams from Mexico, Canada, and the USA, closing with a group photo of six regions.

## Program and Rituals

- Recurring elements include a welcome reception, the Amazing Race across the Strip, a Hard Rock Café dinner with leadership updates and a beer pong tournament, and self-planned activity tracks.
- The 2025 edition added Learning & Games Rooms, an "Authentic Relating" workshop for about 25 women hosted with [Women of Improving](/entities/women-of-improving.md), and a "Global Delivery/New Continents" team uniting South America and Pune-India Improvers.
- CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) addressed the retreat in 2025 and, in one first-timer's account, stopped to talk and dance with a newer Improver and remembered him all weekend.
- Organizers judge success by the friendships that outlast the event rather than the scheduled activities.
