# Vertex AI

Google Cloud's machine learning platform, used by Improving across public sector, healthcare, and retail engagements in Canada and the US. At Google Cloud Next '26 it was rebranded and evolved into the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, with emphasis on governance, security, observability, agent identity, and auditability — a marker of the shift from AI experimentation to governed agentic deployment.

## Role in the Corpus

- Improving used Vertex AI across public sector, healthcare, and retail projects before the rebrand.
- It powers the Research Accelerator Portal for a defense research agency, alongside Jupyter, Gemini, and Terraform, provisioning PBMM-compliant compute often within an hour of approval.
- The portal provides pre-configured templates, budget estimation and approval workflows, and automated spend alerts at 25%, 50%, 75%, 90%, and 100%.

## In the Sovereignty Debate

- [AI sovereignty](/concepts/ai-sovereignty.md) writing names Vertex AI as one of the hyperscaler abstraction surfaces (with Amazon Bedrock and Azure AI Foundry) that run commercial and open-weight models behind one governed interface.
- That abstraction lets an enterprise avoid direct model-vendor dependency without moving the workload off cloud infrastructure.
