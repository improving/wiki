# Levels

Levels is a [continuous glucose monitoring](/concepts/continuous-glucose-monitoring.md) service that pairs a Dexcom G6 sensor with software for interpreting glucose curves. It gave the author per-meal feedback that blood panels alone could not provide.

## Overview

Levels was the metabolic instrument in the health journey: where [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md) supplied periodic lab baselines, Levels supplied live readings that made individual food and activity responses visible.

## Key Claims

- Eating vegetables, then protein, then starch reduces glucose spikes — the basis of [meal ordering](/concepts/meal-ordering.md).
- Creative work caused a 90-point glucose spike that a walk brought back down.
- The Dexcom G6 syncs continuously, unlike the Freestyle Libre.
- Different glucose responses to identical meals convinced the author that one-size-fits-all diet advice is usually selling something.
- Data from Levels, not subjective feeling, guided adjustments to eating and supplementation.
