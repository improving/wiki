# Demis Hassabis

Co-founder of Google DeepMind and, after Google's August 5, 2026 reshuffle, Alphabet's chief scientist. The corpus cites him for a proposed industry regulator and as a case study in research prestige diverging from operational authority.

## Overview

Hassabis proposed a FINRA-style body to review frontier models before release. The corpus treats the warm reception as [safety disclosure as marketing](/concepts/safety-disclosure-as-marketing.md): praise for a self-regulatory body costs supporters nothing before it has teeth, and internal monitoring is not the same evidence status as [independent verification](/concepts/independent-verification.md).

## Key Claims

- Proposed a FINRA-style body to review frontier models before release.
- In the August 2026 Google split, he kept the prestige title of Alphabet chief scientist while Koray Kavukcuoglu gained budget and roadmap authority.
- Jeff Dean and Sanjay Ghemawat left within days — talent follows authority.
- Once an org outgrows one person doing both jobs, research authority becomes advisory.
