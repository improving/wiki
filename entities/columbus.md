# Columbus

Columbus is the Ohio market where Improving launched its "Enterprise Model" of growth and, later, one half of the merged Improving Ohio enterprise. It appears in this corpus as an origin point for the firm's [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) and as a center of Microsoft-focused delivery.

## Entry and Merger

- Improving finalized its acquisition of The Sophic Group, Inc. in Columbus on January 14, 2011, launching the "Enterprise Model" and expanding into the Midwest, with annual revenue expected to rise more than 15%.
- Both firms focused on Agile, Microsoft (.NET), and Java application development, extending the Columbus office's service offerings.
- CEO Curtis Hite said the model's secret is preserving local ownership behavior and accountability.
- On January 1, 2022, the Columbus and Cleveland offices merged into a single Improving Ohio enterprise under President Josh Harrison.
- Cleveland brought infrastructure and Microsoft strengths; Columbus brought application development, especially on Microsoft Azure, making Improving Ohio a full-spectrum Azure destination.

## Leadership Ties

- The merger freed [Lowell Messner](/entities/lowell-messner.md), then Improving Cleveland president, to lead Improving's new corporate Microsoft Services Team.
- Columbus's Azure-and-app-dev focus connects directly to Improving's [Microsoft Partner](/concepts/microsoft-partner.md) positioning.
