# Leniolabs

Leniolabs is a nearshore software development company that Improving acquired to establish a South American presence. Founded in 2013, it leverages talent in Chile, Argentina, Spain, and the US and specializes in backend, frontend, and data technologies with a focus on AI projects.

## Acquisition by Improving

- The transaction was expected to push Improving's annualized revenue past $280 million and extend development services into South America.
- Leniolabs' leadership team stayed intact and continued to manage and grow the business under Improving's Enterprise Strategy.
- The announcement carried quotes from Curtis Hite (CEO, Improving) and Iago Rodriguez (Co-Founder and Partner, Leniolabs).
- Improving is designated SOC 2 compliant, cited as a commitment to protecting client data.

## Role in the Nearshore Footprint

- Improving's nearshore guide names Leniolabs (with iTexico, Zigatta, and MangoChango) among the acquisitions that deepened its LATAM footprint, which spans Mexico, Guatemala, Chile, and Argentina.
