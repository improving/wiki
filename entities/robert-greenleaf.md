# Robert K. Greenleaf

Robert K. Greenleaf coined the term "servant leadership" in 1970, making serving the team, removing obstacles, and growing people the leader's primary responsibility. Improving writer Agostina Formia invokes the concept to contrast genuine agile leadership with micromanagement in disguise.

## Use in This Corpus

- Agile micromanagement hides behind dashboards — tracking individual velocity, demanding task-level check-ins, treating every sprint as a performance review — which contradicts the self-managing teams Scrum requires.
- Individual metrics incentivize ticket hoarding, silo behavior, inflated estimates, and loss of psychological safety.
- Servant leadership's alternative: measure the team (velocity, cycle time, deployment frequency, satisfaction), remove impediments, ask questions, and trust by default, so that leaders' involvement is invited rather than imposed.
