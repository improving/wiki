# Dallas Innovates

A Dallas-area publication and events organizer that documents the region's [regional AI economy](/concepts/regional-ai-economy.md), from startup funding to institutional AI adoption. It is the lens through which several sources in this corpus observe [Dallas-Fort Worth](/entities/dallas-fort-worth.md).

## Overview

Dallas Innovates' coverage tracks both the big-infrastructure story and the wider innovation economy. It hosted the AI awards night that drew 700 people to a [Dallas Regional Chamber](/entities/dallas-regional-chamber.md) event, and its AI 75 list is led by operators and institutions rather than startups — a signal the regional conversation moved from hype to execution.

## Key Claims

- 700 people filled a Dallas Regional Chamber AI awards event, indicating hype has given way to institutional infrastructure.
- The AI 75 list is led by operators and institutions, not startups.
- DFW's AI economy is built on vertical, industry-specific systems in regulated, liability-sensitive industries rather than AI-native startups.
- [Brookings named DFW one of 28 AI Star Hubs](/sources/dfw-is-building-the-other-kind-of-ai-economy.md); 36% of local jobs now have meaningful AI exposure.
- DFW added 37,230 tech workers between 2022 and 2025, and more than 15 Texas cities hosted simultaneous innovation events.
