# EPAM Systems

EPAM Systems is a global engineering services firm that recurs across Improving's partner buyer's guides as a benchmark for engineering depth, in offshore, nearshore, and platform-engineering comparisons.

## Position in the Guides

- Profiled among sixteen offshore delivery partners for its Eastern European engineering network.
- Listed among twelve leading nearshore providers for engineering-driven Latin America delivery.
- Profiled among nine platform engineering companies, a discipline the guide says spans cloud architecture, Kubernetes operations, developer experience, and governance.
- Appears alongside [Thoughtworks](/entities/thoughtworks.md), which originated the "platform as a product" framing, and [Slalom](/entities/slalom.md) in the same comparisons.

## Context

- The platform guide's buyer criteria — Kubernetes/cloud-native depth, multi-cloud fluency, internal developer platform experience, regulated-environment track record, and delivery model — separate a real platform practice from rebranded staffing.
- EPAM is one of several global firms that Improving positions itself against as a [partner selection](/concepts/partner-selection.md) alternative.
