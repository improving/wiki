# John Mackey

John Mackey is the co-founder and CEO of Whole Foods Market and, with Kip Tindell of The Container Store, a co-founder of Conscious Capitalism. The movement and its stakeholder model recur across Improving's culture writing, where they are presented as the firm's foundation.

## Conscious Capitalism

- The movement argues profits are not evil but should feed the ecosystem that produced them, widening focus beyond shareholders and customers to employees, suppliers, industry, and community.
- Its four pillars are higher purpose, conscious leadership, stakeholder integration, and conscious culture.
- In 2018, Improving CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) was one of eight newly appointed members of the Conscious Capitalism International board, joining Mackey and Tindell.

## Use in This Corpus

- David O'Hara's "Profits for Good" uses the stakeholder model to argue Improving was built on relationships-first values.
- "Culture Over Code" applies the four pillars to AI adoption, arguing AI changes tasks but not talent: reskill before replacing, and treat AI as amplification rather than displacement.
