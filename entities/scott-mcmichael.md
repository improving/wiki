# Scott McMichael

Scott McMichael is an [Improving](/entities/improving.md) leader who appears in this corpus both as an acquisition principal and as a recurring host at the firm's flagship recognition events. He came to Improving through its [Atlanta](/entities/atlanta.md) acquisition of [Innovative Architects](/entities/innovative-architects.md) and rose into North American regional leadership.

## Role at Improving

- Was President of Innovative Architects, the Atlanta Microsoft Gold-Certified partner Improving acquired in 2019 as its seventh enterprise location.
- After the acquisition, took over as Managing Director of the Northern region and Managing Director of North America, per the 2022 C-suite announcement.
- By the Improving 100 trips he is described as US/North American Managing Director, speaking at the finale and co-hosting welcome receptions with CEO Curtis Hite.

## Recognition and Culture

- Appears as a host figure across multiple first-person [Improving 100](/entities/improving-100.md) accounts set in [Cancún](/entities/cancun.md).
- His presence at the welcome receptions is used to illustrate how [employee recognition](/concepts/employee-recognition.md) builds cross-border [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md) in a multi-country company.
