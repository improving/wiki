# Mark Kovacevich

An Improving writer and leadership voice on [Conscious Capitalism](/concepts/conscious-capitalism.md) and trust. His four-part series translates the movement's pillars — higher purpose, stakeholder orientation, conscious culture, and conscious leadership — into personal practice, and his trust writing draws on Stephen M.R. Covey's *The Speed of Trust*.

## Topics and Work

- Walks through each Conscious Capitalism pillar applied to individual life: a specific purpose statement, a measurable stakeholder model, chosen culture qualities (he picks trust, caring, and learning), and leadership as awareness, empowerment, and enablement.
- Argues [higher purpose](/concepts/higher-purpose.md) is a North Star that determines one's stakeholder model and culture, and that work-life integration through aligned purpose beats chasing balance.
- Treats [active listening](/concepts/active-listening.md) — Covey's "Listen First" — as a deliberate, hard practice that builds trust and seeds a virtuous cycle, supporting other [trust behaviors](/concepts/trust-behaviors.md).
- Frames [conscious leadership](/concepts/conscious-leadership.md) as everyone's responsibility, inseparable from followership, and as the conduit that turns higher purpose into action and [servant leadership](/concepts/servant-leadership.md).
- Stresses reciprocity in stakeholder relationships, including letting go of relationships that consistently diminish you.
