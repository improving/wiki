# Venture Dallas

Venture Dallas is the region's capital-allocation gathering, cited across the corpus as a marker of how far Dallas-Fort Worth's local venture layer has come. Where earlier coverage of [DFW](/entities/dallas-fort-worth.md) emphasized data centers and corporate relocations, these posts use Venture Dallas to argue the real story is capital formed and deployed at home.

## Key Claims

- Venture Dallas brings 1,500 investors, founders, and corporate development executives to Old Parkland in November.
- It is cited alongside the claim that Dallas entered the top four U.S. startup ecosystems.
- Local VC assets under management grew from roughly $1.3B in 2020 to $4B, with family offices deploying into technology at coastal rates.
- A later post dates Venture Dallas to October 22 and pairs it with the Texas Stock Exchange launch and Island's $400M raise.
- DFW's Q3 2026 was its best VC quarter on record: $962.5M across 52 deals.
