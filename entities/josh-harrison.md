# Josh Harrison

Josh Harrison is an Improving leader and writer. He became president of the combined Improving Ohio enterprise and writes on how AI relocates engineering complexity and on how corporate innovation labs should be run.

## Role at Improving

- Named president of Improving Ohio when the Columbus and Cleveland offices merged on January 1, 2022, combining Cleveland's infrastructure and Microsoft strength with Columbus's Azure application development.
- Led the combined Ohio enterprise, freeing [Lowell Messner](/entities/lowell-messner.md) to head the new Microsoft Services Team.

## Writing

- Argued in "The Benefit of a Corporate Innovation Lab" that innovation is a discipline run through a method, and that labs need a distinct execution model, cross-discipline product teams, and accountability metrics such as new-product revenue share and gross margin; 70% of North American IT budgets go to maintenance.
- Argued in "Complexity Never Goes Away, it Just Moves" that AI shifts engineering effort to the systems around models — harness engineering on the input side and LLMOps on the output side — forming a feedback loop that improves results while cutting token consumption.

## Themes

- Engineering talent is a scarce, non-fungible, moving-target constraint even as imperative coding gets cheaper.
- Innovation needs tension with the parent: shared vision, flexible how and what.
- Complexity relocates across eras rather than disappearing.
