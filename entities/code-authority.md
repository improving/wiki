# Code Authority

Code Authority is a custom software development and digital marketing firm headquartered in Frisco, Texas, acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) in a merger that also brought the [CodeLaunch](/entities/code-launch.md) startup accelerator into Improving.

## Company

- Headquartered in [Frisco, Texas](/entities/frisco-texas.md); focuses on custom software, cloud, mobile, and digital transformation plus digital marketing and UI/UX.
- Had 18 years of organic growth; President Jason W. Taylor stayed on to keep operating and growing the business.
- Taylor credited Frisco's pro-business infrastructure, chamber, EDC, and mayors for enabling the growth.

## Deal

- Continued Improving's "Enterprise Model" of merging firms that share commitments to excellence, trust, and culture while keeping local ownership behavior.
- Built [CodeLaunch](/entities/code-launch.md), a zero-equity seed-accelerator event pairing early-stage startup winners with developers, venture capitalists, and sponsors.
- Improving planned to accelerate CodeLaunch into additional markets.
