# Kathy Henely

Kathy Henely is the first independent member of [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s Board of Directors, appointed on 1 August 2021. She appears in this corpus as evidence of the firm's governance maturing alongside its [conscious capitalism](/concepts/conscious-capitalism.md) commitments, bringing operating experience from scaling a global IT services business.

## Role at Improving

- Added to the Board of Directors on 1 August 2021 as the company's first independent board member.
- Serves actively, advising both the board and the CEO.
- CEO Curtis Hite cites her global IT services operating experience and cultural alignment with Conscious Capitalism.

## Background

- Spent nearly 20 years in senior leadership at Perficient, mostly as COO, helping scale it past 3,000 employees through organic and acquisition growth.
- Named one of the Most Influential Businesswomen in St. Louis, Missouri.
