# Zigatta

Zigatta is a Frisco, Texas software development and consulting firm with offices in Guadalajara, Mexico, acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) to extend its reach across the US and Latin America and push annualized revenue past $275 million.

## Company

- Founded in 2015; specializes in high-end data, cloud, software engineering, and architecture.
- Headquartered in [Frisco, Texas](/entities/frisco-texas.md), with a Latin America presence built through two acquisitions in [Guadalajara](/entities/guadalajara.md), Mexico.
- Leadership stayed intact and continued to run and grow the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Deal

- Pushed Improving's annualized revenue past $275 million.
- CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) cited clear alignment and Zigatta's customer relationships and culture; Zigatta CEO Nahid Giga cited shared values and community giving.
- One of the acquisitions (with iTexico, Leniolabs, and [MangoChango](/entities/mangochango.md)) that deepened Improving's nearshore footprint in [Mexico](/entities/mexico.md).
