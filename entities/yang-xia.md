# Yang Xia

Yang Xia is an [Improving](/entities/improving.md) consultant recognized as the company's first [Apache Software Foundation](/entities/apache-software-foundation.md) Project Management Committee (PMC) member, for her work on [Apache TinkerPop](/entities/apache-tinkerpop.md). She appears in this corpus as both a graph-data technologist and a subject of Improving's women-in-tech storytelling, having moved into software from a research background in neuroscience and cancer genetics.

## Work

- Became Improving's first Apache PMC member; the PMC holds sole authority to vote on formal releases and to vote in new committers and members.
- Works on Apache TinkerPop, an open-source framework for graph databases (OLTP) and graph analytics (OLAP) built on vertices connected by edges.
- Contributes to Gremlin, TinkerPop's graph traversal language, analogous to SQL for relational databases.
- Progressed from a Gremlin-based JDBC driver to a Go implementation of Gremlin, drawn to serialization mechanics.

## Background

- Came to programming via a neuroscience/psychology degree and a master's in pathology and laboratory medicine researching cancer genetics.
- Her path models cross-domain [continuous learning](/concepts/continuous-learning.md), and her profile is part of Improving's [inclusion and diversity](/concepts/inclusion-and-diversity.md) work.
