# Don McGreal

An Improving author, Professional Scrum Trainer, and — as of the 2026 leadership appointments — President of Improving Dallas. His writing argues that Scrum depth and training outcomes matter far more than certificates, and he is a prominent voice for Improving's position as a major [Scrum.org](/entities/scrum-org.md) training provider.

## Role and Work

- Cites ScrumMatch data that 38% of Scrum Masters have little to no understanding of Scrum's core principles, blaming certification-focused hiring over practical proficiency; Improving's response is rigorous assessment, immersive training, and [mentorship](/concepts/mentorship.md).
- Argues Scrum certification's real value is as a forcing function — the post-class test drives review, practice tests teach through explanation, and study groups add cadence and accountability — marking the start of a learning journey, not the end.
- Diagnoses traditional instructor-led training's "four D's" (disruptive, dense, detached, disregarded) and proposes a blend of labs, Deeper Learning Programs, fieldwork, and coaching measured by applied outcomes rather than survey scores.
- Was promoted to President, Improving Dallas after 18 years with the firm, part of a grow-from-within leadership announcement.
