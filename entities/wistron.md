# Wistron

A manufacturer whose $761 million AI supercomputing investment in [Fort Worth](/entities/fort-worth.md) is the concrete anchor of [Dallas-Fort Worth](/entities/dallas-fort-worth.md)'s claim to tier-one AI status. The project recurs across this corpus as the moment the region became load-bearing AI infrastructure.

## Overview

Wistron picked Fort Worth for the supercomputing facility, developed within [AllianceTexas](/entities/alliancetexas.md). The investment is cited alongside Dallas's ranking as North America's number one tech hub and the broader wave of 248 planned data centers across [Texas](/entities/texas.md).

## Key Claims

- Wistron is investing $761 million in AI supercomputers in Fort Worth.
- The project makes DFW load-bearing AI infrastructure and shortens leaders' runway to act.
- 248 data centers are planned across Texas, with the state leading the nation in proposed AI data centers.
- DFW added more tech jobs than any U.S. metro from 2022 to 2024.
- The buildout collides with grid math and cost externalization, making the infrastructure politically contested.
