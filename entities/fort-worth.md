# Fort Worth

Fort Worth is a North Texas city that shows up in this corpus as a landing site for the physical layer of the region's AI economy: supercomputers, manufacturing campuses, and the data center capacity around them.

## Overview

Fort Worth's role is largely as a destination for capital-intensive AI projects inside the broader [Dallas-Fort Worth](/entities/dallas-fort-worth.md) metro and the [Texas](/entities/texas.md) grid that serves them. The city's projects are cited as evidence that DFW is load-bearing AI infrastructure rather than an aspirational hub.

## Key Claims

- [Wistron](/entities/wistron.md) picked Fort Worth for a $761 million AI supercomputing facility.
- Celestica announced an $876M AI manufacturing campus in Fort Worth.
- 248 data centers are planned across Texas, with Fort Worth among the sites of the build-out.
- Brookings named DFW one of 28 AI Star Hubs, and 36% of local jobs now have meaningful AI exposure.
- Proximity of infrastructure, talent, and clients removes leaders' excuses to delay AI; client questions shifted from whether to do AI to catching up.
