# The Improving Edge Podcast

The Improving Edge Podcast is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s podcast, where the firm's leaders and consultants discuss how technology reshapes business. Its conversations are adapted into written thought leadership, most visibly on what an AI-driven business model actually requires.

## Content

- Episodes are turned into posts that argue AI changes how a business executes its purpose, not why it exists.
- One episode (written up by [Janine Jeanson](/entities/janine-jeanson.md)) frames an AI-driven business model as redesigning how value is created, delivered, and protected — with human oversight, data, and security as non-negotiable foundations.
- Recurring guidance: start from what the business already does well, apply AI selectively where it amplifies human judgment, and expect realistic productivity gains of 30–40% on text, research, pattern-recognition, and summarization tasks.
