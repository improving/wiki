# Speed of Trust

Covey's *Speed of Trust* is the named framework the corpus reaches for whenever trust is treated as an operating discipline rather than a feeling. Two posts draw on its set of **13 Trust Behaviors** — one applying it to client-centric consulting, the other to leadership after a missed deadline — making it the shared vocabulary behind the wiki's [trust building](/concepts/trust-building.md) theme.

## Key Claims

- The framework catalogs 13 Trust Behaviors; the corpus names Clarify Expectations, Create Transparency, Listen First, and Deliver Results.
- Clarify Expectations, Create Transparency, and Listen First are applied to consulting: uncover real needs, communicate proactively, and surface issues through listening.
- Among the 13, "Deliver Results" is argued to be the strongest foundation for building trust — and delivering means not missing deadlines.
- A missed deadline is framed as a failure of leadership, not of execution: leaders must own what did and didn't happen and show how the future improves.
