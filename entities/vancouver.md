# Vancouver

Vancouver is where [Improving](/entities/improving.md) acquired [Bit Quill Technologies](/entities/bit-quill.md), a data-and-cloud software consultancy founded in 2017, and it remains one of the firm's major Canadian offices. The city appears in this corpus for both deep data engineering and a strong people-and-culture practice.

## Improving's Presence

- Bit Quill's acquisition pushed Improving's annualized revenue above $250 million, with the leadership team staying in place under the "Enterprise Strategy."
- Vancouver is one of the four Canadian locations (with [Toronto](/entities/toronto.md), [Ottawa](/entities/ottawa.md), and Winnipeg) that grew Improving's Canadian headcount to 400.
- The office runs a paid co-op program giving students real project experience; co-ops can code and test features, configure environments and test automation, and author documentation, and some return as permanent employees and rise to senior roles.
- The Vancouver team is home to [James Duong](/entities/james-duong.md), the Bit Quill co-founder and [Apache Arrow](/entities/apache-arrow.md) contributor behind Flight SQL.

## Culture

- Bit Quillians (as the team calls itself) hold a Picnic & Games day at Fraser Foreshore Park and a family day at Playland and the PNE, used to welcome new team members and build relationships.
- These events illustrate [corporate retreat](/concepts/corporate-retreat.md) and [employee engagement](/concepts/employee-engagement.md) practices.
