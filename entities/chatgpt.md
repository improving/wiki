# ChatGPT

ChatGPT is OpenAI's conversational AI product. In this corpus it functions less as a product to evaluate than as the cultural reference point against which AI adoption, organizational vocabulary, and benchmark claims are measured.

## Key Claims

- In 2025, AI workflows sit at Early Adopter and agents at Innovator on [Rogers' diffusion curve](/concepts/diffusion-of-innovations.md), which describes populations at a moment rather than timelines ([rogers' curve](/sources/rogers-curve-is-the-best-diagnostic-tool-youre-not-using.md))
- Applying an Innovator strategy as a Late Majority organization is the common strategic mistake.
- Terms like "AI adoption" and "integration" are so broad they convey almost no information; stage numbers and named failure mechanisms give strategy a precise [shared vocabulary](/concepts/shared-vocabulary.md) ([the vocabulary of ai-native organizations](/sources/the-vocabulary-of-ai-native-organizations.md))
- Capability claims about assistants are often unfalsifiable: MMLU saturated above 88–90%, making score gaps statistically meaningless ([your ai benchmark is not science](/sources/your-ai-benchmark-is-not-science-until-it-can-fail.md))
- Decontamination removes about 23% of MMLU inflation, so organizations should build internal, falsifiable eval suites from real use cases.
