# Ken Ledyard

An Improving Microsoft Partner Alliance Manager and the firm's public voice on the Microsoft partner ecosystem. He joined Improving from the Ohio enterprise and moved to Improving Corporate with the new Microsoft Services Team, bringing 12 years in Microsoft field sales and 24 years in technology overall.

## Role and Work

- Works to maximize the value of Improving's [Microsoft Partner](/concepts/microsoft-partner.md) relationship for the firm and its clients, strengthening alignment and matching technical skills to customer needs.
- Argues Microsoft's 2022 rebrand to the Microsoft Cloud Partner Program matters less to buyers than how they choose partners, since the technology is identical across partners — deals are won on trust, referrals, industry expertise, and breadth of capability.
- Explains Microsoft's [zero trust security](/concepts/zero-trust-security.md) philosophy — never trust, always verify — through its three principles (verify explicitly, least privilege, assume breach) and seven pillars, illustrated with a credit union's transformation.
- Documents application modernization on Microsoft Azure for a Fortune 500 health insurer, using Kafka to speed ingestion into [Snowflake](/entities/snowflake.md) as a centralized data hub.
