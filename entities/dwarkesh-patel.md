# Dwarkesh Patel

An AI writer and interviewer whose essay contest and commentary anchor two corpus posts: one on where AI labs capture durable value beyond metered API fares, and one on why LLM research agents can execute but not judge what to try next.

## Overview

The value essay gathers competing winning entries — one compares labs to Hong Kong's MTR, which profits from property not fares, while another bets on far-UVC lighting as a hedge independent of AI. The research essay traces the credit-assignment gap back to [AlphaGo](/entities/alphago.md)'s per-move feedback.

## Key Claims

- Two winning essays agree the metered API is not the real business but disagree on where labs capture durable value.
- One entry says labs should own data-trusteeship deals, forward-deployed integration, and RL data.
- AlphaGo's MCTS gave a graded target on every move, sidestepping credit assignment.
- LLM agents get one reward at the end of a hundred-thousand-token trajectory; [process reward models](/concepts/process-reward-models.md) only approximate per-step targets.
