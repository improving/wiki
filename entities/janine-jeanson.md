# Janine Jeanson

An Improving writer on applying AI and the Microsoft stack to real business problems, with a practical, governance-first bent. Her posts pair concrete case studies with warnings that data readiness and oversight, not tool choice, determine whether AI pays off.

## Topics and Work

- Documents a case automating ~100,000 invoices a year with Microsoft's prebuilt invoice model, 13 validation checks, a 70% confidence gate, and human review of exceptions — projected to save 1 FTE (~$75,000) annually.
- Argues an [AI strategy](/concepts/ai-strategy.md) is business-model design, not task replacement, citing realistic 30–40% productivity gains on text, research, and summarization work and positioning AI as [augmentation](/concepts/ai-augmentation.md).
- Contends that because AI agents act end to end, "if your data is messy, your AI will be messy" — [data readiness](/concepts/data-readiness.md), governance, permissions, and audit trails must precede deployment.
- Introduces [AI bias](/concepts/ai-bias.md) from data, design, and leading questions, using a ChatGPT Stanley Cup example, and points to governance, fairness techniques, and human oversight as mitigations.
- Covers Microsoft tooling choices including Teams Premium's $10-per-user security and webinar features and how to find a [Microsoft Partner](/concepts/microsoft-partner.md).
