# Bit Quill Technologies

Bit Quill Technologies is a Vancouver-based data and cloud software consultancy that [Improving](/entities/improving.md) acquired to expand its Canadian presence and push annualized revenue past $250 million. Beyond the deal itself, the firm appears in this corpus through the Apache Arrow engineering of co-founder James Duong.

## Company

- Data and cloud software development firm based in Vancouver, founded in 2017.
- Acquired by Improving under its "Enterprise Strategy," which merges technology service companies sharing a commitment to trust, delivery excellence, and culture.
- Leadership stayed intact and continued to operate the business; CEO/co-founder Kyle Porter framed the fit around shared values.
- The deal was expected to lift Improving's annualized revenue above $250 million.

## Engineering

- Co-founder [James Duong](/entities/james-duong.md) is an Apache Arrow contributor and Dremio contributor who works on Arrow FlightSQL.
- FlightSQL is presented as a roughly 20x faster alternative to JDBC and ODBC, usable from Python, C++, Java, and other languages.
- A JDBC driver built on FlightSQL lets existing tools such as Tableau gain the performance with no application changes.
