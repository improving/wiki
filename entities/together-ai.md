# Together AI

A platform offering managed inference and open-weight model hosting. In this corpus it appears twice: as the site of [IBM](/entities/ibm.md)'s inference cluster that will not go live until early 2027, and as a lens on why open-weight cost advantages come from architecture rather than leaderboard position.

## Overview

The cluster story illustrates the [demand limit](/concepts/demand-limited-vs-collapsable-tasks.md): capacity can be built ahead of confirmed enterprise demand, and more AI capability does not automatically create demand for what it produces. Even an earnings beat from [Nvidia](/entities/nvidia.md) was sold off, suggesting capital markets no longer fund AI buildout on forecasts alone. The second source argues that [open-weight models](/concepts/open-weight-models.md) win on engineering: local intelligence-per-watt improved 5.3x over two years, GLM-5 activates only 8 of 256 experts per token (about 5.9%), and MiniMax M2.5's linear attention decouples cost from context length.

## Key Claims

- IBM's Together AI inference cluster won't go live until early 2027
- More AI capability doesn't automatically create demand for what it produces
- Local intelligence-per-watt improved 5.3x over two years
- Targeted training scaled a 32B open model to 36.6% on SWE-bench Verified
