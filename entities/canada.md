# Canada

Canada is Improving's first market outside the United States and the proving ground for its geographic [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md). What began as a single Calgary acquisition grew within three years to roughly 400 employees across [Vancouver](/entities/vancouver.md), [Toronto](/entities/toronto.md), [Ottawa](/entities/ottawa.md), and Winnipeg, giving the firm coverage across major time zones.

## Entry and Expansion

- Entered Canada by acquiring Calgary's [Quadrus](/entities/quadrus.md), a five-time Inc500/5000 recipient with a 20-year Agile/.NET/Java track record; the deal was expected to raise annualized revenue by more than 20%.
- Added [YoppWorks](/entities/yoppworks.md), an Ottawa cloud-native and Scala consultancy, pushing collective annualized revenue above $200 million.
- Added [Bit Quill Technologies](/entities/bit-quill.md), a Vancouver data-and-cloud firm founded in 2017, pushing annualized revenue past $250 million.
- Added [Jump Analytics](/entities/jump-analytics.md), a Canadian analytics firm with offices in Toronto, Vancouver, and Calgary, pushing annualized revenue above $290 million.
- Grew from 49 employees in Calgary to 400 across Canada in three years.

## Public Sector and Headquarters

- Opened a Canadian headquarters at 171 East Liberty in Toronto, home to 100 employees, with planned investment in platform engineering and data & AI.
- [Ottawa](/entities/ottawa.md) became the base for public-sector work; YoppWorks won Google Cloud's 2023 Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada.
