# Greg Abbott

Governor of [Texas](/entities/texas.md) and the central political figure in the state's AI-infrastructure story. His administration's moves on data-center energy costs and approvals mark the shift from recruiting AI capacity to governing its [regional AI economy](/concepts/regional-ai-economy.md) footprint.

## Overview

Abbott directed the Public Utility Commission and ERCOT to make data centers fund their own grid infrastructure — a July 17, 2026 memo that sets the cost structure for Texas AI buildout. Sources frame this as maturation rather than an anti-AI reversal. Later, on August 3, he paused data center approvals to audit energy usage.

## Key Claims

- ERCOT's large-load queue nearly quadrupled in a year, 89% data centers.
- Abbott told utilities to charge data centers for infrastructure costs rather than passing them to ratepayers.
- The regulatory shift is read as AI-hub maturation, not a reversal of support.
- Abbott paused data center approvals on August 3, 2026, to audit energy usage.
- AI regulation is now a 2027 Texas legislative priority.
