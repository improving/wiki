# Imaginet

Imaginet is a Microsoft consulting and implementation firm with offices in Winnipeg, Canada and Dallas, Texas, acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) to push annualized revenue past $280 million and add both a Winnipeg enterprise and a stronger Dallas presence.

## Company

- Specializes in Microsoft consulting with offices in Winnipeg, Canada and Dallas, Texas.
- A 20-time Microsoft Gold Partner and one of Canada's earliest Microsoft partners.
- Brings more than 25 years of Microsoft 365, application development, and data analytics experience.
- Leadership remained unchanged and continued operating the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Deal

- Expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue beyond $280 million.
- Established an Improving enterprise in Winnipeg and strengthened its Dallas presence.
- Quoted both CEOs, [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) and Imaginet leadership, framing the deal around shared values.
