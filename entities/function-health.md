# Function Health

Function Health is a membership testing service that runs broad blood panels twice a year and returns results with context, causes, foods, and supplement suggestions. It is the measurement backbone of the author's health journey.

## Overview

Discovered through [Mark Hyman](/entities/mark-hyman.md), Function Health was adopted during its beta as the recurring baseline against which [biomarker testing](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md) and lifestyle changes are judged. It sits alongside point-in-time tools such as [Levels](/entities/levels.md) in a data-driven protocol.

## Key Claims

- The $499 annual membership includes two rounds of 100+ biomarker testing, delivered through partner labs such as Quest Labs.
- Recommendations come from a functional medicine perspective rather than a single-disease lens.
- Six months of supplementation, recovery, and diet work dropped [biological age](/concepts/biological-age.md) from 47.6 to roughly 40, while workouts stayed the same.
- Each biomarker is mapped to context, causes, foods, and supplements, turning a number into an action.
- The service supports 6-month baselines plus intermediate testing, on the argument that biology is too individual for one-size-fits-all advice.
