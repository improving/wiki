# ProSource Solutions

ProSource Solutions is a management and IT consulting firm headquartered in Richfield, Ohio, acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) as its sixth enterprise location and a deepening of Improving's [Microsoft Partner](/concepts/microsoft-partner.md) capability.

## Company

- Headquartered in Richfield, Ohio.
- A preferred Microsoft Partner that won "Top U.S. Services Partner of the Year" from Microsoft in five separate years.
- Offerings spanned application development, cloud computing, collaboration, and infrastructure.
- [Lowell Messner](/entities/lowell-messner.md) was President and CEO; he stayed with the business and now leads Improving's Microsoft Services Team.

## Deal

- Expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue by more than $10,000,000 in 2018.
- Expanded Improving's reach in the Midwestern and Southeastern United States.
- Joined the Improving brand as its sixth enterprise location under the "Enterprise Model" of merging like-minded service firms.
