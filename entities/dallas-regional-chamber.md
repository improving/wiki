# Dallas Regional Chamber

The Dallas Regional Chamber is the region's business membership and advocacy organization. In this corpus it is the institution that convenes DFW's AI community and the venue where the region's AI story turns from hype to infrastructure.

## Key Claims

- Seven hundred people filled a Dallas Regional Chamber AI awards event ([when 700 people show up](/sources/when-700-people-show-up-for-an-ai-awards-night-the-hype-is-over.md))
- The AI 75 list it recognizes is led by operators and institutions, not startups
- The region's conversation shifted from whether to do AI to how well it is done
- Buying decisions now form among institutions already deploying AI
- The Chamber is also cited in the account of Dallas being named North America's top tech hub ([Dallas just topped D.C.](/sources/dallas-topped-dc.md))
