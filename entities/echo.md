# Echo

Echo is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s AI-enabled internal knowledge base, built for its sales and support teams to make decades of accumulated case studies and win-wires discoverable. It combines [retrieval-augmented generation](/concepts/retrieval-augmented-generation.md), multiple LLMs, and an automated data engineering pipeline across Microsoft, AWS, and Google toolkits.

## How It Works

- An automated [data integration](/concepts/data-integration.md) pipeline ingests documents regardless of format from Teams, SharePoint, databases, and other sources.
- It uses factual grounding and RAG to answer through a chat interface that cites its sources.
- Echo understands the user's role and typical queries, embedding response collateral directly in the conversation.
- It runs on Microsoft, [AWS](/entities/aws.md), and [Google](/entities/google.md) cloud toolkits, with cost kept approachable through templatization and reuse of existing services.

## Why It Exists

- Institutional knowledge only benefits the few who know or remember it; discovery and reuse unlock its value.
- Echo was built because teams could not recall customer stories and case studies accumulated over decades.
- Improving positions Echo as keeping sensitive data in-house while summarizing across sources without hallucinating.
