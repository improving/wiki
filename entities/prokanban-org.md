# ProKanban.org

ProKanban.org is the organization behind the **Applying Professional Kanban (APK)** certification and the publisher of **The Kanban Guide**. In this corpus it is one half of the Kanban-certification landscape, contrasted with [Scrum.org](/entities/scrum-org.md)'s Scrum-flavored equivalent.

## Certification and Materials

- APK is sourced from **The Kanban Guide**, published by ProKanban.org; the Scrum.org counterpart, PSK, is sourced from The Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams.
- ProKanban offers the **PK I certification**, which never expires and carries no ongoing fee, plus a free open assessment.
- Both bodies **reject prescriptive approaches** because team context varies, offering minimal practices teams build on.
- **APK requires no Scrum prerequisite** and stands alone as a Kanban system — the key difference from PSK, which assumes existing Professional Scrum knowledge.
- A two-day class does not make anyone an expert; both courses aim at foundational understanding through discussion and shared experience.

Supporting evidence for this page is currently thin — ProKanban appears in a single summary here — but it is the reference point for standalone [Kanban](/concepts/kanban.md) certification.
