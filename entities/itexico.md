# iTexico

iTexico is a nearshore software firm, headquartered in Austin with delivery offices in Guadalajara and Aguascalientes, Mexico, that merged into Improving. The merger was Improving's entry into the nearshore market and gave it a local presence in Austin.

## Merger with Improving

- iTexico had offices in Guadalajara and Aguascalientes plus sales offices in Dallas, Chicago, and Northern and Southern California.
- The merger added more than 300 professionals and combined annualized revenue above $115 million.
- Its offerings spanned Agile Product Engineering, UI/UX & Design, Mobile, and Cloud and AI, delivered nearshore.
- iTexico had appeared on the Inc. 5000 list five times in the prior six years.
- Curtis Hite cited unusually aligned values and iTexico's commitment to an inclusive culture and delivery excellence; leadership, including CEO and co-founder Anurag Kumar, continued operating the business.

## Role in the Nearshore Footprint

- Improving's nearshore guide names iTexico (with Leniolabs, Zigatta, and MangoChango) among the acquisitions behind its LATAM presence, with delivery centers in Mexico, Guatemala, Chile, and Argentina.
