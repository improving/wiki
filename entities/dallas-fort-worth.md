# Dallas-Fort Worth

The Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, treated across this corpus as one of the few places where the full AI stack converges: infrastructure, talent, capital, and client demand. Its rise is the concrete case study behind the [regional AI economy](/concepts/regional-ai-economy.md) thesis.

## Overview

DFW's momentum shows up in projects and numbers. [Wistron](/entities/wistron.md) chose Fort Worth for a $761M AI supercomputing facility, and [AllianceTexas](/entities/alliancetexas.md) hosts the development. Q3 2026 was the region's best venture quarter on record — $962.5M across 52 deals — capped by [Island](/entities/island.md)'s $400M raise at a $6.4B valuation for enterprise browsers.

## Key Claims

- Wistron's $761M Fort Worth supercomputing project makes DFW load-bearing AI infrastructure, shortening leaders' runway to act.
- 248 data centers are planned across [Texas](/entities/texas.md).
- Proximity of infrastructure, talent, and clients removes excuses to delay AI adoption.
- Client questions shifted from whether to do AI to catching up.
- DFW is becoming where enterprise AI problems get built and governed, as Island's agent-governance browser illustrates.
