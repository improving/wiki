# Mark Hyman

Dr. Mark Hyman is the functional medicine physician whose framing of epigenetics, nutrition, and longevity opened the health journey chronicled in this corpus. His role is that of a gateway: he supplied the initial functional-medicine lens, and later the pointer to Function Health.

## Key Claims

- Functional medicine rather than conventional symptom management prompted the health journey, and Hyman's work is the stated entry point ([health journey](/sources/health-journey.md)).
- The journey's goal — making the 60th birthday life's midpoint — follows that longevity framing, informed by epigenetics and DNA methylation research ([health journey](/sources/health-journey.md)).
- Function Health was discovered through Dr. Mark Hyman; its beta membership offered two rounds of 100+ biomarker tests a year for $499 ([Function Health](/sources/health-journey-function-health.md)).
- Testing is delivered through partner labs such as Quest Labs, with recommendations given from a functional medicine perspective ([Function Health](/sources/health-journey-function-health.md)).
- Supplements and testing services are cataloged as they are tried, making the journey an explicit experiment log rather than a fixed protocol ([health journey](/sources/health-journey.md)).
