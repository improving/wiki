# Uber

Uber serves as the corpus's reference case for AI adoption outrunning accountability. Two posts use its spending as evidence that the hard problem with enterprise AI is not adoption but proof: usage scaled fast, while the line from token spend to customer-visible value stayed undrawable.

## Key Claims

- Uber burned through its entire 2026 AI budget in four months.
- Its COO could not draw a line from usage stats to a 25% increase in useful consumer features.
- Heavy internal incentives drove the burn without a demonstrable link from tokens to shipped features.
- The case anchors the argument that AI's problem is proof, not adoption — prompting Cognition's $10M productivity guarantee for Devin as the contrast.
- It is cited alongside Microsoft's cancellation of most internal Claude Code licenses as evidence of an accountability backlash.
