# Deep Change

Deep change is the corpus's name for transformation that remakes the person or organization rather than merely improving its position. It is set against the easy achievement of showing up and claiming victory.

## Overview

Two posts develop the idea from different directions: one from personal comfort and deliberate difficulty, the other from organizational decline and personal accountability.

## Key Claims

- We doze in easy sunshine but rarely seek fundamental change ([when are we doing hard things](/sources/hard-things.md))
- Showing up and claiming victory is not true change; hard things hurt, stretch, and remake us into something better
- We should experiment with and disrupt what is not broken
- Organizations decline through practiced denial and finger-pointing, and we rarely acknowledge that we ourselves are the problem ([pointing fingers and slow death](/sources/untitled.md))
- If you see something that can be corrected, you must do it — excellence is a form of deviance, achieved by doing what normal people avoid
