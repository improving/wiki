# Kristin Johnson

Kristin Johnson is the [Improving](/entities/improving.md) writer behind most of the firm's acquisition announcements, leadership changes, and corporate newsroom items. Her posts document the company's expansion strategy and executive appointments in a consistent press-release register.

## Work

- Authored acquisition announcements for [Leniolabs](/entities/leniolabs.md), [Bit Quill](/entities/bit-quill.md), [MangoChango](/entities/mangochango.md), and [Code Authority](/entities/code-authority.md).
- Wrote the [Trinity Hunt Partners](/entities/trinity-hunt-partners.md) majority-interest announcement and the creation of a new Microsoft team.
- Covered the [Conscious Capitalism International](/entities/conscious-capitalism-international.md) board appointment and three new C-suite positions.
- Reported the employee tax-savings bonus and various board and executive appointments.

## Themes

- Her writing consistently surfaces [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md), [company culture](/concepts/company-culture.md) as a selection criterion, and the [employee value proposition](/concepts/employee-value-proposition.md).
- Announcements foreground executive quotes from [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) and acquired-company leaders.
