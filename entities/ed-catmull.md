# Ed Catmull

Ed Catmull is a co-founder of Pixar and co-author of *Creativity, Inc.*, which describes the studio's "Brain Trust." Improving writer Blake McMillan draws on that account to argue that Pixar's feedback model shows what product ownership should look like.

## The Brain Trust

- It consists of movie-making subject matter experts who have made films themselves, so their critiques are grounded in shared experience.
- It has no authority over the director's decisions: it brings problems and root causes to the surface rather than prescribing fixes, leaving the director and team to own the outcome.

## Use in This Corpus

- The essay contrasts the Brain Trust with the common anti-pattern where stakeholders create the list of solutions (requirements) and the Product Owner only sequences them.
- Three roadblocks to adopting the Pixar model: Product Owners lack decision authority, teams aren't trusted to create solutions, and stakeholders are incentivized to write solutions instead of naming problems.
- Real ownership yields decisions made closer to the work, less waste on low-value items, and higher team engagement.
