# Minneapolis

Minneapolis is Improving's Twin Cities market and one of its earliest Midwestern expansions. It appears in this corpus as a case of vertical specialization through [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) and as a community-oriented office.

## Entry and Growth

- Improving acquired The Willow Group, Inc., a Minneapolis management and IT consulting firm, expected to add more than $10,000,000 in annualized revenue in 2015.
- The Willow Group was a six-time Inc500/5000 recipient and focused on two verticals — financial services and healthcare — accelerating Improving's move toward vertical specialization; its leadership team stayed on.
- Improving later acquired [Object Partners](/entities/object-partners.md), a Minneapolis custom software firm with a secondary Omaha office, expected to grow annualized revenue by over 25% in 2020 to more than $150 million.
- Object Partners brought extensive healthcare experience, strengthening Improving's North American healthcare vertical; its president and CEO was Chris Spurgat.
- Chris Spurgat later became Improving's Chief Information Officer, and Ehren Seim became President of the Minneapolis enterprise.

## Office and Community

- The Twin Cities office moved to a new Bloomington location with an open atmosphere and a large classroom for process training and community user groups.
- The ribbon cutting drew the Bloomington Chamber of Commerce, and a foot of snow extended the celebration to a second evening with around 100 attendees.
