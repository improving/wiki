# CBRE

CBRE is a global commercial real estate and investment firm that also publishes tech-talent research. In this corpus its rankings are the quantitative backbone of the claim that DFW has become a top AI hiring market.

## Key Claims

- CBRE ranked DFW eighth in North America for tech talent and second in net new tech workers ([DFW is now the AI talent market](/sources/dfw-is-now-the-ai-talent-market.md))
- Its data sits alongside CompTIA's ranking of DFW fifth nationally for AI-specific hiring
- Talent momentum tells you where a market is going, not where it is
- The same story notes Dallas entering the top four startup ecosystems, with local VC assets under management growing from $1.3B in 2020 to $4B and nearly 30,000 specialized AI workers, up 45% year over year ([DFW is now #4](/sources/dfw-is-now-4-and-the-capital-layer-is-the-real-story.md))
- Dallas family offices now deploy into technology at coastal rates
