# Everlywell

Everlywell is a consumer health-testing company whose at-home food sensitivity panel is one of the first instruments used in this corpus's health-journey series. It matters as an early data point in a broader progression of [biomarker testing](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md) services.

## Overview

The Everlywell panel scores reactivity across many foods at once, producing a broad map rather than a single diagnosis. Its results fed directly into dietary change, and the service was later followed by more comprehensive platforms such as [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md).

## Key Claims

- Everlywell's food sensitivity test scores reactivity across many foods, giving a wide view of potential triggers.
- In the author's results only chickpeas showed significant reactivity, while roughly thirty other foods were mildly reactive ([Health Journey: Everlywell](/sources/health-journey-everlywell.md)).
- Elimination testing removes the high-reactivity foods for 30 days before reintroduction, turning a test result into an experiment.
- The later [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md) update reports biological age falling from 47.6 to roughly 40 in six months, with supplementation, recovery, and diet — not workouts — driving the change ([Health Journey: Function Health [Update]](/sources/health-journey-function-health-update.md)).
