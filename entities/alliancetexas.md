# AllianceTexas

A master-planned commercial and industrial development in [Fort Worth](/entities/fort-worth.md) that serves as a physical anchor of [Dallas-Fort Worth](/entities/dallas-fort-worth.md)'s [regional AI economy](/concepts/regional-ai-economy.md). It appears in this corpus as the site where major infrastructure investments land.

## Overview

AllianceTexas is the host site for [Wistron](/entities/wistron.md)'s $761 million AI supercomputing facility — the project that made Fort Worth a tier-one AI city in the corpus's telling. The development's scale and existing logistics and power footprint make it a natural landing zone for the 248 data centers planned across [Texas](/entities/texas.md).

## Key Claims

- Wistron's $761M AI supercomputing facility is being built at AllianceTexas in Fort Worth.
- The project makes DFW load-bearing AI infrastructure, shortening leaders' runway to act.
- The concentration of data centers raises grid and cost-allocation questions for the state.
- AllianceTexas sits at the center of the region's infrastructure-and-talent convergence story.
