# John Boyd

John Boyd was a United States Air Force fighter pilot and military strategist, best known for the OODA loop (observe–orient–decide–act). He is cited in Daniel Scheufler's Improving essay "From AI Automation to AI Augmentation" for the ordering "people, process, technology" — the idea that technology adoption succeeds only when the people and process dimensions are addressed first.

## Use in This Corpus

- The essay argues AI is an adapter that transforms one specialist's time into average-level output in another domain, so the scarce skill becomes clearly articulating intent and "done."
- Prior transformations such as ERP in manufacturing and Agile produced immense but uneven results; the difference was organizations that rewrote how they worked rather than bolting on the technology.
- Boyd's people-first ordering supports the claim that AI's benefit only materializes if the organization can absorb the extra output.
