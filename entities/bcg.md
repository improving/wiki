# BCG

Boston Consulting Group is a global consultancy. This corpus uses its market projection to size the agentic AI opportunity and cites its job cuts as evidence of the sector-wide talent reset.

## Key Claims

- BCG projects $200B in net new tech services demand from agentic AI ([the $200B gap](/sources/the-200b-gap-is-yours-if-you-want-it.md))
- Big 4 firms price out mid-market work by design, moving upmarket and leaving that gap open to boutique consultancies
- 75% of mid-market enterprises want a partner to build agentic AI, and at that scale proximity, trust, and delivery speed beat brand
- BCG cut jobs in the same quarter as [McKinsey & Company](/entities/mckinsey-company.md) and [Bain](/entities/bain.md), targeting junior research and back-office roles ([the premium you charged](/sources/the-premium-you-charged-was-for-knowing-things.md))
- One post argues AI replaced the expertise that justified firms as knowledge intermediaries, forcing them to sell judgment instead
