# Atulpriya Sharma

Atulpriya Sharma is an [Improving](/entities/improving.md) writer and platform-engineering practitioner, covering [golden paths](/concepts/golden-path.md), internal developer platforms, and [developer experience](/concepts/developer-experience.md). He is a contributor to the [CNCF](/entities/cncf.md) Platform Engineering Maturity Model and writes from conference talks and client engagements.

## Work

- Authored "Everyone Talks About Golden Paths. Nobody Talks About Building Them," mapping the CNCF maturity model onto a four-phase build sequence.
- Wrote "Platform Engineering Maturity," on why teams stall at Level 2 and how parameterized golden paths carry them to self-service.
- Authored "Internal Developer Portal (IDP): What Reduces Cognitive Load," cataloguing portal failure modes and fixes.
- Wrote "How to Fail at Platform Engineering," a six-mode failure catalogue.
- Authored "Know Your Developer," contrasting DX strategy for internal versus external developers.
- Was selected as an [Improving 100](/entities/improving-100.md) winner in the Community Impact category.
