# IDLife

IDLife is a supplement company whose pitch is personalization: a DNA test determines which supplements go into an individual's daily packs. In this corpus it is one component of a wider [supplement stacking](/concepts/supplement-stacking.md) routine and a case study in treating supplement quality as the variable that matters.

## Overview

The author adopted IDLife for personalized packs and for IDLife Hydrate, a low-sugar hydration product used during workouts. Its results are assessed through the same testing loop as the rest of the stack.

## Key Claims

- IDLife starts with a DNA test to personalize its supplement packs.
- Supplement quality and bioavailability matter more than which vitamin you take.
- Switching from Liquid IV to IDLife Hydrate stopped the glucose spikes visible on the author's CGM.
- IDLife products appear in the daily stack split across morning, lunch, and evening, alongside [AG1](/entities/ag1.md) and NAD.
- The stack's effects are judged against six-month baselines from [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md) testing.
