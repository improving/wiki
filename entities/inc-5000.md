# Inc. 5000

The Inc. 5000 is Inc. magazine's annual ranking of the fastest-growing private companies in the United States, ordered by revenue growth over a three-year period. It recurs in the corpus as the yardstick for Improving's sustained growth and as a credibility signal buyers are advised to check when selecting a services partner.

## Improving's Record

- Improving announced its 15th appearance on the list, with 2024 rankings based on percentage revenue growth from 2020 through 2023.
- In its 14th year Improving ranked No. 3820; the 2023 class added 1,187,266 jobs over three years.
- In 2017 Improving made the list for a ninth consecutive year at #4461, one of only 81 other firms to have done so.
- In 2016 it appeared for an eighth straight year at #3,934, among 116 other companies with eight years running.

## As a Credibility Signal

- An offshore partner-vetting guide lists Inc. 5000 placement among credibility signals, alongside Best Places to Work awards and ISO 9001 and CMMI certifications.
- Acquired firm DragonSpears had ranked on the Inc. 5000 four years in a row before its merger.
