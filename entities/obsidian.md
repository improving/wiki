# Obsidian

Obsidian is the markdown note-taking tool the author adopted as the vehicle for deliberate deep thinking. It matters in this corpus because it is presented less as a productivity app than as a way to capture and connect ideas instead of skimming past them.

## Key Claims

- Markdown and linking in Obsidian match how the author thinks associatively ([Obsidian Obsessed](/sources/obsidian-obsessed.md)).
- The author otherwise defaults to skimming rather than reading deeply; Obsidian provides the vehicle to capture and integrate notes ([Deep Thinking](/sources/deep-thinking.md)).
- Intentional time plus the right materials is what enables deep thinking, tooling alone is not enough ([Deep Thinking](/sources/deep-thinking.md)).
- The to-do feature creates an extra inbox to process and adds friction to the workflow ([Obsidian Obsessed](/sources/obsidian-obsessed.md)).
- Writing close to the captured notes supports more consistent posting ([Obsidian Obsessed](/sources/obsidian-obsessed.md)).
- The author still prefers a physical journal for morning quiet time ([Obsidian Obsessed](/sources/obsidian-obsessed.md)).
