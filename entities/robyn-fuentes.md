# Robyn Fuentes

Robyn Fuentes is a tenured [Improving](/entities/improving.md) leader who became President of Improving [Houston](/entities/houston.md) and Austin, and who authored the firm's practitioner piece on [microservices](/concepts/microservices.md) trade-offs. She appears in this corpus as both a delivery-side technologist and a voice in Improving's culture and inclusion work.

## Role at Improving

- Promoted to President of Improving Houston and Austin as part of a 2026 leadership slate in which every role was filled by an existing Improver; she had been with the firm 10 years.
- Set a 2026 theme of "excellence in the fundamentals."
- Her promotion is cited as an example of [career development](/concepts/career-development.md) and [mentorship](/concepts/mentorship.md) through growing leaders from within.

## Writing on Delivery

- Authored "Microservices Architecture: Moving Past the Buzz Words," arguing that [domain-driven design](/concepts/domain-driven-design.md) and delivery discipline — automation, DevOps, extensive testing, clear messaging patterns — determine whether a microservices shift succeeds.
- Warns that avoiding microservices' intentional disadvantages can produce a "microservice-monolith" with the worst of both approaches, and that [technical debt](/concepts/technical-debt.md) accumulates when architecture changes without changing the design process.

## Culture

- Contributed to Improving's International Women's Day 2022 Q&A, arguing that interests and expertise are not tied to gender and that people should be their own biggest cheerleaders.
- Her comments connect to [Women of Improving](/entities/women-of-improving.md) and Improving's [inclusion and diversity](/concepts/inclusion-and-diversity.md) work.
