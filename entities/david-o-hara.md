# David O'Hara

David O'Hara is a consultant and writer whose blog, *Driven To Develop*, supplies much of this corpus. Two threads of his work appear here: a professional argument about durable human skills, and a personal record of health experimentation.

## Key Claims

- In a Forbes Business Council article he argued that people skills remain essential in the age of AI ([Forbes](/sources/forbes.md))
- He documented a recovery studio protocol alternating cold plunge and infrared sauna that dropped morning blood pressure 15-20 points ([health journey: augment](/sources/health-journey-augment.md))
- Cold plunging right after weight training blunted recovery and raised body fat on a DEXA scan
- He frames voluntarily choosing discomfort — hormesis — as building physical and mental resilience
