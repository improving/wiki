# Curtis Hite

Curtis Hite is co-founder, CEO, and chairman of [Improving](/entities/improving.md), and the executive most associated with the firm's growth, its Conscious Capitalism philosophy, and its acquisition-led expansion. He appears throughout the corpus as both newsmaker and quotable voice.

## Role at Improving

- Co-founded Improving in 2007 and has led it from a Dallas custom-software firm to a multi-country consultancy.
- Serves as CEO and Chairman; under his leadership Improving expanded across North and South America and India.
- Holds bachelor's and master's degrees in Computer Science from Texas A&M and served as commanding officer in the Aggie Corps of Cadets.
- Received the Dwight Look College of Engineering's Early Professional Achievement Award as its youngest recipient and first distinguished alumnus, plus the Distinguished Alumni Award in 2009.

## Recognition and Philosophy

- Named a finalist for the EY Entrepreneur Of The Year 2025 Southwest Award, crediting the whole team over 18 years.
- Joined the [Conscious Capitalism International](/entities/conscious-capitalism-international.md) Board of Directors in 2018, alongside figures such as [John Mackey](/entities/john-mackey.md); Conscious Capitalism is described as Improving's foundation and philosophy.
- Frames company success as "a consequence of our collective involvement," tying recognition and rewards to culture.

## Deals and Culture

- Partnered with [Trinity Hunt Partners](/entities/trinity-hunt-partners.md) when it took majority interest, remaining a significant shareholder.
- Quoted across acquisitions including [Leniolabs](/entities/leniolabs.md) and [Bit Quill](/entities/bit-quill.md).
- Hosts the [Improving 100](/entities/improving-100.md) welcome reception and speaks at company gatherings.
