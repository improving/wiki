# HealthSpan

HealthSpan is the healthspan-extension protocol tracked in this corpus, centered on the drug [rapamycin](/concepts/rapamycin.md). It is the corpus's clearest example of a self-experiment that produced a real negative result rather than a testimonial.

## Overview

The two sources form a before-and-after pair: an initial rationale for weekly rapamycin dosing, and an update discontinuing it. Read together they illustrate why [biomarker testing](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md) and honest reporting of adverse effects matter in longevity practice.

## Key Claims

- Rapamycin inhibits mTOR, mimicking caloric restriction and triggering autophagy; dosing weekly on rest days avoids interfering with muscle building ([Health Journey: Healthspan and Rapamycin](/sources/health-journey-healthspan.md)).
- [Peter Attia](/entities/peter-attia.md)'s [Outlive](/entities/outlive.md) prompted the rapamycin experiment.
- Rapamycin caused a hollowed-out feeling, skin issues, and a higher resting heart rate ([HealthSpan - Update](/sources/health-journey-healthspan-update.md)).
- Symptoms returned at half dose, confirming the protocol caused them, so rapamycin was discontinued.
- The conclusion drawn is that individual biology varies, so listen to your body — an argument for [supplement stacking](/concepts/supplement-stacking.md) caution rather than enthusiasm.
