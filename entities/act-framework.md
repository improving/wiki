# ACT Framework

The ACT Framework is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s structured delivery approach for IT projects, standing for Anchor, Consistency, and Transition. It is pitched as a way to raise project success rates and stakeholder trust when unmanaged complexity would otherwise cause poor communication, rising costs, and lower quality.

## The Three Phases

- Anchor builds trust and understanding of the vision and goals while putting foundational elements in place.
- Consistency delivers regular value with disciplined practice, supported by shared success measures, defined roles, and visibility.
- Transition delivers measurable outcomes and equips the client for ongoing success.

## Claims and Support

- The write-up cites PMI data that organizations with a consistent project-management approach report 38% more projects meeting original goals.
- Improving supports adoption with a Launch Workshop, an Engagement HomeBase, and biweekly Calibration Cadence meetings.
- The framework is positioned as part of [digital transformation](/concepts/digital-transformation.md) delivery and is used internally and by other organizations.
