# Chile

Chile is a South American nearshore destination whose capital, Santiago, is cited as a hub for IT consulting and cloud solutions. It matters to this corpus because Chile was one of the talent locations of [Leniolabs](/entities/leniolabs.md), the firm [Improving](/entities/improving.md) acquired to establish its [South America](/entities/south-america.md) presence.

## Role as a Nearshore Hub

- Santiago is named alongside Buenos Aires as a skilled IT market, with Argentina and Chile having invested heavily in STEM education.
- Chile is described as a stable, business-friendly destination with strong IP laws similar to the US and a serious approach to data protection.
- It appears among the key LATAM locations named across Improving's nearshore guides, alongside Guadalajara, Guatemala City, and Buenos Aires.

## At Improving

- The [Leniolabs acquisition](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md) brought talent in Chile, Argentina, Spain, and the US, extending Improving's development services into South America.
- Chile is listed as one of Improving's LATAM delivery centers — with Mexico, Guatemala, and Argentina — supporting 600+ nearshore developers.
- Chile and Argentina were featured as a cross-cultural monthly theme in Improving's 2025 Come Together year, reflecting the company's expanding global family.
