# Docker

A container platform that the corpus references as a lesson in technology adoption and trust. It appears as an illustration on [Rogers' diffusion curve](/concepts/diffusion-of-innovations.md) and in the argument that DevOps stalled on trust rather than tooling.

## Overview

The corpus uses Docker-era DevOps to frame agentic AI: adoption leaders stalled on trust questions, not capability questions, and most organizations sit at Stage 2 or 3 of the [AI Trust Evolution](/concepts/ai-trust-evolution.md). [Observability](/concepts/three-pillars-of-observability.md), reversibility, and [boundary enforcement](/concepts/boundary-enforcement.md) are preconditions for extending autonomy — the same scaffolding Docker-era teams needed.

## Key Claims

- DevOps adoption stalled because of trust, not tooling limitations.
- Applying an Innovator strategy as a Late Majority organization is the common diffusion mistake.
- Agentic AI leaders stall on trust questions, not capability questions.
- Observability, reversibility, and boundary enforcement are preconditions for extending autonomy.
