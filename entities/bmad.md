# BMAD

BMAD is an agentic-AI software development framework that wraps work in a cast of agent personas — product manager, analyst, architect, developer, tester, and Scrum Master. It appears in this corpus as "training wheels" that let a product-side role experience scope, cost, and architectural trade-offs firsthand.

## Role in the Corpus

- A product owner and Scrum Master used BMAD's "party mode" agents, which ask sharper scoping questions than many real meetings, exposing fuzzy users, scope, and value propositions.
- He built a throwaway Cynefin facilitation app with a 72-hour data-retention backend, going from idea to a working front end in a single afternoon.
- An analyst agent rewrote his PRD with clearer problem statements, explicit hardware and training targets, and a challenge to a premature dependency.
- The point is not to become a developer but to feel the trade-offs, which makes a product partner more honest and useful; the slow part of delivery is now thinking, not typing.
- Suggested playbook: pick a tiny selfish problem, commit one weekend, lean into agent conversations rather than code, and bring one artifact back to the team.
