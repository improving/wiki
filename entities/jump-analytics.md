# Jump Analytics

Jump Analytics is a Canadian technology firm specializing in advanced analytics and corporate performance management, acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) to push annualized revenue above $290 million and deepen its business-intelligence capability in Canada.

## Company

- Specializes in Microsoft Data Analytics and SAP Analytics.
- Offices in Toronto, Vancouver, and Calgary.
- Has delivered solutions in over 20 industries, including utilities, mining, oil and gas, manufacturing, retail, and life sciences.
- Leadership stayed intact and continued to operate and grow the business.

## Deal

- Expected to raise Improving's annualized revenue above $290 million and extend its North American reach.
- CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) framed the move as expanding capability and customer service.
- Followed Improving's "Enterprise Strategy" of merging like-minded technology services firms committed to trust, excellence, and culture.
