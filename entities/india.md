# India

India is the dominant [offshore outsourcing](/concepts/offshore-outsourcing.md) destination in this corpus — the largest and deepest engineering talent pool — and the base of Improving's cloud-native and AI capabilities through its InfraCloud acquisition.

## The Offshore Case

- India alone produces over 1.5 million engineering graduates a year, offering unmatched scale and a 10–12 hour offset that supports follow-the-sun work — at the cost of real-time collaboration and higher attrition.
- Offshore delivery more broadly is framed around technical depth, delivery maturity, security and compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA), and cultural fit; ~53% of companies name data security as a top concern and ~54% report cultural or communication challenges.
- A hybrid model that blends a collaboration-friendly nearshore region with a scale-friendly one like India is recommended over picking a single hub.

## At Improving

- Improving acquired InfraCloud, a Pune-based AI cloud services and application modernization firm founded in 2016 and one of the first Kubernetes partners, lifting annualized revenue above $270 million; its leadership stayed in place.
- The deal strengthened Improving's [container orchestration](/concepts/container-orchestration.md) and AI infrastructure expertise and extended its delivery into Asia.
- Improving operated 19 offices across North and South America and India at the time of the deal, later described as 21 offices across three regions.
- Ashok Iyengar was appointed EVP of Global Services to lead sales across 21 offices in North America, South America, and India.
- Improving India works remotely; Atulpriya Sharma was the first India winner of [Improving 100](/entities/improving-100.md), in the Community Impact category, and the 2025 Las Vegas retreat was the first to include India.
