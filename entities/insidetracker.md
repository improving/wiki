# InsideTracker

InsideTracker is a bloodwork platform that combines tested biomarkers into a biological-age estimate called InnerAge. It is one of the testing services catalogued in this corpus's health-journey series, and its verdict is notably mixed.

## Overview

InsideTracker appears at the start of the health journey, framed by [functional medicine](/concepts/functional-medicine.md) and an interest in epigenetics and [biological age](/concepts/biological-age.md). The follow-up review treats it as useful data whose engagement mechanics failed in daily practice.

## Key Claims

- InsideTracker's InnerAge combines tested biomarkers into a single biological age estimate ([Health Journey: InsideTracker](/sources/health-journey-insidetracker.md)).
- The platform's bloodwork was solid but not compelling enough to keep; daily achievement tracking did not stick and was abandoned after a week.
- Multiple tests over time matter more than a single data point — the core argument for repeated [biomarker testing](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md).
- The series began with the aim of making the 60th birthday life's midpoint, tracking supplements and testing services as they are tried ([Health Journey](/sources/health-journey.md)).
- Functional medicine prompted the journey, with epigenetics and DNA methylation research informing the interest in biological age.
