# James Clear

James Clear is the author of *Atomic Habits*, cited in Improving's trust-behavior series as the growth analogy for the behavior "Deliver Results." His habit-building frame supports the essay's argument that consistency over years, not a single outcome, is what builds trust and career capital.

## Use in This Corpus

- Devlin Liles's essay works through four myths about delivering results: effort alone is not enough; the result is not all that matters (how you achieve it counts); hitting a peak does not earn a promotion; and the need to deliver never ends.
- Consistency in delivering results over years is what builds trust and career capital — the same compounding logic Clear describes for habits.
