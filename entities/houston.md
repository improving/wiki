# Houston

Houston is one of Improving's Texas enterprise locations and a recurring base for its community-engagement work. The city appears in this corpus as a training and user-group hub, the site of startup-education and youth AI programs, and an early proving ground for the firm's "Enterprise Model."

## Improving's Presence

- Improving entered Houston by acquiring Alphaware Training Center, its third Enterprise Model integration, adding 100+ technology training classes and a Southwest presence.
- The Houston office moved from Bellaire to the Energy Corridor, adding two training classrooms for software and process training, a project area for onsite development teams, and space for community user groups; over 140 guests attended the ribbon cutting.
- Devlin Liles was Houston President at the time; [Robyn Fuentes](/entities/robyn-fuentes.md) later became President of Improving Houston and Austin with a 2026 theme of "excellence in the fundamentals."

## Community Engagement

- Improving partnered with [The Ion](/entities/the-ion.md), Houston's innovation hub, to run "Ion Startup University powered by Improving," a free six-month curriculum on product thinking, pitching, and networking.
- The three-part [AI Summer Camp](/entities/ai-summer-camp.md) taught high school students and their parents to use AI responsibly as a thought partner, covering what AI is and isn't, using it on real tasks, and study strategies.
- Houston's Come Together activities included training for the Improving MS 150 cycling team.
