# Rob Ulaszek

Rob Ulaszek is an Improving writer focused on the Microsoft Power Platform. His posts cover [Power Apps](/entities/power-apps.md), Copilot Studio, and the rebuilt Microsoft Planner, pairing concrete product walkthroughs with honest caveats about the low-code and citizen-developer model.

## Work

- Wrote the Power Apps overview covering canvas vs. model-driven apps, hundreds of connectors, and use cases such as airline cabin-crew maintenance reporting, plus limitations like weak source control and pixel-perfect UI.
- Wrote "Better Power Apps by Design," arguing that adding UX and architectural design turns a bare-bones low-code app into a maintainable product; a client's 8-week traditional build became a 1-week bare app, extended to 3 weeks with design.
- Wrote the low/no-code AI knowledge-management piece pitching Copilot Studio for natural-language Q&A over corporate knowledge deployed in Teams and SharePoint.
- Traced Microsoft's project-management history from 1984 desktop Project to the cloud-native Planner, covering Copilot plan creation and Power BI reporting.

## Themes

- Low-code shifts application work from IT to citizen developers but still needs developer-minded resources for maintainability.
- Design investment is what separates throwaway low-code apps from durable ones.
- AI copilots are a natural-language front end over existing knowledge and workflow systems.
