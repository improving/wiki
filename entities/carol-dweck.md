# Carol Dweck

Carol Dweck is the psychologist behind the "growth mindset" — the belief that ability and resilience are built rather than fixed. Improving writer Kris Wickett cites her work as the differentiator between thriving and surviving companies in "Agility: to Scale or Not to Scale is NOT the Right Question."

## Use in This Corpus

- The essay distinguishes "scaling Agile" (spreading Scrum across teams) from "Agile at scale" (making the whole organization able to sense, respond, and adapt).
- Agile at scale rests on systems thinking, lean thinking, and a growth mindset — not on any single scaling framework.
- The four most popular scaling frameworks are Nexus, LeSS, SAFe, and Disciplined Agile, and there is no best framework across the board; fit depends on size, culture, and team maturity.
- Alignment on delighting the customer produces autonomy, and autonomy produces agility at scale.
