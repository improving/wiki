# Kathy Sierra

Kathy Sierra is an author and programmer, known for the principle that good products make users feel like "badasses." Two Improving essays by Claudio Lassala invoke her work — once to describe the Product Owner's enduring judgment, and once to explain what over-engineering gets backwards.

## Use in This Corpus

- In "Why AI Skills Are Now Part of the Product Owner's Playbook," AI compresses the information-handling parts of product ownership, but the PO remains a "Badass Maker" in Sierra's spirit: AI can generate a spec or prototype, but only the PO can judge whether it solves the right problem for the right user.
- In "Hidden Cost of Over-Engineering Enterprise Applications," Sierra's principle is flipped by over-engineering, which makes the developer the hero and leaves the user with a button that says "Delete" when they mean "Cancel."
- The over-engineering essay's tell is machine-facing language — a "linguistic tax" on users — and its root cause is starting with the tool instead of the problem.
