# Great Place to Work

Great Place to Work is a workplace-certification and research organization that certifies and ranks employers based on employee feedback. It appears in the corpus as the body behind Improving's Best Workplaces recognitions and as the source of a survey on recognition.

## Improving's Awards

- Improving ranked number 28 on the 2024 Best Workplaces for Millennials list from Great Place to Work and Fortune.
- Rankings are based on employee feedback about workplace experience, which the organizations say predicts retention, agility, and business success.
- Great Place to Work is cited as the certification context for Improving's Texas Monthly Best Company to Work for award.

## Cited Research

- A Great Place to Work survey found recognition is the most important driver of great work, cited in an essay adapting the five love languages to leadership.
