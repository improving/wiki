# Albert Mehrabian

Albert Mehrabian is the psychologist behind the "7-38-55" model of communication, which Improving's remote-work essay cites to explain why virtual teams lose signal. The model attributes roughly 55% of communication to body language and 38% to tone, leaving only 7% to words.

## Use in This Corpus

- The essay cautions that context matters and words remain essential in written and technical settings, but notes virtual work loses the non-verbal richness, so people lean on written and verbal clarity alone.
- That loss is why alignment must be built deliberately in remote teams, rather than assumed from shared words.
- The essay pairs Mehrabian's model with Lencioni's five dysfunctions to argue the deeper problem is a trust gap.
