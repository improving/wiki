# Jira

Jira is the issue and project-tracking tool that recurs across this corpus as the assumed backlog system of a delivery team. It appears less as a subject in its own right than as a marker: of the Scrum Master role that should move past ticket-policing, of the collaboration stack a distributed team runs on, and of the tooling access a mature delivery partner provides.

## Role in the Corpus

- The modern [Scrum mastery](/concepts/scrum-mastery.md) argument says the role is "no longer scheduling standups and being 'Jira police'"; the 2020 [Scrum Guide](/entities/scrum-guide.md) reframes it as a servant-leader, coach, and change agent.
- In a [product ownership](/concepts/product-ownership.md) automation demo, Jira is named as a similar target tool to [Notion](/entities/notion.md) for MCP-driven PRD-to-task generation.
- Jira sits in the integrated collaboration stack for distributed teams, alongside Asana and Azure DevOps for tracking, Slack/Teams for communication, and Confluence/Notion for knowledge.

## Delivery-Maturity Signal

- The offshore-partner vetting guide treats direct access to project-tracking tools like Jira or Azure DevOps as evidence of delivery maturity and governance — more important than raw coding ability.
