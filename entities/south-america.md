# South America

South America is the southern arm of [Latin America](/entities/latin-america.md)'s nearshore corridor and the region [Improving](/entities/improving.md) entered through its [Leniolabs](/entities/leniolabs.md) acquisition. The move extended Improving's development services into South America and pushed its annualized revenue past $280 million.

## Why South America

- Shared time zones with the US and Canada enable real-time collaboration without the delays of Asia and Europe.
- Argentina and Chile have invested heavily in STEM education, producing talent in software development, AI, data analytics, and cybersecurity, with Buenos Aires and Santiago as the named hubs.
- Cultural similarity to North America and Europe plus strong English proficiency reduce collaboration friction.
- Proximity lowers travel cost and time, making it practical for clients to visit teams and collaborate on complex projects.

## Improving South America

- Leniolabs (founded 2013) specialized in backend, frontend, and data technologies using agile methods, with a focus on AI projects on cloud services and language models.
- Leniolabs became "Improving South America" and had been remote-first since 2013; the post argues culture and structured communication — not proximity — are what make such teams work.
- Leniolabs leadership stayed in place and continued to manage and grow the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."
- Improving's 2025 Las Vegas retreat was the first to bring South America, Guatemala, and India together with teams from Mexico, Canada, and the USA.
