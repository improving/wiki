# Microsoft Teams

Microsoft Teams is Microsoft's collaboration hub, consolidating chat, meetings, files, and front-line work into one surface. Improving's writing covers it as a consolidation target for other Microsoft apps, a paid-premium meeting platform, and the host for Copilot and low-code tools.

## Key Claims

- Microsoft retired StaffHub, its shift-and-schedule app for front-line workers, on October 1, 2019, folding its features into Teams' Home screen and Shifts tab.
- Teams Premium costs $10 per person per month and adds watermarking, end-to-end encryption for meetings and calls, reusable compliance templates, live translated captions, and larger Town Hall capacity.
- [Microsoft Copilot](/entities/microsoft-copilot.md) in Teams transcribes meetings and generates summaries and action items; one consultant estimates the transcribe-and-summarize feature saves roughly 4–10 hours of work per week.
- Transcription appears limited to about 18,000 words, so long meetings should be broken into 60-minute blocks, and separate policies are needed for 1:1 versus 3+ person meetings.
- Teams focuses on task management and project groups, whereas [Microsoft Viva](/entities/microsoft-viva.md) Engage focuses on community and culture; the two integrate rather than replace each other.
- Teams is a host surface for Microsoft Planner, Power Apps embedding, and Copilot Studio copilots, and is cited as a collaborative platform enabling remote and hybrid work.
