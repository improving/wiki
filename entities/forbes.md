# Forbes

Forbes is a business publication that appears in this corpus as a platform for practitioner argument rather than a news source. Two Forbes pieces anchor claims about how businesses should be run and what skills still matter as AI spreads.

## Overview

The relevant material comes through Forbes' contributor channels — one essay on [conscious capitalism](/concepts/conscious-capitalism.md) and one Business Council article on people skills. Both argue from experience rather than data, so their claims are best read as positions to weigh against other sources.

## Key Claims

- A Forbes piece argues conscious capitalism assumes profit but focuses on how it is spent, and extends value beyond shareholders to partners and community via the [stakeholder model](/concepts/stakeholder-model.md) ([How Conscious Capitalism Can Help You And Your Business Grow](/sources/a-case-for-conscious-capitalism.md)).
- The same essay holds that culture happens by default or by decision and needs deliberate attention — a claim tied to [company culture](/concepts/company-culture.md).
- A 2024 Forbes Business Council article argues that people skills remain essential in the age of AI ([Forbes: Why People Skills Are Still Important In The Age of AI](/sources/forbes.md)).
- Both pieces land on the same theme: growing yourself is the best way to grow your business.
