# Patrick Lencioni

Patrick Lencioni is the author of *The Five Dysfunctions of a Team*, cited in an Improving essay on remote-work communication. His model supplies the diagnosis for a team that misses a deadline even though everyone "did their part": the root challenge is usually a trust gap, not a lack of effort.

## Use in This Corpus

- The absence of trust means people stay silent rather than admit confusion or disagree, and fear of conflict makes even well-intentioned disagreement feel risky, so people nod along instead of questioning.
- The essay argues agreement is not alignment: alignment is active and depends on shared interpretation, not on hearing the same words.
- Remedies follow from the model — encourage curiosity over certainty, model vulnerability, normalize "can we double-check we're on the same page?", and invite healthy tension.
