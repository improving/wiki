# Hard Fork

Hard Fork is a technology podcast from The New York Times. It shows up in this corpus as a recurring venue where the cultural and governance consequences of AI consolidation get argued out — from who controls recommendation to who holds frontier models accountable.

## Overview

- The curation discussion treats a handful of general-purpose assistants as replacing many independent recommendation systems, collapsing cultural filters into four or five models.
- Lepore's framing, discussed on the show, is that AI amounts to corporate rule by machine, concentrating infrastructure control in four or five firms.
- Frontier model terms are described as private contract rather than public accountability.
- The comparison offered is five industries each hiring their own consultant versus all hiring the same one.
- Whether competition or structural concentration wins is left unsettled.
