# Latin America

Latin America is the premier [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md) region in this corpus: geographically close, time-zone-aligned, culturally familiar, and cost-competitive relative to onshore delivery. It anchors [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s global delivery model, which the company expanded repeatedly through acquisition.

## The Nearshore Case

- Nearshore teams maintain a 6–8 hour overlap with US business hours, enabling live stand-ups and faster feedback; one estimate puts the general LATAM overlap at 4–8 hours with US and Canadian hours.
- Nearshore services typically cut costs 20–40% versus onshore without sacrificing quality, and savings can be reinvested in innovation.
- The region's talent base is deep: 437 universities across Latin America produce over 220,000 STEM graduates a year, feeding a pool of more than 2 million developers.
- Statista projects the nearshore market to grow at a CAGR above 12% over five years, as North American organizations prioritize agile delivery, real-time collaboration, and proximity.

## Improving's Footprint

- Improving is credited with 600+ nearshore developers, 500+ outsourcing projects, 30+ industries, and a 94% client satisfaction score, with LATAM centers in Mexico, Guatemala, Chile, and Argentina.
- The footprint was deepened through the [iTexico](/entities/itexico.md), [Leniolabs](/entities/leniolabs.md), Zigatta, and [MangoChango](/entities/mangochango.md) acquisitions.
- Key locations named across the sources include Guadalajara and Aguascalientes in Mexico, Guatemala City, Costa Rica, Buenos Aires, and Santiago.
- One essay argues that for Mexico specifically, the stronger reason to nearshore is innovation and partnership, not cost savings alone — a [stakeholder orientation](/concepts/stakeholder-orientation.md) framing.
