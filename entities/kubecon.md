# KubeCon

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon is the [CNCF](/entities/cncf.md)'s flagship conference series for the cloud-native community. In this corpus it is the venue for a cluster of Improving talks and write-ups on [platform engineering](/concepts/platform-engineering.md), security, and developer experience — most of the platform-engineering pieces here originate as KubeCon talks.

## Talks and Write-Ups Drawn From It

- **KubeCon Europe 2026 Platform Engineering Day** — Atulpriya Sharma's talk on building [golden paths](/concepts/golden-path.md) incrementally (standardize, validate, GitOps, self-service) without a full IDP.
- **A KubeCon London talk on "Abstraction Debt"** — the recurring finding that teams have golden paths, catalogs, and guardrails, yet still read logs and SSH into clusters when things break.
- **KubeCon India keynotes by Sonali Srivastava** — the human cost of security in the AI era, and cloud-native security as shared responsibility.
- **A KubeCon + CloudNativeCon India 2025 panel** — security mapped through five lenses: developers, security engineers, product managers, platform engineers, and leadership.
- **A KubeCon India keynote on "Know Your Developer"** — distinct DX playbooks for internal versus external developers.

## Why It Recurs

- It is where the [CNCF](/entities/cncf.md) Platform Engineering Maturity Model and the golden-path discussion are aired, so its talks supply much of this corpus's platform vocabulary.
- Write-ups often pair an event talk with agent-era extensions, such as treating AI agents as a new class of privileged platform operator.
