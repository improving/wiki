# Globant

Globant is a Latin-America-rooted digital engineering firm that recurs in Improving's partner buyer's guides as a benchmark competitor, and in the corpus's AI-pricing writing as an early mover on token-metered commercial models. It appears as the "digitally native" counterpart to traditional IT services firms across both the nearshore and offshore comparisons.

## In the Partner Guides

- Named among the twelve leading nearshore providers in Improving's nearshore guide, described as a digital-first LATAM provider.
- Listed in the offshore buyer's guide as a "digitally native engineering firm" among sixteen offshore partners.

## AI-Era Pricing

- Globant's AI Pods are token-metered — roughly 100M tokens for about $20,000 per month — a break from headcount-based revenue.
- The source argues this is a metered subscription rather than strict outcome pricing, illustrating how delivery firms are repackaging work as AI consumption.
