# Women of Improving

Women of Improving is an employee-led initiative at [Improving](/entities/improving.md), envisioned by Chief of Staff Susan Fojtasek, to help women build long careers in IT. It runs global virtual and hybrid events plus localized office gatherings, and frames [belonging](/concepts/belonging.md) and [inclusion and diversity](/concepts/inclusion-and-diversity.md) as business strengths rather than slogans.

## Program

- Hosts four to five global virtual or hybrid events a year spanning regions, time zones, and roles.
- Topics center on career confidence, self-advocacy, imposter syndrome, work-life balance, and well-being, discussed in deliberately safe spaces.
- Each office has a leader who localizes events and hosts in-person gatherings for deeper local relationships.
- Encourages women to act as visible role models beyond Improving via student talks and community volunteering.
- The initiative's annual International Women's Day Q&As profile women across the company on what inclusion means and the advice they would give their former selves.

## Themes

- Contributors stress tenacity, owning your strengths, and believing in your own abilities over fitting a stereotype.
- [imposter syndrome](/concepts/imposter-syndrome.md) recurs as a shared, discussable experience rather than a private failing.
- Flexible, remote structures are framed as making IT a sustainable long-term career across life stages.
