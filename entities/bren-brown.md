# Brené Brown

Brené Brown is a researcher and author known for work on vulnerability, shame, empathy, and courage. In this corpus she is cited on the proper place of emotions and appears alongside the four writing roles.

## Key Claims

- Emotions are great servants but poor masters — they inform but should not steer ([emotions](/sources/emotions.md))
- Feelings are not who we are and should not direct our actions
- "Follow your passion" fails because it puts emotions before actions, inverting the real order
- Working hard and becoming good at something is what creates passion ([follow your passion myth](/concepts/follow-your-passion-myth.md))
- She is cited in a post on the [madman, architect, carpenter, judge](/concepts/madman-architect-carpenter-judge.md) roles, where separating generation from judgment is the discipline ([the four roles](/sources/madman-architect-carpenter-and-judge.md))
