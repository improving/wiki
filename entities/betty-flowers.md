# Betty Flowers

Betty Flowers is the writing teacher credited with the four-role framework for drafting: madman, architect, carpenter, and judge. This corpus treats her framework less as writing advice than as a model for any creative or analytical work.

## Overview

Flowers's insight is that the roles are not styles to choose between but stages that must occur in order. Two posts extend it from prose to strategy, team design, and building AI systems.

## Key Claims

- Flowers identified four thinking modes that must occur in sequence, not simultaneously ([beyond writing](/sources/beyond-writing-madman-architect-carpenter-judge.md))
- The judge showing up early kills divergent generation — the failure mode is criticism arriving before material exists
- Distance between stages prevents organizing around enthusiasm instead of insight
- The architect groups ideas and shapes underlying arguments only after a break ([the four roles](/sources/madman-architect-carpenter-and-judge.md))
- The framework applies beyond writing, to strategy, teams, and building AI systems
