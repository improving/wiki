# CAMH

CAMH (the Centre for Addiction and Mental Health) is Canada's largest mental health teaching hospital. It appears in this corpus as a case study in extending, rather than replacing, on-premises high-performance computing with a governed cloud option built with Improving and Google Cloud.

## Research Computing Modernization

- CAMH's on-prem HPC cluster suffered high CapEx, underutilization, limited elasticity, and lengthy provisioning cycles.
- Its Krembil Centre for Neuroinformatics needed short-term access to large compute and AI/ML hardware not available on-prem, without requiring cloud expertise from researchers.
- Improving hardened Google Cloud's prototype Research Accelerator Portal for daily research use and simplified it so non-expert users could launch compute via a web interface.
- Provisioning that once required manual intervention now runs rapidly through a guided web interface.
- CAMH realized an estimated 40% cost reduction versus equivalent on-prem infrastructure, and the work frames the future of research computing as hybrid by design.
