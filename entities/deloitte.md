# Deloitte

Deloitte is one of the large professional-services firms whose delivery model and staffing are being reshaped by AI. Across this corpus it appears twice: as an example of the industry-wide restructuring now underway, and as part of the wider move toward [forward deployed engineering](/concepts/forward-deployed-engineering.md) as a baseline capability rather than a differentiator.

## Overview

Deloitte's relevance here is as a barometer for the [consulting talent model](/concepts/consulting-talent-model.md). When a firm of its scale reorganizes practices around AI, it signals that the change is structural rather than experimental.

## Key Claims

- Deloitte is restructuring practices as AI absorbs the document-heavy work first- and second-year analysts used to do (per [The Restructuring Is Not Coming. It's Here.](/sources/the-restructuring-is-not-coming-its-here.md)).
- It sits alongside peers making similar moves: [Accenture](/entities/accenture.md) announced 11,000 layoffs in an $865 million AI restructuring, and KPMG cut 400 US advisory roles.
- The emerging shape is fewer senior people augmented by AI, with growth concentrated in AI-aligned roles — a [labor compression](/concepts/labor-compression.md) effect rather than a simple headcount cut.
- In the FDE framing, converting engineers to forward-deployed roles is a capability everyone can now field, so differentiation shifts to closeness to the business problem and driving outcomes.
