# Camp Gladiator

Camp Gladiator is a group fitness program built around outdoor, coach-led workouts. In this corpus it appears as the thing that broke a comfortable gym routine and showed that community, not programming, sustains commitment.

## Key Claims

- A comfortable routine can be mistaken for growth; the camp disrupted a stale gym habit ([dragging me to a camp](/sources/dragging-me-to-a-workout.md))
- The people and banter, more than the workout, keep members returning
- Mixing up workouts benefits the body and breaks stale movement patterns
- Group training appears alongside the daily supplement, peptide, eating, and sleep protocols in a health snapshot ([health journey](/sources/health-journey-current-state.md))
