# Neil Peart

Neil Peart was the drummer and lyricist of the band Rush, quoted in two Improving essays for the same idea: "If you choose not to decide, you still have made a choice." The line anchors arguments that tolerating the status quo is itself a decision.

## Use in This Corpus

- In "What You Don't Change, You Choose," Blake McMillan pairs the Peart line with a remark from his wife to argue that non-cross-functional teams are choosing delays, dependencies, and coordination waste by not changing; the fix is to start small by embedding missing skills.
- In "Strategic Demand Management: What's the Rush?", Kris Wickett uses the same line while arguing businesses must balance demand against fixed team capacity using systems thinking, early viability tests, and WIP limits.
