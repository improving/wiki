# Gallup

Gallup is a research and polling firm whose workplace surveys are cited across the corpus on engagement, quiet quitting, and AI adoption. Its figures supply the quantitative spine for several Improving and partner-authored essays on culture and management.

## Cited Findings

- A Gallup poll found at least half of American workers admit to doing the bare minimum to meet their job description — the anchor for an essay on quiet quitting.
- Gallup found 45% of US employees now use AI at work, nearly doubling from 21% in 2023.
- Gallup's State of the Global Workplace 2026 found employees whose managers actively support AI use are 8.7 times more likely to say AI transformed their work.
- The same work found manager engagement has declined at nearly three times the rate of employee engagement over three years.
