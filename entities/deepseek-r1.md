# DeepSeek R1

DeepSeek R1 is an open-weight reasoning model whose release became a market event, and in this corpus it serves mainly as a script: when a rival open-weight model later won benchmark categories, the market "replayed its DeepSeek R1 script" without verifying the capability claim.

## Overview

- A semiconductor selloff triggered by another open-weight benchmark win reflected leveraged, headline-reactive capital, not a change in AI fundamentals.
- The market replayed its DeepSeek R1 script without verifying the capability claim.
- Integration work and enterprise context, not model weights, are the durable moat.
- Without a feedback loop, agentic systems are just expensive software; RLVR replaces annotation pipelines with verifiable outcomes like tests.
- Most systems implement only working memory, so they reset every session.
