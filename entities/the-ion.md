# The Ion

The Ion is Houston's innovation hub, which partnered with [Improving](/entities/improving.md) to deliver "Ion Startup University powered by Improving" — a free, open-to-the-public curriculum on business topics for local entrepreneurs.

## Partnership

- The program is free, open to the public, and runs six months, twice a year, with new sessions added annually.
- Sessions run bi-weekly, each with 45 minutes of expert-led content followed by hands-on workshops.
- The first three sessions covered product thinking ("Product – Start Well"), pitching ("Pitch & Sell"), and networking ("Why – Networking").
- Improving's [Devlin Liles](/entities/devlin-liles.md) and the Ion's Jan E. Odegard framed the partnership as widening access to startup resources in [Houston](/entities/houston.md).
- Attendees may take the full curriculum or individual sessions as needed.
