# Netflix

Netflix appears in this corpus as a reference case for strategic risk-taking — the bold bet that the surrounding writing contrasts with cautionary tales of unchecked ambition — and again in leadership writing about how organizations handle being wrong.

## Key Claims

- One post argues unchecked ambition without risk assessment leads to disaster, citing Blockbuster, while crediting boldness backed by meticulous market research, with Apple's iPhone as the stated example ([strategic risk-taking](/sources/navigating-strategic-risk-taking.md)).
- Nokia's complacency and lack of strategic foresight cost it the smartphone market, reinforcing the cost of not acting on a shifting market ([strategic risk-taking](/sources/navigating-strategic-risk-taking.md)).
- Listing assumptions and unknowns beats weighing pros and cons for surfacing hidden risk ([strategic risk-taking](/sources/navigating-strategic-risk-taking.md)).
- The mistake-handling post holds that owning errors builds team trust and models honesty ([being wrong](/sources/how-do-you-handle-being-wrong.md)).
- Root-causing a mistake with feedback and data comes before correcting it, and a corrective plan plus reflection turns mistakes into growth ([being wrong](/sources/how-do-you-handle-being-wrong.md)).
- Deflecting blame and offering excuses wreck credibility and morale ([being wrong](/sources/how-do-you-handle-being-wrong.md)).
