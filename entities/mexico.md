# Mexico

Mexico is the anchor of Improving's Latin American [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md) strategy and the country its writers profile most often as the answer to US companies seeking nearby, time-zone-aligned engineering talent. Improving entered the market through the 2020 [iTexico](/entities/itexico.md) merger and has deepened its presence through later acquisitions such as [Zigatta](/entities/zigatta.md).

## Why Mexico for Nearshore

- Graduates over 130,000 engineers annually and holds a talent pool exceeding 700,000 skilled engineers.
- Most of Mexico is within a five-hour flight of the US, with direct nonstop flights from major hubs, enabling hands-on relationships.
- Offers lower labor cost than India or Eastern Europe while maintaining quality, plus strong cultural affinity and English proficiency.
- The government supports IT growth through technology parks, innovation centers, and digital-skills programs.
- Key hubs include [Guadalajara](/entities/guadalajara.md) ("Silicon Valley of Mexico"), Mexico City, Monterrey, and Tijuana.

## Improving's Footprint

- The iTexico merger added more than 300 professionals and combined annualized revenue above $115 million, giving Improving nearshore teams in Guadalajara and Aguascalientes plus a local Austin presence.
- Zigatta, a Frisco firm with two Guadalajara acquisitions, pushed annualized revenue past $275 million and deepened the Latin America footprint.
- Improving's leadership positions Mexico as a nearshore delivery engine within a global delivery network spanning LATAM, India, the USA, and Canada.
- Writers argue the relationship is [stakeholder-oriented](/concepts/stakeholder-orientation.md) — collaborating with chambers, institutes, academia, and local governments — rather than resource extraction.
