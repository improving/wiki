# Faizan Fahim

Faizan Fahim is an Improving writer who authors the firm's outsourcing buyer's guides. His posts survey nearshore and offshore sourcing, compare commercial engagement models, profile LATAM delivery hubs, and give clients checklists for vetting a services partner — consistently funneling toward [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s hybrid LATAM-plus-India delivery model.

## Work

- Authored the region-by-region comparison of outsourcing hubs, weighing Asia Pacific, Latin America, Eastern Europe, and the Middle East & Africa on cost, talent, time zones, and stability, and recommending a hybrid model that blends a collaboration-friendly region with a scale-friendly one.
- Wrote the seven-model comparison of offshore engagement structures — fixed price, dedicated team, time & material, staff augmentation, managed services, SLA/milestone, and hybrid — arguing that mismatch between expectations and model drives overruns.
- Updated the nearshore explainer, which reports 87% of IT organizations have considered nearshoring and cost savings of 40–70% versus onshore.
- Wrote the 2025 Guadalajara guide, noting the city hosts 1,000+ tech companies and that local developers earn about $36,833 a year.
- Wrote the twelve-point offshore partner vetting guide, warning of "ghost firms," IP hostage-taking, and hidden subcontracting in a $150B+ market.

## Themes

- Location selection trades cost, talent depth, communication, time-zone overlap, and stability; no hub wins on every dimension.
- Delivery maturity and governance matter more than raw coding ability.
- Cultural and communication challenges hit roughly 54% of offshore projects.
