# Microsoft Copilot

Microsoft Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant threaded across its products, appearing in this corpus as a Teams meeting assistant, a Power BI report generator, a Planner helper, and — through Copilot Studio — a low-code platform for building copilots over corporate knowledge.

## Key Claims

- In [Microsoft Teams](/entities/microsoft-teams.md), Copilot transcribes meetings, generates summaries and action items, tracks goals and agenda, and integrates with calendar and email; one consultant estimates 4–10 hours saved per week.
- In [Power BI](/entities/power-bi.md), Copilot translates natural-language questions into queries and builds reports, but still requires a usable data model containing the right artifacts organized for analysis.
- Copilot Studio builds on Power Virtual Agents and lets power users create copilots over corporate knowledge via templates or from scratch, deploying them in Teams and SharePoint.
- In Microsoft Planner, Copilot assists with plan creation and generating status reports.
- It is named as an off-the-shelf option (alongside Google Gemini and Amazon Q) in Improving's AI maturity roadmap, and Copilot Studio appears in its list of off-the-shelf platforms; M365 Copilot costs roughly $30 per user per month.
- Its practical value depends on underlying data readiness, not on the assistant alone.
