# MangoChango

MangoChango is a Dallas-based technology consulting and custom software firm whose primary developer workforce sits in Guatemala, acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) in September 2025 to expand its LATAM development capabilities and push annualized revenue past $300 million.

## Company

- Founded in 2019; Dallas-based for sales and client operations, with its developer workforce in Guatemala.
- Acquired by Improving on September 9, 2025; terms were not disclosed.
- Leadership stayed in place and continued to lead the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Deal

- Expected to increase Improving's annualized revenue to exceed $300 million.
- Expanded Improving's LATAM development capabilities and nearshore delivery capacity.
- CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) cited MangoChango's technical expertise and agile delivery model; founder Rene Larrave framed the deal as expanding impact.
- Listed among the acquisitions (with iTexico, Leniolabs, and [Zigatta](/entities/zigatta.md)) that deepened Improving's nearshore footprint in [Guatemala](/entities/guatemala.md) and [Latin America](/entities/latin-america.md).
