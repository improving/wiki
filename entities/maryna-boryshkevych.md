# Maryna Boryshkevych

An Improving writer and author of a three-part series on [application modernization](/concepts/application-modernization.md). The series moves from defining legacy versus modern applications, through strategy and the seven R options, to the delivery practices — DevOps, microservices, and containers — that make modernization succeed.

## Topics and Work

- Defines legacy applications by their symptoms (hard to maintain, hard to integrate, insecure, costly, unautomated) and modern ones by resilient modular design, streaming data, containers, microservices, and cloud — arguing modernization enhances existing systems rather than replacing them.
- Lays out how to start: engage stakeholders, audit applications for business criticality and technical debt, and match the seven R strategies (retain, retire, repurchase, rehost, replatform, refactor/rearchitect, rebuild) to objectives, while preparing for the cultural shift.
- Details the delivery practices: DevOps maturity and CI/CD, DevSecOps with DORA KPIs, and agile testing, plus decomposing a monolith into microservices by domain-driven bounded context, each with its own database and pipeline.
- Notes that a modernized base enables AI, streaming, IoT, and analytics while improving security posture and compliance.
