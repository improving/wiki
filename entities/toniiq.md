# Toniiq

Toniiq is a supplement vendor trialed in the Health Journey series. It appears first as a deliberate alternative to anonymous Amazon sellers and later as one of the supplements credited in a striking [biological age](/concepts/biological-age.md) result — before the author retracts his recommendation over supply problems.

## Key Claims

- Toniiq was chosen over anonymous Amazon sellers because unknown supplement quality was treated as a gamble.
- The stack trialed: Resveratrol+ 98%, PQQ, NAC 98%, FŪEL 99%, and Berberine 1500.
- The author dropped 10 lbs in two weeks with diet and exercise unchanged, which he called interesting but not direct evidence, and planned a 90-day observation.
- Toniiq supplements were later credited, alongside IDLife and AG1, in a six-month drop in biological age of nearly 7.5 years (47.6 to about 40).
- UPDATE: the author retracted the recommendation because Toniiq became unreliable at shipping and communicated poorly.
