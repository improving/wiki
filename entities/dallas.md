# Dallas

Dallas is the anchor city of the DFW metroplex. Across this corpus it appears in two registers: as a technology hub whose ranking jumped, and as the setting of the author's own medical story.

## Key Claims

- Site Selection ranked Dallas the number one tech hub in North America ([Dallas just topped D.C.](/sources/dallas-topped-dc.md))
- DFW added more tech jobs than any U.S. metro from 2022 to 2024, and Wistron is investing $761 million in AI supercomputers in Fort Worth
- DFW is one of few places where the full AI stack — talent, demand, infrastructure, and capital — converges
- In a personal register, a misdiagnosed back-pain case in Dallas resolved only after self-advocacy and a second specialist's different differential ([Tarlov cysts and back pain](/sources/tarlov-cysts-and-back-pain.md))
- The medical system, that post argues, is not set up to advocate for the patient
