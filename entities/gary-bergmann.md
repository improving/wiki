# Gary Bergmann

An Improving Agile coach and writer on why [agile transformations](/concepts/agile-transformation.md) stall and how to recover them. His "Unseen Impact" series diagnoses cognitive and process traps, while "Go Lean or Go Home" and "Like Water on Rock" supply the lean values and pacing that make change stick.

## Topics and Work

- Argues [confirmation bias](/concepts/confirmation-bias.md) leads coaches and organizations to apply familiar frameworks regardless of fit, and offers five countermeasures, including returning to Agile/lean values and coaching mindset before tools.
- Diagnoses the process-adoption trap — Scrum Masters acting as process cops, estimates used to show readiness, retros with no action — and prescribes shifting from process to practice so teams own the "why."
- Prescribes recovery by re-anchoring to the [Agile Manifesto](/entities/agile-manifesto.md)'s four values and the five Scrum values (commitment, courage, focus, openness, respect).
- Walks through Lean's seven principles — eliminate waste, build quality in, create knowledge, defer commitment, deliver fast, respect people, optimize the whole — mapping each to concrete Agile team practices.
- Uses the water-on-rock metaphor to argue transformation is slow, culture-first work: understand the team, earn trust, then introduce process changes.
