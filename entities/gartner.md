# Gartner

Gartner is the analyst firm whose Magic Quadrant shifts, adoption forecasts, and failure-rate predictions are cited throughout this corpus as the backdrop for governance, observability, and platform-engineering decisions.

## Key Claims

- Gartner predicts 80% of data and analytics governance initiatives will fail by 2027.
- Gartner retired its Metadata Management Magic Quadrant for Active Metadata Management in 2021, then replaced metadata management with a governance platforms quadrant, signaling consolidation into the catalog.
- Gartner projects 40 percent of enterprise apps will embed task agents by end of 2026.
- Only 8% of organizations rated their data governance programs highly successful in 2020, with behavioral barriers topping budget as the cause.
- The observability market will reach $14.2 billion by 2028.
- Gartner projects 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027, with unclear value, escalating costs, and governance gaps as the causes.
- It projects 40% of enterprises will demote or decommission agents by 2027, arguing agents die from governance design rather than technical failure.
- It says uniform governance across agents leads to failure, and that mature organizations roll back agents fastest because governance surfaced buried problems.
- 65% of enterprises deploying AI agents had a confirmed security incident by April 2026.
- Gartner estimates $234 billion of enterprise SaaS spending is at risk by 2030 as agents calling APIs bypass per-seat licenses.
