# Peter Attia

Dr. Peter Attia is the longevity physician and author of *Outlive* whose work prompted the rapamycin experiment in this corpus's health journey. He represents the measurement-first, healthspan-oriented strand of the author's protocol.

## Key Claims

- Attia's *Outlive* is the stated prompt for the rapamycin experiment ([healthspan and rapamycin](/sources/health-journey-healthspan.md)).
- Rapamycin inhibits mTOR, mimicking caloric restriction and triggering autophagy ([healthspan and rapamycin](/sources/health-journey-healthspan.md)).
- Weekly dosing on rest days is used so the intervention does not interfere with muscle building ([healthspan and rapamycin](/sources/health-journey-healthspan.md)).
- The health-journey update's insistence on data over subjective feeling, and on individualized rather than one-size-fits-all protocols, extends the same evidence-driven stance ([health journey update](/sources/health-journey-update.md)).
- That stance is backed by Function Health six-month baselines plus intermediate testing and a Levels CGM ([health journey update](/sources/health-journey-update.md)).
