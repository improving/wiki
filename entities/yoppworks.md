# YoppWorks

YoppWorks is an Ottawa-based application-development and cloud-native consultancy that [Improving](/entities/improving.md) acquired under its Enterprise Strategy, pushing Improving's collective annualized revenue past $200 million and deepening its Canadian footprint. The firm is known in this corpus both for its Google Cloud public-sector work and for the culture story behind its name.

## Company

- Founded in 2016 in Ottawa, Ontario; specializes in Scala, Akka, cloud-native technologies, and Reactive Architecture.
- Describes itself as an application and infrastructure modernization leader using Reactive Architecture, Domain-Driven Design, and Agile.
- CEO Jack Gulas called the Improving deal "1+1=3" and named Lightbend and Google Cloud as partners.
- Leadership stayed in place after the acquisition and continued to run and grow the business.

## Google Cloud Public Sector Work

- Named the 2023 Google Cloud Public Sector Partner of the Year for Canada, announced from Ottawa on August 29, 2023.
- Clients included National Defence Canada, Health Canada, and the governments of Ontario, Quebec, British Columbia, and New Brunswick.
- Recognized work: a scientific computing portal provisioning HPC, AI, and ML resources on demand, Document AI backlog and ATIP workflows, and cloud landing zones.

## Culture

- The name traces to Dr. Seuss's *Horton Hears a Who!*: a quiet Who shouts "Yopp!" and saves Whoville, read as a statement that every voice matters.
