# CodeLaunch

CodeLaunch is a free, zero-equity, high-velocity startup accelerator and pitch competition that [Improving](/entities/improving.md) produces, pairing early-stage founders with volunteer engineering teams who build real MVP features at no cost. It came to Improving through the [Code Authority](/entities/code-authority.md) merger and has grown into a staged, tournament-style event.

## Model

- Free to founders, with no equity taken and no strings attached.
- Professional Coder League (PCL) teams — a roster made up entirely of Improvers — donate two full days (two 10-hour days) of engineering to build real MVPs, prototypes, and features.
- Teams include seasoned engineers, architects, designers, and technical leaders who volunteer for the high-intensity build.

## Events

- The CodeLaunch World Championship is staged like a live show, with motion graphics, paced performances, audience participation, and judges questioning founders; Well Water Finders won every award and $50,000 in one finale.
- At CodeLaunch 2026, a four-person team built a TravelSpoken booking feature and finished the 20-hour marathon nearly three hours early, crediting 8–10 hours of upfront tech-stack review and a refined playbook.
- Improving does not just support CodeLaunch — it produces it, and planned to accelerate the event into additional markets.
