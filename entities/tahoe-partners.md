# Tahoe Partners

Tahoe Partners is a Chicago professional services firm specializing in cloud and Microsoft digital transformation that [Improving](/entities/improving.md) acquired, lifting collective annualized revenue past $185 million and expanding Improving's Midwest reach.

## Company

- Headquartered in Chicago, Illinois.
- A Microsoft Gold partner for over 10 years, specializing in digital strategy, experience design, project management, and technology implementation.
- Leadership stayed intact and continued to operate and grow the business under Improving's "Enterprise Strategy."

## Deal

- Expected to raise Improving's collective annualized revenue above $185 million and expand its Midwest geographic reach.
- Improving CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md), alongside Tahoe's Ray Conrad and Ralph Keipert, framed the merger as alignment on culture, quality, and Microsoft capability.
