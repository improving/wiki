# GlobalLogic

GlobalLogic, a Hitachi Group company, is a digital engineering services firm that appears in the corpus not as a competitor profile but as the previous employer of two Improving leaders. Its executives' track records at GlobalLogic are the credentials Improving cites when announcing senior hires.

## Where It Appears

- Ashok Iyengar was SVP and Head of the Hitachi Business Unit at GlobalLogic, one of three founding leaders of the post-acquisition synergy organization, where he scaled the unit to over $275 million across Energy, Mobility, Rail, and Connected Industries; he became Improving's EVP of Global Services.
- Vasu Sarangapani served as Chief Growth and Sales Officer at GlobalLogic, leading 28 consecutive quarters of expansion and growing revenue from $250 million to over $900 million; he joined Improving's board of directors.
