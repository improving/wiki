# AG1

Athletic Greens' AG1 is a daily greens powder and a fixture of the author's [supplement stacking](/concepts/supplement-stacking.md). Across the health-journey posts it appears both as a standalone product review and as one component of a broader longevity protocol measured through [biomarker testing](/concepts/biomarker-testing.md).

## Overview

A first-hand review found the drink's taste acceptable and its solubility in water good, with noticeable energy and focus changes appearing within days. Its ingredient list informed adjustments to other supplements. The travel packets are awkward to pour into narrow water bottles.

By January 2025 AG1 sat in a daily stack spanning AG1, IDLife, NAD, and other supplements split across morning, lunch, and evening.

## Key Claims

- AG1 tastes acceptable for a greens drink and dissolves well in water.
- Noticeable energy and focus changes appeared within days of first use.
- The product's ingredients informed adjustments to other supplements.
- AG1 is one component of a daily stack that also includes [IDLife](/entities/idlife.md) and NAD.
- Six months of supplementation, recovery, and diet — with workouts unchanged — reversed biological age by nearly 7.5 years per [Function Health](/entities/function-health.md) testing.
