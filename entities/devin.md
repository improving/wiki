# Devin

An autonomous coding agent that the corpus uses as the emblem of the gap between polished demos and production delivery. Two posts cite it while arguing that benchmark scores and single-agent gains overstate what these tools deliver. A later source inverts the framing: Devin is also the vehicle for Cognition's $10M AI Productivity Guarantee, which makes the vendor financially accountable for measurable engineering value.

## Overview

New benchmarks found coding agents resolving only 13–39% of real merge-ready enterprise tasks, far below demo claims — a [benchmark validity](/concepts/benchmark-validity.md) problem. Separately, single-agent tools are said to plateau at a 15–50% ceiling, the [single-agent ceiling](/concepts/single-agent-ceiling.md), because one perspective cannot hold every competing delivery concern.

## Key Claims

- FrontierCode grades merge-readiness with maintainers; Claude Opus 4.8 scored 13.4% on the hardest tasks.
- SaaSBench found over 95% of agent failures happen before the agent reaches business logic.
- RoadmapBench tasks touch a median of 3,700 lines across 51 files.
- Single agents optimize one objective and collapse competing concerns; orchestration architecture matters more than model quality.
- Cognition guarantees Devin's engineering value, funding usage up to $10M rather than promising vague ROI.
- The guarantee requires estimating human-equivalent engineering hours per session, turning value into a countable unit.
- Uber spent its entire 2026 AI budget in four months, the contrast case for unprovable AI spend.
