# Ernst & Young

Ernst & Young (EY) is a global professional services firm that appears in the corpus in two roles: as the sponsor of the Entrepreneur Of The Year award that recognized Improving's CEO, and as a source of cited research on AI productivity gaps. It also shows up as an institution advising North Texas on AI governance.

## Entrepreneur Of The Year

- EY runs the Entrepreneur Of The Year award; Improving CEO Curtis Hite was a finalist for the 2025 Southwest Award.
- Hite was also a finalist for the 2018 award, selected from 900 nominees in the North Texas, Arkansas, Oklahoma, and Kansas-Wichita region.
- Finalists are judged on entrepreneurial spirit, purpose, growth, and impact.

## Research and Regional Role

- EY found companies leave up to 40% of productivity gains on the table due to talent-strategy gaps, cited in an argument about AI training.
- EY's innovation lead was named among Dallas-Fort Worth leaders advising the region on AI governance.
