# Gary Chapman

Gary Chapman is the author of *The 5 Love Languages*, the relationship framework that Improving writer Mark Runyon adapts to the workplace in "The 5 Love Languages of Leadership." The adaptation argues that employees, like partners, need appreciation expressed in the specific way each person values.

## The Adaptation

- Runyon proposes five "love languages of leadership": recognition, professional growth, autonomy, financial fairness, and feedback.
- Recognition is not one-size-fits-all — some want private praise, others public acknowledgment — so leaders must ask how each person prefers it; a Great Place to Work survey found recognition the top driver of great work.
- A LinkedIn Workforce Learning Report found 94% of employees would stay longer at a company that invests in their growth.
- A PwC survey found 60% of employees want feedback weekly or daily, making annual reviews insufficient.
- The unifying point is curiosity and honest communication about what motivates each person.
