# Eric Evans

Eric Evans is the author of *Domain-Driven Design*, the book Improving's microservices writing recommends for breaking a system into business domains. In "Microservices Architecture: Moving Past the Buzz Words," Robyn Fuentes argues that clients pursue microservices for scalability without understanding the trade-offs, and that Evans's domain-driven decomposition is what keeps the shift from failing.

## Use in This Corpus

- Microservices bring structural complexity, forced integration patterns and duplicate storage across services, and cross-cutting concerns that spread through every service.
- Avoiding microservices' *intentional* disadvantages can leave a "microservice-monolith" with the worst of both approaches.
- Shifting architecture without changing the design process recreates worse problems, so the post recommends domain-driven design to decompose complexity into business domains.
- Success also requires automation and DevOps discipline, extensive testing, and clear integration patterns.
