# SAFe

SAFe (the Scaled Agile Framework) is one of the most widely used frameworks for scaling agile practice across a large enterprise. In this corpus it is treated as one option among several rather than a default — a tool whose fit depends on context, and one that coaches can over-apply through confirmation bias.

## Where It Appears

- SAFe is named among the **four most popular scaling frameworks**, alongside Nexus, LeSS, and Disciplined Agile.
- The corpus's recurring argument is that **there is no best framework across the board** — fit depends on size, culture, and team maturity.
- It is cited as a framework Agile coaches may force regardless of fit, when [confirmation bias](/concepts/confirmation-bias.md) and sunk cost in a single certification path take over.
- SAFe's flow and value-delivery metrics are the team-level alternative to the individual story points that undermine [psychological safety](/concepts/psychological-safety.md).
