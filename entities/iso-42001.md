# ISO/IEC 42001

ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard specifying requirements for an AI management system — the management-system analogue of ISO 27001 for AI. In this corpus it is cited alongside [NIST AI RMF](/entities/nist-ai-rmf.md) as a framework that describes good governance but does not, by itself, enforce it.

## How It Appears Here

- It is described as a standard that "lists controls" — a catalogue of what good governance should cover — rather than a system that assigns who invokes each control at each stage.
- The [AI operating model](/concepts/ai-operating-model.md) is positioned as complementary: it assigns decision rights, where ISO 42001 and NIST AI RMF define the controls.
- A responsible-AI program must enforce its principles continuously; a one-time audit against a standard cannot demonstrate ongoing compliance.
- The four-layer agentic governance framework (risk rulebook, gating, runtime enforcement, evidence) is framed as the runtime scaffolding these standards imply but do not build.
