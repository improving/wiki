# Toronto

Toronto is the site of [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s Canadian headquarters and one of the anchors of its Canadian expansion. The city appears in this corpus as a talent pool, a delivery location, and a stop on the firm's partner and community programs.

## Improving's Presence

- The Canadian headquarters opened at 171 East Liberty in Toronto and is home to 100 employees.
- The office anchors further investment in [platform engineering](/concepts/platform-engineering.md) and data & AI services.
- [Jump Analytics](/entities/jump-analytics.md), the Canadian analytics firm Improving acquired, listed Toronto among its offices.
- Ontario's Minister of Economic Development cited the region's 70,000 annual STEM graduates and 420,000 tech workers.
- Improving supported the local tech community through [CodeLaunch](/entities/code-launch.md), which held its inaugural Canadian show in Toronto in August.

## Partner and Event Presence

- Toronto was a stop on Confluent's 2025 Data Streaming World Tour, which reached over 1,500 attendees across North America from Mexico City to Toronto.
- The city also features in the Confluent Enablement Partner of the Year recap that describes the same tour.
