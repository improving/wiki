# Lakeshore Learning

Lakeshore Learning is an educational retailer that appears in this corpus as a running case in Improving's agentic-AI writing. It is cited both as a fast, successful agent deployment and as the pipeline whose multi-step chain illustrates compounding reliability failure.

## Agentic Deployment

- In a deployment delivered in about 90 days, agents crawl funding and bond sites, scrape data, and map it into the sales queue.
- Within roughly 90 days, sales reps worked three times as many qualified leads with the process 42% faster.
- Improving's AI strategy buyer's guide lists the Lakeshore Learning agentic deployment as a headline case (delivered in 90 days) alongside its 250+ AI projects.

## As a Reliability Case Study

- A three-step Lakeshore pipeline is used to illustrate Lusser's law: per-step accuracy multiplies down a chain, so longer chains are less trustworthy end to end.
- It grounds the argument that the fix for multi-step failures is a quality gate at each transition rather than a better model.
