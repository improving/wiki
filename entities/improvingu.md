# ImprovingU

ImprovingU is [Improving](/entities/improving.md)'s internal training program, through which any employee can teach a class on a topic they know well. It is built on the idea that everyone is the smartest person in the room when their specialty comes up, and that teaching sharpens the teacher as much as it informs the class — an attempt to institutionalize [continuous learning](/concepts/continuous-learning.md) rather than treat it as a perk.

## How It Works

- Any employee can contribute a course; courses are **not limited to technology** — past sessions covered networking tips, wine tastings, maple syrup tapping, and SCUBA certification.
- Built by [Devlin Liles](/entities/devlin-liles.md) as President, it ran **two tiers**: a semester-based global tier (4–12 meetings) for structure across geography, and a slower quarterly local tier with journaling between sessions.
- Local topics were chosen by **dot-voting from a submitted backlog**, applying Agile prioritization to learning culture.
- It delivers virtual training and workshops drawing on perspectives from all 19 offices rather than job roles or tech stacks.

## Why It Matters Here

- The design argument: relying on intrinsic motivation alone grows self-directed learners but leaves capability on the table for everyone else; structured learning produces shared vocabulary, peer accountability without hierarchy, and compound depth.
- ImprovingU is one of three internal programs — with [PATH](/entities/path-program.md) and [Come Together](/entities/come-together.md) — credited for keeping 19 global offices collaborative.
- It is also cited as one of the ways Improving gives back to its community, alongside user-group events, volunteering, and open-source work.
