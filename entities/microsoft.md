# Microsoft

Microsoft is a platform and tooling vendor. Across this corpus it appears as a participant in the licensing-plus-hiring acquisition pattern, as a source of .NET tooling that shaped an author's talks, and as one of the firms whose AI pricing is becoming a meter on work.

## Key Claims

- Microsoft's Inflection deal used the same licensing-plus-hiring structure as Amazon-Adept and NVIDIA-Poolside, absorbing an AI team while avoiding a pre-merger antitrust filing ([buying the team](/sources/buying-the-team-without-buying-the-company.md))
- Its Entity Framework Code-First CTP release the day before a talk let the author rewrite the material overnight in four to five hours to stay current ([rewriting a talk](/sources/rewriting-a-talk-the-day-before-awesome.md))
- Flat-rate AI pricing was customer acquisition; every seat product is a meter in disguise, and spend follows new model releases with no price check ([the meter picks the winners](/sources/the-meter-picks-the-winners.md))
- A tokenizer change effectively raised prices up to 35% with no announcement, making cost per unit of finished work the metric a vendor cannot quietly move.
- Training-data provenance is becoming a procurement question after Anthropic's settlement ([the anthropic settlement](/sources/anthropics-book-settlement-drew-a-narrower-line-than-the-headlines-suggest.md))
- Microsoft canceled most internal Claude Code licenses as companies that said yes to everything AI face invoices that do not match their business cases — Uber burned its annual AI coding budget in four months ([the audit nobody wanted](/sources/the-audit-nobody-wanted.md))
- It is now competing with its own model partners, training salespeople to pitch against OpenAI and Anthropic and swapping their models out of Word and Excel ([the safe bet isn't safe anymore](/sources/the-safe-bet-isnt-safe-anymore.md))
- It committed $2.5 billion and 6,000 engineers to customer AI deployments ([the urgency trap](/sources/the-urgency-trap-is-why-your-ai-pilot-didnt-work.md))
- Joint research with McKinsey makes leadership the rate limiter on AI impact: 88% of organizations experiment while 81% report no bottom-line impact, and 67% of impact comes from culture, manager support, and talent practices ([the leadership gap](/sources/the-leadership-gap-just-got-a-number.md))
- Agent identity turns the enterprise directory into the governance battleground, the contest Microsoft's own identity stack competes in ([the day your agent got its own email address](/sources/the-day-your-agent-got-its-own-email-address.md))
- Its position in the platform war rests on profitable products embedding AI rather than dot-com math, though the urgency to deploy is outrunning the discipline to deploy well ([the bubble you are inside](/sources/the-bubble-you-are-inside-does-not-feel-like-one.md))
