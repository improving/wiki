# Trinity Hunt Partners

Trinity Hunt Partners is a growth-oriented middle-market private equity firm that took majority interest in Improving, the Dallas-based technology consultancy. It appears in this corpus as the capital partner behind Improving's acquisition-led expansion and its stated long-term vision.

## Partnership with Improving

- Trinity Hunt partnered with Improving, taking majority interest; co-founder and CEO Curtis Hite and the management team remained significant shareholders.
- The partnership was framed as accelerating growth through both organic initiatives and continued strategic acquisitions, aiming to build a market-leading North American provider.
- Trinity Hunt partner Blake Apel cited Improving's culture as its true differentiator for hiring and retaining top professionals.
- Improving had partnered with Trinity Hunt since 2019 and, as of a later board announcement, operated 20 offices across North and South America and India.

## Board and Governance

- A subsequent newsroom notice announced board appointments (Vasu Sarangapani and Raghu Chandra) tied to Improving's 10-Year Vision to be the "premier challenger brand" in digital services.
- That announcement again referenced the Trinity Hunt partnership as context for Improving's global expansion.
