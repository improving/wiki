# Claude

Claude is Anthropic's family of large language models. It recurs across this corpus as the subject of interpretability findings, character-based alignment experiments, coding-agent capability, and viral cost claims.

## Key Claims

- Anthropic engineers ship roughly 8x more code per quarter, over 80% written by Claude.
- Anthropic's J-space finding is an interpretability claim, not evidence of consciousness.
- The Parrhesia project trained first-person identity declarations, raising trait correlation from 0.44 to 0.87; the model scored 20/20 on golden prompts and 19/19 on hard sycophancy prompts.
- Hitting a $500M monthly bill on Claude Code pricing would require 2 to 3.3 million developer seats.
- RLHF optimizes for annotator engagement rather than helpfulness, producing sycophancy, overconfidence, and verbosity.
- Anthropic passed OpenAI in business AI subscription spending, 41% to 39.5%, while OpenAI pivoted toward government contracts and an IPO.
- Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation, and Claude holds 42–54% of the enterprise code-generation market share.
- Ramp's AI Index shows Claude passing GPT in business AI spend, so platform-agnosticism now reads as a hedge against a question the market has largely answered.
