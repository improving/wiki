# Guatemala

Guatemala is a Central American [nearshore outsourcing](/concepts/nearshore-outsourcing.md) destination that became an Improving development hub through the 2025 acquisition of [MangoChango](/entities/mangochango.md). It appears in this corpus as part of Improving's [Latin America](/entities/latin-america.md) delivery network and as a bilingual, time-zone-aligned alternative to offshore regions.

## Improving's Presence

- Improving acquired MangoChango on September 9, 2025; the firm was Dallas-based for sales and client operations with its developer workforce in Guatemala, founded in 2019.
- The deal was expected to push Improving's annualized revenue above $300 million and expand its LATAM development and nearshore delivery capacity.
- MangoChango's leadership stayed in place under the "Enterprise Strategy"; founder Rene Larrave framed the deal as expanding impact.
- Improving's LATAM centers, including Guatemala, are cited as part of a network of 600+ nearshore developers across Mexico, Guatemala, Chile, and Argentina.

## Why Guatemala

- Guatemala City is listed among key LATAM nearshore locations alongside Aguascalientes and Guadalajara, Buenos Aires, and Santiago.
- Described as an emerging bilingual nearshore hub with cultural affinity and a 4–8 hour overlap with US and Canadian hours.
- Guatemala has strong IP laws similar to the US and takes data protection seriously, easing [partner selection](/concepts/partner-selection.md).
