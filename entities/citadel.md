# Citadel

Citadel appears in this corpus as the counterparty in a leveraged fund's near-collapse and as a symbol of the concentrated, headline-reactive capital now trading around AI news.

## Overview

In one account, a fund running 4x leverage faced a blowup — at that leverage a 25 percent drawdown erases the capital base — and liquidated its whole book to Citadel before the July 30 open. A concentrated Anthropic stake up 620 percent masked the loss, so leveraged trading and conviction bets were reported as one 80 percent return.

## Key Claims

- At 4x leverage a 25 percent drawdown erases the entire capital base
- The fund liquidated its whole book to Citadel before the July 30 open
- A concentrated Anthropic stake up 620 percent masked the leveraged loss
- Leveraged trading and conviction bets are different risks reported as one number
- Leveraged, concentrated capital amplifies reactions to unverified headlines
