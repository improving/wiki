# NIST AI RMF

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework is a US reference framework for identifying and managing AI risk. In this corpus it functions as a foil: writers respect its structure while arguing it stops short of the data and runtime concerns that make AI governance real.

## Overview

The recurring critique is that the framework speaks in policy and risk categories but omits explicit data governance references and data steward roles, and that a document cannot see, stop, or detect a violation. Governance, these posts argue, must be a runtime control plane with policy, inventory, monitoring, and enforcement layers.

## Key Claims

- The framework omits explicit data governance references and data steward roles.
- EU AI Act Article 10 separately imposes data quality requirements on high-risk training data.
- A policy document cannot see, stop, or detect violations; a control plane can.
- Only 8 percent of organizations rated their governance program highly successful.
- Agents act at machine speed without the judgment that made human audit viable.
