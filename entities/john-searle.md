# John Searle

John Searle is a philosopher of mind and language whose Chinese Room thought experiment argues that symbol manipulation alone does not constitute understanding. This corpus uses that argument literally: production LLMs are the Chinese Room, producing competent-seeming behavior without causal reliability, which undermines behavioral benchmarks as evidence.

## Key Claims

- Behavioral benchmark scores and causal reliability on novel inputs are diverging.
- Distribution-shifted evaluations expose the gap standard accuracy tests hide.
- Graceful incompetence, calibration, and abstention are distinct architectural goals.
- Tool-using outputs are performatives: the utterance is the action, not a description.
- Incorrect read-only outputs are recoverable; wrong write actions are not.

## Work

Searle's speech-act vocabulary is repurposed to argue governance must scale with performative depth — intent validation, permission scoping, confirmation gates. The Chinese Room framing drives the case for evaluating distribution shift and graceful incompetence rather than accuracy scores, and for training-time disposition shaping over runtime-only filters.
