# Lowell Messner

Lowell Messner is a senior [Improving](/entities/improving.md) leader who heads the firm's corporate Microsoft Services Team, the body charged with maximizing the value of Improving's [Microsoft](/entities/microsoft.md) partnership across every enterprise. He appears in this corpus as the executive behind the firm's [Microsoft Partner](/concepts/microsoft-partner.md) strategy and as a product of its [acquisition strategy](/concepts/acquisition-strategy.md).

## Role at Improving

- Appointed by CEO Curtis Hite to lead the new corporate Microsoft Services Team, working with each Improving enterprise to help clients get more from their Microsoft investments.
- Joined [ProSource Solutions](/entities/prosource-solutions.md) in 2006 and continued as president after Improving acquired it in 2017.
- Served as president of Improving Cleveland until the Cleveland and Columbus offices combined under Josh Harrison, freeing him for the corporate role.
- Previously held leadership roles at National City Bank (now PNC), Fabri-Centers of America (now Joann), CCAi, and University Hospitals of Cleveland.

## Microsoft Partnership

- Improving is a Microsoft Managed Partner supporting M365, Azure, and D365 across account segments and industries.
- Messner cites the firm's US offices plus Canadian and Mexico resources as a competitive advantage few Microsoft partners can offer.
- Continued success in the service area was expected to expand the team.
