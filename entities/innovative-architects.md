# Innovative Architects

Innovative Architects is an Atlanta-based consultancy acquired by [Improving](/entities/improving.md) as its seventh enterprise location and its entry into the Southeastern United States.

## Company

- Based in [Atlanta](/entities/atlanta.md), Georgia, with satellite offices in Washington D.C. and Florida.
- A Microsoft Gold-Certified Partner focused on cloud computing, enterprise integration, and data-driven IT solutions.
- President [Scott McMichael](/entities/scott-mcmichael.md) and the leadership team stayed on to keep operating and growing the business.

## Deal

- Expected to grow Improving's annualized revenue by more than 30% in 2019, reaching close to $100 million collectively.
- Expanded Improving into the Southeastern US as its seventh enterprise location.
- Continued Improving's "Enterprise Strategy," pairing local ownership and incentives with geographically leveraged services.
- CEO [Curtis Hite](/entities/curtis-hite.md) cited the firm's Microsoft and cloud expertise and strong culture as accelerating Improving's regional goals.
