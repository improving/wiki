# Shribha Kalia

Shribha Kalia is an [Improving](/entities/improving.md) writer who produces the firm's partner buyer's guides — long comparison pieces that rank IT services, data, and platform vendors against selection criteria. The guides are structured and data-heavy, consistently ranking Improving favorably.

## Work

- Authored buyer's guides for nearshore software development partners, offshore software development companies, and managed services partners.
- Wrote comparison guides for platform engineering companies, legacy modernization and cloud replatforming, business intelligence, and data platform modernization.
- Authored "AI Strategy & Roadmap Assessment," framing enterprise AI failure as an execution-discipline problem rather than a budget one.
